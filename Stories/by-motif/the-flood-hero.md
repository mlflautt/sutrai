# Motif Index — The Flood Hero

## the-flood-hero

### Description

A hero who survives a great flood, often with the help of a divine warning and a vessel of preservation. The flood hero is the righteous or chosen individual who ensures the continuation of life after the deluge.

### Cultural Context

The flood hero motif reflects the universal human fear of catastrophic destruction and the hope of renewal through obedience, righteousness, or divine grace. The hero is typically warned by a deity and given instructions for survival—building a vessel, gathering seeds or animals, or finding refuge.

### Adaptation Notes

The flood hero provides a relatable protagonist through whom audiences experience the epic scale of destruction. The themes of divine judgment, obedience, and the preservation of life offer moral complexity. The ark or vessel as a microcosm of the world is a powerful visual symbol.
