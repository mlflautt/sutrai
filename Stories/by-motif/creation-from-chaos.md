# Motif Index — Creation from Chaos

## creation-from-chaos

### Description

The emergence of cosmic order from a primordial state of disorder, void, or confused mass. One of the most widespread creation motifs across cultures.

### Cross-Cultural Distribution

- **Mesopotamian** — Enuma Elish: Marduk slays Tiamat and creates the world from her body
- **Greek** — Hesiod's Theogony: Chaos, then Gaia, Tartaros, Eros
- **Norse** — Ginungagap, the meeting of fire and ice
- **Hebrew** — Tohu va-bohu (without form and void)
- **Chinese** — Pangu emerges from the cosmic egg
- **Hindu** — The Hiranyagarbha (golden egg)

### Cultural Context

The chaos-to-order motif reflects the universal human experience of imposing meaning on the unknown. Creation from chaos often involves separation (light from darkness, sky from earth, sea from land) and the establishment of boundaries that sustain life.

### Adaptation Notes

This motif offers strong visual and thematic potential. The transformation from formless void to ordered cosmos provides a clear narrative arc. The contrast between chaos and order can be expressed through color, light, and spatial composition.
