# Stories by Theme — Flood

## The Flood Theme

Flood narratives explore divine judgment, the destruction of corrupt humanity, and the preservation of life through obedience or righteousness. They address themes of renewal, covenant, and the cyclical nature of destruction and rebirth.

### Cross-Cultural Parallels

The flood motif appears across cultures: Mesopotamian (Gilgamesh, Atrahasis), Greek (Deucalion and Pyrrha), Hindu (Manu), and many others. The Hebrew account emphasizes covenant and divine promise; the Norse Ragnarok presents destruction as cyclical and necessary for rebirth.

### Adaptation Notes

Flood narratives offer epic scale, dramatic tension, and moral complexity. The ark as vessel of life, the destruction of the old world, and the emergence of the new provide powerful visual and thematic material. The covenant that follows destruction offers hope and resolution.
