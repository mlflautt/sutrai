# Stories by Theme — Creation

## The Creation Theme

Creation stories explore how the world, humanity, and order emerged from chaos or void. They address fundamental questions of existence, the relationship between humanity and the divine, and the establishment of cosmic order.

### Cross-Cultural Parallels

All three traditions share the motif of **creation from chaos** — a primordial void or confused mass from which order is established. The Hebrew account uses divine word; the Greek uses divine separation and Prometheus' craft; the Norse uses cosmic sacrifice (Ymer's body becomes the world).

### Adaptation Notes

Creation narratives offer epic visual scope and fundamental thematic resonance. The progression from chaos to order provides a clear narrative arc. Cross-cultural comparison reveals shared human concerns about origins and our place in the cosmos.
