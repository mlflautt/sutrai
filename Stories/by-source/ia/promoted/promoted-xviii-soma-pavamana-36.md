# Xviii Soma Pavamana 36

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7012-7029

XVIII.  Soma  Pavamana. 

For  first  possession  of  your  juice,  for  the  exhilarat- 
ing drink, 

Drive  ye  away  the  dog,  my  friends,  drive  ye  the 
long-tongued  dog  away  ! 

2  He  who  with  purifying  stream,  effused, comes flow- 

ing hitherward, 
Indu,  is  like  an  able  steed. 

3  With  prayer  all-reaching  let  the  men  tend unassail- 

able Soma  :  be 
The  stones  prepared  for  sacrifice !
