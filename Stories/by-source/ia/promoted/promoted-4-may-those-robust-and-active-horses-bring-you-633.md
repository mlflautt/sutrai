# 4 May Those Robust And Active Horses Bring You 633

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5270-5274

4. May those robust and active horses bring you, 
.(Ashwins), hither at the breaking of the dawn, and may 
these Soma juices prepared, showerers (of benefits), for 
your drinking, exhilarate you at this (our sacrifice).
