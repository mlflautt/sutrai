# 6 And Who Issuing Forth The First In Those Encount 860

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7021-7025

6. And who, issuing forth the first in those encounters, 
rushes in various directions with rows of chariots ; like 
an elegant (courser), friendly to man, decorated with a 
garland, raising the dust, and champing his bit.
