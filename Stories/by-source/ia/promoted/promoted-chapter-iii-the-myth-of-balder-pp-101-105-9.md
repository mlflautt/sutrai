# Chapter Iii The Myth Of Balder Pp 101 105 9

**Source:** The Golden Bough (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 484-494

Chapter  III. — The  Myth  of  Balder . Pp.  101-105 

How  Balder,  the  good  and  beautiful  god,  was  done  to  death  by  a 
stroke  of  mistletoe,  loi  sq.  %  story  of  Balder  in  the  older  Edda, 
102  sq.  ;  story  of  Balder  as  told  by  Saxo  Grammaticus,  103  ;  Balder 
worshipped  in  Norway,  104 ;  legendary  death  of  Balder  resembles  the 
legendary  death  of  Isfendiyar  in  the  epic  of  Firdusi,  104  sq.  ;  the  myth 
of  Balder  perhaps  acted  as  a  magical  ceremony  ;  the  two  main  incidents 
of  the  myth,  immely  the  pulling  of  the  mistletoe  and  the  burning  of  the 
god,  have  perhaps  their  counterpart  in  popular  ritual,  105.
