# 3 Verily He Was The Doer Of A Good Work In The 983

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7893-7897

3. Verily he was the doer of a good work in the' 
regions, who generated these two, Heaven and Earth, and, 
firm of purpose, gave an impulse by his deed to the two 
vast, immoveable, beautiful, unsupported worlds.
