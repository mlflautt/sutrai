# 5 The Maruts Harness Their Easy Going Chariot Mitr 1539

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11768-11773

5. The Maruts harness their easy-going chariot, Mitra 
and Varuna, for (the emission of) water, as a hero 
(harnesses his war-car): their forms traverse the different 
spheres to distribute the rain : do you, therefore, supreme 
rulers, shed upon us water from heaven.
