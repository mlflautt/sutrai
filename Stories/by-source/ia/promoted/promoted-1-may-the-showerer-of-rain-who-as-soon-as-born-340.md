# 1 May The Showerer Of Rain Who As Soon As Born 340

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3187-3191

1. May the showerer (of rain), who, as soon as born,, 
is the object of affection, protect the offerer of the effused 
libation: drink at will, Indra, before (the other gods), 
of the pure Soma juice mixed with milk.
