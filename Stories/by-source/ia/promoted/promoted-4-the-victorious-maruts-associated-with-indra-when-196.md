# 4 The Victorious Maruts Associated With Indra When 196

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2101-2109

4. The victorious ( Maruts) associated (with Indra when 
contending with Vritra) perceived a great light issuing 
from the darkness. The dawns recognizing him (as the 

32 Rig-veda Translation [lit 3.2: 

sun) arose, and Indra was the sole sovereign of the rays 
(of light).
