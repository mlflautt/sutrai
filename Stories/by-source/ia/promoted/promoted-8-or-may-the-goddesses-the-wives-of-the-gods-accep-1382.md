# 8 Or May The Goddesses The Wives Of The Gods Accep 1382

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10660-10665

8. Or may the goddesses, the wives of the gods, accept 
(the offering): Indrani, Agnayi, the radiant Ashwini, 
Rodasi, Varunani, may each hear (our prayer): may 
the goddesses partake (of the oblation): may the (per¬ 
sonified) season of the wives of the gods, accept it.
