# 3 You Priests Possess The Wise Lord Of Human Being 1044

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8307-8311

3. You (priests) possess the wise lord of human beings, 
the pure, the purifying, Agni, cherished with oblations of 
butter, the offerer of the burnt-offering, the all-knowing ; 
he among the gods bestows desirable (riches).
