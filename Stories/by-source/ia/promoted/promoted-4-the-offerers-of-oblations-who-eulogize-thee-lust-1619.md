# 4 The Offerers Of Oblations Who Eulogize Thee Lust 1619

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12365-12370

4. The offerers of oblations who eulogize thee, lustrous 
Ushas, with sacred hymns, become prosperous with 
affluence, (through thy favour), bestower of wealth, bright- 
born goddess, (who art) sincerely praised for (the gift of) 
horses.
