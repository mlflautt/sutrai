# 5 The Same Inscription Records That The Vienna Cod 17

**Source:** Facsimile Of An (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 626-633

5. The same inscription records that the Vienna Codex 
passed from the possession of Clement VII into that of 
another ecclesiastical member of the Florentine Medici 
family. A natural inference is that it must also, at one 
time, have been in Florence, in which city other rarities, 
specified in the inventory of 1519, were stored in the 
Medici palace until a short time ago.
