# 19 May The Messenger Of The Gods Engendered In Man 417

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3699-3703

19. May the messenger of the gods, engendered in many 
places, everywhere proclaim us void of offence: may 
earth and heaven, the waters, the sun, and the vast firma¬ 
ment, with the constellations, hear us.
