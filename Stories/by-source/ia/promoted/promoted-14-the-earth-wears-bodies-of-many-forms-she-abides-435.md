# 14 The Earth Wears Bodies Of Many Forms She Abides 435

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3816-3820

14. The earth wears bodies of many forms: she abides 
on high cherishing her year and a half old (calf): knowing 
the abode of the truth (the sun), I offer worship: great 
and unequalled is the might of the gods.
