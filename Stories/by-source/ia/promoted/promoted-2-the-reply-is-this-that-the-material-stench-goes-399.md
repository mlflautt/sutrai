# 2 The Reply Is This That The Material Stench Goes 399

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11326-11355

2. The reply is this, that the material stench goes 

as far and in such proportion as there are filthiness 
and fetidness in the stinking existences, and the 
spiritual stench goes unto there where there are 
appliances (s Am An 6) for acquiring stench , a misera- 
ble place ; on account of the separation (ga.rdih) of 
the sky, everywhere where it goes in the direction 
of the sky it does not reach to the undisturbed 
existences ^ 3. Information about the stench is 

manifest in the omniscient creator, whose^ omni- 
science is among the luminaries, that persistent 
creator and the primeval angels and archangels 
are free from its attack ; and his information 
about the deception which is practised upon that 
labourer for hell and mind allied with the demons ® 
is certain. 

* The sky being divided into three parts, and the uppermost part 
being inaccessible to evil (see Chap. jbiXVII, 24, 25). 

® Reading az'S-rSdi-mtnirnSih, but it is possible that aoA 
may have originally been khasdak, for the Av. khavzd, ‘male 
paramour,’ of Vend. VIH, 99, 103 (trans. D.).
