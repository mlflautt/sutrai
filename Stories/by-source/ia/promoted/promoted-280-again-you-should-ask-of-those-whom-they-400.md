# 280 Again You Should Ask Of Those Whom They 400

**Source:** The Text Is Fly (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11174-11188

280. Again, you should ask of those whom they 
call Mutazalik^ (281) thus: ‘Is it the will of the 
sacred being for all mankind to abstain from crime 
through their own free will^, to escape from hell, and 
to make them proceed to heaven, or not?’ 282. If 
one says that it is not, (283) that implies that an® 
opinion is formed by him as to the little goodness of 
the sacred being and the evil of his will ; (284) a 7 id, 
for the same reason, it is not fitting to glorify hun 
as the divine existence. 285. If one says that it is 
his will, (286) that implies that an opinion is formed 
by him as to the good will of the sacred being ; (287) 
and, for the same reason, it is fitting to glorify him 
as the divine existence.
