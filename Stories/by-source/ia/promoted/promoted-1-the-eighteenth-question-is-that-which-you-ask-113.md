# 1 The Eighteenth Question Is That Which You Ask 113

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3358-3363

1. The eighteenth question is that which you ask 
thus: When the souls of the righteous ami the souls 
of the wicked go out to the spirits, will it then be 
possible for them to see Ahharma^frt^ and Aharman^, 
or not ?
