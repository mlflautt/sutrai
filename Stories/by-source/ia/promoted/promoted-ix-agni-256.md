# Ix Agni 256

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15118-15132

IX.  Agni. 

INVINCIBLE  is  Agni,  he  who  goes  before  the  tribes 

of  men, 
A  chariot  swift  and  ever  new. 

2  By  bringing  offerings  unto  him  the  mortal  worship- 

per obtains 
A  home  from  him  whose  light  is  pure. 

3  Inviolable  power  of  Gods,  subduing  all  his  enemies, 
Agni  is  mightiest  in  fame.
