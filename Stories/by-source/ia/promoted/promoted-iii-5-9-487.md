# Iii 5 9 487

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4195-4202

III. 5. 9. 

This hymn is divided into six Trichas or triplets, the deities of 
■which are severally, Indra and Varuna, Brihaspati, Pushan, Savitri, 
Soma, and Mitra and Varuna : Vishwamitra is the Rishi, or, ac¬ 
cording to some, the last triplet is ascribed to Jam ad agni : the metre 
-of the three first stanzas is Trishtubh , of the rest Gayatri.
