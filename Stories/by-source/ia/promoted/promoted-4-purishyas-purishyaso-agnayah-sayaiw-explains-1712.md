# 4 Purishyas Purishyaso Agnayah Sayaiw Explains 1712

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13128-13142

4. Purishyas. — Purishyaso agnayah : Sayaiw explains 
the term, sikata sammlshrah , mixed with sand : Mahidhara , 
pashubhyo hitah , good for animals : Puri sham occurs in the 
Nirukta amongst the synon vines of water, and is elsewhere 
explained as that which pleases or which fills- -Nir. u. 22. 

With the Instruments.— Pravanebhih sajoshasah , the 
first, Sayana explains, mritkhananasadhanabhutair abhrya- 
dibhih ; abhrya and others, which arc 1 he implements for 
digging the earth : Mahidhara explains it by manansi ; minds, 
being pleased in mind, manasa pritiyuktali: his interpretation, 
however, seems to rest on a fancied etymology, pra-prakar- 
shena vananti-sambhajanti vishayan , what especially apprehend 
objects of sense.
