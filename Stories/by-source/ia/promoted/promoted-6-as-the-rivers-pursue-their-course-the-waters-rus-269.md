# 6 As The Rivers Pursue Their Course The Waters Rus 269

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2623-2627

6. As the rivers pursue their course, the waters rush to 
;the ocean, like the drivers of cars (to a goal), so the vast 
Indra (hastens) from his dwelling (in the firmament), 
when the humble Soma libation propitiates him.
