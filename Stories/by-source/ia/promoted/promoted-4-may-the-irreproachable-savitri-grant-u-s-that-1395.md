# 4 May The Irreproachable Savitri Grant U S That 1395

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10786-10791

4. May the irreproachable Savitri (grant) u»s that 
desirable (wealth): may the flowing rivers hasten to 
(convey) it to us ; for which purpose 1, the ministering 
priest of the sacrifice, repeat (pious praises): affluent 
in food, may we be the lords of (manifold) riches.
