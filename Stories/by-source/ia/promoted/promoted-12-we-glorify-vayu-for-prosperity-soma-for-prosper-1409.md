# 12 We Glorify Vayu For Prosperity Soma For Prosper 1409

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10887-10892

12. We glorify Vayu for prosperity, Soma for prosperity, 
he who is the protector of the world : (we praise) Brihas- 
pati (attended by) all the companies (of the deities), for 
prosperity, and for our prosperity may the Adityas be 
ours.
