# V Agni 347

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17722-17729

V.  Agni. 

THE  sacred  hymns  love  him  who  wakes  and  watches: 
to  him  who  watches  come  the  holy  verses. 

This  Soma  saith  to  him  who  wakes  and  watches, 
I  rest  and  have  my  dwelling  in  thy  friendship.
