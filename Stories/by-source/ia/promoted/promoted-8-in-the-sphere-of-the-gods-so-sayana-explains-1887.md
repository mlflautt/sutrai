# 8 In The Sphere Of The Gods So Sayana Explains 1887

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15171-15180

8. In the Sphere of the Gods.— So Sayana explains, 
no nabhih sadane ; nabhi , bandhanam, binding or fastening: 

342 

Rig-veda Translation 

devanam sthane , in the place of the gods, and sasminnudhan , 
sarvasmin yajne , in every sacrifice.
