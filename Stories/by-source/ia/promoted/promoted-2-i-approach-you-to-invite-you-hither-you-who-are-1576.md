# 2 I Approach You To Invite You Hither You Who Are 1576

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12057-12061

2. I approach you (to invite you) hither, you who are 
the encouragers of many ; (who are) the achievers of 
many (great) exploits, most excellent and irresistible : 
I invoke you, who are most mighty, for protection.
