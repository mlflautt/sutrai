# 2 Maghavan Who Fills His Belly With The Soma Is 1246

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9732-9736

2. Maghavan, who fills his belly with the Soma, is 
exhilarated (by drinking) of the sweet-savoured beverage, 
whereupon he has lifted up his destructive thousand- 
edged weapon, desiring to slay (the Asura) Mriga.
