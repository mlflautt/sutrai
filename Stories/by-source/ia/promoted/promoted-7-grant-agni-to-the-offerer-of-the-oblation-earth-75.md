# 7 Grant Agni To The Offerer Of The Oblation Earth 75

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1278-1283

7. Grant, Agni, to the offerer of the oblation, earth 
the giver of cattle the means of many sacred rites, such 
that it may long endure: may there be to us sons and 
grandsons, and may thy favour, Agni, be productive of 
good unto us.
