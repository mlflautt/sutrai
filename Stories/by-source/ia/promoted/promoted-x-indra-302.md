# X Indra 302

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16356-16372

X.  Indra. 

COME,  priest,  and  of  the savoury  juice pour  forth  a 

yet  more  gladdening  draught ! 
So  is  the  hero  praised  who  ever  prospers  us- 

2  Indra, whom  tawny coursers  bear, praise  such  as 

thine,  preeminent, 
None  by  his  power  or  by  his  goodness  hath  attained. 

3  We,  seeking  glory,  have  invoked  this  God  of  yours, 

the  Lord  of  wealth, 
Who  must  be  magnified  by  constant  sacrifice.
