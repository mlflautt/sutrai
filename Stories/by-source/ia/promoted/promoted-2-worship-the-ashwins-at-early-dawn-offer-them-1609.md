# 2 Worship The Ashwins At Early Dawn Offer Them 1609

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12264-12270

2. Worship the Ashwins at early dawn; offer them 
oblations : the evening is not for the gods ; it is un¬ 
acceptable to them ; and whether it be any other than 
ourselves who worships them or propitiates them, the 
worshipper who is foremost (in his devotion) is the most 
approved of.
