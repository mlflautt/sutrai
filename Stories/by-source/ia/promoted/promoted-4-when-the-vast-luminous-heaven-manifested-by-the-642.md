# 4 When The Vast Luminous Heaven Manifested By The 642

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5355-5360

4. When the vast luminous heaven manifested by the 
rays (of light) is displayed, then are (the deities) resplendent 
according to (their heavenly) abode: the chief of leaders, 
(Indra), in his approach has scattered the thick glooms 
so that men may see.
