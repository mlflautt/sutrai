# 7 Thou Hast Liberated The Fettered Shunahshepa Fro 1024

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8175-8179

7. Thou hast liberated the fettered Shunahshepa from 
a thousand stakes, for he was patient in endurance: 
so, Acni, free us from our bonds, having sat down here 
(at our sacrifice), intelligent offerer of oblations.
