# 4 Fierce Indra And Varuna You Hurled The Bright 882

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7171-7175

4. Fierce Indra and Varuna you hurled the bright* 
shining and most mighty thunderbolt against this (our 
foe), who is difficult to be resisted by us ; (who is) rapacious, 
malevolent: grant us strength to overcome him.
