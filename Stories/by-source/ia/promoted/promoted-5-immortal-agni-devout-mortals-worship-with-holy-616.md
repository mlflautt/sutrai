# 5 Immortal Agni Devout Mortals Worship With Holy 616

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5161-5165

5. Immortal Agni, devout mortals worship with holy 
rites thee the first deity (of the gods), whose tongue exhila¬ 
rates (them), the dissipator of sin, the humiliator (of the 
demons), the lord of the mansion, the unperplexed.
