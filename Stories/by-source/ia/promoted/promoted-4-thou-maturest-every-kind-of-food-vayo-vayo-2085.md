# 4 Thou Maturest Every Kind Of Food Vayo Vayo 2085

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17342-17347

4. Thou Maturest every Kind of Food. — Vayo vayo 
jarase yad dadhanah : the Scholiast interprets yada dharya - 
mano bhavasi tada sarvam annam jarayasi, when thou art 
being detained, then tliou causest all food to decay, i.e ., to 
digest.
