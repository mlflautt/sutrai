# 1 May The Illustrious Indra The Granter Of Desires 703

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5777-5782

1. May the illustrious Indra, the granter of desires, 
•come to us, whether from afar or nigh, for our protection: 
he who is the lord of men, armed with the thunderbolt, 
overcoming his foes in conflict and in combats, (attended) 
by the most illustrious (Maruts).
