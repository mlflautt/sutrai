# 5 Swarbhanu Swarbhanu Is A Name Of Rahu The 2137

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17897-17903

5. Swarbhanu. Swarbhanu is a name of Rahu, the 
personified ascending node, and the causer of an eclipse : he 
was a son of Kashyapa , by Danu, the mother of the Danavas, 
or Asuras : Vishnu Pur. p. 147 : another genealogy makes 
him the son of Viprachitli , by Sinhika , the sister of Hiranya - 
kashipu , ibid., p. 148.
