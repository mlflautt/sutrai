# 1 Worship The Two Who Come First Of The Gods At 1608

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12259-12263

1. Worship the two who come first (of the gods) at 
dawn: let them drink before the greedy withholders (of 
the offering); for the Ashwins verily claim the morning 
sacrifice : the ancient sages praised them (at dawn).
