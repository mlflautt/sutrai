# 11 The Golden Handed Soft Tongued Savitri Is Desce 408

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3644-3648

11. The golden-handed, soft-tongued Savitri is descend¬ 
ing from heaven (to be present) thrice (daily) at the sacri¬ 
fice : accept, Savitri, the praise (recited by) the worship¬ 
pers, and thereupon grant to us all our desires.
