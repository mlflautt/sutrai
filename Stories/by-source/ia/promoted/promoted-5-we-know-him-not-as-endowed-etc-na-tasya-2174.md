# 5 We Know Him Not As Endowed Etc Na Tasya 2174

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18283-18288

5. We Know Him not as Endowed, etc. —Na tasya 
vidma purushatvata vayam : the Scholiast says tasya is for 
tam, we do not know him, joined with or possessed of yuktam,. 
manliness, purushatvena , or the property of fulfilling desires,. 
kamanam purakatvena.
