# 1 Arousing Men To Their Labours The Adorable Dawn 1384

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10671-10676

1. Arousing (men to their labours), the adorable (Dawn) 
comes from the heaven, a mighty mother, the awakener 
of her daughter (earth) : pious, ever young, and glorified, 
(she comes), when invoked, to the chamber of sacrifice 
with the protecting (gods).
