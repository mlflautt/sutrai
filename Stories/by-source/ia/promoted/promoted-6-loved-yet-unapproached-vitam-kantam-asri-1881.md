# 6 Loved Yet Unapproached Vitam Kantam Asri 1881

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15104-15113

6. Loved yet Unapproached.— Vitam-kantam ; asri- 
tani, dahabhayadasevitam, bright or beloved, not honoured or 
served, through fear of being burnt. 

Seeking Oblations from any Quarter. —Kuchid 
artkinajn : the first is for kwachit , anywhere, anyhow ; seek¬ 
ing fuel, butter, etc., samidajyadihavih swikurvantam.
