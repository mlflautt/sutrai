# 11 The Whole World Agni Finds An Asylum In Thine 1004

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8038-8053

11. The whole world, (Agni), finds an asylum in thine 
effulgence, whether it be in the ocean, in the heart (of 
man), in the life (of living beings), in the assemblage of 
the waters, or in warfare: may we attain that sweet* 
flavoured wave which is established in thy (essence). 

'V. 1.1] 

Fifth Mandala 

173 

FIFTH MANDALA. 

ANUVAKA I.
