# 1 Offer Praise To The Company Of The Maruts The 1442

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11106-11111

1. Offer praise to the company of the Maruts,' the 
self-irradiating, the precipitators of mountains : present 
liberal oblations to the assuagers of heat, to those who 
come from the sky, to whom solemn rites are familiar, to 
the givers of abundant food.
