# 8 I Glorify You Leaders Of Rites Cherishers Of Man 1303

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10097-10103

8. I glorify you, leaders (of rites), cherishers (of many), 
gratifying (with oblations, you) Twashtri the lord of 
foundations, and the goddess of speech, bestower of opu¬ 
lence, and sharer in the satisfaction (of the other divinities) : 
also the lords of the forest, and the herbs, that I may 
obtain riches.
