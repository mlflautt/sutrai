# 12 Radiance Has First Proceeded To Thee Agni Who 505

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4380-4385

12. Radiance has first proceeded to thee, (Agni), who 
art glorified by praise, in the womb of the water, in the 
nest of the rain-cloud: the seven attached (priests) have 
engendered (praise) to the showerer (of benefits), who is 
desirable, ever young, embodied, and resplendent.
