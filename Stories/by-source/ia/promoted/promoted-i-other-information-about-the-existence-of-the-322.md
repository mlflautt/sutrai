# I Other Information About The Existence Of The 322

**Source:** The Text Is Fly (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9705-9713

I. Other information about the existence of the 
competitor, similarly testified by the Dinkaraf® manu- 
script (nipik), is here well noted for you. 2. For 
both this which is written above and that which is 
written here are all grown from the seed which the 
glorified Atfir-pa^/iyivand sowed, (3) and from the 
original thanksgiving (spis) of the supremely learned 
Atur-fr6bag, son of Farukh-ziisf, himself.
