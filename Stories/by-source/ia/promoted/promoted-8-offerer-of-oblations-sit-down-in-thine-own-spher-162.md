# 8 Offerer Of Oblations Sit Down In Thine Own Spher 162

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1870-1875

8. Offerer of oblations sit down in thine own sphere, 
for thou art cognizant (of holy acts), and station the 
institutor of the ceremony in the chief place of the sacred 
rite: cherisher of the gods, worship the gods, Agni, and 
bestow abundant food on the founder of the sacrifice.
