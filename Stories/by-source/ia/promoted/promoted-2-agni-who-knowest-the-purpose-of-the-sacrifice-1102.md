# 2 Agni Who Knowest The Purpose Of The Sacrifice 1102

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8715-8721

2. Agni, who knowest (the purpose of) the sacrifice, 
assent to this rite : be favourable to the copious showera 
of water: (endowed) with strength, (I undertake) not 
(to obstruct) sacred acts, nor adjure the rite with both 
(truth and untruth), but (repeat the praise) of the res¬ 
plendent showerer (of benefits).
