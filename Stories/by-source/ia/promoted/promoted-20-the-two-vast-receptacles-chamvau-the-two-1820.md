# 20 The Two Vast Receptacles Chamvau The Two 1820

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14422-14429

20. The Two Vast Receptacles.— Chamvau, the two 
vessels in which gods and men eat, chamanty adanty anayor 
devamanushyah: this would imply vessels or ladles, but the 
Scholiast also proposes, the two that are eaten or enjoyed 
bv living beings ; that is, heaven and earth. 

Page 74.
