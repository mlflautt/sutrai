# 9 Thy Auspicious Hands Indra Are The Doers Of Good 722

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5911-5916

9. Thy auspicious hands, Indra, are the doers of good 
deeds: thy two hands, Indra, are the extenders of wealth 
to him who praises thee: what, Indra, is this delay ? 
why dost thou not exhilarate us ? why art thou not de¬ 
lighted to make us gifts ?
