# 14 Therefore Agni When We Labour For Thee With 526

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4525-4531

14. Therefore, Agni, when we labour for thee with 
hands and feet, and all our members, the pious performers 
of rites, (the Angirasas ), exercise their arms in the work 
(of attrition), as wheelwrights fabricate a car.
