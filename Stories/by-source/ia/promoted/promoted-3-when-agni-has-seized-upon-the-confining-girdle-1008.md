# 3 When Agni Has Seized Upon The Confining Girdle 1008

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8070-8076

3. When Agni has seized upon the (confining) girdle 
of the aggregated (world), then, bright-shining , he makes 
all manifest with brilliant rays: thereupon the precious 
food-desiring (oblation) is added (to the flame), and Agni, 
soaring aloft, drinks it as it is (spread out) recumbent 
by the ladles.
