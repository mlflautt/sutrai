# 5 Divine Agni May The Sacrificial Food The Vast 1116

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8847-8852

5. Divine Agni, may the (sacrificial) food, the vast 
yielder (of benefits to men), the sustainer of riches, support 
the utmost of thy vigour; and do thou, considering 
(the means of acquiring) great riches, like a thief keeping 
concealed the article (he has stolen), be propitious to Atri.
