# 7 And That Swift Horse Enduring In Battle Bestowin 861

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7026-7031

7. And that swift (horse) enduring in battle, bestowing 
food, and doing service with his limbs, rushing swiftly 
upou the quick-moving (host of the enemy), going straight 
onward, and tossing up the dust, throws it above his 
brows.
