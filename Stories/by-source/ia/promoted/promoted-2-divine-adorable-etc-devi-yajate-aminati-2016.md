**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))

ukshamane , ritavari, adruha, deva-putre, yajnasya netri , are 
the epithets ; literally, the shining, or, as usually rendered in 
this translation, divine, objects of worship, unharming,
