# 5 In Like Manner At Thy Command They Stayed 2013

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16486-16504

5. In Like Manner at thy Command they Stayed.— 
Yatha-yatha-pa,tayantah. gachchantas. twain viyemire evaiva 

3T2 

Rig-veda Translation 

tasthuh , savaya te ; iavanujnaya evam eva tishthanti 1 it does 
not appear very clearly who are alluded to, but possibly it 
applies to the worshippers. 

J. Aditl —Dyavabhumi adite trasitham nah: adite, un¬ 
divided, indivisible, may be an epithet here, according to the 
comment, of heaven and earth. 

Who is it, Gods, that Offers you. — Ko vo adhware 
varivo dhati devah may also mean, which of you gods bestows 
wealth at the sacrifice ?
