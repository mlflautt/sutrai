# 3 The Offerings To The Gods Are Engendered Agni 614

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5148-5152

3. The offerings (to the gods) are engendered, Agni,. 
of thee; from thee (proceed) praises ; from thee effective 
prayers ; from thee come a vigorous frame and wealth 
to the man who worships with sincerity and offers oblations.
