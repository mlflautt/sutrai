# 15 The Vase Is Filled For Him With Soma Welcome 226

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2325-2329

15. The vase is filled for him (with Soma) ; welcome 
Tndra : I pour it out for thee to drink, as a water-carrier 
*(pours water) from his bag: may the grateful Soma juice 
flow in reverence round Indra for his exhilaration.
