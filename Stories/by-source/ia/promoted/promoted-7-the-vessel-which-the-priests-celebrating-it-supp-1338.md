# 7 The Vessel Which The Priests Celebrating It Supp 1338

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10336-10340

7. The vessel which the priests, celebrating (it), supply 
with butter, as if roasting a marrow-yielding animal with 
fire, has been placed, desirous of the sacrifice, upon the 
fire, like a son upon the lap of his father.
