# 5 He Cherishes His Kin He Reigns In Welfare And 1272

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9918-9922

5. He cherishes (his kin) ; he (reigns) in welfare and 
prosperity; he is victorious in present and continuous 
(time): dear is he to the sun, dear to Agni, who, with 
prepared libation, offers it to Indra.
