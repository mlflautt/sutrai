# Xvi Dawn 323

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17057-17078

XVI.  Dawn. 
THESE  Dawns  have  raised  their  banner:  in  the  eastern 

half  of  middle air they spread abroad their 
shining  light. 

Like heroes  \vho  prepare  their  weapons  for  the  fray, 
the  cows  are  coming  on,  the  mothers,  red  of  hue. 

2  Rapidly  have  the  ruddy  beams  of  light  shot  up  :  the 

red  cows  have  they  harnessed,  easy  to  be  yoked. 
The Dawns have  made  their  pathways  as  in  former 
times : red-hued, they have attained  refulgent 
brilliancy. 

3  They  sing  their  song  like  women  active  in  their  tasks, 

along  their  common  path  hither  from  far  away, 
Bringing  refreshment to the  liberal devotee, yea, 
all  things  to  the  worshipper  who  pours  the  juice.
