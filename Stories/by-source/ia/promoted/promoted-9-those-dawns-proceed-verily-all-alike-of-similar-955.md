# 9 Those Dawns Proceed Verily All Alike Of Similar  955

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7675-7681

9. Those Dawns proceed verily all alike, of similar form> 

; 3 64 Rig-veda Translation [IV. 5. 7 

of infinite hues, pure, bright, illumining, concealing by 
their radiant persons the very great gloom.
