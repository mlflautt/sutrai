# 3 Showerer Of Benefits Beholder Of Men Radiant 71

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1246-1251

3. Showerer (of benefits), beholder of men, radiant 
amidst darkness, shine, Agni, with abundant (rays) in 
due order: granter of dwellings, conduct us (to good),, 
keep off all ill, and youngest (of all the gods), gratify our 
desires for wealth.
