# 10 Inasmuch As I Who Am A Mortal Earnestly Invoke 1051

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8349-8353

10. Inasmuch as I who am a mortal earnestly invoke 
thee who art an immortal, praising thee with a devoted 
heart; therefore, Jatavedas, grant us food, and may I 
obtain immortality through my posterity.
