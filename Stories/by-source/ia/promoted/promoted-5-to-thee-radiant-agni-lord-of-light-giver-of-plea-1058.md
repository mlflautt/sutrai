# 5 To Thee Radiant Agni Lord Of Light Giver Of Plea 1058

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8439-8443

5. To thee, radiant Agni, lord of light, giver of pleasure, 
destroyer (of foes), protector of man, the bearer of ob¬ 
lations, to thee the oblation is offered with the sacred 
verse: do thou bring food to thine adorers.
