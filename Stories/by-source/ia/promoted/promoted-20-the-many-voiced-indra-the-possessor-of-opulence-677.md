# 20 The Many Voiced Indra The Possessor Of Opulence 677

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5600-5605

20. The many-voiced Indra, the possessor of opulence, 
the supporter of men, the irresistible, bestows upon us, 
when praised by us, assured (rewards) : thou, (Indra), 
art the king of men: grant to us abundantly that great 
fame which (is due) to (thy) worshipper.
