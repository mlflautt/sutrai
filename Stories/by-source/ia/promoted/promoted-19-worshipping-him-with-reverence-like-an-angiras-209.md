# 19 Worshipping Him With Reverence Like An Angiras 209

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2207-2212

19. Worshipping him with reverence, like an Angiras , 
I make the ancient (Indra) renovate to accept the oblation: 
destroy the many impious oppressors (of thy worshippers), 
and bestow upon us, Maghavan, thine own (wealth) for 
our acceptance.
