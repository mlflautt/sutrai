# 3 Indra Has Slain The Dasyus Soma In Battle Agni 781

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6332-6337

3. Indra has slain the Dasyus , Soma, in battle: Agni 
has consumed them before the noon: he (Indra) has 
destroyed the whole of many thousands, as (robbers are 
the destroyers of those) going upon (their own) business, 
in a difficult and dangerous (place).
