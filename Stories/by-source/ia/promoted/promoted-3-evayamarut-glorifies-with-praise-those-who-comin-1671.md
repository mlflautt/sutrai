# 3 Evayamarut Glorifies With Praise Those Who Comin 1671

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12707-12712

3. Evayamarut glorifies with praise those who, (coming), 
brilliant and happy from the vast heaven, hear (his invo¬ 
cation) ; in whose dwelling there is no one able to disturb 
them, and who, like self-radiant fires, are the impellers of 
the rivers.
