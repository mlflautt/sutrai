# Viii A1Zan This God Is The Protector Of Markets 22

**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)
**Lines:** 2087-2095

VIII. A1zan.—This god is the protector of markets, 
public places, gates, and the doors of houses, and here 
performs the functions of the multitudinous local 
market-gods of the Tshi-speaking peoples. He is repre- 
sented by a cone of clay, large or small indifferently, 
with a stone or a clay saucer at the apex or base, on 
which are poured daily small offerings of palm-oil, palm- 
wine, and food.
