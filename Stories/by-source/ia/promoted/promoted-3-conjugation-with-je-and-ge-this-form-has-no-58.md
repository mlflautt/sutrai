# 3 Conjugation With Je And Ge This Form Has No 58

**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)
**Lines:** 8855-8876

3. Conjugation with Je and ge. This form has no 
imperfect and no future tense. 
Example— 

le-du-ge, to intend to eat (literally, to be in a place—to 
eat—to intend), 

LANGUAGE. 237 
Present Tense. Past Tense. 
me le-du-ge me ele-du-ge 
wo le-du-ge wo ele-du-ge 
&c. &e. 

This form has a negative conjugation, as under— 
nye me-le-du-ge-o, not to intend to eat. 

Present Tense. Past Tense. 
nye me-le-du-ge-o nye me-ele-du-ge-o 
wo me-le-du-ge-o wo me-ele-du-ge-o 

&e. &e.
