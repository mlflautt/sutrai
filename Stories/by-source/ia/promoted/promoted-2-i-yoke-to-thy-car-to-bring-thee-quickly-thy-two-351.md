# 2 I Yoke To Thy Car To Bring Thee Quickly Thy Two 351

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3271-3275

2. I yoke (to thy car), to bring thee quickly, thy two 
docile horses, whose speed thou hast of old employed: 
may thy horses, handsome Indra, bring thee hither, and 
drink promptly of this well-effused agreeable libation.
