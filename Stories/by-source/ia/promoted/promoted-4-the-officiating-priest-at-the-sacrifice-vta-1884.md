# 4 The Officiating Priest At The Sacrifice Vta 1884

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15135-15144

4. The Officiating Priest at the Sacrifice.-— Vta 
gna agnir adhwara : the meaning of gna is, usually, devapatni , 
a wife of a deity, agreeably to which, one rendering suggested 
by Sayana is agnir yaje devapatnir yajati , Agni worships the 
wives of the gods at the sacrifice ; or, as an alternative, gna , 
as equivalent to gachchhan , going, may designate the adhwaryu , 
who moves about at the ceremonial.
