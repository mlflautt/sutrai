# 6 When They Rub The Sticks With Their Arms The Rad 160

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1859-1864

6. When they rub (the sticks) with their arms the radi¬ 
ant Agni bursts forth from the wood like a fleet courser, 
and like the many-coloured car of the Ashwins unresisted 
in its course, Agni spreads wide around consuming stones 
and trees.
