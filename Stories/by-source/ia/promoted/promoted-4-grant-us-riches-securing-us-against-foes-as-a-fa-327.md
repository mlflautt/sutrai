# 4 Grant Us Riches Securing Us Against Foes As A Fa 327

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3095-3099

4. Grant us riches, securing (us against foes), (as a father 
bestows) his portion on (a son) arrived at maturity: send 
down upon us, Indra, wealth adequate (to our desires), 
as a crook brings down the ripe fruit from a tree.
