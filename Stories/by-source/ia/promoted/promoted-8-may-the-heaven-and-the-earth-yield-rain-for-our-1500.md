# 8 May The Heaven And The Earth Yield Rain For Our 1500

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11491-11496

8. May the heaven and the earth yield (rain) for our 
sustenance: may the wonderfully bounteous dawns 
exert themselves (for our good): may these sons of 
Rudra, lauded, Rishi, (by thee), send down the celestial 
rain.
