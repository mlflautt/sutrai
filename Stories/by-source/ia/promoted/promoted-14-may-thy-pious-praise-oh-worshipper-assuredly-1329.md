# 14 May Thy Pious Praise Oh Worshipper Assuredly 1329

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10265-10269

14. May thy pious praise, oh worshipper, assuredly 
reach the thundering, roaring lord of Ila, who, impelling 
the clouds and distributing the rain, proceeds, illuminating 
the heaven and earth with lightning.
