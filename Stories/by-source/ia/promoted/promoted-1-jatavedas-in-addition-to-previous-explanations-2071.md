# 1 Jatavedas In Addition To Previous Explanations 2071

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17192-17198

1. Jatavedas.— -In addition to previous explanations, 
the name is here said to imply, he whom all know to be identi¬ 
cal with all that exists ; or, vedas may mean wealth, when it 
will signify, he from whom all wealth is generated. 

Page 186.
