She kept moving backwards and forwards into the temple, 
where Konkroma’s and other shrines were kept, and in front 
of which had been placed bundles of yams. Between her wild 
dances she continued to walk round and round. Shortly before 
dawn broke she placed the back of her right hand upon the 
palm of her left and laid her hands against her cheek, where¬ 
upon the ‘ linguist * said : 

* King, we give you the path.* 

The priestess then wheeled round and round and threw herself 
into the arms of four men, who carried her on their shoulders, 
up the steps and into the temple of the gods, where they laid 
her down, and every one went home.
