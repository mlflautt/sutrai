# 8 All The Gods Cannot Detract From The Many Glorio 220

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2279-2284

8. All the gods cannot detract from the many glorious 
exploits, and pious works of Indra: he who has upheld, 
earth, and heaven, and the firmament, and who, the 
performer of great deeds, has engendered the sun and the 
dawn.
