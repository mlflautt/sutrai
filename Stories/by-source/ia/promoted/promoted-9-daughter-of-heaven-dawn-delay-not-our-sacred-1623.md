# 9 Daughter Of Heaven Dawn Delay Not Our Sacred 1623

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12398-12402

9. Daughter of heaven, Dawn! delay not our (sacred) 
rite ; let not the sun scorch thee with his ray, as (a prince 
punishes) a. thief, or (subdues) an enemy: bright-born 
goddess, (who art) sincerely praised for (the gift of) horses.
