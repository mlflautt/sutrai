# 1 Ribhus The Text Has Ribhukshah Which Is Pro 1971

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15994-16004

1. Ribhus. —The text has Ribhukshah which is pro- 
perly the nom. sing, of Ribhukshin , a name of Indra: here 
Sayana identifies it with Ribkavah , the plur. nom. of Ribhu : 
in some of the following verses of this hymn the word appears 
as Ribhukshanahy the nom. or voc. plural of Ribhukshin 
substituting optionally, the short for the long vowel in the 
antepenultimate, Ribhukshanah for Ribhukshanah : see Sans¬ 
krit Gr., 2nd. edit., p. 460. 

Page 147.
