# 1 Through That Friendship Soma Which Has United 779

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6320-6325

1. Through that friendship, Soma, which has united 
thee with thy (friend) Indra, he has made the waters 
flow for man; he has slain Ahi ; he has sent forth the 
seven rivers, and has opened the shut-up sources (of the 
streams).
