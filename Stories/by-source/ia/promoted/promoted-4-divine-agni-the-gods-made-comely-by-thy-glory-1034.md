# 4 Divine Agni The Gods Made Comely By Thy Glory 1034

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8234-8239

4. Divine (Agni), the gods, (made) comely by thy glory, 
and bearing (the) great (affection), sip the ambrosia: men 
adore Agni, the conveyer of the burnt-offering, presenting 
oblations on behalf of the institutor of the rite, desirous of 
(its) reward.
