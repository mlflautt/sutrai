# 3 And After Him Who Is Etc This Is A Very Elliptic 1981

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16104-16125

3. And after him who is, etc.— This is a very elliptical 
passage: asya dravatas-turanyatahparnam na vcr-anu vati 
pragavdhimdi. lit. of him running, quick-going, as after the 
flight of a bird, go they eager: the Scholiast supplies the 
ellipse, and as the verb anuyati is in the singular, lie supplies 
a nominative, sarvah janali, every man. 

As Swift as a Hawk.— Shyenasyeva dhrajato ankasam 
pari Dadhihravnah sahorja taritratah. literally, as of a hawk 
quick-going with respect to the insertion of the foot or to the 
breast, ankasam-padadhnvam arah- p radesham va of Dadhi - 
kravan , together with strength, or for the sake of strength 
together, enabling to cross : it is not easy to make any sense 
of this even with the help of the Scholiast, although there is 
nothing very difficult in the words : Mahidhava, Yajush , 9. 15, 
explains ankasam pari, a horse’s trappings, the cloth, chaunri 
tail, etc., vastrachamaradikam , over all his body, which fly 
open as the horse gallops, like the wings of a bird, whilst the 
horse himself has the speed of the hawk.
