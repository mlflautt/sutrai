# 5 Shedders Of Abundant Showers Wearers Of Ornament 1480

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11357-11362

5. Shedders of abundant showers, wearers of ornaments, 
munificent, of brilliant aspect, of inexhaustible wealth, 
well descended by birth, wearing golden breast-plates, 
entitled to adoration, (coming) from heaven, accept the 
ambrosial oblation.
