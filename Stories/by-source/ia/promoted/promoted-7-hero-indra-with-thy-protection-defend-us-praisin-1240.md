# 7 Hero Indra With Thy Protection Defend Us Praisin 1240

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9694-9698

7. Hero, Indra, with thy protection defend us, praising 
and worshipping (thee), and be propitiated (by drinking) 
of the well-effused and agreeable Soma juice that yields 
(a defensive) covering in combat.
