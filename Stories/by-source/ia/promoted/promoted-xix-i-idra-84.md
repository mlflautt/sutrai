# Xix I Idra 84

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8781-8813

XIX.  I'idra. 

O  INDRA,  hear  Tirschl's  call,  the  call  of  him  who 
serveth  thee. 

Satisfy  him  with  wealth  of  kine  and  valient  off- 
spring !  Great  art  thou. 

2  For  he,  O  Indra,  hath  produced  for  thee the newest 

gladdening  song, 

A  hymn that springs from careful drop thought, 
ancient  and  full  of  sacred  truth. 

3  That India will we  laud  whom  songs  and  hymns  of 

praise  have  magnified. 

Striving  to  win,  we  celebrate  his  many  deeds  of 
hero  might. 

o 

RigvQda VIII.  84-  6. The Rishi is Tiraschi. The metre  is 
Anushtup. Stanza  1  is  a  repetition  of  I.  iv.  ii.  1.  5. 

2  Newest ancient :  recent  in form  and  expression,  but  ancient 

in  its  subject. See  Muir,  Original  Sanskrit  Texts,  III.  pp.  238,  239. 

BOOK  III.
