# Xxiii Ladra 135

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10931-10943

XXIII.  ladra. 
MAY we, with Indra and the Gods to aid us, 

bring these existing worlds  to  full  completion  ! 

2  Our  sacrifice,  our  bodies,  and  our  offspring,  let  Indra 

with  the  Adityas  form  and  finish  ! 

3  With the Adityas, with the band of  Maruts,  let 

Indra  send  us  medicines  to  heal  us  !
