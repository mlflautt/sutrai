# Chapter Iii 216

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13934-13974

CHAPTER  III. 

I, .  Soma  Pavamana. 

POUR  down  the  rain  upon  us,  pour  a  wave of  waters 

from  the  sky, 
And  plenteous  store  of  wholesome  food  ! 

2  Flow  onward  with  that  stream  of  thine,  whereby  the 

cows  have  come  to  us, 
The  kine  of  strangers  to  our  home. 

3  Dearest  to  Gods  in  sacred  rites, pour  on us  fatness 

with  thy  stream, 
Pour  down  on  us  a  flood  of  rain  ! 

Rigveda  I.  175.  1—3.  The  Rishi  is  Agastya.  The  metre  is 
Skandhogrivi  (8  + 12  +  8  +  8)  in  stanza  I,  and  Anushtup  in  2  and  3. 

1  Thy  glory  hath  been  quaffed :  thou  hast  drunk  what  incites  thee 
to  glorious  deeds,  the  Soma  juice  contained  in  the  bowl. 

Rigveda  IX.  49.  1—5. The  Rishi  is  Kavi. The  metre  is  Gayatri. 
1 Wholesome  ;  ayakshmd  :  literally, without  yakshma,  or  pulmonary 
consumption.

fleecy  straining-cloth  ! 
For  verily  the  Gods  will  hear, 

5  Onward  hath  Pavamana  flowed  and  beaten off  the 

Rakshasas,
