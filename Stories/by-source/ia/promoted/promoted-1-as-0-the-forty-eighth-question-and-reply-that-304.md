# 1 As 0 The Forty Eighth Question And Reply That 304

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9286-9291

1. As ^0 the forty-eighth question and reply, that 
which you ask is thus : As to them who shall buy 
corn and keep it in store until it shall become dear, 
and shall then sell it at a high price (pa van girA- 
noih), what is the nature of the decision ?
