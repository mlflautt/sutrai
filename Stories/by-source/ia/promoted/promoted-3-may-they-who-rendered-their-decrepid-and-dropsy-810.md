# 3 May They Who Rendered Their Decrepid And Dropsy 810

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6680-6684

3. May they who rendered their decrepid and dropsy 
parents, when, like two dry posts, again perpetually 
young, Vaja, Vibhwan, and Ribhu associated with Indra,. 
drinkers of the Soma juice, protect our sacrifice.
