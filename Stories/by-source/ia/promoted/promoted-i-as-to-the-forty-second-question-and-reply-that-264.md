# I As To The Forty Second Question And Reply That 264

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7799-7803

I. As to the forty-second question and reply, that 
which you ask is thus: Regarding a man who 
is consecrating a sacred cake®, and the fire is his 
household attendant (khavag-i mdn6) from afar, 
when he sees it, at how many steps is it improper ?
