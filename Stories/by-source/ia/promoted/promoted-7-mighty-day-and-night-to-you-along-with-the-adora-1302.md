# 7 Mighty Day And Night To You Along With The Adora 1302

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10091-10096

7. Mighty Day and Night, to you, along with the adorable 
(deities) of heaven, I present (the oblation) with prayers, 
delighting and explicit: knowing, like two sages, all (that 
is required), do you bring (it) to the worshipper for his. 
sacrifice.
