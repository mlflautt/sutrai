# 6 Thus May We Offer Worship With Sacrifices With 941

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7586-7595

6. Thus may we offer worship with sacrifices, with 
oblations, with praise, to the paternal, universal deity, 
the showerer (of benefits) ; and may we, Brihaspati, 
become possessed of riches, and be blessed with excellent 
progeny and valiant descendants.
