# 286 Song Of The Eagles A Song Of The Bead Chant 208

**Source:** Collected And Translated (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14285-14314

286. SONG OF THE EAGLES. — A SONG OF THE BEAD CHANT. 

I. 

Adodo aid-hena" an an anaii anai6. 

(Meaningless prelude.) 

Ki;znakfye ydye j-aaiyista an an, 

Kmnakfye there he sits, 

Haydaaa" ydye jaaiyista an an, 

When he rises, there he sits, 

Yiltsa* ad ydye ^aaiyista an an, 

We shall see, there he sits, 

7a/pi/ aa* ydye .yaafyista an an. 

He will flap, there he sits. 

Aia^bjdye aiadbsdye an an an o^aneye*. 

(Meaningless refrain.) 

Ki;makiye=Ki/m{ki. The vocables not translated have no meaning now.
