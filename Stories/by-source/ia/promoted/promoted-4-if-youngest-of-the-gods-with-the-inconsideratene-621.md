# 4 If Youngest Of The Gods With The Inconsideratene 621

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5191-5195

4. If, youngest (of the gods), with the inconsiderateness 
common to men, we have ever committed any offence 
against thee, make us free from the defects of earth 
efface entirely, Agni, our offences.
