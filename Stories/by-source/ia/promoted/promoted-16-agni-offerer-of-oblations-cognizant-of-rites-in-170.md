# 16 Agni Offerer Of Oblations Cognizant Of Rites In 170

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1919-1927

16. Agni, offerer of oblations, cognizant of rites, in¬ 
asmuch as we have recourse to thee to-day in this progress¬ 
ing sacrifice, therefore do thou steadily convey (the offering 
to the gods), or tranquilly be at rest, and knowing (the 
purport of our acts), and wise (in all respects), approach 
(and accept) the Soma libation. 

ANUVAKA III.
