# 4 The Winds Blow Strong The Lightnings Flash The 1643

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12549-12555

4. The winds blow strong, the lightnings flash, the 
plants spring up, the firmament dissolves : earth becomes, 
(fit) for all creatures when Parjanya fertilizes the soil with, 
showers. 

280 Rig-veda Translation [V. 6.12
