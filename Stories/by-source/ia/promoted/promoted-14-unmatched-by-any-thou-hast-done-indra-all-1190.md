# 14 Unmatched By Any Thou Hast Done Indra All 1190

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9366-9371

14. Unmatched (by any), thou hast done, Indra, all 
these many (deeds) by thine innate energy : wielder of 
the thunderbolt, whatever thou, the humbler of (foes), 
hast undertaken, there is no one the arrester of this thy 
prowess.
