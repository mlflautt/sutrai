# 7 Seven Sages With Five Ministering Priests Attend 11

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 856-861

7. Seven sages with five ministering priests attend the 
station that is prepared for the rapid (Agni) : the undecay¬ 
ing divine (sages), with their faces to the east, sprinkling 
(the libations), rejoice as they celebrate the worship of the 
gods.
