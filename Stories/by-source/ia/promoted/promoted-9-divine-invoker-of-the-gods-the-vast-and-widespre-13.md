# 9 Divine Invoker Of The Gods The Vast And Widespre 13

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 867-872

9. Divine invoker of the gods, the vast and widespread- 
ing rays shed (moisture) for thee, the mighty, the victorious, 
the wonderful, the showerer (of benefits): do thou who 
art all-knowing, joy-bestowing, bring hither the great gods, 
and heaven and earth.
