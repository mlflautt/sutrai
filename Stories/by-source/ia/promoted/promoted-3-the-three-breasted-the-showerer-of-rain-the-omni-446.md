# 3 The Three Breasted The Showerer Of Rain The Omni 446

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3893-3903

3. The three-breasted, the showerer (of rain), the omni¬ 
form, the three-uddered, the parent of multiform progeny, 
the possessor of magnitude, followed by three hosts (the 
year), advances, the vigorous impregnator of the perpetual 
plants. 

4. (The year) is awake, the path in the vicinity of 
those (plants): I call upon the beautiful name of the 
Adityas; the divine waters wandering severally (now) 
give it delight, and (now) depart from it.
