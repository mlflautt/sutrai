# 10 By Suffixing Ko To Other Nouns This Suffix Is T 70

**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)
**Lines:** 9229-9236

10. By suffixing ko to other nouns. This suffix is the 
verb k6, to be high, or raised. It is generally 
reduplicated, as— 

juti-k6k6, greatness, From fiuti, form, shape. 

tsi-ko, thirst, ,, tsi, water.
