# 3 Do Thou The Protector Of Us Thy Friends And Prai 800

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6508-6513

3. Do thou, the protector of us thy friends and praisers, 
be present with a hundred protections. 

4. (Induced) by the praises of men, return like a re¬ 
volving wheel to us, dependent (upon thy favour).
