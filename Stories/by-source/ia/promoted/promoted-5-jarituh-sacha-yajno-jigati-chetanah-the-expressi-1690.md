# 5 Jarituh Sacha Yajno Jigati Chetanah The Expressi 1690

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12901-12910

5. Jarituh sacha yajno jigati chetanah ; the expression 
is rather obscure : yajna and chetana are both applied by 
Sayana to the Soma, as the material of the sacrifice, yajna - 
sadhanam , and the animator or giver of consciousness to the 
organs of perception; imlriyamm chetayita,' and the co- 
operator with the worshipper in obtaining heaven or other 
benefits —jarituh sacha s wargadiphalapraptau sahayo bhulah : 
this and the next stanza occur in the Sama , II. 20, 2P: Pro¬ 
fessor Benfey has understood it differently.
