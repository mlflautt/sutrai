# 10 Ribhus Who Possess Wealth Comprising Cattle 828

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6799-6811

10. Ribhus, who possess wealth, comprising cattle, 
food, progeny, dwellings, and abundant sustenance, do 
you, who are the first drinkers (of the Soma), bestow upon 
us, when exhilarated, (that wealth, and upon those) who 
laud your liberality. 

11 . Ribhus go not away ; let us not leave you (thirst¬ 

ing) ; (be present) unreproached at this sacrifice; be 
exhilarated, deities, along with Indra, with the Maruts, 
and with (other) brilliant (divinities), for the distribution 
of wealth. -
