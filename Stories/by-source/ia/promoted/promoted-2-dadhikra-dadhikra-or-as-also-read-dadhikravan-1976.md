# 2 Dadhikra Dadhikra Or As Also Read Dadhikravan 1976

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16051-16064

2. Dadhikra.— Dadhikra , or as also read, Dadhikravan , 
is given in the Nighantu of Yaska amongst the synonymes 

362 

Rig-veda Translation 

of ashwa , a horse: the form is noted in Panini , hi. 2. 67: 
according to Mahidhara on Rich 6, of Sukta vii, Yajush, 23, 
32, the etymology is dadhi , who bears, who carries his rider, 
and krama , to go: according to the Aitareya Brahnana , 
hi. 15. 5. Agni , in the form of a horse, destroyed the Asuras 
by the desire of Bharadwaja.
