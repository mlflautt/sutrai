# 7 The Ancient Seekers After Happiness Have Kindled 1079

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8571-8580

7. The ancient seekers after happiness have kindled 
thee, Agni, when invoked, (and fed thee) with abundant 
fuel; and thou (thereby) increasing, and supplied with 
shrubs, art dominant over all terrestrial viands. 

Y. 1. 9. 

The deity is Agni ; Gaya, of the race of Atri, is the Rishi; the 
metre of the fifth and seventh stanzas is Pankti , of the rest Anusktubh .
