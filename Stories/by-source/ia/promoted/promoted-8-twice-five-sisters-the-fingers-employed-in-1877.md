# 8 Twice Five Sisters The Fingers Employed In 1877

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15067-15075

8. Twice Five Sisters. The fingers employed in 
producing fire by attrition. 

Like Females awaking him at Dawn. — Usharbudham 
atkaryo na dantam , striya iva ushasi budhyamanam havisham 
bhakshakam : there is no verb, unless jijanan is borrowed 
from the first half of the stanza: the meaning of danta may 
be also questionable.
