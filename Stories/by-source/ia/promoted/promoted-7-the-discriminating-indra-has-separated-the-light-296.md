# 7 The Discriminating Indra Has Separated The Light 296

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2835-2840

7. The discriminating (Indra) has separated the light 
from the darkness; may we, removed afar from evil, be 
ever in security: Indra, drinker of the Soma juice, who 
art nourished by the beverage, be pleased with these 
praises of thy worshipper who is the discomfiter of his foes.
