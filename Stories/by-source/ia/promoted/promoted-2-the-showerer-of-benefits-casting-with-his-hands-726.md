# 2 The Showerer Of Benefits Casting With His Hands 726

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5948-5953

2. The showerer (of benefits), casting with his hands 
the quadrangular bolt that causes rain, fierce, the chief 
of leaders, the achiever of (glorious) acts, is desirous of 
the prosperity of the investing Parushni (river), whose 
(bordering) districts he has frequented through regard.
