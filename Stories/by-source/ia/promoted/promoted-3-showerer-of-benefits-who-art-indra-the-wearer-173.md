# 3 Showerer Of Benefits Who Art Indra The Wearer 173

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1947-1954

3. Showerer (of benefits, who art) Indra, the wearer 
of the helmet, the professor of opulence, the conveyer 
'(of men beyond evil), the leader of the numerous troop 
Xof the Maruts), the achiever of many great deeds, the 
devastator of hostile (regions), the terror of thy foes, where 
are those heroic deeds (which thou hast performed) against 
the mortal ( Asuras) ^hen opposed (by them in battle).
