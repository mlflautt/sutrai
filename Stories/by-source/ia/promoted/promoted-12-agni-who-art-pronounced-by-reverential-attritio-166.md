# 12 Agni Who Art Pronounced By Reverential Attritio 166

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1891-1895

12. Agni, who art pronounced by reverential attrition, 
and deposited with reverential care, and who art far-seeing,, 
render our rites (exempt from defects), and worship the 
gods (on behalf) of the devout worshipper.
