# 1 After The Munificent Indra Indram Anu 1969

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15981-15988

1. After the Munificent Indra. — Indram anu , 
Indram anusritya or anugamantu: having followed, or may 
they come after, Indra ; or it would be more consistent to 
render it, may they come after (you) to Indra , etc., see the 
preceding note. 

Page 146.
