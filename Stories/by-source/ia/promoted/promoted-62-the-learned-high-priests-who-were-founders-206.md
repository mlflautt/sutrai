# 62 The Learned High Priests Who Were Founders 206

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5865-5870

62. The learned high-priests who were founders 
(piy ini kino) of the religion knew 2V {the evidence), 
and those portions of it were transmitted by them 
to the ancients which the successive realisers of it, 
for the ages before me (levin am), have possessed.
