# 2 Fulfiljer Of Religious Rites I Reiterate The Pra 867

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7064-7069

2. FulfilJer of religious rites, I reiterate the praise of 
the great Dadhikra , the liberal, many-honoured showerer 
(of benefits), whom Mitra and Varuna gave for the good 
of many, the transporter (beyond calamity), as brilliant 
as Agni.
