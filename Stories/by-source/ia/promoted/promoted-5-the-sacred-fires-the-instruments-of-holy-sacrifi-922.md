# 5 The Sacred Fires The Instruments Of Holy Sacrifi 922

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7410-7417

5. The sacred fires, the instruments of holy sacrifice, 
the conveyers of libations, praise the associated Ashwins 
at the break of day, when the observant (priest) the con¬ 
ductor of the rite, with washed hands has expressed by 
the (grinding) stones the sweet-favoured Soma juice. 

168 Rig-veda Translation [IV. 5.1
