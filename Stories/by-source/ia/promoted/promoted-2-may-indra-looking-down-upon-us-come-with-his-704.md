# 2 May Indra Looking Down Upon Us Come With His 704

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5783-5787

2. May Indra, looking down upon us, come with his 
steeds to our presence for our protection and enrichment: 
may the mighty thunderer, the possessor of wealth, (aiding 
us) in battle, be present at this our sacrifice.
