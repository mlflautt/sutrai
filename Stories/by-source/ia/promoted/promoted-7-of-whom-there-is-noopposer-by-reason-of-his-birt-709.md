# 7 Of Whom There Is Noopposer By Reason Of His Birt 709

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5817-5822

7. Of whom there is noopposer by (reason of) his birth,, 
nor any destroyer of the wealth that accomplishes (pious 
works): powerful and resplendent (Indra), the invoked 
of many, do thou who art the showerer (of benefits) bestow 
upon us riches.
