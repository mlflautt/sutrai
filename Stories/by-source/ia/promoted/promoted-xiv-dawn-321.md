XIV.  Dawn. 

This  light  is  come,  amid  all  lights  the  fairest :  born 
is  the  brilliant,  far-extending  brightness. 

Night,  sent  away  for  Savitar's  uprising,  hath  yielded 
up  a  birth-place  for  the  morning.
