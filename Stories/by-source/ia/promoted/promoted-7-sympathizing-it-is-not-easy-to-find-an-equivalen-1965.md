# 7 Sympathizing It Is Not Easy To Find An Equivalen 1965

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15948-15954

7. Sympathizing. —It is not easy to find an equivalent 
for 8ajo shah, although the sense is obvious enough: sajosha 
Indra Varunena somam pahi , is, literally, Indra, who art 
co-pleased with Varum , drink the libation ; implying that 
they both derive the like satisfaction from the beverage 
which they imbibe together.
