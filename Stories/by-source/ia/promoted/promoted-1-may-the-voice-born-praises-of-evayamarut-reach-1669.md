# 1 May The Voice Born Praises Of Evayamarut Reach 1669

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12694-12699

1. May the voice-born praises of Evayamarut reach 
you, Vishnu, attended by the Maruts : (may they reach) 
the strong, the adorable, the brilliantly-adorned, the 
vigorous, praise-loving, cloud-scattering, quick-moving 
company of the Maruts.
