# 6 The Child Of Two Mothers Sleeps In The West But 427

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3764-3768

6. The child of two mothers sleeps in the west, but 
(in the morning) the single infant proceeds unobstructed 
(through the sky): these are the functions of Mitra and 
Varuna: great and unequalled is the might of the gods.
