# 1 The Rite According To Mahidhara Yajur Veda 1879

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15082-15095

1. The Rite. —According to Mahidhara, Yajur-Veda, 
hi. 15, the Agni here intended is the AJiavaniya which is 
kindled before the Dakshina . 

340 

Rig-veda Translation 

Apnavana and other Bhrigus. —Apnamno bhrigavah: 
according to Sayana , Apnavanali is the name of a Rishi of the 
family of Bhrigu : Mahidhara makes it the plur. apnavanah, 
and other Rishis , and the Bhrigus : he says it maj also mean 
putravantah, having sons, an epithet of the Bhrigus .
