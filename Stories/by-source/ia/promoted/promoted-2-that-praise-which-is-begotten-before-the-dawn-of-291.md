# 2 That Praise Which Is Begotten Before The Dawn Of 291

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2798-2802

2. That praise which is begotten before (the dawn) of 
day is the awakener of Indra, when repeated at the 
(morning) sacrifice: auspicious and clothed in white 
raiment is this our ancient and paternal hymn.
