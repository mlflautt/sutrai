# 2 The Reply Is This That Even In The Reply To An 72

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2537-2545

2. The reply is this, that even in the reply to an 
accompanying question ^ it is written that the crea- 
tures arc achieved for* justice and the performance 
of what is desirable for the creator ; and to prepare 
thoroughly well that which is unlimited and the 
virtuous progress of the creatures, whose distress 
is like fear, there is the unparalleled (abrd^i^’arvatd) 
renovation of the mdvcrsc.
