# 2 Conjugation With Ga This Form Has All Four 57

**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)
**Lines:** 8840-8854

2. Conjugation with ga. This form has all four 
tenses, which are formed in the same way as in (1). 

Example— 
ga-du, to eat again (literally, to repeat—to eat). 
Present Tense. Imperfect Tense. 
me ga-du me ga-dua 
vo ga-du wo ga-dua 
&e. &c. 
Future Tense. Past Tense. 
me aga-du me ega-du 
wo aga-du wo ega-du 
é&e. &e. 

This form has no negative conjugation.
