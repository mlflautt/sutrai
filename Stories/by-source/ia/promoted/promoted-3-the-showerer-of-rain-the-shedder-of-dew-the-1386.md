# 3 The Showerer Of Rain The Shedder Of Dew The 1386

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10682-10687

3. The showerer (of rain), the shedder of dew, the 
radiant and quick-going (car) has entered the region of 
the paternal east: the many-tinted and pervading (lumi¬ 
nary) proceeds to both extremities of the firmament, (and 
so) preserves (the world).
