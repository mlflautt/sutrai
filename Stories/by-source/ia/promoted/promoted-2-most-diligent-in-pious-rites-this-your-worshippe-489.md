# 2 Most Diligent In Pious Rites This Your Worshippe 489

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4213-4217

2. Most diligent (in pious rites) this (your worshipper),. 
Indra and Yaruna, desirous of wealth, incessantly invokes 
you for protection: associated with the Maruts , with, 
heaven and earth, hear my invocation.
