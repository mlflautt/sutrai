# 10 So That When It Is The Propitiatory Dedication 269

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7963-7975

10. So that when it is the propitiatory dedication 
for the day Khdr of the month of consecration Az/dnS 
the day and month are such that their order and the 
Athr (‘fire’) and A&in (‘waters’) succeeding them 
are thereby set in reverse order to the proper se- 
quence^. II. Then, too, when in the same month® 
its propitiatory dedication for the day and month 
becomes alike for day and month *, it is recited as 
regards both the month and the ‘waters’ (i&Sn), 
because they are not connected together and have 
again become non-inclusive ; and then one is to con- 
sider them as proper.
