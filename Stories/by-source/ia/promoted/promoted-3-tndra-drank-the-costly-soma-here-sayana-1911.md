# 3 Tndra Drank The Costly Soma Here Sayana 1911

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15392-15396

3. Tndra drank the Costly Soma. —Here, Sayana 
observes, Vamadeva vindicates his own wilfulness by the 
example of Indra , who came to Twashtri’s house uninvited, 
and, by force, drank the Soma prepared for other gods.
