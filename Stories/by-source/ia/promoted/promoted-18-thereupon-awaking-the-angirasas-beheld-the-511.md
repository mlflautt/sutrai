# 18 Thereupon Awaking The Angirasas Beheld The 511

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4418-4423

18. Thereupon awaking, the (Angirasas) beheld (the 
•cattle set free), and seized upon the precious treasures: 
the universal gods then cam3 to all (their) dwellings: 
Mitra and Varuna may your truth be (kept) to him who 
worships (you).
