# 3 I Celebrate The Ruddy Food Bestowing Water Shed 515

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4456-4461

3. I celebrate the ruddy, food-bestowing, water-shed¬ 
ding, and swifter-than-thought-going, steeds of him who is 
the truth: harnessing the brilliant pair (to thy chariot), 
thou passest between the deities of whom thou art, and 
human worshippers.
