# 5 He Is The Most Diligent Offerer Of Worship Who I 86

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1355-1362

5. He is the most diligent offerer of worship who is the 
presenter of oblations before thou art, and who in two 
places (the middling and the best) seated with the sacri¬ 
ficial food is (the source of) happiness (to the worshippers): 
do thou, cognizant (of the objects of devotion), officiate in 
accordance with his piety, and thus render our rite accept¬ 
able to the gods.
