# 11 I Offer To You Gods For The Sake Of Water An Al 1373

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10594-10599

11. I offer to you, (gods), for the sake of water, an all- 
bestowing sacrifice, whereby the nine-months ministrants 
have completed the ten-months rite : may we, by this 
sacrifice, be the protected of the gods : may we, by this 
sacrifice, cross over the boundaries of sin.
