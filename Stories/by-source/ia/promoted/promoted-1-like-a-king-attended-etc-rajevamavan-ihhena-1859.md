# 1 Like A King Attended Etc Rajevamavan Ihhena 1859

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14897-14922

1. Like a King attended, etc. — Rajevamavan ihhena : 
the latter (ibha) may mean fearless, (host understood) gatabha- 
yena, or, as usual, hastina : ama has also different interpre¬ 
tations, a minister, for amatya , or ama, an associate ; or 
sickness, inflicting it on the foe : the verse occurs in the 
Nirukta, vi. 13, and is explained as in the text ; and again 
in the Yajush , xm. 9, with, upon the whole, a similar expla¬ 
nation. 

Following the swift-moving Host. —Trishuim anu 
prasitim drunamh is explained hy Sayana , kshipraga manim

Rig-veda Translation 

prakrishtam senam anugachchhan : Yaska puts the two first, 
into the third case, trishvya anu prasitya, but does not give 
any meaning to the latter: the first he explains quick:. 
Sayana also gives the reading, and explains the noun by 
santataya gatya , with extended or continuous inarch: Mahi- 
dhara gives prasiti the import it had in the first part of the 
verse, a net, a snare : this and the three following verses occur 
in the Yajush, xiii. 10, 13: the explanation occasionally 
slightly varies.
