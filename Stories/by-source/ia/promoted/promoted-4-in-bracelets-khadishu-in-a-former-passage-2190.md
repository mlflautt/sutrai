# 4 In Bracelets Khadishu In A Former Passage 2190

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18431-18435

4. In Bracelets.— Khadishu : in a former passage 
Jchadi was interpreted hasta tranaka, a guard for the hand or 
arm, Vol. n, p. 87, verse 3 : it is here rendered hastajmlasthita 
katahah , rings for the hands or feet, bracelets or anklets.
