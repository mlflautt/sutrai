# 6 For The Remaining Ketr Ln A Sin It Undergoes 134

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3848-3857

6. For the remaining (ketr<ln<a?) sin it undergoes 
punishment at the bridge, and the evil thoughts, evil 
words, and evil deeds are atoned for ; and with the 
good thoughts, good words, and good deeds of its 
own commendable and pleasing spirit it steps for- 
ward unto the supreme heaven (gardifmind)^ or to 
heaven (vahijtd), or to the ever-stationary (hami- 
stdnag^ino) of the righteous^, there where there is 
a place for it in righteousness.
