# 5 We Present To Thee The Densest Marrow That Has B 108

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1506-1510

5. We present to thee the densest marrow that has been 
extracted for thee from the interior (of the victim) : granter 
of dwellings, the drops fall for thee upon the skin: distri¬ 
bute them amongst the gods.
