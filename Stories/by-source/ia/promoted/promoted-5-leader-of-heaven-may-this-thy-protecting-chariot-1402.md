# 5 Leader Of Heaven May This Thy Protecting Chariot 1402

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10831-10835

5. Leader (of heaven), may this thy protecting chariot, 
laden with riches, (come for our) happiness : praisers of 
the honoured (Savitri), we glorify him for felicity through 
well-being : praisers of the gods, we glorify (them).
