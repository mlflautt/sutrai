# 4 Messenger Of The Worshipper Dutam Vivas 1880

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15096-15103

4. Messenger of the Worshipper.— Dutam vivas- 
watah : the second is explained manushyasya yajamamsydj 
of the man, of the worshipper. 

The Resplendent. — Bhrigavamm-bhriguvad-acharantam, 
going like Bhrigu , that is, dipyamanam-ityartha, shining, or 
being kindled.
