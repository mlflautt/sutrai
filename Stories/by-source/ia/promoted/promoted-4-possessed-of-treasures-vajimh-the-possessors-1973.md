# 4 Possessed Of Treasures Vajimh The Possessors 1973

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16016-16031

4. Possessed of Treasures.— Vajimh, the possessors 
either of horses or food, is here used somewhat irregularly 
for Vaja : ayahshipra , according to the commentator, means 
as hard or strong as iron, ayovat , sarabhutashiprah: sunishkah 
having good nishkas , a certain weight of gold, if not a coin. 

Sons of Indra, Grandsons of Strength.— The text 
has here the nouns in the singular, son of Indra , sen or 
grandson of strength, and inconsistently follows with vah-vos , 
you, in the plural. 

This Last Sacrifice. — Ityarjriyam , agre bhavam , would 
mean the first, the preceding, but Sayana explains it, tritiyam 
savanam, which is most consistent with the worship of the 
Ribhus.
