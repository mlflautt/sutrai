# 10 May Thy Roaring Flames Agni Be Manifest In The 1027

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8190-8194

10. May thy roaring (flames), Agni, be manifest in the 
sky as sharp weapons wherewith to slay the Rakshasas : 
in his exhilaration his shining (rays) inflict (destruction), 
and undivine opposing (hosts) arrest him not.
