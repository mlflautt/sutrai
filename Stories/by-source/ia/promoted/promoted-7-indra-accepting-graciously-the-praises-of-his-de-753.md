# 7 Indra Accepting Graciously The Praises Of His De 753

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6132-6139

7. Indra accepting graciously the praises of his devoted 
(worshipper), w'ho to-day pours out the libation to him, 
who toasts the buttered cakes, or fries the barley for 
Indra, exercises towards him the power that grants his 
desires. 

&28 Rig-veda Translation [IV. 3 .4
