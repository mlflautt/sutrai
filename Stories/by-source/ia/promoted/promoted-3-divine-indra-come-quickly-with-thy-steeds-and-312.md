# 3 Divine Indra Come Quickly With Thy Steeds And 312

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2995-2999

3. Divine Indra, come quickly with thy steeds, and 
well pleased, to our food-augmenting sacrifice: offering 
(sacred) food with butter, I invoke thee verily with praises 
in the chamber of the sweet libations.
