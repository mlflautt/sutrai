# 12 And Stir The Bright Water Rushat Pippalam 2197

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18483-18492

12. And Stir the Bright Water- -Rushat pippalam , 
shubhravarnam udakam , or it may be applied to naham , the 
heaven of bright water, the firmament. 

You Utter a Loud Shout.— The text changes the person 
of the verb, yat swaranti ghosham, when they sound a noise ; 
and this suggests to the Scholiast to propose other explana¬ 
tions, as when they, the Asuras , make a fearful noise, or 
when the worshippers utter loud praises.
