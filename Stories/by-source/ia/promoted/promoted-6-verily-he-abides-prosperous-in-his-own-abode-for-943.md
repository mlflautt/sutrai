# 6 Verily He Abides Prosperous In His Own Abode For 943

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7601-7605

6. Verily he abides prosperous in his own abode; for 
him the earth bears fruit at all seasons; to him (his) 
subjects willingly pay homage, the prince, to whom the 
Brahmana first, (duly reverenced), repairs.
