# 1 Convey Our Sacrifice Jatavedas To The Immortals 104

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1488-1492

1. Convey our sacrifice, Jatavedas, to the immortals,, 
and do thou accept these oblations : Agni, invoker (of the 
gods), seated (on the altar) first partake of the drops of 
the marrow and of the butter.
