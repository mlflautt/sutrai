# 13 May All The Gods Be With Us To Day For Our Pros 1410

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10893-10898

13. May all the gods be with us to-day for our prosperity : 
may Agni, the benefactor of all men, and giver of dwel¬ 
lings, (be with us) for (our) prosperity : may the divine 
Ribhus protect us for (our) prosperity : may Rudra pre¬ 
serve us from iniquity for (our) prosperity.
