# 6 Be Around Us Paritakmyayam It Is Rendered 1985

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16196-16204

6. Be around us .—Paritakmyayam: it is rendered 
paritakane , which is not more intelligible: paritakma occurs 
also in the sense of night, in which case it may mean, may 
Indra and Varuna protect us in the night against evil spirits. 

Long Life.— Suro drishike is the text, which is inter¬ 
preted suryasya chirakaladarshanaya , for the sight of the sun 
for a long time, i.e ., chirajivanaya , for long life.
