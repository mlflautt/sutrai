# 1 I Offer Praise To Agni The Creator The Far Seein 1112

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8816-8821

1. I offer praise to Agni, the creator, the far-seeing, 
the adorable, the renowned, the first (of the gods) ; he 
who is propitiated by oblations, the strong, the giver 
of happiness, the possessor of riches, the receiver of obla¬ 
tions, the bestower of dwellings.
