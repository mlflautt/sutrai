# 4 I Know Him Not As The Lord Etc Na Aham 2081

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17303-17309

4. I (know) him not as the Lord, etc.— Na aham 
patim sanituh asya myall; the verb is understood: the 
Scholiast renders it bhajamanasya asya mama rayah swami- 
nam tarn agnim aham na janami , 1 do not know that Agni r 
the master of the riches of this one, or me, the enjoyer: 
what it means is not very obvious.
