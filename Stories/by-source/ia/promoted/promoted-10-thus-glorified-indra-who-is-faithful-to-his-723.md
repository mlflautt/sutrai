# 10 Thus Glorified Indra Who Is Faithful To His 723

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5917-5922

10. Thus (glorified), Indra, who is faithful (to his 
word), the lord of wealth, the slayer of Vritra, bestows 
riches on man ; so thou, the praised of many, give us 
riches for our pious acts, that I may eat of thy divine 
food.
