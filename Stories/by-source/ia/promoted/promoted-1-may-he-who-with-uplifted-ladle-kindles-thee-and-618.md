# 1 May He Who With Uplifted Ladle Kindles Thee And 618

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5176-5180

1. May he who with^ uplifted ladle kindles thee, and 
thrice every day presents to thee the (sacrificial) food, 
knowing thy glory, Jatavedas, to be invigorated by the 
act, surpass (all others) in riches.
