# 4 Possessed Agni Of Good Steeds An Excellent Car 516

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4462-4466

4. Possessed, Agni, of good steeds, an excellent car, 
and abundant wealth, do thou, amidst these (worshippers)' 
bring to the man who offers worthy oblations, Aryaman, 
Varuna, Mitra, Vishnu, the Maruts, or the Ashwins.
