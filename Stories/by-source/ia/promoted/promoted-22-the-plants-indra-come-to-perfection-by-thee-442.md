# 22 The Plants Indra Come To Perfection By Thee 442

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3866-3876

22. The plants, Indra, come to perfection by thee ; 
from thee the waters (flow) ; earth bears her treasures 
for thee: may w'e, thy friends, be sharers of these bless¬ 
ings: great and unequalled is the might of the gods..
