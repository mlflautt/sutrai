# Ix Sonia Pavamana 26

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6786-6799

IX.  Sonia  Pavamana. 

CLEANSING  thee,  Soma,  in  thy  stream,  thou  flowest  in 

a  watery  robe. 
Giver  of  wealth,  thou  sittest  in the place of  Law, 

O  God,  a  fountain  made  of  gold. 
2  He, milking for dear meath the  heavenly udder, 

hath  sat  in  the  ancient  gathering-place. 
Washed by the men, far-sighted, strong, thou 
streamest  to  the  honourable  reservoir.
