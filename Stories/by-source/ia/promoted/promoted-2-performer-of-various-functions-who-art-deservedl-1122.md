# 2 Performer Of Various Functions Who Art Deservedl 1122

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8894-8898

2. Performer of various functions, who art deservedly 
renowned, thou praisest by thy words that Agni, who 
is possessed of wonderful splendour ; who is exempt from 
pain ; who is adorable and supreme in understanding.
