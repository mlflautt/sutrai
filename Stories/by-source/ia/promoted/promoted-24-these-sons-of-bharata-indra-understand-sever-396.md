# 24 These Sons Of Bharata Indra Understand Sever 396

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3565-3571

24. These sons of Bharata, Indra, understand sever¬ 
ance (from the Vasishthas ), not association (with them) ; 
they urge their steeds (against them) as against a constant 
foe ; they bear a stout bow (for their destruction) in battle. 

ANUVAKA V.
