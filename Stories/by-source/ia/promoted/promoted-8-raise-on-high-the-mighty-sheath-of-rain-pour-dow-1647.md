# 8 Raise On High The Mighty Sheath Of Rain Pour Dow 1647

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12571-12575

8. Raise on high the mighty sheath (of rain), pour down 
(its contents); let the rivers flow unimpeded to the east; 
saturate with water both heaven and earth, and let there be 
abundant beverage for the kine.
