# Xii Indra 29

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6850-6865

XII.  Indra. 

WITH  what  help  will  he  come  to  us,  wonderful,  ever- 
waxing  friend  ? 
With  what  most  mighty  company  ? 

2  What  genuine  and most liberal draught  will spirit 

thee  with  juice  to  burst 
Open  e'en  strongly-guarded  wealth  ? 

3  Do  thou  who  art protector of  us thy friends  who 

praise  thee 
With  hundred  aids  approach  us  !
