# 3 And Its Modes Theft Or Spoliation Are Just As 415

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11676-11680

3. And its modes ^ theft or spoliation are just as 
much more heinous than other theft and spoliation 
as a man and that which arises from his procreation 
of man are greater than the position of property.
