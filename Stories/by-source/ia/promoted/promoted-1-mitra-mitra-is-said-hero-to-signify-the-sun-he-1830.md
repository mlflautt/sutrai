# 1 Mitra Mitra Is Said Hero To Signify The Sun He 1830

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14515-14521

1. Mitra.- Mitra is said hero to signify the sun, he 
who is measured or appreciated (miyate) by all, and who 
preserves (trayate) the world, by bestowing rain : much the 
same rendering of the text is given by Yaska, Nir., x. 22. 

Page 78.
