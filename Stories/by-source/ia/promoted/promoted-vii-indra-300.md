# Vii Indra 300

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16290-16303

VII.  Indra. 

SUNG  is  the  song  of  ancient  time  :  to  Indra  have  ye 

said  the  prayer. 
They  have  sung  many  a  Byihati  of  sacrifice, poured 

forth  the  worshipper's  many  thoughts. 
2  Indra  hath  tossed  together  mighty  stores  of  wealth, 

and  both  the  worlds,  yea,  and  the  sun- 
Pure,  brightly-shining, mingled with  the  milk,  the 
draughts  of  Soma  have  made  Indra  glad.
