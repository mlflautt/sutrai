# 21 Indra Hero Possessor Of Wealth Protect Us This 393

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3550-3554

21. Indra, hero, possessor of wealth, protect us this 
day against our foes with many and excellent defences r 
may the vile wretch who hates us fall (before us) ; may 
the breath of life depart from him whom we hate.
