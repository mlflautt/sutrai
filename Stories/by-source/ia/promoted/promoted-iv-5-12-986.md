# Iv 5 12 986

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7922-7933

IV. 5. 12. 

The deities, as will appear from the hymn, are so only in relation 
to the stanzas reforring to them, being, in fact, personifications of 
.circumstances connected with agriculture; and accordingly it is said 
in the Qrihya Sutras that each verse is to be silently repeated, with an 
oblation to fire, at the commencement of ploughing : the deity then of 
the first three verses is termed Kshetrapati ; of the fourth, Shuna; 
of the fifth and eighth, Shunashira; of the sixth and seventh, Sita ; the 
Rishi is, as before, Vamadeva ; the metre of the first, fourth, sixth, 
nnd seventh verses is Anushtubh , of the rest Trishtubh .
