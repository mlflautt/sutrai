# 4 The Wielder Of The Thunderbolt The Render Of The 1224

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9587-9592

4. The wielder of the thunderbolt, the render of the 
rain-cloud, has destroyed with his bolt the mighty Shushna, 
the wrath-born (son) of the Danava , the walker in darkness, 
the protector of the showering cloud, exhilarating himself 
with the food of these (living creatures).
