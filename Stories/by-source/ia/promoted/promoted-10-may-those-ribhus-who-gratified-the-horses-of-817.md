# 10 May Those Ribhus Who Gratified The Horses Of 817

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6722-6727

10. May those Ribhus who gratified the horses (of 
Indra) by pious praise, who constructed for Indra his 
two docile steeds, bestow upon us satiety of riches, and 
wealth (of cattle), like those who devise prosperity for a 
friend.
