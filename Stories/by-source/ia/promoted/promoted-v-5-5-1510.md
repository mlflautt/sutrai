**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11553-11566

V. 5. 5. 

The deities are various : those of the first four, and the eleventh 
to the sixteenth verses are the Maruts ; the several persons whose 
names occur in the other stanzas are considered to be their divinities ; 
the llishi is Shyavashwa ; the metre of the fifth stanza is Anuahtubh , 
of the ninth Satobrihati, of the rest Gayatri : the occasion of the hymn, 
according to the Scholiast, is a wonderful old story, related by those 
learned in sacred lore : a priest of the family of Atbi, named Archa- 
nanas, having been employed as Hotri by the Raja , Rathaviti, the son 
of Darbhya, saw at the ceremonial the daughter of the Raja , and, 
being pleased by her appearance, asked her as a wife for his son Shya¬
