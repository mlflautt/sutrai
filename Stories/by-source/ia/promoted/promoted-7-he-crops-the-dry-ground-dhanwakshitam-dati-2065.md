# 7 He Crops The Dry Ground Dhanwakshitam Dati 2065

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17149-17153

7. He Crops the Dry Ground .—Dhanwakshitam dati: 
dhanwa the commentator explains a place without water, 
nirvidakapradesham ; and akshitam, trinakashthadibhir akship- 
tam, tossed over with grass, timber, and the like.
