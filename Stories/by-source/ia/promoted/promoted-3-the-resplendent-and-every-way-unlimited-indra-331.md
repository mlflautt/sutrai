# 3 The Resplendent And Every Way Unlimited Indra 331

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3120-3125

3. The resplendent and every way unlimited Indra, 
drinking the spiritless Soma juice, far surpasses the ele¬ 
ments, (far exceeds) the gods in power: he is more vast 
than heaven and earth, or the widespread spacious firma¬ 
ment.
