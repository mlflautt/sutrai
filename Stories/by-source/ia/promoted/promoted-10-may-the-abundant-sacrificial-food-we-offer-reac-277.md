# 10 May The Abundant Sacrificial Food We Offer Reac 277

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2697-2702

10. May the abundant (sacrificial) food (we offer) reach 
thee, Indra: grant us wealth that may not easily be^ 
surpassed: we augment thy vigour (by our offerings). 

-46 Rig-veda Transition [III. 3. 9
