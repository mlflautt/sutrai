# 7 Like Birds That Fly In Rows They Pass Along In T 1499

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11480-11484

7. Like birds (that fly) in rows they pass along in their 
strength above the vast summit (of the sky) to the ends 
of the firmament: their horses have caused the waters 
of the cloud to descend, as both (gods and mortals) know.
