# 1 The Twelfth Question Is That Which You Ask 89

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2790-2797

1. The twelfth question is that which you ask 
thus : In the fourth night do they score off (bar^ 
angdrend) the sin by the good works, and does 
he go by the residue (bon); or do they inflict 
punishment on him for die sin which_ has happened 
to him, and give reward and recompense for the 
good works which he has done ?
