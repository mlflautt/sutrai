# Xx Soma Pavuiuaua 115

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10130-10152

XX.  Soma Pavuiuaua. 

STRONG  Indu,  bathed  in  milk,  flows  on  for  Indra, 
Soina  exciting  strength,  for  his  carousal. 

He  quells  malignity  and  slays  the  demons,  King  of 
the  homestead,  he  who  gives  us  comfort. 

2  Then  in  a  stream  he  flows, milked out with  press- 

stones,  mingled  with  sweetness,  through  the  fleecy 
filter— 

Indu  rejoicing  in  the  love  of  Indra,  the  God  who 
gladdens,  for  the  God's  enjoyment. 

3  He  flows,  as  he  is  cleansed,  to sacred  duties,  a  God 

bedewing  Gods  with  his  own  juices. 

Indu hath, clothed in  powers  that  suit  the  season, 
on  the  raised  fleece  engaged  the  ten  swift  fingers.
