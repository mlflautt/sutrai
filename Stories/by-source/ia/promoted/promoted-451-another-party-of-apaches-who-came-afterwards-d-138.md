# 451 Another Party Of Apaches Who Came Afterwards D 138

**Source:** Collected And Translated (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7632-7638

451. Another party of Apaches, who came afterwards, dwelt a 
long time among the To'dbko-si ; but later they abode with the 
TM'paha, and became closely related to the latter. They are still 
affiliated with TM'paha, but these call them To'afoko^i. 

158 Navaho Legends.
