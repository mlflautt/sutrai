# 286 The Holy Ones Then Gave To The Young Hero The  88

**Source:** Collected And Translated (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4895-4903

286. The holy ones then gave to the young hero the name of 
Zeyaneyani, or Reared Under the Ground, because they had hid- 
den him in the earth when his brethren fled from the wrath of his 
sister. They bade him go and dwell at a place called A/ahyitsoi 
(Big Point on the Edge), which is in the shape of a kog&n> or Navaho 
hut, and here we think he still dwells. 

104 Navaho Legends.
