# 5 Be Alert For Our Prosperity Shagdhi Swastaye 2089

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17375-17379

5. Be Alert for our Prosperity. — Shagdhi swastaye : 
the commentator says the verb may here be put for the first 
person, and mean yache , I ask for, or it may mean, as trans¬ 
lated, shakto bhava, be able.
