# 26 That Curtailment Of The Good Effect Is Not Afte 377

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10756-10763

26. That curtailment of the good effect is not after- 
wards demandable (pasin-sakhuniko), if it has to 
be accepted by him ; and if that acceptance of less 
remuneration by him be an opposing of him to the 
malice and ill-temper (vushil) of the priests, this 
also is not the way that they should cause progress 
as regards their owrr business.
