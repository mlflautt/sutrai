# 10 May That Prayer Be Agreeable To Thee Agni Who 522

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4501-4506

10. May that prayer be agreeable to thee, Agni, who 
art a gracious deity, (which is uttered) by the man with 
whose well-conducted sacrifice thou art well pleased, 
youngest (of the gods), of whose (rites) when worshipping 
thee may we be the promoters.
