# 63 The Deceivers Of The Transmitters Who Have 207

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5871-5879

63. The deceivers^ of the transmitters, who have 
existed at various times, even among those who are 
blessed®, have remained a mass of knowledge for 
me, by being my reminder of the mature and proper 
duty of those truly wise (hu-y^iraginiko), through 
the directions issuable by even worldly decision, and 
of so many of which I have a remembrance®, for 
the writing of which there would be no end.
