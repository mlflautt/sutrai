# 20 As The Bhrigus Fabricate A Car For Use 1905

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15332-15339

20. As the Bhrigus fabricate a Car for Use.— 
Bhrigavo na rathim is all we have in the text : the Scholiast 
explains the first diptas-takshanah , bright or dexterous 
carpenters : the object of the comparison is not very obvious, 
but apparently it intends, that as a wheelwright makes a 
chariot for a special purpose, so the worshipper performs 
worship in order to secure Indro? s favour.
