# 9 Dark Is The Path Of Thee Who Art Bright The Ligh 597

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5002-5008

9. Dark is the path of thee who art bright; the light 
is before thee; thy moving radiance is the chief of (all 
luminous) bodies: when the present (worshippers) take 
up the germ (in the sticks of attrition) thou art speedily 
generated , and becomest indeed the messenger (of the 
sacrifice).
