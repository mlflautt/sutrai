# 10 To Thee Youngest Of The Gods Men Present Obla 1013

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8113-8117

10. To thee, youngest (of the gods), men present obla¬ 
tions, whether nigh or from far: accept the praise of him 
who most extols thee ; for the felicity (which thou con- 
ferrest), Agni, is great, vast, auspicious.
