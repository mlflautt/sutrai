# 9 The Great Rishi The Generator Of The Gods The At 381

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3477-3482

9. The great Rishi the generator of the gods, the at¬ 
tracted by the deities, the overlooker of the leaders (at 
holy rites), Vishwamitra arrested the watery stream 
when he sacrificed for Sudas ; Indra, with the Kushikas, 
was pleased.
