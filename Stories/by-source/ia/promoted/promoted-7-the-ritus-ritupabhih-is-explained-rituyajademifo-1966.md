# 7 The Ritus Ritupabhih Is Explained Rituyajademifo 1966

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15955-15963

7. The Ritus. — Ritupabhih is explained, rituyajademifo 
the deities to whom the Rita sacrifice is dedicated. 

The Wives of the Gods.- - Gnaspatnibhih : gna is usually 
rendered the wives of the gods: the compound is here ex¬ 
plained strwampalayitryah, the female protectors of women: 
it may imply the goddesses, but there is no authority for 
such an interpretation.
