# 13 Although Knowing The Heroic Acts Which Magha 1189

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9358-9365

13. Although, knowing the heroic acts which, Magha¬ 
van, thou hast performed, how may I adequately offer 
thee adoration : most mighty Indra, we ever celebrate 
at sacred rites the recent exploits which thou hast 
achieved. 

204 Rig-veda Translation [V. 2. 16
