# 4 Either Thou Traversest Savitri The Three Regions 1634

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12475-12479

4. Either thou traversest, Savitri, the three regions,, 
or combinest with the rays of Surya; or thou passest,. 
between the night on either hand ; or thou, divine Savitri, 
art Mitra, through thy (benevolent) functions.
