# 2 When The Sun Has Risen Some Says The 1698

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12967-12981

2. When the Sun has Risen.— Some, says the 
Scholiast, perform the worship of fire before sun-rise, some 
after the sun has risen. 

With (Embodied) Form.— Tanwa sujatak, well-born 
with a body, that is, with flames ; or it may mean self-born, 
swayambhu , according to the comment. 

As a Father, etc. —Janmeva tanayam, yatha pita putrarn 
sevate , janma^ being put for janaka.
