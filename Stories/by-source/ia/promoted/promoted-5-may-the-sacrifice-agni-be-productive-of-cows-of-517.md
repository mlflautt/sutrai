# 5 May The Sacrifice Agni Be Productive Of Cows Of 517

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4467-4472

5. May the sacrifice, Agni, be productive of cows, of 
sheep, of horses, and, celebrated by thy worshipper, 
aided by the priests, be ever uninterrupted: may it, 
mighty Agni, be productive of food and progeny, long 
continued, affluent, wide based, and held in full assembly.
