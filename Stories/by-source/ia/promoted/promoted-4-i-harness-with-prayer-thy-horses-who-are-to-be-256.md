# 4 I Harness With Prayer Thy Horses Who Are To Be 256

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2533-2537

4. I harness with prayer thy horses, who are to be 
harnessed with prayer, who are allies and swift-paced in 
battle: arise, and, all-knowing Indra, mounting on thy 
firm and easy chariot, come to the libation.
