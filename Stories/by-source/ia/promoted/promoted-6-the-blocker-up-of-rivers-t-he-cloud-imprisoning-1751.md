# 6 The Blocker Up Of Rivers T He Cloud Imprisoning 1751

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13609-13617

6. The Blocker-up of Rivers.—T he cloud imprisoning 
the water of the rains. 

Savitri.— Sayana considers Savitri as an epithet of 
Indra, the impeller or animator of the world, Savita sarvasya 
jagatah prerakah :the Nirukta has a similar explanation, n. 26. 

Page 39.
