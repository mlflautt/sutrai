# X Agni 196

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13344-13376

X.  Agni. 

FROM  the  two  fire-sticks  have  the  men  engendered, 
with  thought,  urged  by  the  hand,  the  glorious 
Agni, 

Far-seen,  with  pointed  flame,  Lord  of  the  Homestead. 

2  The Vasus set that Agni in the  dwelling,  fair  to 

behold,  for  help,  from  every  quarter  : 
Who,  in  the  house  for  ever,  must  be  honoured. 

3  Shine thou  before us, Agni, well-enkindled,  with 

flame,  most  youthful  God,  that  never  fadeth  ! 
To  thee  come  goods  and  treasures  all  together. 

3  The  Bull  :  Soma.  The  cows  :  the  streams  of  sacrificial  milk.  The 
Goddesses  :  according  to  Sayana,  the  propitiatory  hymns  of  praise. 
Resting-place :  the  reservoir.  A  garment  newly  ivashed :  the  milk 
with  which  the  Soma  juice  is  mixed. 

Rigveda  VII. 1.  1—3. The  Rishl  is  Vasishtha. The  metre  is  Viraj. 
Stanza  1  is  a  repetition  cf  I.  i.  ii.  2.  10. 

2  The  Vasus  :  meaning  the  Gods  generally. 

3  To  thee  come  goods  and  treasures  all  together  : Sayana interprets 
differently  :— 'To  thee  come  all  the  sacrificial  viands.' 

vl.  ii.  1,]  THE  SlMA  J E DA.  247
