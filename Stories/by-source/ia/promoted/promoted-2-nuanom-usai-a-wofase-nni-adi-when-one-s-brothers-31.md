# 2 Nuanom Usai A Wofase Nni Adi When One S Brothers 31

**Source:** Ratan Tata Library (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2269-2274

2. Nuanom usai a, wofase nni adi^ * When one’s brothers are 
not exhausted the sister’s child does not inherit *. 

This saying again proves that the general idea that it is the 
sister’s son who always succeeds is totally erroneous.
