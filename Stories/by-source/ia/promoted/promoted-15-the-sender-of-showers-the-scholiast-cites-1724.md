# 15 The Sender Of Showers The Scholiast Cites 1724

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13296-13300

15. The Sender of Showers.— The Scholiast cites 
Manu in. 76, for this property of Acjni : “the offering that is 
sacrificed with fire proceeds entirely to the sun, from the sun 
is generated rain, and from rain food ; from thence mankind. ”
