# 1 Along With Us Mahidhara Yajur Veda Xx 47 1922

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15507-15515

1. Along with us. — Mahidhara , Yajur-Veda, xx. 47, 
says, with the gods, devaih saha: Sayana has asmabhih saha. 

Like the Radiant Sun. —Dyaur na kshatram abhibhuti 
pushyat : Mahidhara connects the simile with what precedes, 
whose many exploits (are celebrated) like the heaven : he 
refers, also, kshatram to the sacrifices may he, ( Indra ), 
cherish (our) strength.
