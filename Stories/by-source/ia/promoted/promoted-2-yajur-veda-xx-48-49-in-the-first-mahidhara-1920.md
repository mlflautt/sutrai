# 2 Yajur Veda Xx 48 49 In The First Mahidhara 1920

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15494-15500

2. Yajur-Veda, xx. 48, 49: in the first, Mahidhara 
supplies balaih instead of marudbhih as the subst. to 
ojishthebhih ; and in the second renders vajasatau for the 
sake of bestowing food. 

Page 120.
