# V 4 9 1430

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11013-11020

V. 4. 9. 

The deities and Rishi as before ; the metre varies : that of the 
first, fifth, tenth, eloventh, and fifteenth verses is Kakubh; of the 
sixth, seventh, ninth, thirteenth, and fourteenth is Satobrihati; of the 
eighth and twelfth Gayatri ; of the second Brihati ; of the third Anush- 
tubh ; and of the fourth Puravshnih.
