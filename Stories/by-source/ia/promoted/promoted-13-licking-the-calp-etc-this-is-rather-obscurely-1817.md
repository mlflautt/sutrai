# 13 Licking The Calp Etc This Is Rather Obscurely 1817

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14379-14386

13. Licking the Calp, etc.- -This is rather obscurely 
•expressed : the calf is said to be Agni ; the cow with milk is 
the sky, whose udder is the cloud, whence the rain of which 
the earth is in want descends ; Ritasya sa payasa pinvatela , 
is also explained Jalavarjita prithivy-adityasyodakena sikta 
bhavati varshakale , the earth without water is sprinkled by 
the water of the sun in the rainy season.
