# 5 Thou Art Possessed Of Opulence Indra Thou Art 328

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3100-3104

5. Thou art possessed of opulence, Indra ; thou art 
the lord of heaven, auspicious and renowned: mayest 
thou who art adored of many, increasing in vigour, be to 
ns a most (bountiful bestower) of' food.
