# Iv 5 4 935

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7522-7530

IV. 5. 4. 

The deities are Indra and Brihaspati ; the Mishi is Vamadeva ; 
the metre Gayatri. 

1. (I present) the agreeable oblation to your mouths, 
Indra and Brihaspati, and the hymn and the exhilarating 
beverage are offered.
