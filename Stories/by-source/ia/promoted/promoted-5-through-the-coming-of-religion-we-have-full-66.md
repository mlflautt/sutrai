# 5 Through The Coming Of Religion We Have Full 66

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2435-2442

5. Through the coming of religion we have full 
enjoyment (bari gAkirem), and owing to religion, 
unlike bondsmen (abiirfl^ogdnvdr), we do not 
become changeable among the angels ; our spiritual 
life (ahvdlh) of praise then arrives in readiness, 
and owing to the angels there are joyous salutation, 
spiritual life, and glory for the soul.
