# 13 Sutambhara Is The Ministrant Priest Of The Inst 1359

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10498-10503

13. Sutambhara is the ministrant priest of the insti- 
tutor of the sacrifice, the causer of the upward ascent 
of all holy rites : the milch-cow offers juicy (milk) ; the 
milk is distributed : announcing this in order, (Avatsara) 
studies (the holy texts) without repose.
