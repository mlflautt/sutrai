# 7 May The Liberal Man Ever Be Prosperous Who Pro 555

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4714-4718

7. May the liberal man ever be prosperous who pro¬ 
pitiates thee with constant oblations and praises: may 
all the days in his arduous life be prosperous, and may 
this (his) sacrifice be (productive of reward).
