# 4 Inasmuch As Atri Escaping By Your Aid From The 1613

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12312-12316

4. Inasmuch as Atri, escaping by your aid from the 
fire of chaff, conciliates you, like a wife soliciting (the 
affection of a husband), therefore come with (your) pro¬ 
pitious cars, with the new-born rapidity of the falcon.
