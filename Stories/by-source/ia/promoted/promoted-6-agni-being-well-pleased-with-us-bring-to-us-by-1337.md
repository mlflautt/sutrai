# 6 Agni Being Well Pleased With Us Bring To Us By 1337

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10330-10335

6. Agni, being well pleased with us, bring to us, by 
paths frequented by the gods, the divine (female) Gna, 
mighty and omnipresent, to whom oblations are offered 
with reverence, who is vast and cognizant of rites, to 
partake of the exhilaration of the sweet Soma.
