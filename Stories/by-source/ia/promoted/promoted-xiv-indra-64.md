# Xiv Indra 64

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8065-8079

XIV.  Indra. 

O  THUNDERER,  zealous  worshippers gave thee drink 

this  time  yesterday  : 
So,  Indra,  listen  here  to  him  who  offers  lauds  : come 

near  unto  our  dwelling-place  ! 
2  Lord  of  bay steeds, fair-helmed, rejoice  thee : thee 

we  seek. Here  the  disposers  wait  on  thee. 
Thy glories, meet for praise  !  are highest  by the 

juice,  O  Indra,  lover  of  the  song.
