# 5 Eclipsing By Your Splendour Many Regions Ash Win 461

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4009-4014

5. Eclipsing (by your splendour) many regions, Ash!wins, 
(come hither) ; loud praise awaits you opulent (Ashwins) 
amongst men: come to this rite by the paths traversed 
by the gods: here, Dasras, are ample stores of the exhila¬ 
rating juices.
