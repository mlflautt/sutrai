# 7 And The Words Of The Expressive Utterance Of 145

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3996-4002

7. And the words of the expressive utterance of 
tire high-priests are these, that where there is a fear 
of every other thing it is more than the thing itself, 
hit hell is a thing worse than the fear of it. 

ClIAPTKR XXVI IL
