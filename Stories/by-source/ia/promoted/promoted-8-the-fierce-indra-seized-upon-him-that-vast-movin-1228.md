# 8 The Fierce Indra Seized Upon Him That Vast Movin 1228

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9608-9621

8. The fierce Indra seized upon him, that vast moving 
(Vritra), when slumbering, (after) having drunk the 
Soma , subduing (his foes), and enveloping (the world), 
and then slew him with his great weapon in battle, footless, 
measureless, speechless.
