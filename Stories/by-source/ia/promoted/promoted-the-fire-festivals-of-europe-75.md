These  forms  of  divination  in  the  house  were  practised 
by  the  company  in  a  body  ;  but  the  following  had  to  be 
performed  by  the  person  alone.  You  took  an  apple  and 
stood  with  it  in  your  hand  in  front  of  a  looking-glass. 
Then  you  sliced  the  apple,  stuck  each  slice  on  the  point 
of  the  knife,  and  held  it  over  your  left  shoulder,  while 
you  looked  into  the  glass  and  combed  your  hair.  The 
spectre  of  your  future  husband  would  then  appear  in  the 
mirror  stretching  forth  his  hand  to  take  the  slices  of  the 
apple  over  your  shoulder.  Some  say  that  the  number  of
