**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))

5. Presented Victims to the Vasus. —Pra ye vasubhya 
ivada namah : ivad namah are interpreted gamanavad annam, 
food having motion, that is, pashwatmakam , the same with 
animals.
