# 8 When Angered Agni Depart From Me The Pro 1025

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8180-8184

8. When angered, (Agni), depart from me: the pro¬ 
tector of the worship of the gods, (Indra), has spoken to 
me: the wise Indra has looked upon thee, and, instructed 
by him, I have come, Agni, to thee.
