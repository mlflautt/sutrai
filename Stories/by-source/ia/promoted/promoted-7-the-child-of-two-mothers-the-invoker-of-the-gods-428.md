# 7 The Child Of Two Mothers The Invoker Of The Gods 428

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3769-3775

7. The child of two mothers, the invoker of the gods at 
sacrifices, the universal sovereign, proceeds thenceforward 
<(in the sky): the root (of all), he abides (in the houses of 
the pious): the reciters of pleasant (words) offer him 
agreeable (praises): great and unequalled is the might 
of the gods.
