# 16 Five Races Of Men Panchajanyasu Krishtishu 1803

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14183-14194

16. Five Races of Men.— Panchajanyasu krishtishu : 
.here, therefore, the five distinctions are restricted to human 
beings, confirming the Scholiast’s notion that the four castes 
and barbarians are intended. 

Daughter of the Sun.— Pakshya , the daughter of 
Paksha : pakshanirvahakasya, the distributor of the parts 
(of the year ?), that is, mryasya, of the sun. 

Bestower of New Life.— Navyam ayur dadhana, having 
new life or food : the Scholiast adds, mama kurvana bhavatu.
