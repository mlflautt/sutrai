# 12 This Our Sacrifice Indra Is Thy Augmentation Fo 223

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2301-2306

12. This our sacrifice, Indra, is thy augmentation, for* 
the rite in which the Soma is effused is acceptable to thee : 
do thou who art deserving of worship protect the worship¬ 
per by (the efficacy of) the worship, and may this sacrifice 
strengthen thy thunderbolt for the slaying of Ahi.
