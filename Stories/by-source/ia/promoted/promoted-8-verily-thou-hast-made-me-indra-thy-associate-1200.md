# 8 Verily Thou Hast Made Me Indra Thy Associate 1200

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9431-9435

8. Verily thou hast made me, Indra, thy associate 
when grinding the head of the slave Namuchi like a sound- 
ing and rolling cloud: and the heaven and earth (have 
been caused) by the Maruts (to revolve like a wheel).
