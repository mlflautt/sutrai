# 9 In Those Plants The Aged Messenger Of The Gods 430

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3782-3787

9. In those (plants) the aged messenger (of the gods) 
constantly abides: mighty, he passes with the radiant 
(sun) through the regions of space; assuming various 
.forms, he looks (complacently) upon us: great and un¬ 
equalled is the might of the gods.
