# 3 May Indra Accompanied By The Maruts Come 716

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5867-5872

3. May Indra, accompanied by the Maruts, come 
quickly for our protection, from the heaven, from the 
earth, from the firmament, or from the waters ; from 
the sphere of the sun, from (any) distant region, from 
the abode of the rains.
