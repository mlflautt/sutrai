# 2 Thou Indra Meditating Upon Us Fastenest The 1235

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9665-9669

2. Thou, Indra, meditating upon us, fastenest the 
traces of thy horses, (incited), showerer (of benefits), by 
those praises in which thou takest suitable delight, and 
do thou therefore overcome (for us) hostile men.
