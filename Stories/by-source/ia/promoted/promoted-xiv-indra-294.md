# Xiv Indra 294

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16094-16122

XIV.  Indra. 

KIND-THOUGHTED  is  the  noble,  gladdening,  friendly  one. 

2  Approach,  O  beauteous  hero,  this  auspicious  pair  that 

draws  the  car ! 
These  two  are  coming  near  to  us. 

3  Bend  lowly  down,  as  twere,  your  heads  :  he  stands 

amid  the  water-flood, 
Pointing  with  his  ten  horns  the  way. 

Eigveda  VIII.  6.  4,  6,  5.  The  Kishi  is  Vatsa.  The  metre  is  Gayatri. 
Stanza  1  is  a  repetition  of  I.  ii.  i.  5.  3t  and  stanza  3  of  1.  ii.  ii.  4.  8. 

This  hymn  is  not  taken  from  the  Rigveda.  The  first  line  appears 
to  be  a  fragment.  The  metre  of  stanzas  2  and  3  ia  Gayatri. 

1  Friendly  one :  s&nart :  Dawn  appears  to  be  intended.  According 
to  Benfey,  stutih,  song  of  praise,  is  understood. 

3  Bend  lowly  down:  to  receive  Indra's  blessing.  Ten  horns:  fingers, 
according  to  Sayana.  The  Scholiast  does  not  name  the  deity  of  ti» 
hymn.
