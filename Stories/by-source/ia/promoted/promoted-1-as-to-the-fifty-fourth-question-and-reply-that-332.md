# 1 As To The Fifty Fourth Question And Reply That 332

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9862-9867

1. As to the fifty-fourth question and reply, that 
which you ask is thus : What is the occupation and 
capacity (giriftirih) of the person that has to pre- 
serve those who are in their three nights’ trials^, 
and who is he ?
