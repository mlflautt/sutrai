# 2 Indra And Pushan The Showerers Of Benefits 452

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3941-3946

2. Indra and Pushan, the showerers (of benefits), 
and the happy-handed AshWins, well affected towards us, 
have milked the (cloud) reposing in heaven; wherefore, 
Vasus , universal deities, sporting on this (altar), may I 
here obtain the happiness derived from you.
