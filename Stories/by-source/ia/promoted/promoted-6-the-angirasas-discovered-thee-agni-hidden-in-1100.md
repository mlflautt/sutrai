# 6 The Angirasas Discovered Thee Agni Hidden In 1100

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8699-8703

6. The Angirasas discovered thee, Agni, hidden in 
.secret, and taking refuge from wood to wood; thou art 
.generated, being churned with great force ; therefore they 
have called thee, Angiras, the son of strength.
