# 8 May Those Ten Bright Horses The Gift To Me Of Th 1241

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9699-9706

8. May those ten bright horses, the gift to me of the 
pious gold-possessing Trasadasyu, the son of Purukutsa, 

212 Rig-veda Translation [V. 3.2 

of the race of Girikshita, convey me (to the sacrifice), 
and may I proceed quickly with the rites.
