# 9 With Numerous Adherents Shravo Virovat Might 1970

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15989-15993

9. With Numerous Adherents. — Shravo-virovat might 
be rendered food-eomprehending-oft-spring, but as praja has 
been already specified, the Scholiast interprets viravat , viros 
habenSy by bhrityadibhirupetam , endowed with dependants.
