# 4 May Heaven And Earth Vast Universal Adorable 984

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7898-7903

4. May Heaven and Earth, vast, universal, adorable, 
united in satisfaction, and disposed to give us food, protect 
us with our spacious dwellings, inhabited by our wives, 
and may we for our (pious) acts be possessed of chariots 
and slaves.
