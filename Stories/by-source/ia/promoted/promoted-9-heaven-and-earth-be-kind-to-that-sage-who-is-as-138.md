# 9 Heaven And Earth Be Kind To That Sage Who Is As  138

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1707-1717

9. Heaven and earth be kind to that sage who is as it 
were a many-ohannelled and inexhaustible stream (of 
knowledge) ; the parent (of his disci pies), the collater of 
holy texts, rejoicing on the lap of his parents, whose words 
are truth. 

ill. 2. 15. 

The deity is Agni, or of the first verse, Ritu, or the personified 
season; Vishwamitra is the Rishi; the metre is Gayatri.
