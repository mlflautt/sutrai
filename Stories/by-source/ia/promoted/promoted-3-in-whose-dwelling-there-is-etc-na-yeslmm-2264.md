# 3 In Whose Dwelling There Is Etc Na Yeslmm 2264

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 19175-19186

3. In Whose Dwelling there is, etc.— Na yeslmm 
irisadhaslhe ishte is explained bv Sayana, Marutam swakiye 
nivase tishlata?n prerita na ishte na ishate chalayitum , there is 
no impeller who has power to cause the movement of the 
Maruts when abiding in their own abode. 

Impellers of the Rivers. — That is, the lightning, as 
associated with rain, may be considered as giving impulse 
to the rivers.
