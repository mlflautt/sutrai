**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10516-10533

V. 4. 1. 

The deities are the Vishwadevas ; the Rishi is Sadapjiina ; the 
metre is Trishtubh. 

1. (Indra) recovered (the hidden cattle), hurling his 
thunderbolt from heaven at the prayers of the Angirasas : 

Kig-vepa Translation 

[V. 4. 1 

232 

the rays of the approaching dawn are spread around: 
the divine sun, scattering the clustered gloom, has risen 
and set open the doors of (the habitations of) men.
