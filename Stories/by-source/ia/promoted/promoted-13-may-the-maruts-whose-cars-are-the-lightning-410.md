# 13 May The Maruts Whose Cars Are The Lightning 410

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3656-3662

13. May the Maruts , whose cars are the lightning, 
who are armed with spears, resplendent, destroyers of 
foes, from whom the waters proceed, (who are) unresting 
and adorable, and may Saraswati hear (my prayer) ; 
and may you (Maruts), speedy in your liberality, bestow 
(upon us) riches and good offspring.
