# 3 By The Power Of The Emitter Of Showers 2216

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18693-18700

3. By the Power of the Emitter of Showers. — 
Asurasya mayaya is explained udaka nirasituh parjanyasya 
samarthyena, by the power of Parjanya , the caster-fortli of 
water, influenced by Mitra and Varum as the deities presiding 
over day and night. 

Page 262.
