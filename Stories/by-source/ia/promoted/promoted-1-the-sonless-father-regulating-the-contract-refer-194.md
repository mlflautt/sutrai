# 1 The Sonless Father Regulating The Contract Refer 194

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2084-2095

1. The sonless father, regulating (the contract), refers 
to his grandson, (the son) of his daughter, and relying on 
the efficiency of the rite, honours (his son-in-law) with 
valuable gifts: the father, trusting to the impregnation 
of the daughter, supports himself with a tranquil mind. 

2. (A son) born of the body, does not transfer (paternal) 
wealth to a sister: he has made (her) the receptacle of the 
embryo of the husband: if the parents procreate children 
{of either sex), one is the performer of holy acts, the other 
is to be enriched (with gifts).
