# 3 Yajush 11 6 Sa Etashah Savitri Sayana Explains 2245

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 19005-19013

3. Yajush , 11. 6. sa etashah savitri: Sayana explains 
etashah by etavarnah, shubhrah , shobhamana , white-coloured, 
bright, shining : Mahidhara resolves it into etad , this, this 
world, and shete, who reposes in, who pervades ; or, as etasha 
ordinarily means a horse, Nighantu , 1. 14. it may be 
intended to designate the sun under that form.
