# 8 May The Sacrifice Most Solemnly Offered By The D 1163

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9191-9200

8. May the sacrifice most solemnly offered by the devout 
duly proceed to-day (to the gods): spread the sacred 
grass for their seat. 

20d Rig-veda Translation [V. 2. IS 

U May the Maruts, the Ashwins, Mitra, Yaruna, 
the gods, with all their attendants, sit down upon this 
grass.
