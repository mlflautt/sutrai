# 6 May Thy Mighty Horses Indra Harnessed In Thy 315

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3018-3024

6. May thy mighty horses, Indra, harnessed (in thy 
car), and alike exhilarated (by the beverage), bring thee 
to our presence, for they are the destroyers (of the foes) 
of the showerer (of benefits), and kindly rubbed by his 
hand they traverse, going quickly, the regions of the sky, 
making them as it were twofold.
