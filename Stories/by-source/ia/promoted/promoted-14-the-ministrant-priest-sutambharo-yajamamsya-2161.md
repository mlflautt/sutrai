# 14 The Ministrant Priest Sutambharo Yajamamsya 2161

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18150-18158

14. The Ministrant Priest. —Sutambharo yajamamsya 
satpatih : the last, according to Sayana , is put for kotri ; the 
first is the name of a Rishi: its etymological purport, the 
bearer or offerer of the libation, is possibly only a metaphorical 
personation, and, with the context, though mystically ex¬ 
pressed, merely describes the effusion of the Soma.
