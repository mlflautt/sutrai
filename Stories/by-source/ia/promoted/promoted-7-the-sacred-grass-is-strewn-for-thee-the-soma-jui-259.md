# 7 The Sacred Grass Is Strewn For Thee The Soma Jui 259

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2548-2553

7. The sacred grass is strewn for thee ; the Soma juice,. 
Indra, is poured forth ; the grain wherewith to feed thy 
horses is prepared ; the oblations are offered to thee abid¬ 
ing on the sacred grass, who art praised of many, and 
(art) the showerer (of benefits), attended by the Maruts.
