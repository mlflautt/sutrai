# 2 That Embryo Did Not Beguile Me Into Satisfaction 775

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6286-6291

2. That embryo did not beguile me into satisfaction, 
but by the keen energy (of divine wisdom), I triumphed 
over it: the impeller of all, the sustainer of many, aban¬ 
doned the foes (of knowledge), and, expanding, passed 
beyond the winds (of worldly troubles).
