# 14 The Gloom Investing Night Has Dispersed With Th 1206

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9462-9466

14. The (gloom-)investing night has dispersed with the 
dawn (upon the appearance of) Rinanchaya, the Raja 
of the Rusamas: Babhru being summoned, going like a 
fleet courser, has received the four thousand (cattle).
