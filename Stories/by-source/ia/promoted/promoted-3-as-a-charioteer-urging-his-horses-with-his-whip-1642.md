# 3 As A Charioteer Urging His Horses With His Whip 1642

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12542-12548

3. As a charioteer, urging his horses with his whip,, 
brings into view the messenger (of war), so Parjanya, 
(driving the clouds before him), makes manifest the mes¬ 
sengers of the rain: the roaring of the lion-(like cloud) 
proclaims from afar that Parjanya overspreads the sky 
with rainy clouds.
