# 8 Mag Ha Van Becomes Repeatedly Manifest In Variou 380

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3470-3476

8. Mag ha van becomes repeatedly (manifest) in various 
forms, practising delusions with respect to his own peculiar 
person ; and invoked bv his appropriate prayers, he comes 
in a moment from heaven to the three (daily rites), and, 
although observant of seasons, is the drinker (of the Soma) 
irrespective of season.
