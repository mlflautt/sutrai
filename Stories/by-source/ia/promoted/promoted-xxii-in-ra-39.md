# Xxii In Ra 39

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7128-7140

XXII.  In'ra. 

WE  call  on  thee,  O  matchless  one.  We,  seeking  help, 

possessing  nothing  firm  ourselves, 
Call  on  thee,  wondrous,  thunder-armed  ' 

2  On  thee  for  aid  in  sacrifice. This  youth  of  ours, the 

bold,  the  terrible,  hath  gone  forth. 
We  therefore, we thy  friends, Indra, have chosen 
thee,  spoil-winner,  as  our  suceourer.
