# 2 My Desire Proceeds Kamo Me Ichchan Charati 1809

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14283-14287

2. My Desire Proceeds. —Kamo me ichchan charati, 
lit. my desire wishing goes, or exists : the Scholiast adds to 
ichchan, sarvan bhogan, all enjoyments, but this does not make 
the expression more definite.
