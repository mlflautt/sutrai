# 1 Where Is That Hero Who Has Seen Indra Seated 1193

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9383-9388

1. Where is that hero ? who has seen Indra seated 
in his easy chariot, travelling with his horses, the thun- 
derer, the invoked of many, who, desirous of the libation, 
is proceeding with riches to the habitation (of his wor¬ 
shipper) for his preservation ?
