# 5 Offer With Your Prostrations Due Praise To The 485

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4178-4182

5. Offer, with your prostrations, due praise to the 
divine Us has, shining upon you: the repository of sweet¬ 
ness manifests her brightness aloft in the sky, and, radiant 
and lovely, lights the regions.
