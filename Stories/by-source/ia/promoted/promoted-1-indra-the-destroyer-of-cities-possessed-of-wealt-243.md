# 1 Indra The Destroyer Of Cities Possessed Of Wealt 243

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2440-2448

1. Indra, the destroyer of cities, possessed of wealth, 
manifesting (his greatness), merciless to his enemies, has 

40, Rig-veda Translation [III. 3.5 

•overspread the day with his radiance: attracted by prayer, 
increasing in bulk, and armed with many weapons, he 
has delighted both heaven and earth.
