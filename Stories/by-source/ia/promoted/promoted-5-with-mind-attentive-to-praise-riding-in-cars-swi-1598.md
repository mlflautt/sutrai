# 5 With Mind Attentive To Praise Riding In Cars Swi 1598

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12189-12193

5. With mind attentive (to praise), riding in cars, swift- 
moving, listening to invocations, you hastened with your 
steeds to the single-purposed Chyavana : masters of 
mystic lore, hear my invocation.
