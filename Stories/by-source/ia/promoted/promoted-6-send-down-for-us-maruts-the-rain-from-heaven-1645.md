# 6 Send Down For Us Maruts The Rain From Heaven 1645

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12561-12565

6. Send down for us, Maruts, the rain from heaven : 
drops of the rainy charger descend : come down Parjanya, 
sprinkling water by this thundering (cloud); thou who art 
the sender of rain, our protector.
