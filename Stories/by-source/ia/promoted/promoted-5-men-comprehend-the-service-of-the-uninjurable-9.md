# 5 Men Comprehend The Service Of The Uninjurable 9

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 846-850

5. Men comprehend the service , of the uninjurable 
showerer (of benefits), and exult in the commands of the 
mighty (Agni) : their frequent and earnest hymns of 
praise, bright and radiant, are illuminating heaven.
