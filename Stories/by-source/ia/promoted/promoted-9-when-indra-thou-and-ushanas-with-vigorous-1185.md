# 9 When Indra Thou And Ushanas With Vigorous 1185

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9336-9340

9. When, Indra, thou and Ushanas, with vigorous, 
and rapid courses went to the dwelling of Kutsa, then, 
destroying his foes, you went in one chariot with Kutsa 
and the gods, and verily thou hast slain Shushna.
