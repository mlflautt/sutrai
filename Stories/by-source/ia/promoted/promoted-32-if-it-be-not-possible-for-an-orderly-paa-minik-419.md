# 32 If It Be Not Possible For An Orderly Paa Minik 419

**Source:** The Text Is Fly (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11573-11580

32. If it be not possible for an orderly (paa^minik) 
religion to exist, without rescue from these incon- 
sistent assertions of many kinds, (33) they then^ say 
this of it, that to the supposers of two original evolu- 
tions ® the work of the sacred being is weak and un- 
resisting; (34) and they say it is not as it were 
adapted to the grandeur of the sacred being.
