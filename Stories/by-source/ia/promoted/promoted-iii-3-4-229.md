# Iii 3 4 229

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2340-2346

III. 3. 4. 

As this hymn consists of a dialogue between VishwamUra and 
the rivers Vipash and Shutudri , they are considered to be respectively 
the Rishis of the verses ascribed to either; the divinity is Indra ; 
the metre is Trishtubh, except in the last verse, in which it is Anushtubh .
