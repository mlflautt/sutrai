# 6 May Indra Heaven And Earth Sindhu With The 971

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7815-7819

6. May Indra, heaven and earth, Sindhu with the 
waters, and Aditi with the Adityas, bestow happiness 
upon us, who, offering libations, Savitri, pour out the 
auspicious Soma , day by day, thrice a day.
