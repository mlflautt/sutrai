# 9 By What Wisdom Is He Who Is Most Wise Renowned 711

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5828-5833

9. By what wisdom is he who is most wise renowned ? 
by that wherewith the mighty Indra repeatedly does 
(great things): he is the especial effacer of the manifold 
sin of the worshipper, and bestows wealth upon his 
adorer.
