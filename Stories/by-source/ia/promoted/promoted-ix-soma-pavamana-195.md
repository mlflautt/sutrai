IX.  Soma  Pavamana. 

EVEN  as  the  beams  of  Surya,  urging men to speed, 
they  issue  forth  together,  gladdening  as  they  flow, 
These  swift  outpourings  in  long  course  of  holy  rites  : 
no  form  save  only  Indra  shows  itself  so  pure.
