# 10 Thou Agni Art The Protector Of Him Who Possesse 558

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4730-4736

10. Thou, Agni, art the protector of him who, possessed 
of good horses and a golden car, approaches thee with a 
chariot laden with wealth: thou art the friend of him who 
gratifies thee by the due performance of hospitality to thee* 

Fourth Mandala
