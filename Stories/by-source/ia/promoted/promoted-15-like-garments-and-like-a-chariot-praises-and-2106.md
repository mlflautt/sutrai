# 15 Like Garments And Like A Chariot Praises And 2106

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17601-17610

15. Like Garments and like a Chariot. —Praises and 
hymns, the Scholiast says, are compared to garments, as 
being fit to be received as a respectful present, upasanhara- 
vadgrahyani : if correct, this shews the custom of presenting 
honorary dresses to be of Indian origin and considerable 
antiquity : the same are compared to a chariot, as the means 
of conveying lndra and the gods to sacrifices.
