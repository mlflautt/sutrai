# 6 Come Friends Let Us Celebrate That Solemn Rite 1367

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10557-10562

6. Come, friends, let us celebrate that solemn rite 
which was effectual in setting open the (secret) stalls 
of the (stolen) cattle; by which Manu overcame 
Vishishipra ; by which the merchant, going to the wood 
(for it), obtained the water.
