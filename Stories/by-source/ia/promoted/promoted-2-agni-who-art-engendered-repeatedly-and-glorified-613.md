# 2 Agni Who Art Engendered Repeatedly And Glorified 613

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5142-5147

2. Agni, who art engendered repeatedly, and glorified 
by sacrifice, set open heaven to him who offers thee ado¬ 
ration: resplendent (Agni), bestow upon us that ample 
and acceptable (wealth), which, radiant (deity), thou, 
with all the gods, hast given (to other worshippers).
