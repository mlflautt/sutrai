# 11 They Uphold The Distant Objects The Text 2187

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18397-18407

11. They Uphold the Distant Objects. —The text 
has only adha paravatah , the latter being formed from paravat T 
duradeshah , a distant place or country, tat sanibandhinah being 
in relation to it; that is, according to Sayana , the winds 
being in the firmament give support to the distant stars 
and planets. 

416
