# Xiv Bawn 46

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7513-7527

XIV.  Bawn. 

ADVANCING,  sending  forth  her  rays, the  daughter of 

the  Sky  is  seen. 
The  mighty  one  lays  bare  the  darkness  with  her  eye, 

the  friendly  Lady  makes  the  light. 

2  The  Sun  ascending,  the refulgent star,  pours down 

his  beams  together  with  the  Dawn. 
O  Dawn, at thine arising, and  the Sun's,  may  we 
attain  the  share  allotted  us  I
