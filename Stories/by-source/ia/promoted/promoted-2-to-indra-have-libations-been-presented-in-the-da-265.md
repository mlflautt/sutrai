# 2 To Indra Have Libations Been Presented In The Da 265

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2595-2600

2. To Indra have libations been presented in the days 
of old, whereby he hath become illustrious, the regulator 
of time, the granter of desires: accept, Indra, these 
prescribed (offerings), and drink of this auspicious (beve¬ 
rage), expressed by the stones.
