# 17 Jars Khari A Certain Measure By Metonymy 1958

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15891-15895

17. Jars. — Khari , a certain measure ; by metonymy, 
a jar or ewer, drona-kalasha, holding such a quantity: in mo¬ 
dern use it is the name of a grain measure, equal to sixteen 
dronas , or about three bushels.
