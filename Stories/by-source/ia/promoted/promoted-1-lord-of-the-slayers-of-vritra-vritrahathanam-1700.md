# 1 Lord Of The Slayers Of Vritra Vritrahathanam 1700

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12991-12999

1. Lord of the Slayers of Vritra.— Vritrahathanam 
ishe , but Vritra may here imply an enemy, or iniquity, as by 
another text, twayi samarpitakarmanam asmakam twat pra- 
sadat papakshuyo bhavati , through thy favouj is the destruction 
of the sins of us whose good works have been delivered to 
thee: also Sama-Veda, I. 60. 

Page 13.
