# Viii Sorna Pavamana 204

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13598-13617

VIII.  Sorna  Pavamana, 

MADE  pure  by  this  man's  argent  zeal  and  impulse, 
the  God  hath  with  his  juice  the  Gods  pervaded. 

Pressed,  singing,  to  the  sieve  he  goes,  as  passes  the 
Hotar  to  enclosures  holding  cattle. 

2  Robed in fair raiment  meet to  wear  in combat, 

a  mighty  Sage  pronouncing  invocations, 
Roll  onward  to  the  press-boards  as  they  cleanse  thee, 
far-seeing  at  the  feast  of  Gods  and  watchful ! 

3  Dear, he is  brightened on the  fleecy summit, a 

prince  among  us,  nobler  than  the  noble. 
Roar  out  as  thou  art  purified, run forward  !  Do  ye 
preserve  us  evermore  with  blessings  !
