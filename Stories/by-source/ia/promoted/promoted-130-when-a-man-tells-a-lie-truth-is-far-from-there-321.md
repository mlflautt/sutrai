# 130 When A Man Tells A Lie Truth Is Far From There 321

**Source:** The Text Is Fly (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9669-9694

130. When a man tells a lie, truth is far from there 
(131) and when he speaks true, falsehood has no 
position there, and that man is called truthful. 132. 
So also when sickness has come, health is not there ; 
(133) and when health has come, sickness has gone. 

' See § 95 n. 

^ Reading a din aj ax, 'then its thereby’ (with a double pro- 
nominal sufiix), which is the original Pahlavi indicated by Paz, 
ainax (see Mkh. IX, 6 n). 

® The archangel ‘ good thought/ who is said to hold the posi- 
tion and vanquish 'evil thought/ while the angel Srbsh does the 
same as regards 'wrath’ (see Dinkar^ quoted in Dd. XCIV, i n; 
also Bd. XXX, 29). 

^ Sans, adds ‘ and that man is called false/ which JE also inserts 
in Pdzand in the margin, but all other manuscripts omit.
