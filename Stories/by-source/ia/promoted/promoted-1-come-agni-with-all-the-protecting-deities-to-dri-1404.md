# 1 Come Agni With All The Protecting Deities To Dri 1404

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10842-10848

1. Come, Agni, with all the protecting deities, to drink 
the libation: come with the gods. 

2. (Gods who are) devoutly praised and worshipped 
in truth, come to the sacrifice, and drink the libation with 
the tongue of Agni.
