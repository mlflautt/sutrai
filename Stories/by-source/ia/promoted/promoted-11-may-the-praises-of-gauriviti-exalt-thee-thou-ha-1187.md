# 11 May The Praises Of Gauriviti Exalt Thee Thou Ha 1187

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9347-9352

11. May the praises of Gauriviti exalt thee : thou hast 
humbled Pipru for the son of Vidathin : Kijishwan 
preparing dressed viands, has, through thy friendship, 
brought thee (to his presence), and thou hast drunk of 
his libation.
