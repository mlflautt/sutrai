# 7 May A Son Firm In Devotion And Liberal In Offeri 519

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4478-4483

7. May a son, firm in (devotion) and liberal (in offerings), 
be born to him who presents (sacrificial) food to thee 
when needing food, who gives thee constantly the exhila¬ 
rating (Soma juice), who welcomes thee as a guest, and 
devoutly kindles thee in his mansion.
