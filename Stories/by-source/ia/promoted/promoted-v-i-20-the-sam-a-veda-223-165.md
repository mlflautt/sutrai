# V I 20 The Sam A Veda 223 165

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12143-12154

V.  i.  20.]  THE  SAM  A  VEDA.  223 

2  Whom  as  a  Sage  who  merits  praise  the  Gods have, 

from  the  olden  time, 
Established among mortal men. 

3  Do thou, most  youthful  God,  protect  the  men  who 

offer, hear  their  songs, 
And  of  thyself  preserve  their seed  !
