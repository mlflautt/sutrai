# 3 Proud Of His Descendants And Vain Op His 2126

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17788-17796

3. Proud of his Descendants and Vain op his 
Person. — Tatanushtim , tanushubhram, are explained, he who 
desires, (vashti) an extended ( tata ) race (santatim ) and he 
whose person (tanu) is decorated (shubhra ) with ornaments, 
and in both implying swaposhakam ayajvamm. a self- 
cherisher not offering sacrifice. 

Page 213.
