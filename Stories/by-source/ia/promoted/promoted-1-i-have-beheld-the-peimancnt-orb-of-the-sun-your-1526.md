# 1 I Have Beheld The Peimancnt Orb Of The Sun Your 1526

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11682-11687

1. I have beheld the peimancnt orb of the sun, your 
(dwelling place), concealed by water, where (the hymns 
of the pious) liberate (his) steeds ; where a thousand rays 
abide together ; the one most excellent of the (embodied) 
forms of the gods.
