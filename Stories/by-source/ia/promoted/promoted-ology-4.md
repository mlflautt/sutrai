# Ology 4

**Source:** Bureau Of Ethnology (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 326-348

OLOGY 

J  designates  increased  stress  of  articulation. 

!  designates  increased  stress  of  articulation  due  to*  the 

elision  of  q. 

s  is  a  very  deep  laryngeal  intonation,  due  to  tlie  elision  of  q. 

2,  4  designate  excessive  length  of  vowels,  representing  approx- 

imately the  double  and  fourfold  mora. 

Words  ending  with  a  short  vowel  must  be  contracted  with  the  first 
vowel  of  the  next  word.  When  a  word  ends  with  a  long  vowel  and  the 
next  begins  with  a  vowel,  a  euphonic  -y-  is  inserted.  The  last  conso- 
nant of  a  word  is  united  with  the  first  vowel  of  the  next  word  to  one 
syllable. 

TKIANA^MUKC. 
Myths.
