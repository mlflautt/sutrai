# V Surya 220

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14085-14106

V.  Surya. 

MAY  the  bright  God  drink  glorious  Soma-mingled 
meath,  giving  the  sacrifice's  lord  unbroken  life  ; 

He  who,  wind-urged,  in  person  guards  our  offspring 
well,  nourishes  them  with  food  and  shines  o'er 
many  a  land. 

2  Radiant, as  high  Truth, cherished,  best  at  winning 

strength,  Truth  based  upon  the  statute  that  sup- 
ports the  heavens, 

He  rose,  a  light  that  kills  Vritras  and  enemies,  best 
slayer  of  the  Dasyus,  Asuras,  and  foes. 

3  This  light, the  best  of  lights, supreme, all-conquer- 

ing, winner  of  riches,  is  exalted  with  high  laud. 
All-lighting, radiant, mighty as the Sun to see, 
he  spreadeth  wide  unshaken  victory  and  strength.
