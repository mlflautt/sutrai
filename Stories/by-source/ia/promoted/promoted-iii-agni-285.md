# Iii Agni 285

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15802-15815

III.  Agni. 

WONDERFUL,  with  thy  saving  help,  send  us  thy  boun- 
ties, gracious  Lord  ! 

Thou  art  the  charioteer,  Agni,  of  earthly  wealth : 
find  rest  and  safety  for  our  seed  ! 

2  Prosper  our  kith  and  kin  with  thy  protecting  powers 
inviolate,  never  negligent ! 

Keep  far  from  us,  O  Agni,  all  celestial  wrath,  and 
wickedness  of  godless  men  !
