# 2 Worshipped In A Hundred Kites Shatakratu 1765

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13748-13752

2. Worshipped in a Hundred Kites. — Shatakratu 
is a name of Indra. or it may be used as an epithet, he to whom 
a hundred (i.e., many) sacrifices are offered, or by whom many 
great acts are performed.
