# 15 Indra Be Firm For There Are Obstructors Of Thy 185

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2028-2034

15. Indra, be firm, for there are obstructors of (thy) 
path: secure to the worshipper and his friends (the means 

* of completing) the sacrifice: mortal enemies bearing bows, 
armed with mischievous weapons, and coming with evil 
intent, must be destroyed (by thee).
