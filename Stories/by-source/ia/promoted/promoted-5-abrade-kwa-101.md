# 5 Abrade Kwa 101

**Source:** Ratan Tata Library (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6708-6718

5. Abrade Kwa.) 

* Nhyidwo was the word she used, which 1 took down at the time without 
knowing to what it referred. It will be seen that it is a particular day in the 
forty-two day cycle still kept up among the Ashanti (Brong) of the far north 
of Ashanti. See Chap. IX. 

* Dogs, until quite recently, were not allowed to be kept at Asubengya.
