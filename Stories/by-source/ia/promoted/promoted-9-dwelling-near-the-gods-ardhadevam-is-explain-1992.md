# 9 Dwelling Near The Gods Ardhadevam Is Explain 1992

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16270-16277

9. Dwelling Near the Gods.— Ardhadevam is explain¬ 
ed devanam samipe vartamanam ; or we might render it, 
demi-god, though such an appellation would not apply to 
Indra , to whom Trasadasyu is compared : on the contrary, 
Indra is declared to be one half of all the gods, and therefore 
entitled to the largest share of offerings, yat sarvesham ardham 
Indrah prati tasmad Indro devatanam bhuyishthabhaktamah.
