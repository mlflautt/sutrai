# 85 That Original Deluder And Misleader Is Allowed 365

**Source:** The Text Is Fly (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10536-10542

85. That original deluder and misleader is allowed 
an eternal life. 86. And, even till now, his will is 
more triumphant and absolute than that of the sacred 
being, through deluding and misleading, (8 7) because 
those deluded and astray are much more numerous 
than those hi the true path and undeluded.
