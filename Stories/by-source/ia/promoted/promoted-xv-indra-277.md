# Xv Indra 277

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15584-15600

XV.  Indra. 

THIS  is  thine  own. Thou  drawest  near,  as  turns  a 

pigeon  to  his  mate  : 
Thou  carest,  too,  for  this  our  prayer. 

2  O  hero, Lord  of  bounties,  praised  in  hymns,  may 

glorious  fame  and  might 
Be  his  who  sings  the  laud  to  thee  ! 

3  Lord  of  a  Hundred  Powers,  rise  up  to  be  our  succour 

in  this  fight : 
In  other  fights  let  us  agree !
