RECEIVE THEIR LIGHT FROM 

THE SUN 

ONCE upon a time there was great scarcity of 
food in the land. Father Anansi and his 
son, Kweku Tsin, being very hungry, set out 
one morning to hunt in the forest. In a short time 
Kweku Tsin was fortunate enough to kill a fine deer 
— which he carried to his father at their resting-place. 
Anansi was very glad to see such a supply of food, 
and requested his son to remain there on guard, while 
he went for a large basket in which to carry it home. 
An hour or so passed without his return, and Kweku 
Tsin became anxious. Fearing lest his father had 
lost his way, he called out loudly, " Father, father ! " 
to guide him to the spot. To his joy he heard a 
voice reply, " Yes, my son," and immediately he 
shouted again, thinking it was x\nansi. Instead of 
the latter, however, a terrible dragon appeared. 
This monster breathed fire from his great nostrils,
