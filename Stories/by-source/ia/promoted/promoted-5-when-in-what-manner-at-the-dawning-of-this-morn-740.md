# 5 When In What Manner At The Dawning Of This Morn 740

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6043-6048

5. When, in what manner, at the dawning of this morn¬ 
ing, has the divine Indra accepted the friendship of a 
mortal ? when, and in what manner, (is) his friendship 
(manifested) to the friends who have spread out the desira¬ 
ble and suitable (offering) before him ?
