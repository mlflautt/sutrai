# 2 The Rays Of Light Extending Round Fulfilling The 1385

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10677-10681

2. The rays (of light) extending round, fulfilling their 
duty (of bringing on the day), abiding in contact witli 
the orb of the immortal (sun), unlimited and diffusive, 
spread everywhere through heaven and earth.
