# 1 Stay Indra Having Yoked The Horses To The Car As 253

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2511-2516

1. Stay, Indra, having yoked the horses to the car, as 
Vayu stops when lie has put to his steeds, and come to 
our presence: solicited by us to drink of the (Soma) 
beverage, which with reverence we offer for thv exhila¬ 
ration .
