poured from the ladle on the fire is alluded to: Mahidhara 
interprets dhena, which Sayana makes an epithet of saritah, 
and renders jirmayitryah, by vachah, words, texts, which he 
says flow like rivers, freed from error by the heart and the 
mind ; separating antar-hrida by the copulative from tnanasa
