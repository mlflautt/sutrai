# 11 He Indra Has Formerly Arrested In Battle The 1218

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9548-9552

11. He, (Indra), has formerly arrested in battle the 
rapid chariot of the sun : Etasa has borne away the 
wheel, and (with it Indra) demolishes (his foes): may he, 
giving us precedence, be propitiated by our rite.
