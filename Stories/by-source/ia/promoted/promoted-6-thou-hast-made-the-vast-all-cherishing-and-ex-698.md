# 6 Thou Hast Made The Vast All Cherishing And Ex 698

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5732-5742

6. Thou hast made the vast, all-cherishing, and ex¬ 
uberant earth, delighted with (abundant) food, and 
tremulous water, for (the sake of) Turviti and Vayya: 
thou hast made the rivers easy to be crossed. 

7k Indra has filled the youthful rivers, the parents of 
plenty, the corroders (of their banks), like armies destruc¬ 
tive (of their foes): he has inundated the dry lands, and 
(satisfied) the thirsty travellers: he has milked the barren 
cows whom the Asuras had become the lords of.
