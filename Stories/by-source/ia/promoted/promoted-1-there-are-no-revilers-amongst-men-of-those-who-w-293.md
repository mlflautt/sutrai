# 1 There Are No Revilers Amongst Men Of Those Who W 293

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2809-2813

1. There are no revilers amongst men of those who were 
our progenitors and combatants for (the recovery of) the * 
cattle ; for the mighty Indra, the achiever of great deeds,, 
liberated for them the numerous herds.
