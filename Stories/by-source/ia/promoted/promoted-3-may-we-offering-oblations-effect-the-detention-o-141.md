# 3 May We Offering Oblations Effect The Detention O 141

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1731-1737

3. May we offering oblations effect the detention of 
thee who art divine (until the completion of the rite), 
and may we (thereby) overcome all animosities. 

• 4. We solicit that Agni, who is being kindled at the 
sacrifice, the purifier, the adorable, whose hair is flame.
