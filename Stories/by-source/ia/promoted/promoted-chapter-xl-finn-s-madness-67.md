One  time  Finn  and  the  Fianna  were  come  to  a  ford  of 
the  Slaine,  and  they  sat  down  for  a  while.  And  as  they 
were  sitting  there  they  saw  on  the  round  rock  up  over 
the  ford  a  young  woman,  having  a  dress  of  silk  and  a 
green  cloak  about  her,  and  a  golden  brooch  in  the  cloak, 
and  the  golden  crown  that  is  the  sign  of  a  queen  on  her 
head.  "Fianna  of  Ireland,"  she  said,  "let  one  of  you 
come  now  and  speak  with  me."
