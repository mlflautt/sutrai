# 8 The Seven Rishis Were The Protectors Of This Our 898

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7262-7267

8. The seven Rishis were the protectors of this our 
(kingdom) when the son of Durgaha was in bonds: per¬ 
forming worship they obtained for (his queen) from the 
favour of Indra and Varuna, Trasadasyu, like Indra 
the slayer of foes, dwelling near the gods.
