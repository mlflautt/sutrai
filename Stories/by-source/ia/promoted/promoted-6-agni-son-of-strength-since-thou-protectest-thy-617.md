# 6 Agni Son Of Strength Since Thou Protectest Thy 617

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5166-5171

6. Agni, son of strength, since thou protectest (thy 
worshippers), far (remove) from us all iniquity; far 
(remove from us) sin; far (from us) all evil thoughts 
for prosperous is he of whom thou, who art radiant by 
night, promotest the well-being.
