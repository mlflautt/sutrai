# 5 It Is The Custom To Give An Infidel Ak Din6 357

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10293-10300

5. It is the custom to give an infidel (ak-din6), 
who is not a foreigner, food, clothing, and medicine, 
when his renunciation (vi^) has come, for keeping 
away matters (his^no) of death and sickness owing 
to hunger and thirst, cold and heat ; but wealth, 
horses, accoutrements, wine, and land are not given 
authorisedly, it is said, unto foreigners and idolaters \
