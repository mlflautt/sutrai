# 5 Your Chariot Travels Widely Round The Heaven Unt 907

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7313-7319

5. Your chariot travels widely round the heaven until 
it places you beyond the firmament: dispensers of sweet 

• (water, the priests) are diluting the Soma juice with milk, 
that the boiled (barley) may be united with the libation 
offered to you.
