# 6 These Fires Cherish Etc Pro Tyc Agnayo Agnishu 2060

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17095-17101

6. These Fires cherish, etc. —Pro tyc agnayo agnishu 
vishwam pushy aw ti varyyam : prapushyanti dhishnya garha- 
patyadishu , they nourish exceedingly, by their intensity, 
wealth dhanam, understood, in the household and other 
fires ; that is, the primary flames of Agni, when manifested 
in the household fires, are the source of riches.
