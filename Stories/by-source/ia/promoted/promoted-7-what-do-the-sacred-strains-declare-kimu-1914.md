# 7 What Do The Sacred Strains Declare Kimu 1914

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15411-15423

7. What do the Sacred Strains Declare.— Kimu 
shwid asmai nivido bhananti ; the Nivids are certain verses 
repeated at some sacrifices to Indra and the Marvts in their 
honour, and are tantamount to an acquittal of the charge 
imputed to Indra , and here anticipated by Aditi of brahmani- 
cide, Vritra being a Brahman: the crime was transferred to 
the waters in the shape of foam: these explanations are 
rather, perhaps, derived from the Pauranik developments of 
.the original legends, imperfectly handed down. 

8 and //.—The rest of the Sukta is by the Rishi in 
praise of Indra.
