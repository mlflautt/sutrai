# Vii Mitra Varuna 57

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7866-7898

VII.  Mitra-Varuna. 

MITRA  and Varuna  we  call  to drink  the  draught  of 

Soma  juice, 
Those  born  endowed  with  holy  strength. 

Rigveda  IX.  61.  4 — 6.  Ascribed  to  Amahiyu. The  metre  is  Gayatri, 

Rigveda  I.  12.  1 — 3.  Ascribed  to  Medhatithi. The  metre  is  Gayatri.
