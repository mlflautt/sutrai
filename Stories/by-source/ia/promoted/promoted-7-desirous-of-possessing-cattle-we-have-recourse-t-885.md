# 7 Desirous Of Possessing Cattle We Have Recourse T 885

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7186-7191

7. Desirous of (possessing) cattle, we have recourse to 
you, Indra and Varuna, for full protection, you who 
are powerful and kind as (kinsmen) ; we have recourse 
to you, adorable heroes, for (your) friendship and affection^ 
(to you who are), like parents, givers of happiness.
