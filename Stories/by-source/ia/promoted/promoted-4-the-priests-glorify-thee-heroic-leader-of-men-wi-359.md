# 4 The Priests Glorify Thee Heroic Leader Of Men Wi 359

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3322-3326

4. The priests glorify thee, heroic leader of men, with, 
praises and with prayers: Indra, the author of many 
delusions, exerts himself for (the acquirement of) strength: 
he from of old is the lord of this (oblation).
