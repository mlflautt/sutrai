# 21 Our King The Nourislier Of All Abides In The Fi 441

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3860-3865

21. Our king, the nourislier of all, abides (in the firma¬ 
ment) nearest to this earth like a benevolent friend: the 
valiant ( Maruts ) precede him (when abroad), and dwell 
in his mansion: great and unequalled is the might of the 
gods.
