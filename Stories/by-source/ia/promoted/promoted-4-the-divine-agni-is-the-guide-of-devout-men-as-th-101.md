# 4 The Divine Agni Is The Guide Of Devout Men As Th 101

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1471-1475

4. The divine Agni is the guide of devout men, as the 
sun is the regulator of the seasons : may he, the observer 
of truth, the slayer of Vritra, the ancient, the omniscient,, 
convey his adorer (safe) over all difficulties.
