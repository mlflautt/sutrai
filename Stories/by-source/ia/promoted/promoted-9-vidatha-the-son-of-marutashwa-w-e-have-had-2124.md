**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))

9. Vidatha, the Son of Marutashwa.—W e have had 
Vidathin before, Sukta xxix. verse 11 , the father of Rijishwan : 
these nfrmes are not found in the principal Puranas.

Rig-veda Translation

dadana , causing to alight upon, giving.
