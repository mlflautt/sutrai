# 15 May We Angirasas Be The Sons Op Heaven 1853

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14798-14814

15. May we, Angirasas, be the Sons op Heaven.— 
Dimsputra angiraso bhavema, or may we, the sons of heaven, 
be Angirasas; or, according to the Scholiast, bhutimantah . 
possessed of superior power : according to a text cited by the 
Commentator, which, however, is not very explicit, the 
Angirasas are the sons of Aditya, angirasam adityaputratwam 
amnayate : the text is tasya yad retail pratharmm udadipyata 
tad asau adityo abhavat ityupalcramya ye angara asans-te- 
angiraso abhavan , that which was his seed was first mani¬ 
fested as Aditya , thence, in succession, those which were the 
cinders became the Angirasas: see vol. i, p. 198, note on 6. 

Wealth-containing Mountain.— Adrim rujema dhani- 
nam, may allude to the rock in which the cows were hidden, 
or adri may be rendered cloud (megha), by the disruption of 
which rain is made to fall.
