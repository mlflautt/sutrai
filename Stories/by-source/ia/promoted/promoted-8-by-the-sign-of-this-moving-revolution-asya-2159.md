# 8 By The Sign Of This Moving Revolution Asya 2159

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18120-18132

8. By the Sign of This Moving Revolution.— Asya 
yatumsya kctuna is the text ; the explanation is karmano- 
dayadilakshanena , characterized by functions such as rising 
and the like. 

He also.Acquires Abundant Reward. — Ya u 

sway am vahate so aram karat , he who, not urged by another, 
entertains of his own mind the hope of reward, he does much, 
or enough: atyartham karoti is one explanation ; another is, 
atyartham kuryat , let him do much, or enough : neither is 
very explicit.
