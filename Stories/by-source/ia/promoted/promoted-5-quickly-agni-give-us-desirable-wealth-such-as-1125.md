# 5 Quickly Agni Give Us Desirable Wealth Such As 1125

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8908-8918

5. Quickly, (Agni, give us) desirable (wealth), such as 
the devout, combining with (pious) praise, (obtain): 
son of strength, (be favourable) to (my) desires ; protect 
us (from calamity) ; be alert for our prosperity; come 
for our success in battles. 

Y. 2. 4. 

The deity and metre as before; the Rishi is Dwita, of the race 
of Atri.
