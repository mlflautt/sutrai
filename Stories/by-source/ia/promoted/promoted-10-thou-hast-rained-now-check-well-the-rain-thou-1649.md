# 10 Thou Hast Rained Now Check Well The Rain Thou 1649

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12580-12584

10. Thou hast rained : now check well the rain: thou 
hast made the deserts capable of being crossed : thou hast 
given birth to plans for (man’s) enjoyment: verily thou 
hast obtained laudation from the people.
