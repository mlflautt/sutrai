# 7 Divine Agni Mighty And Omniscient These Are The 67

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1226-1230

7. Divine (Agni), mighty and omniscient, these (are the 
offerings) which we mortals present to thee in the sacrifice : 
do thou be cognizant of every respectable worshipper, and 
partake, immortal, of all (his offerings) on this occasion.
