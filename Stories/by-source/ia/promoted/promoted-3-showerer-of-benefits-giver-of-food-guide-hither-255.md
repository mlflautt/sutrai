# 3 Showerer Of Benefits Giver Of Food Guide Hither 255

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2522-2532

3. Showerer (of benefits), giver of food, guide hither 
thy vigorous horses, defending (us) against foes, and pro¬ 
tect (thy worshippers): here unharness the bay steeds, 
and give them fodder, and do thou eat daily suitable food.
