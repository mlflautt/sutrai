# 2 Indra Hero Rejoicing With And Accompanied By 335

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3153-3157

2. Indra, hero, rejoicing with and accompanied by 
the host of Maruts , drink the Soma , for thou art the slayer 
of Vritra, the sage ; subdue our enemies, drive away the 
malevolent, make us safe on every side from peril.
