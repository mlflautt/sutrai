# 1 But As A Hawk I Came Forth With Speed That 1942

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15726-15736

1. But as a Hawk I came forth with Speed.— That 
is, according to the Scholiast, until the sage comprehended 
the difference between the body and soul, and learned that 
soul was unconfined, he was subject to repeated births ; but 
in this stage lie acquired divine knowdedge, and burst through 
his bonds with the force and celerity of a hawk from its 
nest ; as the Nitimanjari says, Vamadeva ahyenarupam 
asthaya garb had-yogena nihsritah, Vamadeva , having assumed 
the form of a hawk, came forth from the womb by the power 
of Yoga.
