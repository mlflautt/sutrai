# 7 The Sage Surya Advancing Accompanied By His 1353

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10456-10461

7. The sage Surya, advancing, accompanied by his 
bride, (the dawn), proceeds boldly, intent on combat with 
his enemies : may lie on whom riches are dependent, secure 
our entire felicity, (and grant) a brilliant and everywhere 
protecting mansion.
