# 6 May Emit A Downward Sound Avosriyo Vri 2203

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18557-18563

6. May Emit a Downward Sound. — Avosriyo vri- 
shabhah krandatu dyauh : dyaur — vrishabhah, mean, it is said, 
the shining showerer, that is, Parjanya, or Indra , in that capa¬ 
city, usriyah , in connection with the usra or rays of the sun, 
avakrandatu, avanmukham shabdayatu , may sound with his 
face turned downwards, vrishtyartham, for the sake of rain.
