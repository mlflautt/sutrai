# Ix Indra 351

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17816-17835

IX.  Indra. 

IP  I,  0  Indra,  were,  like  thee,  the  single  ruler over 

wealth 
My  worshipper  should  be  rich  in  kine. 

2  I  should  be  fain,  O  Lord  of  power,  to  strengthen  and 

enrich  the  sage, 

o ' 

Were  I  the  lord  of  herds  of  kine. 

3  Thy  goodness,  Indra,  is  a  cow  yielding  in  plenty  kine 

and  steeds 
To  worshippers  who  press  the  juice.
