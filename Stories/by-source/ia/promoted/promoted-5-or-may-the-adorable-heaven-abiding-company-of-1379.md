# 5 Or May The Adorable Heaven Abiding Company Of 1379

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10636-10640

5. Or may the adorable, heaven-abiding company of 
the Maruts, come to us to take their seats on the sacred 
grass ; or may Brihaspati, Pushan, Varuna, Mitra, 
Aryaman, bestow upon us domestic happiness.
