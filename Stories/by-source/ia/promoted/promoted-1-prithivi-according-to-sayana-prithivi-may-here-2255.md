# 1 Prithivi According To Sayana Prithivi May Here 2255

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 19076-19084

1. Prithivi. —According to Sayana , Prithivi may here 
admit a two-fold meaning, and apply also to the antariksham , 
or firmament, when the subsequent phrases, parvatanam 
khidram bibharshi, will mean, thou sustainest the fracture, or 
opening of the clouds, and mahna jinoshi bhumim , thou de- 
lightest the earth with great or abundant rain.
