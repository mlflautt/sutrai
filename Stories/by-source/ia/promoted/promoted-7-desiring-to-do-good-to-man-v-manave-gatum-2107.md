# 7 Desiring To Do Good To Man V Manave Gatum 2107

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17614-17620

7. Desiring to do good to Man v.--Manave gatum 
ichchhan : gatum is explained sukhatn , but the Scholiast 
identifies Matin with the Rishi of the Sukta , namuchina 
apahritagodhamya mahyam , to me whose wealth of cattle 
has been carried oft by Namuchi , an Asura who has been 
before named.
