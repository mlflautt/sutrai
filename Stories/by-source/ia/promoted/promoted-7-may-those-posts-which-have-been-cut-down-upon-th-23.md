# 7 May Those Posts Which Have Been Cut Down Upon Th 23

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 931-935

7. May those posts which have been cut down upon the 
earth, and which have been fabricated by the priests, those 
which are the accomplishers of the sacrifice, convey our 
acceptable (offering) to the gods.
