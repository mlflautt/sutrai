# 9 I Arya Agni Praise Shatri The Son Of Agnivesha 1254

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9780-9791

9. I, Arya, Agni, praise Shatri, the son of Agnivesha, 
the bestower of thousands, a type for comparison : may 

Rig-veda Translation 

214 

[V. 3 -3 

the collected waters yield him abundance: may wealth, 
and strength, and glory be upon him.
