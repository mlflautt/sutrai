# 3 Agni Is The Possessor Of Great Strength Of Excel 620

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5186-5190

3. Agni is the possessor of great strength, of excellent 
food, of riches, the youngest (of the gods): abounding in 
sustenance, he gives to the mortal who worships him. 
precious (wealth) according to (his devotion).
