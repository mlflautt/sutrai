# 3 And After Him Who Is Quick Going Hastening Eager 875

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7114-7125

3. And after him who is quick-going, hastening, eager 
(to arrive at his goal, men) follow' (as other birds pursue) 
the flight of a swift (bird) striving together to keep up by 
the side of Dadikravan the transporter (of others) as swift 
as a hawk. 

TV. 4.9]
