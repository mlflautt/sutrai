# 3 Worship Therefore The Leaders Of Our Rites Our 1400

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10813-10817

3. Worship, therefore, the leaders (of our rites), our 
guests, (the gods), and the wives (of the deities) : may 
the (divine) discriminator drive to a distance every ad¬ 
versary, (and all our) enemies.
