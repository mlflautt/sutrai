# 8 A Horse With Golden Caparisons Ashwo Na Swe 1851

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14773-14778

8. A Horse with Golden Caparisons. — Ashwo na swe 
dame liemyamn, that is, accordir g to the Scholiast, suvarna - 
nirmita-kakshyavan , having a girth made of gold, applying 
the epithet to the horse, although separated by swe dame , in 
his own house.
