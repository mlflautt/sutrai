# 18 Slayer Of Vritra Do Thou Who Art Long Lived The 208

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2202-2206

18. Slayer of Vritra, do thou, who art long lived, the 
showerer (of benefits), the giver of food, be the lord of our 
true praises, repairing (to the sacrifice), come to us great, 
with great, friendly, and auspicious protections.
