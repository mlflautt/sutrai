# 5 Liberal Donor Of Riches Bestow Upon Us The Most 91

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1399-1404

5. Liberal donor (of riches) bestow upon us the most 
precious of treasures, for therefore is it, Agni, that thou 
art kindled: thou hast arms promptly stretched forth, 
whose (radiant) forms (bestow) wealth on the dwelling of 
thy fortunate adorer.
