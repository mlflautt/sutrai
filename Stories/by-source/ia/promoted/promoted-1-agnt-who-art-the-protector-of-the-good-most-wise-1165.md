# 1 Agnt Who Art The Protector Of The Good Most Wise 1165

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9209-9214

1. Agnt, who art the protector of the good, most wise, 
powerful, and opulent; Tryaruna, the son of Trivrishna, 
has become renowned, Vaishwanara, in that he has 
bestowed upon me a pair of cattle with a waggon, and 
with ten thousands of treasure.
