# 7 I Agni Am By Birth Endowed With Knowledge Of All 136

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1695-1700

7. I, Agni, am by birth endowed with knowledge of all 
that exists: clarified butter is my eye : ambrosia is my 
mouth: I am the living breath of three-fold nature, the 
measure of the firmament, eternal warmth : I am also the 
oblation.
