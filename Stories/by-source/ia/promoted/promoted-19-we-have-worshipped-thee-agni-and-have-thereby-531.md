# 19 We Have Worshipped Thee Agni And Have Thereby 531

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4556-4561

19. We have worshipped thee (Agni), and have thereby 
become the performers of a good work, adoring the full 
and variously delighting Agni, the beautiful lustre of the 
radiant divinity, when the brilliant dawns have arrayed 
(themselves) in light.
