# 1 When The Verb Is Conjugated With The Prefixes 56

**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)
**Lines:** 8820-8839

1. When the verb is conjugated with the prefixes, 
or auxiliary verbs, wd and fa, it has no imperfect tense. 
The formation of the other tenses is the same as in the 

236 THE EWE-SPEAKING PEOPLES. 

indicative mood, the prefixes a and e, denoting the 
future and past tenses, being placed before the wd, or 
ja. This seems to show that the latter, used with 
another verb, form an inseparable compound verb. 

Example— 
wd-du, to eat at once (literally, to set forth—to eat). 
Present Tense. Future Tense. Past Tense. 
me wd-du me awd-du me ewd-du 
wo wd-du wo awd-du wo ewd-du 
&e. &e. &e. 

This form has no negative conjugation.
