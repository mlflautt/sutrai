# 8 No One Distinguishes My Golden Lustre From That  287

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2764-2768

8. No one (distinguishes) my golden lustre from that of 
this Savitri, in which (lustre) he has taken refuge: grati¬ 
fied by pious praise, he cherishes the all-fostering heaven 
and earth as a woman cherishes her offspring.
