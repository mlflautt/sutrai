# 6 I Have Recourse To The Protection Of Parvata Of  976

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7850-7854

6. I have recourse to the protection of Parvata, of the 
Maruts, and of the divine protector, Bhaga: may the. 
lord (Varuna) preserve us from human wretchedness,, 
and may Mitra defend us with a friendly regard.
