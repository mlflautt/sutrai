# 6 A Brahman Coming Quickly Ivate Brahmane 1862

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14942-14954

6. A Brahman coming quickly.— Ivate brahmane ; 
we have no explanation of the latter except parivridhaya , 
to the greatly augmented. 

Shine upon his Dwelling.— Aryo vi duro abhidyaut , is 
also, as Sayana observes, differently rendered by some, he 
the worshipper especially shines over his house, aryo yajamano 
grihan abhi visheshena dyotate.
