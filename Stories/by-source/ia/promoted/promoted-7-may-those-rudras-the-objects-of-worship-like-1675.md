# 7 May Those Rudras The Objects Of Worship Like 1675

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12736-12741

7. May those Rudras, the objects of worship, like 
resplendent fires, protect Evayamarut: they, whose 
ethereal dwelling, extended and wide, has been made 
illustrious (by them), and of whom, exempt from blame, 
the mighty energies (are manifested) in their courses.
