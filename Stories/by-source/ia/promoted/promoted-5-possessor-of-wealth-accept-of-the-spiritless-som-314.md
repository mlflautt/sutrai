# 5 Possessor Of Wealth Accept Of The Spiritless Som 314

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3013-3017

5. Possessor of wealth, accept of the spiritless Somctr 
juice; make me the protector, or rather a monarch of 
men ; make me verily, a holy sage, a drinker of the libation ; 
make me verily the possessor of imperishable wealth.
