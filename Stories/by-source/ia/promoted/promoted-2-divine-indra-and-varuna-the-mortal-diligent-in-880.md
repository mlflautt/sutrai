# 2 Divine Indra And Varuna The Mortal Diligent In 880

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7153-7158

2. Divine Indra and Varuna, the mortal diligent in 
offering (you) sacrificial food, who has through friendship 
.made you his kinsmen, destroys (his own) sins, and his 
enemies in battle ; and by your great favours he becomes 
renowned.
