# 16 Nasatyas My Protectors Inquirers After The 414

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3682-3687

16. Nasatyas, my protectors, inquirers (after the 
wishes) of your kinsman, beautiful is your cognate appel¬ 
lation of Ashwins ; be to us the liberal donors of riches: 
do you, who are irresistible, protect the offerer (of the 
oblation) with unblameable (defences).
