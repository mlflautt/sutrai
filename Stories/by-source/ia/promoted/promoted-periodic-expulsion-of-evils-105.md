the  demons  or  embodied  evils  of  the  year,  who  are  defeated 
and  driven  away  by  the  champions  of  the  people.  The 
month  in  which  these  combats  take  place  (November 
and  the  beginning  of  December)  is  sometimes  named  after 
the  grass-arrows  and  sometimes  after  the  sea-slug.1
