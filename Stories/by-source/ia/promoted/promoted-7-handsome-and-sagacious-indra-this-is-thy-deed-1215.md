# 7 Handsome And Sagacious Indra This Is Thy Deed 1215

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9518-9536

7. Handsome and sagacious Indra, this is thy deed, 
that, slaying Ahi, thou hast here* displayed thy vigour: 
thou hast arrested the devices of Shushna urging the 
combat: thou hast overcome the Dasyus. 

Q. Thou, Indra, (abiding on the further bank), hast 
rendered the fertilizing waters agreeable to Yadu and 
Turvasu : you two, (Indra and Kutsa), have assailed 
the fierce (Shushna), and, (having slain him), thou hast
