# 11 Indra The Slayer Of Vritra Let Loose The Milch 202

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2154-2159

11. Indra, the slayer of Vritra, let loose the milch 
kine, assisted by the Maruts , bom for his aid, and entitled 
to praises and oblations, and the excellent (cows) contri¬ 
buting abundantly to sacred offerings, and yielding the 
butter of libation, milked for him sweet (sacrificial) food.
