# Xiii Soma Pavam Na 177

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12762-12783

XIII.  Soma  Pavam&na. 

EVEN  as  a  King  hath  Soma,  red  and  tawny  Bull, 
been  pressed  :  the  wondrous  one  hath  bellowed  to 
the  kine. 

While  purified  thou  passest  through  the  filtering 
fleece  to  seat  thee  hawk-like  in  the  place  that 
drops  with  oil. 

2  Parjanya  is the sire of  the  leaf-bearing  Bull  :  on 

mountains,  in  earth's  centre  hath  he  made  his  home. 
The  waters  have  flowed  forth,  the  Sisters,  to  the  kine  : 
he  meets  the  pressing-stones  at  the  beloved  rite. 

3  To glory  goest  thou,  a  Sage  with  ordering  skill,  like 

a  groomed  steed  thourushest  forward  to  the  prize. 
Be gracious to  us,  Soma,  driving  off  distress  !  Thou 
goest,  clothed  in  butter,  to  a  robe  of  state.
