# 2 Your Cohort Maruts Is Conspicuous Mighty 1443

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11112-11119

2. Your (cohort), Maruts, is conspicuous, mighty, 
shedding water and augmenting food : yoking your horses 
(to your cars), spreading everywhere, and combined with 
the lightning, the triple-(stationed company) roars aloud > 
and the circumambient waters fall upon the earth. 

246 Rig-vbda Translation [V. 4.10
