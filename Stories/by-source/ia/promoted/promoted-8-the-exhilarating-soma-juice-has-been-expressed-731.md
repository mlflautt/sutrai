# 8 The Exhilarating Soma Juice Has Been Expressed 731

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5987-5992

8. The exhilarating Soma juice has been expressed: 
now may the current flow to thee, and may the expiatory 
power of the illustrious utterer of praise be directed to¬ 
wards us, as the quick rider holds firmly the reins of the 
steed.
