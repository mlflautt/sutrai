# 7 They Seek The Pastures Of The Hoofed Cattle 2061

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17102-17110

7. They seek the Pastures of the Hoofed Cattle.— 
Ye patwabhih shaphanam vraja bhuranta gonam is explained 
by Say ana, ye rashmayali , those rays which, patanaih . by 
fallings, ichchhanti yuthani shaphanam gonam, wish for or 
proceed to the herds of hoofed cattle ; an explanation which 
does not render the sense more obvious : it means, he adds, 
that the spreading flames long for the burnt-offering, ayata 
jwala homaya kanhshanti-ityarthah.
