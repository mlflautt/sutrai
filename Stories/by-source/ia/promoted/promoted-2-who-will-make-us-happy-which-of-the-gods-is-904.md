# 2 Who Will Make Us Happy Which Of The Gods Is 904

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7297-7302

2. Who will make us happy ? which of the gods is 
the most prompt to come to our sacrifice ? which the 
most willing to grant us felicity ? what chariot do they 
say is quick and drawn by rapid steeds ? that which the 
-daughter of Surya selected.
