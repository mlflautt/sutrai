# 11 He Is First Engendered In The Habitations Of Th 504

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4374-4379

11. He is first engendered in the habitations (of the 
sacrifices ; then upon his station, (the altar), the base of 
the vast firmament; without feet, without head, conceal¬ 
ing his extremities, combining with smoke in the nest of 
the rain-cloud.
