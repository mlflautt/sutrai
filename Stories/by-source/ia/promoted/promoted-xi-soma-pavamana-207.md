# Xi Soma Pavamana 207

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13668-13687

XI.  Soma  Pavamana- 

To  him,  praiseworthy,  sacred  tones  have  sounded, 
Steer  of  the  triple  height,  the  life-bestower. 

Dwelling  in  wood,  like  Varuna,  a  river,  lavishing 
treasure,  he  distributes  blessings. 

2  Great  conqueror, warrior-girt,  Lord of  all  heroes, 

flow  on  thy  way  as  he  who  winneth  riches  ; 
With  sharpened  arms, with  swift  bow, never van- 
quished in  battle,  vanquishing  in  fight  the  foemen  ! 

3  Giving  security, Lord of  wide dominion, send us 

both  heaven  and  earth  with  all  their  fulness  ! 
Striving  to win the  Dawns, the light,  the  waters, 
and  cattle,  call  to  us  abundant  booty  !
