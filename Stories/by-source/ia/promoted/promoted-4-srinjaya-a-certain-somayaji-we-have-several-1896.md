# 4 Srinjaya A Certain Somayaji We Have Several 1896

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15255-15259

4. Srinjaya.—-A certain Somayaji : we have several 
princes of the name in the Puranas, but none distinguished by 
this patronymio: the Srinjayas are also a people in the west 
of India: Vishnu P. 193.
