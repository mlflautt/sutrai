# Xvii Soma Pavamaua 340

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17562-17574

XVII.  Soma  pavamaua. 

FOR  Indra  flow  most  rich  in  sweets,  O  Soma,  bring- 
ing him  delight  ! 

2  Bright,  meditating  sacred  song,  these  juices have 

sent  Vayu  forth. 

3  They  were  sent  forth  to  feast  the  Gods,  like  chariots 

speeding  in  the  race.
