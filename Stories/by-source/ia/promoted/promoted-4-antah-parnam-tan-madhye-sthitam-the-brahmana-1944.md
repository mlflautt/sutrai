# 4 Antah Parnam Tan Madhye Sthitam The Brahmana 1944

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15750-15757

4. Antah-parnam, tan-madhye sthitam: the Brahmana 
says, as we have seen, one nail of the left-foot, but the shaft 
was broken by the collision, the fragments of the nail be¬ 
came the quills of the fretful porcupine, those of the arrow, 
water-snakes, flying foxes, and worms. 

Page 132.
