# 5 Now May Maghavan Accept The Pure Nutritious 778

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6310-6315

5. Now may Maghavan accept the pure nutritious 
(sacrificial) food in a white pitcher, mixed with milk and 
curds, offered by the priests ; the upper part of the sweet 
(beverage) to drink for his exhilaration: may the hero 
accept (it) to drink for (his) exhilaration.
