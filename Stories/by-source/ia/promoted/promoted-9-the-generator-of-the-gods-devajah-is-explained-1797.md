# 9 The Generator Of The Gods Devajah Is Explained 1797

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14098-14115

9. The Generator of the Gods.— Devajah is explained 
by Say ana, the generator of radiances or energies, tejasmn 
janayitri: the compound is not devaja god-born, nor was 
Vishwmnitra of divine parentage : Deva-juta , which follows, 
is explained taistejobhir-akrishla , drawn or attracted by those 
energies. 

Arrested the Watery Stream. Astabhnat sindhum 
arnavam : he is said to have stopped the current of the conflu¬ 
ence of the Vipasha and Shatudri rivers. 

Indra with the Kushikas was Pleased.— Apriyayata 
JcushiJcebhir Indra : Sayana explains this, KushiJcagotrot'pan - 
nair-risbibhih saha , with the Rishis of the race of Kushila, 
or it might be rendered, pleased by the Kushikas.
