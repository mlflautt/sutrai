# 2 The One Chariot Of You Two Goes Round 2212

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18645-18651

2. The One Chariot of You Two goes Round.— 
Vam ekali pavir a vavarta: pavi is the circumference of a 
wheel, put by metonymy for the chariot ; as Sayan a explains 
the phrase, yuvayor elco mtho anukramanena parihhmmate . 

Page 260 .
