# 3 Overpowering Is The Might Of These Two The Brigh 1664

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12668-12672

3. Overpowering is the might of these two : the bright 
(lightning) is shining in the hands of Maghavan, as they 
go together in one chariot for the (recovery of the) cows, 
and the destruction of Vritra.
