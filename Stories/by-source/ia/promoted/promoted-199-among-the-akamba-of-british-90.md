# 199 Among The Akamba Of British 90

**Source:** The Golden Bough (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7339-7353

199.  Among  the  Akamba  of  British 
East  Africa  boys  and  girls  at  circum- 
cision have  to  interpret  certain  picto- 
graphs  cut  on  sticks  :  these  pictographs 
are  called  "riddles."  See  C.  W. 
Hobley,  Ethnology  of  A-Kamba  and 
other  East  African  Tribes  (Cambridge, 
1910),  pp.  71  sq.  In  Vedic  times  the 
priests  proposed  enigmas  to  each  other 
at  the  great  sacrifice  of  a  horse.  See 
The  Satapatha  Brahmana,  translated 
by  J.  Eggeling,  Part  v.  (Oxford, 
1900),  pp.  314-316  (Sacred  Books  of 
the  East,vo\.  xliv.);  H.  Oldenberg,  Die 
Religion  des  Veda  (Berlin,  1894),  p.
