# 3 Varuna Has Set Free The Water Of The Downward 1655

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12621-12625

3. Varuna has set free the (water of the) downward 
opening cloud for the (benefit of the) heaven, the earth, 
and the firmament; thence is he monarch of all the world, 
watering the soil as the rain bedews the barley.
