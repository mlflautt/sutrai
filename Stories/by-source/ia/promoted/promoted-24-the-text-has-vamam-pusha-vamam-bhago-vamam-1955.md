# 24 The Text Has Vamam Pusha Vamam Bhago Vamam 1955

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15856-15866

24. The text has vamam pusha vamam bhago vamam 
devah Jcarulati ; the triple repetition of varna , vananiyam , 
dhanam, would seem to separate not only Bhaga , but Karulati 
from Pushan , but if the last be rightly rendered by kritta- 
danta or adantaka , the broken-toothed, or toothless, it applies 
to • Pushan : the attribute has not occurred before, though 
the Scholiast quotes the Veda for it, Pusha prapishtabhago 
adantako hityadi shrulishu ; according to the Puranas, Pushan 
had his teeth knocked out by Virabhadra's followers at 
Dakslia’s sacrifice: Vishnu Pur. 67, note 6.
