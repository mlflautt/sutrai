# 13 Mighty Maruts Of Goodly Aspect Quickly Hear 1308

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10135-10140

13. Mighty Maruts, of goodly aspect, quickly hear 
(the praises) that we who repair to you repeat, offering 
acceptable (oblations): (the Maruts) coming hither, well 
disposed, come down to us, (destroying) with their weapons 
the mortals opposed to them, (overcome) by agitation.
