# 10 The Divine Self Sustaining Heaven Comes To Him 1230

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9628-9633

10. The divine, self-sustaining (heaven) comes to him ; 
the moving (earth), like a loving (wife), resigns herself to 
Indra : when he shares all his vigour with these (his 
people), then, in due succession, men offer reverence to 
the potent Indra.
