# 8 The Pious Man Proceeds To Thee Most Excellent 1354

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10462-10469

8. The (pious man) proceeds to thee, most excellent 
(of the gods, who art indicated) by the sign of this moving 
(revolution) ; who art hymned by the Rishis in whose 
praises thy name (is glorified) : he obtains that blessing 
by his devotion, on whatsoever (his desire) has been fixed ; 
and he also, who of his own accord offers (worship), 
acquires abundant (reward).
