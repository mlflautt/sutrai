# 12 Surya Harms Not The Quarters Of The Horizon Set 182

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2005-2009

12. Surya harms not the quarters (of the horizon), set 
open daily (for his journey), the progeny of Harya^hwa : 
when he has traversed the roads (he is to travel), then he 
lets loose his horses, for such is his office.
