# 2 Hasten To Fulfil Their Offices Yat Suryam 1893

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15231-15239

2. Hasten to Fulfil their Offices.— Yat suryam 
divy-arohanti the Scholiast explains, yada rashmayah suryasya 
•arohanam karayanti, when the rays of light cause the ascent 
of the sun, otherwise the nominative of the verb might be 
thought to be Mitrci , Vctruna, and the rest, Mitra being the 
<deity presiding over the day, Varuna over the night.
