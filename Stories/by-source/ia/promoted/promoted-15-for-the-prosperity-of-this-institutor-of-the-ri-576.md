# 15 For The Prosperity Of This Institutor Of The Ri 576

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4870-4875

15. For the prosperity of this (institutor of the rite), 
the host (of the flames) of the kindled (Agni), the showerer 
(of benefits), the giver of dwellings, has blazed in the hall 
(of sacrifice) ; clothed in radiance, beautiful in semblance, 
and glorified by many, he shines like a man with opulence..
