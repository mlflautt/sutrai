# 8 Invoked Of Many When Thou Hadst Divided The 646

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5384-5389

8. Invoked of many, when thou hadst divided the 
cloud for (the escape of) the waters, Sarama appeared 
before thee ; and thou, the bringer of abundant food, 
hast shewn us favour, dividing the clouds and glorified 
by the Angirasas.
