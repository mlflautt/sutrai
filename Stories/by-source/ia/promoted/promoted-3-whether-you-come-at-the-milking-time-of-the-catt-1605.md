# 3 Whether You Come At The Milking Time Of The Catt 1605

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12237-12242

3. Whether you come at the (milking time) of the cattle, 
at the dawn of day, at noon, when the sun is high, or by 
day or by night, (come) with felicitous protection: the 
drinking of the Soma has not now extended beyond the 
Ashwins.
