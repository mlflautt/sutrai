Kindred  Festivals . . . Pp.  306-411 

§  1.  The  Roman  Saturnalia,  pp.  306-312. — Saturn  and  the  Saturnalia,  306^.; 
license  granted  to  slaves  at  the  Saturnalia,  307  sq.  ;  mock  King  of  the 
Saturnalia,  308  ;  personation  of  Saturn  at  the  Saturnalia  by  a  man  who 
afterwards  suffered  death,  308  sq.  ;  the  martyrdom  and  tomb  of  St. 
Dasius,  309  sq.  ;  the  mock  King  of  the  Saturnalia  probably  the  successor

death  in  the  character  of  the  god,  311  sq.  ;  the  modern  Carnival  perhaps 
the  equivalent  of  the  ancient  Saturnalia,  312.
