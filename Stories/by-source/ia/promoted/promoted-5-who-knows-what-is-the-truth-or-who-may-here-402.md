# 5 Who Knows What Is The Truth Or Who May Here 402

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3606-3610

5. Who knows what is the truth, or who may here 
declare it ? what is the proper path that leads to the 
gods ? their inferior abiding places are beheld, as are 
those which (are situated) in superior mysterious rites.
