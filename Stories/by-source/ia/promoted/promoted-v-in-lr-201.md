V.  In-lr*. 

A  THOUSAND and a hundred steeds are harnessed 

to  thy  golden  car  : 
Yoked  by  devotion,  Indra,  let  the  long-maned bays 

bring  thee  to  drink  the  Soma  juice  ! 

2  Yoked  to  thy  chariot  wrought  of  gold,  may  thy two 

bays  with  peacock tails. 

Convey  thee  hither,  steeds  with their white backs, 
to  quaff  sweet  juice  that  makes us eloquent  !
