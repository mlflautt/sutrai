# 15 Therefore Do I Solicit Wealth Of You Who Are Pr 1454

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11194-11202

15. Therefore do I solicit wealth of you, who are prompt 
to grant protection, whereby we may multiply our descen¬ 
dants, as the sun (spreads wide his rays): be propitiated, 

248 Rig-veda Translation [V. 4. 11 

Maruts, by this my praise, by the efficacy whereof may 
we pass over a hundred winters.
