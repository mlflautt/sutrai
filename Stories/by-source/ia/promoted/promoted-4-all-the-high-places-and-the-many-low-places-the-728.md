# 4 All The High Places And The Many Low Places The 728

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5959-5964

4. All the high places, and the many low places, the 
heaven and the earth, trembled (through fear) of the 
mighty (Indra) at his birth: the strong (Indra) cherishes 
the parents of the moving (sun), and the winds, like men, 
make a noise in their peregrination.
