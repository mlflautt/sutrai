# 4 Puts To Shame The Adversary Of The Pious Man 1977

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16065-16070

4. Puts to Shame the Adversary of the pious Man. 
- Vidatha nichikyat tiro aratim paryyapa ayoh , is explained 
jnatavyani janam , aramanam, arim va, tiraskaroti stotur- 
manushyasya , knowing things cognizable, who disgraces the 
opponent, or the foe of the man, the praiser.
