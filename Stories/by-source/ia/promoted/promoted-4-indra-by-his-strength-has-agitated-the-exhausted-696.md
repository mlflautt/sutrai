# 4 Indra By His Strength Has Agitated The Exhausted 696

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5721-5725

4. Indra, by his strength, has agitated the exhausted 
firmament, as wind, by its violent (gusts, agitates) the 
water: exulting in his strength, he has divided the solid 
(clouds), and has shattered the peaks of the mountains.
