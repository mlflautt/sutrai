# 13 Let Your Waves Rivers So Flow That The Pin Of T 242

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2430-2434

13. Let your waves (rivers) so flow that the pin of the 
yoke may be above (their) waters: leave the traces full, 
and may (the two streams), exempt from misfortune or 
defect, and uncensured, exhibit no (present) increase.
