# 1 Agni Ininistrant Of The Sacrifice Do Thou Who Ar 577

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4880-4884

1. Agni, ininistrant of the sacrifice, do thou who art 
entitled to worship, be above us in this offering to the 
gods ; for thou prevailest over all that is desirable ; thou 
inspirest the praise of the worshipper.
