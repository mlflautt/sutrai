# Ii Agni 199

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13438-13454

II.  Agni. 

HARNESS,  O  Agni,  O  thou  God,  thy  steeds  which  are 

most  excellent ! 
The  fteet  ones  bring  thee  rapidly. 

2  Come hither, bring the Gods to us to  taste  the 

sacrificial  feast, 
To  drink  the  draught  of  Soma  juice  ! 

3  O Agni  of  the Bharatas, flame splendid  with  un- 

fading might : 
Shine  forth  and  gleam,  eternal  one  !
