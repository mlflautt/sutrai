# 3 Do Thou Who Art Most Rapid Direct Thy Flames 551

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4686-4697

3. Do thou, who art most rapid, direct thy (flames) 
against opposing (rays), and, unresisted, become the 
protector of this thy people against the calumniator who 
is remote or who is nigh: let no malevolent (foe) prevail 
.against us (who are) thy worshippers. 

94
