# 5 By Your Marvellous Deeds You Have Made Your 833

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6837-6848

5. By your (marvellous) deeds you have made your 
parents young ; by your deeds you have made the ladle 
(fit) for the drinking of the gods: by your deeds you have 
made the two horses, the bearers of Indra, swifter than 
(an arrow from) a bow, Ribhus, who are rich in (sacrificial) 
food. 

G. Distributors of food, Ribhus, showerers (of bene¬ 
fits), exhilarated (by the Soma draught), fabricate wealth, 
comprising all posterity for him who pours out for your 
exultation, the acrid libation at the decline of day.
