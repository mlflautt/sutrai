# 2 Knowing Their Greatness Offer Worship To The Vas 399

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3588-3593

2. Knowing their greatness, offer worship to the vast 
heaven and to the earth: my desire, wishing (for felicity) 
proceeds (to them), in whose praise the gods, desirous of 
adoration, delight together with their worshippers at the 
sacrifice.
