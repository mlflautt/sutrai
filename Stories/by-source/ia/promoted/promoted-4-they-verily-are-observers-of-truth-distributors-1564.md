# 4 They Verily Are Observers Of Truth Distributors  1564

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11926-11930

4. They verily are observers of truth, distributors of 
water, protectors of holy rites amongst men : guides 
in the right way, liberal donors, and benefactors even of 
the sinner (who worships them).
