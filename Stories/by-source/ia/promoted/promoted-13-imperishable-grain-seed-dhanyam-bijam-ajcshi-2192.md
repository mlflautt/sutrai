# 13 Imperishable Grain Seed Dhanyam Bijam Ajcshi 2192

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18444-18448

13. Imperishable Grain-Seed. — Dhanyam bijam aJcshi- 
tam : unperishing grain-seed is the literal interpretation : 
perhaps a copulative is required, grain and seed, only, in fact, 
the grain is the seed.
