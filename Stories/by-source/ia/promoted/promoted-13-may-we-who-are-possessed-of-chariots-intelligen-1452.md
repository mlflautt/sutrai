# 13 May We Who Are Possessed Of Chariots Intelligen 1452

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11183-11188

13. May we, who are possessed of chariots, intelligent 
Maruts, become (proprietors) of wealth, comprising food 
bestowed by you : of wealth that vanishes not, as Tishya 
(declines not) from the sky : therefore, Maruts, gratify 
us with infinite (riches).
