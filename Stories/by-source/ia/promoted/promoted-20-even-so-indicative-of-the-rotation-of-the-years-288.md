# 20 Even So Indicative Of The Rotation Of The Years 288

**Source:** The Text Is Fly (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9087-9096

20. Even so, indicative of the rotation of the years, 
months, days, a^id hours, is the revolution of the 
celestial sphere and stars which are settled (pasikh- 
tak), and of the sun and moon which are adjusted 
(ni viri^ak), a well-horsed ^ progress and conspicuous 
revolution. 21. This, too, is an indication that the 
movements of every appearance (Aiharih) are owing 
to an exhibitor, by whom the movement of that 
appearance is exhibited.
