# 9 Protectress And Progenitrix Makah Pitur 1813

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14318-14326

9. Protectress and Progenitrix.- Makah pitur 
janitur , jami tan tutli is explained mahatyah palayitryah 
janayitryastava , of thee, that is. of the heaven ; jami is put 
for jamitwam , or bhaginitwam, sisterhood, the condition of a 
relative or sister : this will not allow of pitri gnd janitri 
being rendered father and progenitor, else we hove had 
Dyus, heaven, which is here addressed, characterized by 
these attributes : see vol. II. p. 76, v. 33.
