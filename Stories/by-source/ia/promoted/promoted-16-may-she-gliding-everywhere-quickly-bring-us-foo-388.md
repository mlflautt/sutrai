# 16 May She Gliding Everywhere Quickly Bring Us Foo 388

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3522-3526

16. May she, gliding everywhere, quickly bring us food 
(suited) to the five races of men: may she, the daughter 
of the sun whom the grey-haired Jamadagnis gave to me, 
(be) the bestower of new life.
