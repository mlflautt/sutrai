# Xv Agni 230

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14345-14361

XV.  Agni. 

IMMORTAL, Hotar-priest,  and God, with wondrous 

power  he  leads  the  way, 
Urging  the  congregations  on. 

2  Strong,  he  is  set  on  deeds of  strength. He is led 

forth  in  holy  rites, 
Sage  who  completes  the  sacrifice. 

3  Excellent,  he  was  made  by  thought. The  germ  of 

beings  have  I  gained. 
Yea,  and  the  Sire  of  active  power.
