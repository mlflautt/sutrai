# 8 They Spread Around Of Similar Form Coming From 954

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7670-7674

8. They spread around of similar form, (coming) from 
the east, (coming) from the same region alike renowned: 
the divine Dawns, arousing the assembly of the sacrifice, 
are glorified like the (rays) creative of the waters.
