# 3 The Divine Savitri Fills With Radiance The Celes 961

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7742-7746

3. The divine (Savitri) fills (with radiance) the celestial 
and terrestrial regions, and boasts of his own function: 
Savitri puts forth his arms for (the work of) production, 
regulating the world, and animating it with light.
