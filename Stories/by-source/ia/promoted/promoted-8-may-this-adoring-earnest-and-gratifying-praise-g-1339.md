# 8 May This Adoring Earnest And Gratifying Praise G 1339

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10341-10346

8. May this adoring, earnest, and gratifying praise go 
like a messenger to summon the Ashwins hither : come, 
Ashwins, givers of happiness, riding in one chariot, come 
down to the deposited (Soma), as the bolt (is essential) 
t-o the axle of the waggon.
