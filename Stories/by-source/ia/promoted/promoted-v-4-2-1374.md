# V 4 2 1374

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10600-10605

V. 4. 2. 

The deities of the first six stanzas are the Vishwadevas, of the 
last two the wives of the gods ; the Rishi is Pratikshatra ; the metre 
of the second and eighth stanzas is Trifihtubh, of the rest Jagati.
