# 7 Whose Parents Need Not Urge Na Imtara 1876

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15059-15066

7. Whose Parents need not urge. — Na imtara 
pitara nu chid ishtau, nor mother and father, i.e. y heaven and 
earth, are quickly powerful in urging him : yasya preshane 
kshipram eva na prabhavatah , is Sayana's interpretation. 

Descendants of Manu. — Manushishu vikshu , may 
mean only human beings.
