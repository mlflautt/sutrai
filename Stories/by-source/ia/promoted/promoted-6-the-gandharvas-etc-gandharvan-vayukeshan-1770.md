# 6 The Gandharvas Etc Gandharvan Vayukeshan 1770

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13794-13801

6. The Gandharvas, etc .—Gandharvan vayukeshan ; 
the Qandharvas , according to the Scholiast, are the guardians 
of t he Soma , Somarakshakan : he quotes t he Taittiriyakas 
for the specification of similar beings, although the particular 
name is not given : Swambhrajan, bambhare . hasta, suhasta, 
krishanah , ete vah soma kray anas, tan rakshadhwam ma vo 
dabhan.
