# 2 They Are Detaining By Sacrifice The True Recipie 1113

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8822-8827

2. They are detaining by sacrifice the true recipient 
(of offerings) in the most eminent place of the ceremony, 
who brings together the leaders (of the rite), the unborn 
with the born, (or gods and men), as seated at the solemnity 
which is the stay of heaven.
