# 8 This Savitri Asya Savitur Nakir Me Savitri 1771

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13805-13810

8. This Savitri.— Asya savitur nakir me: Savitri, 
according to the Scholiast, here means Indra, sarvasya jagato 
antaryamitaya prerayitur Indrasya, of Indra the impeller, 
through his being the internal pervader of the whole world : 
this verse is little less unintelligible than the preceding.
