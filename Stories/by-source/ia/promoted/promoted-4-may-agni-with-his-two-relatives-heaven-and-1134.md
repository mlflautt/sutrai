# 4 May Agni With His Two Relatives Heaven And 1134

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8977-8981

4. May (Agni) with his two relatives, (heaven and 
earth), hear this faultless (praise), acceptable as milk: 
he who, like the mixed oblation, is filled with food, and, 
unsubdued, is ever the subduer of his foes.
