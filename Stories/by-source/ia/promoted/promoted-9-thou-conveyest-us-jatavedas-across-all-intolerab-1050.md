# 9 Thou Conveyest Us Jatavedas Across All Intolerab 1050

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8344-8348

9. Thou conveyest us, Jatavedas, across all intolerable 
evils, as (people are carried) over a river bv a boat: Agni. 
who art glorified by us with reverence, such as (that shewn) 
by Atri, know thyself the protector of our persons.
