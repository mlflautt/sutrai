# 3 From The Body Of Vritra It Is Said Sprang The Mo 2117

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17708-17712

3. From the body of Vritra, it is said, sprang the more 
powerful asura , Shushna , that is, allegorically, the exhaustion 
of the clouds w ? as followed by a drought, which Indra , or the 
atmosphere, had then to remedy.
