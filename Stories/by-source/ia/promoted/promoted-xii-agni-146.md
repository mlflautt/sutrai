# Xii Agni 146

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11421-11437

XII.  Agni. 

MAY  Vatsa draw thy mind away even from thy 

loftiest  dwelling-place ! 
Agni,  I  yearn  for  thee  with  song. 

2  Thou  art  alike in many a  place  : through all the 

regions  thou  art  Lord. 
In  fray  and  fight  we  call  on  thee. 

3  When  we  are seeking  spoil  we  call Agni to  help  us 

in  the  strife, 
Giver  of  wondrous  gifts  in  war.
