# 17 Borne Across When Denied Inauguration 1951

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15822-15829

17. Borne across when denied Inauguration. — 
Aparayat , he enabled to cross : according to the Scholiast, 
he made them worthy to be inaugurated, or crowned, which 
they were not at first, as the text implies, by asnatarau , not 
bathers ; alluding to their exclusion from the succession in 
favour of the youngest son Puru by their father Yayati: 
see Vishnu Purana , p. 414.
