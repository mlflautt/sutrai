**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)

5. Indefinite Pronouns. 

These are— 
ame-deke, somebody, anybody. 
nu-deke, something, anything. 

Used with the negative form of the verb they mean,

“nobody” and “nothing”. De is the numeral, one, and 
ke means “to separate”. The adverbial forms are— 
afi-deke, somewhere, anywhere. 
gbea-deke, some time, any time.
