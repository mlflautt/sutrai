# 2 Do Thou Indra Who Art Radiant In Heaven Spread 1348

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10416-10422

2. Do thou, (Indra), who art radiant in heaven, spread 
through the regions, for the good (of mankind), those the 
beautiful (waters) of the unyielding cloud : doer of good 
deeds, thou art the preserver (of men), not (destined) for 
their detriment: thou art superior to all delusions ; thy 
name abides in the world of truth.
