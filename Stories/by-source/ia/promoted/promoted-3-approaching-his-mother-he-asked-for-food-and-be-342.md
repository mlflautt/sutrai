# 3 Approaching His Mother He Asked For Food And Be 342

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3198-3202

3. Approaching his mother he asked for food, and be¬ 
held the acrid Soma upon her bosom: eager he proceeds,, 
dislodging the adversaries (of the gods) ; and, putting 
forth manifold (energy), he performed great (deeds).
