# 4 How Does He Who Glorifies Indra And Is Diligent  739

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6037-6042

4. How does he who glorifies Indra, and is diligent in 
his worship, although encountering opposition, obtain 
from him wealth ? may the divine Indra be the appreciator 
of my praises, when accepting the sacrificial food he is 
propitiated towards me.
