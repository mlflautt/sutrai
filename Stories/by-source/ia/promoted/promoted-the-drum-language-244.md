^ Adwo ; a title of respect, given to chiefs, by women to their husbands,

* Beginning of the third phonograph cylinder and commencement of

* Asiama Toku Asare was the first Queen Mother of the Beretuo clan; 
m3rthology has it that she descended from the sky on a chain. Her blackened 
stool is preserved and has the centre place of honour at the Adae ceremonies. 
She was the head of the Beretuo clan before they migrated to Mampon. She 
ruled over the dan at the village of Ahensan in Adanse.
