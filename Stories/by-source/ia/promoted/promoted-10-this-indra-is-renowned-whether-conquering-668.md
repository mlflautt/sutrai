# 10 This Indra Is Renowned Whether Conquering 668

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5529-5533

10. This (Indra), is renowned, whether conquering 
or slaying (his foes), or whether in conflict he recovers 
the cattle: when Indra truly entertains anger, all that 
is stationary or moveable is in fear of him.
