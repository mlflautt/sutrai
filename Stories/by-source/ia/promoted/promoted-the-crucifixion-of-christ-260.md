# The Crucifixion Of Christ 260

**Source:** The Golden Bough (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 25631-25644

THE  CRUCIFIXION  OF  CHRIST 

423 

Jesus  of  Nazareth  to  the  level  of  a  multitude  of  other  victims  of  a 
barbarous  superstition,  and  will  see  in  him  no  more  than  a  moral 
teacher,  whom  the  fortunate  accident  of  his  execution  invested  with 
the  crown,  not  merely  of  a  martyr,  but  of  a  god.  The  divergence 
between  these  views  is  wide  and  deep.  Which  of  them  is  the  truer 
and  will  in  the  end  prevail  ?  Time  will  decide  the  question  of 
prevalence,  if  not  of  truth.  Yet  we  would  fain  believe  that  in  this 
and  in  all  things  the  old  maxim  will  hold  good — Magna  est  Veritas 
et  praevalebit.
