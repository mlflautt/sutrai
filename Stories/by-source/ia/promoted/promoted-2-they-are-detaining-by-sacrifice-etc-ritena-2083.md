# 2 They Are Detaining By Sacrifice Etc Ritena 2083

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17324-17329

2. They are detaining by Sacrifice, etc. — Ritena 
ritam dharunam dharayantah : they, the Yajamanas . hold or 
detain, ritam dharunam , yajnasya dharakam satyam, the true* 
holder of sacrifice, that is. Agni , by the ceremony, parame 
vyomani, utkrishte sthane , in the best place, that is. the altar.
