# 1 As To The Sixty First Question And Reply That 351

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10160-10165

1. As to the sixty-first question and reply, that 
which you ask is thus : How stand the shares in the 
inheritance (mtrdto) of property among those of the 
good religion, and how is it necessary for them to 
stand therein ?
