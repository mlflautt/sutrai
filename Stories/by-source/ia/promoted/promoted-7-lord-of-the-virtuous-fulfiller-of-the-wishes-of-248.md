# 7 Lord Of The Virtuous Fulfiller Of The Wishes Of  248

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2475-2479

7. Lord of the virtuous, fulfiller of the wishes of men, 
he gave to the gods the wealth (that had been won) in 
fierce battle, and therefore far-seeing sages glorify those 
his exploits with praises in the dwelling of the worshipper.
