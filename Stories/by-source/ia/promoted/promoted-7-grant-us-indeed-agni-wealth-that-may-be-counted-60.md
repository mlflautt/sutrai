# 7 Grant Us Indeed Agni Wealth That May Be Counted 60

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1177-1183

7. Grant us indeed, Agni, wealth (that may be counted) 
by thousands, and comprising offspring, nourishment, 
brilliant treasure, and vigour, and be infinite and in¬ 
exhaustible.
