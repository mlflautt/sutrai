# I The Sage Asked The Spirit Of Wisdom 2 Thus 200

**Source:** The Text Is Fly (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7201-7207

I. The sage asked the spirit of wisdom (2) thus : 
‘ What are the vices of priests ? 3. What are the 

vices of warriors ? 4. What are the vices of hus- 

bandmen ? 5. And what are the vices of artizans^?’
