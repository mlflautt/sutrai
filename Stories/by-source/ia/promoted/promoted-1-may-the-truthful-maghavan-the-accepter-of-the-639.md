# 1 May The Truthful Maghavan The Accepter Of The 639

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5331-5335

1. May the truthful Maghavan, the accepter of the 
spiritless Soma . come to us; may his horses hasten to 
us: to him we offer this sacrifice, the very potent beve¬ 
rage: may he grant the fulfilment of our desires.
