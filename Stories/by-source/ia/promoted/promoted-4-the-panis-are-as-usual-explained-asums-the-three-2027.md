# 4 The Panis Are As Usual Explained Asums The Three 2027

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16698-16705

4. The Panis are as usual explained Asums : the three 
forms or states in which the ghi was deposited in the cow were 
milk, curds, and butter, of which Indra engendered jajana, 
milk, Surya butter, and the gods (devasah) fabricated ( tatak - 
shuh) curds from the shining, venat, that is, Agni : Mahidhara 
renders devasah dwijatayah , the twice-born, but does not difier 
materially in the rest.
