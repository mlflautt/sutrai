# 8 Agni Thoroughly Comprehending The Light That Is  137

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1701-1706

8. Agni, thoroughly comprehending the light that is to- 
be understood by the heart, has purified himself (bv the 
three) purifying (forms) ; he has made himself most 
excellent treasure by (these) self-manifestations, and has 
thence contemplated heaven and earth.
