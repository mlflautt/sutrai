# 12 May The Divine Twashtri The Able Artificer The 409

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3649-3655

12. May the divine Twashtri, the able artificer, the 
dextrous-handed, the possessor of wealth, the observer 
of truth, bestow upon us those tilings (which are necessary) 
for our preservation: Rirhus, associated with Pushan, 
make us joyful, as they (the priests), with uplifted stones, 
prepare the sacred libation.
