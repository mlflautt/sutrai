XIII.  Soma  Pavamana. 

FOR  thee  this  Soma  is  effused,  O  Indra  :  drink  of 
this  juice  ;  for  thee  the  stream  is  flowing — 

Soma,  which  thou  thyself  hast  made  and  chosen,  even 
Indu  for  thy  special  drink  to  cheer  thee  ! 

Rigveda  I.  6.  1—3. The  Rishi  is  Kanva. The  metre  is  Gsyatri. 

1  They  who  stand  round  him  :  paritasthushah  : lokatrayavarlinah 
prdninah,  f  the  living  beings  of  the  three  worlds,' is  Sayana's  explana- 
tion. Probably  the  Maruta,  India's  constant  companions,  are  intended. 
The  bright,  the  ruddy  steed  :  the  rising  Sirnv

hero  :  the  Sun. As  M.  Bergaigne  remarks  . — The  Sun  rs  sometimes  a 
wheel, sometimes  a  chariot, sometimes  a  horse  drawing  the  chariotr 
sometimes  a  hero  mounted  CHI  the  chariot  and  directing  the  horses.' — 
La  Religion  Vedique,  III.  p.  324. 

3  Ihou :  O  Sun. O  men. !  is  perhaps  merely  an  exclamation  of 
admiration. If  marydh,  men, betaken  to  mean  the  Maruts,  the  words,

to  these  Gods  regarded  as  one  host  or  company  and  born  at  one  birth.
