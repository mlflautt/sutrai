# 11 Lances Gleam Maruts Upon Your Shoulders 1450

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11172-11177

11. Lances (gleam), Maruts, upon your shoulders, 
anklets on your feet, golden cuirasses on your breasts, 
and pure (waters shine) in your chariots : lightnings blaz¬ 
ing with fire glow in your hands, and golden tiaras are 
towering on your heads.
