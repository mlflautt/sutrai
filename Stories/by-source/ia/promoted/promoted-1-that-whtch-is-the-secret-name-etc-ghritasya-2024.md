# 1 That Whtch Is The Secret Name Etc Ghritasya 2024

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16631-16638

1. That whtch is the Secret Name, etc.— Ghritasya 
nama guhyam yad astijihva devanam amritasya nabhih ; that 
is, the material of sacrifice commonly called ghi is, in the 
mantras of the Veda, designated the tongue of the gods, being 
used metonymically for Agni ; also the navel or binding, that 
is, the means of securing immortality for him who offers it in 
oblations.
