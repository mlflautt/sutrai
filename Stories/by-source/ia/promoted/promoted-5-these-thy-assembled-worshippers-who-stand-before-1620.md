# 5 These Thy Assembled Worshippers Who Stand Before 1620

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12371-12375

5. These, thy assembled (worshippers), who stand before 
thee to distribute wealth, entertain towards us kindly 
intentions, offering unlimited riches : bright-born goddess, 
(who art) sincerely praised for (the gift of) horses.
