# 1 I Send My Prayer As A Messenger To The Ribhus 808

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6667-6673

1. I send my prayer as a messenger to the Ribhus 

I solicit (of them) the milch cow, the yielder of the white 
milk, for the dilution (of the Soma libation) ; for they, 
as swift as the wind, the doers of good works, were borne 
quickly across the firmament by rapid steeds.
