# V 1 4 1042

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8292-8300

V. 1 . 4. 

The deity, Riahi, and metre as before. 

1 . Royal Agni, I glorify at sacrifices thee who art the 
lord of vast riches; may we who are in want of food 
obtain food through thee, and (through thee) may we 
overcome hosts of (hostile) men.
