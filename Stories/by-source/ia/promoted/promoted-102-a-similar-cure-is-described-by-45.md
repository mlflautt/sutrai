# 102 A Similar Cure Is Described By 45

**Source:** The Golden Bough (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3271-3316

102.  A  similar  cure  is  described  by 
Pliny  {Nat.  Hist.  xxii.  149) ;  you  are 
to  touch  the  warts  with  chick-peas  on 
the  first  day  of  the  moon,  wrap  the 
peas  in  a  cloth,  and  throw  them  away 
behind  you.  But  Pliny  does  not  say 
that  the  warts  will  be  transferred  to 
the  person  who  picks  up  the  peas. 
On  this  subject  see  farther  J.  Hardy, 
"Wart  and  Wen  Cures,"  Folk-lore 
Record,  i.  (1878)  pp.  216-228.
