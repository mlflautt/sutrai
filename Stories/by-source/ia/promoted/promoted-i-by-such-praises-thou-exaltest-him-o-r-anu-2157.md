# I By Such Praises Thou Exaltest Him O R Anu 2157

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18104-18109

I. By such Praises Thou Exaltest Him.—O r anu 
yasu vardhase may be applied to Indra , thou prosperest, 
Indra , by such praises : this is Mahidhara's explanation, 
which differs in some other respects from that of Sayana , 
Yajur- Veda, 7. 12.
