# 1 He Who Has Been Placed Immortal Among The Mortal 514

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4438-4455

1. He who has been placed immortal among the mortals, 
the observer of truth, a deity triumphant among gods, 
the invoker of the gods, the most diligent sacrificer, Agni : 
<he has been placed (upon the altar) to lighten (the ceremony) 
by his (lustre), and for the elevation of the worshipper, 
•through oblations (in heaven). 

Rig-veda Translation 

[IV. 1. 2* 

,88 

.2. Agni, son of strength, generated to-day at this our 
rite, as intermediate between both (gods and men) thou 
proceedest, the invoker (of the gods), harnessing, graceful 
Agni, thy robust, vigorous, and resplendent steeds.
