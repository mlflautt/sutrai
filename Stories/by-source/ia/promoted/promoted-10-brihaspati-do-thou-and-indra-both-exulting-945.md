# 10 Brihaspati Do Thou And Indra Both Exulting 945

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7611-7615

10. Brihaspati, do thou and Indra, both exulting 
and showering riche3, drink the Soma at this sacrifice: 
may the all-pervading drops enter you: bestow upon us 
riches comprising all male descendants.
