# Vii Mitra Varuna 90

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9045-9060

VII.  Mitra-Varuna. 
THIS  Sonia  hath  been  pressed  for  you,  Law-strength- 

eners,  Mitra,  Varuna  ! 
List,  list  ye  here  to  this  my  call  ! 

2  Both  Kings  who never  injure aught  have come to 

their  sublimest  home, 
The  thousand- pillared,  firmly  based. 

3  Worshipped  with  fat  libation,  Lords  of  gifts,  Adityas, 

sovran  Kings, 
They  wait  on  him  whose  life  is  true.
