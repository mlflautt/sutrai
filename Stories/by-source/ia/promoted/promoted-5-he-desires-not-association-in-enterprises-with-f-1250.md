# 5 He Desires Not Association In Enterprises With F 1250

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9755-9761

5. He desires not (association in) enterprises with five 
or with ten ; he associates not with the man who does not 
present libations ; and cherishes not (his dependants) : 
nay, the terrifier (of foes) punishes him or slays him, but 
lie places the devout man in a pasturage stocked with 
cattle.
