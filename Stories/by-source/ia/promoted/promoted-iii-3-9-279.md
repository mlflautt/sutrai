# Iii 3 9 279

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2707-2713

III. 3. 9. 

The deity is Indra, associated in the last six verses with Varuna ; 
the Riahi is Prajapati, son of Vishwamitra, or the son of Vaoh of the 
race of Vishwamitra, either or both: or it may be Vishwamitra 
alone : the metre is Trishtubh.
