# 8 Three Are The Excellent Uninjurable Bright Regio 450

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3926-3934

8. Three are the excellent uninjurable bright regions, 
three scions of the powerful (year) are shining; practisers 
of truth, quick moving, of surpassing radiance: may the 
deities be present thrice daily at the sacrifice. 

ill. 5. 4. 

The deities, Rishi, and metre as before.
