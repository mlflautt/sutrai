# 1 Come Indra To Our Sacrifice Drink Lord Of The 1285

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9984-9992

1. Come, Indra, (to our sacrifice) ; drink, lord of the 
Soma , of the juice expressed by the stones : showerer (of 
benefits), utter destroyer of Vritra, (come) with the 
showering (Maruts). 

Fifth Mandala 

219
