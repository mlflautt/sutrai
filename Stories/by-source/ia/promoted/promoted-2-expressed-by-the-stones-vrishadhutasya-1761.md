# 2 Expressed By The Stones Vrishadhutasya 1761

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13714-13724

2. Expressed by the Stones.- - Vrishadhutasya 
vrishnah , shew 1 he usual predilection for, wish, showering, 
raining : the literal acceptation of these terms would be of 
rain-shaken rainer. but the second is explained, the Soma , 
that bestows stvarga , and such other benefits, and the first 
gravabhir-abhishutam , juice expressed by the stones used for 
the purpose ; the same idea of their being showerers, or 
bestowers of blessings, suggesting this appellation of them.
