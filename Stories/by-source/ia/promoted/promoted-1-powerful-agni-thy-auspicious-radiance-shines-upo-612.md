# 1 Powerful Agni Thy Auspicious Radiance Shines Upo 612

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5136-5141

1. Powerful Agni, thy auspicious radiance shines upon 
the proximity of the sun (by day) ; thy bright and visible, 
(lustre) is conspicuous by night, as the bland and pleasing 
food (of sacrifice, the oblation) becomes manifest in thy 
form.
