# 20 He Has Filled The Two Vast Receptacles Heaven A 440

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3854-3859

20. He has filled the two vast receptacles (heaven and 
earth) united (with creatures): they are both penetrated 
by his lustre: the hero spoiling the treasures (of the foe) 
is renowned: great and unequalled is the might of the 
gods.
