# X The Waters 352

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17836-17852

X.  The  Waters. 

YEA,  Waters,  ye  bring  health  and  bliss :  so  help ye 

us  to  energy 
That  we  mRy  look  on  great  delight  ! 

2  Give  us  a  portion  of  the  dew, the most auspicious 

that  ye  have, 
Like  mothers  in  their  longing  love  ! 

3  For  you  we  gladly  go  to him to whose abode ye 

speed  us  on, 
And,  Waters,  give  us  procreant  strength  !
