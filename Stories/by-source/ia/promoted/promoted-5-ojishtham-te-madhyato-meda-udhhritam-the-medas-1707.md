# 5 Ojishtham Te Madhyato Meda Udhhritam The Medas 1707

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13061-13067

5. Ojishtham te madhyato meda udhhritam; the medas 
or vapa is described as the fatty matter that lubricates the 
abdomen like coagulated butter : it is evidently the same that 
is described ir the Old Testament as “the fat that covereth 
the inwards, and all the fat that is upon the inwards . 99 Levit. 
iv: 9, etc.
