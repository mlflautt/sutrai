# 8 Whatever Rite Has Been Addressed To You Maruts 1463

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11252-11257

8. Whatever (rite has been addressed to you), Maruts, 
of old ; whatever is recent; whatever (hymn) is recited, 
Vasus, whatever prayer is repeated, do you be cognizant 
of all: may the cars of the quick-moving (Maruts) arrive 
for our good.
