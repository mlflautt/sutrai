# 1 The Tenth Question Is That Which You Ask Thus 85

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2737-2741

1. The tenth question is that which you ask thus; 
Does the growth which increases become as com- 
mendable in the fourth night® as the original good 
work® in his possession, or does it become otherwise?
