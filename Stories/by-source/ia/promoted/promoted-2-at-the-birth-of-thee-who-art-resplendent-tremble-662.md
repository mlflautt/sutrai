# 2 At The Birth Of Thee Who Art Resplendent Tremble 662

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5487-5492

2. At the birth of thee who art resplendent, trembled 
the heaven (and) trembled the earth through fear of thy 
wrath; the mighty clouds were confined ; they destroyed 
(the distress of drought), spreading the waters over the 
dry places.
