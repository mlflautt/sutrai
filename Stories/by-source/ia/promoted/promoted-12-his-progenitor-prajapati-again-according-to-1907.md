# 12 His Progenitor Prajapati Again According To 1907

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15349-15361

12. His Progenitor.— Prajapati , again, according to 
the Scholiast. 

As the Wind is driven by Thundering Clouds.— 
The simile is, however, applied to Indra by the Scholiast in 

346 

Rig-veda Translation 

another sense ; as the wind is impelled by thunder-clouds,, 
so Indra is influenced by the hymns of the worshippers.
