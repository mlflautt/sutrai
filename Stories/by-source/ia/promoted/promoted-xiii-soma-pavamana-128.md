# Xiii Soma Pavamana 128

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10652-10669

XIII.  Soma  Pavamana. 

HIM here,  the  offspring  of  the sea, the ten swift 

fingers beautify  : 
With  the  Adityas  is he seen. 

2  With  Indra  and  with  Vayu  he,  effused,  flows  onward 

with  the  beams 
Of  Surya  to  the  cleansing  sieve. 

3  Flow  rich  in  sweets  and  lovely  for  our  Bhaga, Vayu, 

Pushan,  fair 

For  Mitra  and  for  Varuna  !
