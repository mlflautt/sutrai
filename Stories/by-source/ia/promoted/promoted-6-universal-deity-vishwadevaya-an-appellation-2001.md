# 6 Universal Deity Vishwadevaya An Appellation 2001

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16362-16368

6. Universal Deity. — Vishwadevaya , an appellation 
of Brihaspati, because, as the deity presiding over mantras 
he is the same with every deity ; or deva may here, it is said, 
mean praise, he who has the praise of all. 

Page 162.
