# 6 He Experiences Thy Good Favour Youngest Of The 554

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4708-4713

6. He experiences thy good favour, youngest (of the 
gods), who offers praise to thee, a Brahman, coming quickly 
(to bestow felicity): to him are all prosperous days and 
wealth (of cattle) and treasures: do thou, as the lord 
of sacrifice, shine upon his dwelling.
