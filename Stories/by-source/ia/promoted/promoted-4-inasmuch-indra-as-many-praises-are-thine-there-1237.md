# 4 Inasmuch Indra As Many Praises Are Thine There 1237

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9676-9682

4. Inasmuch, Indra, as many praises are thine, there¬ 
fore, combating for the sake of (shedding) water on fertile 
(lands), thou hast effected (the discomfiture of its ob¬ 
structors) ; thou, who art the showerer (of benefits), hast, 
on behalf of the sun, destroyed in his own dwelling the very 
name of (the Asura), Dasa, in battle.
