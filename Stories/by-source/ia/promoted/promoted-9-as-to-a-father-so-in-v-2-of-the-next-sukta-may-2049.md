# 9 As To A Father So In V 2 Of The Next Sukta May 2049

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16992-16998

9. As to a Father. —So in v. 2 of the next Sukta, may 
the bearer of the oblation, the undecaying Agni, be a father 
to us: or the relationship may be reversed ; protect him, 
Agni , who, like a father, cherishes thee as a son, Agni being 
generated and maintained by the sacrificer ; as, by another 
text, thou art the son of him who maintains thee.
