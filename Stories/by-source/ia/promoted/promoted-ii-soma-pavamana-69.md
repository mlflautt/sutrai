# Ii Soma Pavamana 69

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8277-8293

II.  Soma  Pavamana. 

KINO  Pavamana  is implored with holy songs, on 

man's  behalf, 
To  travel  through  the  realm  of  air. 

2  Pressed  for  the  banquet  of  the Gods,  O  Soma,  bring 

us  might,  and  speed, 
Like  beauty  for  a  brilliant  show  ! 

3  Bring us, O Indu,  ^'hundredfold  increase of  June, 

and  noble  steeds. 
The  gift  of  fortune  for  our  help  !
