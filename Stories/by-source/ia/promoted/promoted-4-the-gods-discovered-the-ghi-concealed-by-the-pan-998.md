# 4 The Gods Discovered The Ghi Concealed By The Pan 998

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7996-8000

4. The gods discovered the Ghi concealed by the Panis, 
placed three-fold in the cow: Indra generated one 
(portion), Surya another, the (other gods) fabricated one 
from the resplendent (Agni), for the sake of the oblation.
