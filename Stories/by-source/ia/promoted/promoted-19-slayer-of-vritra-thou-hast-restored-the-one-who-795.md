# 19 Slayer Of Vritra Thou Hast Restored The One Who 795

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6467-6473

19. Slayer of Vritra, thou hast restored the (one who 
was) blind, the (other who was) lame, both abandoned 
{by their kin): (it is not possible) to exceed the happiness 
that is given by thee. 

136 Rig-veda Translation [IV. 3* 10
