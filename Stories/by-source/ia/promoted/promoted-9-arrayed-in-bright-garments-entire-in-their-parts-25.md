# 9 Arrayed In Bright Garments Entire In Their Parts 25

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 941-945

9. Arrayed in bright (garments), entire (in their parts),, 
these pillars ranging in rows like swans, have come to us 
erected by pious sages on the east (of the fire) ; they 
proceed resplendent on the path of the gods.
