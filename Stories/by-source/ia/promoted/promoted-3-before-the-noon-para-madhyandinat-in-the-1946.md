# 3 Before The Noon Para Madhyandinat In The 1946

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15762-15770

3. Before the Noon. — Para madhyandinat : in the 
forenoon, when the Soma is drunk, and thence Ivdra ; and 
Agni have been invigorated. 

As Robbers are the Destroyers of those, etc. —The 
text has only durge durone kratwa na yatam , as of t hose gone 
on account of business in a difficult place : where protection 
is difficult, Sayana supplies, from robbers.
