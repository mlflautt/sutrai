# 4 Amongst Whom The Ceremonial Of Many Kinds Is 1129

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8942-8947

4. Amongst whom the ceremonial of many kinds is 
observed ; who perpetuate the sacred hymns by their 
recital; by them the sacrificial viands are placed upon 
the strewn grass (at the sacrifice which) leads (the wor¬ 
shipper) to heaven.
