# 4 Divine Dawns May Your Chariot Whether Old Or 950

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7648-7653

4. Divine Dawns, may your chariot, whether old or' 
new, be frequent at this day’s (worship), wherewith, 
affluent Dawns, possessing riches, (you shine) upon the 
seven-mouthed (troop of the) Angirasas, the observers of 
the nine or ten days’ rite.
