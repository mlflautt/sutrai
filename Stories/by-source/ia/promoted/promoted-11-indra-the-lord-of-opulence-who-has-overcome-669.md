# 11 Indra The Lord Of Opulence Who Has Overcome 669

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5534-5547

11. Indra, the lord of opulence, who has overcome 
many (enemies), has completely won (their) cattle, (their) 
gold, (their) horses: chief leader by his energies, praised 
by these liis worshippers, he is the distributor of riehes, 
the bestower of wealth.
