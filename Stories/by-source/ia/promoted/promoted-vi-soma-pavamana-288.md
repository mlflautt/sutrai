# Vi Soma Pavamana 288

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15881-15923

VI.  Soma  Pavamana. 

THEN  thou,  made  beautiful  by  night,  enterest  into 

mighty  deeds, 
When  prayers  impel  the  golden-hued  to  hasten  from 

Vivasvan's  place. 

3  Vashat  :  an  exclamation — meaning,  probably,  may  he  (Agni) 
bear  it  (to  the  Gods)! — used  at  the  moment  of  pouring  the  sacrificial 
oil  or  clarified  butter  on  the  fire.  Ye  :  Gods.  The  concluding  Pdda, 
or  half-line  is  common  to  many  of  the  hymns  ascribed  to  Vasishtua 
and  his  family. 

Rigveda  IV.  47. 1—3.  The  Rishi  is  Vamadeva,  Themetreis  Anushtup. 
1  The  bright :  juice,  understood. 

Rigveda  IX.  99.  2—4.  Tho  Rishis  are  the  two  Rebhaaunus,  of  the 
family  of  Kasyapa.  The  metre  is  Anushtup. 

1  By  night:  ks/tapd  :  Professor  Ludwig  translates  the  word  by  'der 
fiirst,'  'the  prince,'  meaning  Soma.  Enterest  into  mighty  deeds:  or, 
more  in  accordance  with  Sayana,  pluugest  into  the  sacred  food : 
'  tauchst  in  die  Opferspeisen.' — Benfey.  .  Vivasvdn's  place:  the  chapel 
or  sacrificial  chamber,  Ste  Yediscfo  Sludien,  I,  p.  p.  241,  242, 

296  THE  HYMNS  OF  \PARTI7. 

2  We  cleanse  this  gladdening  drink  of  his,  the  juice 

which  Indra  chiefly  drinks, 

That  which  kine  took  into  their  mouths,  of  old,  arid 
princes  take  it  now. 

3  They  with  the  ancient  psalm  have  sung  to  him  as  he 

is  purified, 

And  sacred  songs  which  bear  the  names  of  Gods 
have  supplicated  him.
