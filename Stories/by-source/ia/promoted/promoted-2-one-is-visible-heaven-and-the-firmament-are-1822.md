# 2 One Is Visible Heaven And The Firmament Are 1822

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14434-14447

2. One is Visible. —Heaven and the firmament are 
not wholly discernible, earth is. 

Notes on Volume III 

325 

S. The Three-breasted. —That is, as subsequently 
specified, the year, personified: the number three, repeatedly 
mentioned, is said to apply to the seasons of the year, reduced 
irom the usual number of six to three, the hot, rainy, and 
•cold, by merging into them, severally, the vernal, autumnal, 
.and dewy.
