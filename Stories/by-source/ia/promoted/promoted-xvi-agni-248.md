# Xvi Agni 248

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14899-14917

XVI.  Agni. 

AGNI  is  head  and  height  of  heaven,  the  master  of 

the  earth  is  he  : 
He  quickeneth  the  waters'  seed. 

2  Yea,  Agni,  thou  as  Lord  of  light  rulest  o'er  choicest 

gifts  :  may  I, 
Thy  singer,  find  defence  in  thee  ! 

3  Upward,  O  Agni,  rise  thy  flames,  pure  and  resplend- 

ent, blazing  high, 
Thy  lustres,  fair  effulgences- 

CHAPTER  |I.
