# X Agni 206

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13652-13667

X.  Agni. 
EAGER  for  wealth  we  meditate  Agni's effectual laud 

to-day, 
Laud  of  the  God  who  touches  heaven. 

2  May Agni who is Hotar-priest among mankind 

accept  our  songs, 
And worship  the  celestial  folk  ! 

3  Thou,  Agni,  art  spread  widely  forth,  Priest  dear  and 

excellent  :  through thee 
Men  make  the  sacrifice  complete.
