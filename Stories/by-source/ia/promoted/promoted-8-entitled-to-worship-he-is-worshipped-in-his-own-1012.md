# 8 Entitled To Worship He Is Worshipped In His Own 1012

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8102-8112

8. Entitled to worship, he is worshipped in his own 
(abode) ; humble-minded, eminent amongst sages, our 
auspicious guest, the thousand-rayed, the showerer (of 
benefits), of well-known might, thou, Agni, surpassest 
all others in strength. 

9. (Too) quickly, Agni, dost thou pass to others from 
him to whom thou hast been manifest: most beautiful, 
adorable, radiant, many-shining, the loved of people, the 
guest of men.
