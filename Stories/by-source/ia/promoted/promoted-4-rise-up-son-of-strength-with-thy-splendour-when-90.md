# 4 Rise Up Son Of Strength With Thy Splendour When 90

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1386-1392

4. Rise up, son of strength, with (thy) splendour when 
hymned, and bestow abundant food and wealth, Agni, 
upon the descendants of Vishwamitra celebrating thy 
praise; and grant them exemption from sickness and 
danger: Encourager of pious works (Agni), we repeatedly 
sprinkle thy substance (with milk and butte*).
