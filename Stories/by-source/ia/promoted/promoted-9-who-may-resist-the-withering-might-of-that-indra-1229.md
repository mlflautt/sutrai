# 9 Who May Resist The Withering Might Of That Indra 1229

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9622-9627

9. Who may resist the withering might of that Indra : 
he, single and irresistible, carries off the riches (of the 
enemy): these two divine (beings, heaven and earth), 
proceed swiftly through fear of the strength of the quick- 
moving Indra.
