# 16 Thou Hast Repressed The Strong Flame Etc 1748

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13573-13587

16. Thou hast Repressed the Strong Flame, etc.— 
Dridham chid arujo gavyam uroam , is interpreted by Sayana, 
prabalam api avatevartamamim urvanalam samyagabhankshih. 
thou hast entirely broken tin' strong (vrva) fire abiding in 
the deep. 

TIT. 3. 4. Shutudri.— -According to the legend cited 
by Sayana , given also by Yaska, Nir, ii. 24, Vishwamiira, 
the family priest of Sudas, the son of Piyavana, having gained 
much wealth in his service, was returning with it home, when 
his road brought him to the confluence of the Vipash and 
Shutudri: in order to make them fordable, he addressed to 
them the first stanzas of this hymn, to which they replied, 
and allowed him and his followers to cross.
