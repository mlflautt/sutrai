# 6 When Thou Hast Drunk The Soma Then Indra Go 378

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3459-3463

6. When thou hast drunk the Soma, then, Indra, go 
home: an auspicious life (abides) pleasantly in thy dwell¬ 
ing: in either (case) there is the standing in thy car or 
liberating the steeds for provender.
