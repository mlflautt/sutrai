# 4 With A Wheelless Car Achakraya Wadhaya Is 1941

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15715-15725

4. With a Wheelless Car. —Achakraya wadhaya is 
explained, chakrarakitena rathena , with a car without wheels : 
the text has havyam , but the Scholiast says this is a metonymy 
for the Soma , which, as we have before seen, (note on III. 
4. 5. 7), is said to have been brought from heaven by the 
Gayalri , in the form of a hawk: according to Sayana we are 
to understand here by the hawk 4 he Supreme Spirit, Para- 
brahma, but this seems to be the notion of a later day. 

Page 131.
