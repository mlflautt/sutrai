# 13 The Worshipper By His Conservatory Sacrifice Ha 224

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2307-2313

13. The worshipper, by his conservatory sacrifice, has 
made Indra present: may I bring him to my presence to 
obtain new wealth, him who has been exalted by praises,, 
whether ancient, medieval, or recent. 

Third Mandala
