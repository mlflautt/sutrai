# 2 The Second Class Of Verbs Is Formed From The 50

**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)
**Lines:** 8554-8601

2. The second class of verbs is formed from the 
first, either— 
(a) By adding a vowel, or— 
(b) By interposing a consonant.
