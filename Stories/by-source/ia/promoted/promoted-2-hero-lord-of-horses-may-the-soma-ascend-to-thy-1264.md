# 2 Hero Lord Of Horses May The Soma Ascend To Thy 1264

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9850-9854

2. Hero, lord of horses, may the Soma ascend to thy 
destructive jaw, as if to the summit of a mountain : may 
we all, royal Indra, who art invoked of many, give pleasure 
to thee with our hymns, as to horses with fodder.
