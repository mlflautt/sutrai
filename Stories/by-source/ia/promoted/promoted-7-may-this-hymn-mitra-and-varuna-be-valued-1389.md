# 7 May This Hymn Mitra And Varuna Be Valued 1389

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10713-10718

7. May this (hymn), Mitra and Varuna, be valued 
(by you): may it, Aoni, be valued (by thee) as the means 
to us of happiness unmixed : may we (thence) obtain 
.stability and permanence : reverence be to thee, radiant 
and mighty asylum (of the universe).
