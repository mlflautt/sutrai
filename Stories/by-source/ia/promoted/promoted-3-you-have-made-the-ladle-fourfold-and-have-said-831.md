# 3 You Have Made The Ladle Fourfold And Have Said 831

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6827-6832

3. You have made the ladle fourfold, and have said 
(to Agni), assent (to the division): therefore have you 
gone, Vajas, the path of the immortals: dexterous¬ 
handed Ribhus (you have joined) the company of the 
gods.
