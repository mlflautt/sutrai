# 2 Agni Born Of Sacrifice Three Are Thy Viands Thre 99

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1459-1464

2. Agni, born of sacrifice, three are thy viands ; three 
thine abiding places ; three the tongues satisfying (the 
gods): three verily are thy forms, acceptable to the deities, 
and with them never heedless (of our wishes), be propitious 
to our praises.
