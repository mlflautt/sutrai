# 9 This And The Two Preceding Stanzas Are Cited In  2002

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16369-16383

9. This and the two preceding stanzas are cited in the 
Aitasreya Brahmana, vm, 5, 24, 26, as authority for the in¬ 
dispensable employment by a prince of a Brahmana as Purohit, 
or priest, to conduct solemn rites on his behalf ; “the gods 
eat not the food of a Raja who has no Purohita ; therefore, 
when about to sacrifice, let him appoint (lit. place before) a 
Brahmana, na ham apurohitasya Rajno deva annam adanti , 
tasmad Baja yakshyamana Brahmanam puro dadhita : Sayanu, 
in commenting upon the verses cited, adopts some expla¬ 
nations differing from those here followed, but the variations 
are not of any great moment ; as Ila, food, instead of Earthy 
his food increases at all seasons ; and sajanya, allies of his 
enemies, instead of his own people : wherever Brahma occurs 
he renders it Brahmana .
