# 3 Who Divine Most Divine As Soon As Born Was 727

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5954-5958

3. Who divine, most divine, as soon as born (was 
endowed) with abundant viands, and great energies, 
holding in his arms the willing thunderbolt, and causing 
by his strength (both) heaven and earth to tremble.
