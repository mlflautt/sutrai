# 6 The Cloud Through Your Will Mttra And Varuna 1540

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11774-11780

6. The cloud, (through your will), Mttra and Varuna, 
utters a wonderful sound, indicative of radiance, and 
announcing (abundant) food : the Maruts thoroughly 
invest the clouds with (their) devices, and, (along with 
them), you two cause the purple and faultless sky to send 
down rain.
