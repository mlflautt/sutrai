# 1 The Milch Cow The Dawn Yields The Desired Milk 457

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3979-3984

1. The milch-cow (the dawn) yields the desired milk 
to the ancient (Agni) ; the son of the south passes within 
(the firmament) ; the hright-Uoured (day) brings the 
illuminative (sun) : the praiser awakes (to glorify) the 
Ashwins preceding the dawn.
