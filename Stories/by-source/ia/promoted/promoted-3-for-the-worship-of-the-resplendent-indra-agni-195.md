# 3 For The Worship Of The Resplendent Indra Agni 195

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2096-2100

3. For the worship of the resplendent (Indra), Agni, 
glowing with flame, has begotten mighty sons: great was 
the germ, and great was their birth, and great was their 
efficacy, for the worship of Haryashwa.
