# 6 Such As The Deity Is Beheld Such Is Lie Said To  1352

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10450-10455

6. Such as (the deity) is beheld, such is lie said (to be) : 
they abide with concentrated splendour in the waters : 
(may they bestow) upon us honourable and ample (riches), 
great energy, numerous male offspring, and undecaying 
vigour.
