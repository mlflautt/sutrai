# 7 An Excellent And Agreeable Form Has Been Assumed 844

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6919-6924

7. An excellent and agreeable form has been assumed 
by you: (this is our own) praise: Vajas and Ribhus 
be gratified (thereby), for you are wise, experienced, and 
intelligent: such we make you known (to be) by this (our)< 
prayer.
