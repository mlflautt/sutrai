# 5 Of Thee Indra Who Art Mighty Great Are The Deeds 729

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5965-5981

5. Of thee, Indra, who art mighty, great are the deeds, 
and to be proclaimed at all sacrifices, inasmuch as, high- 
minded hero, thou, sustaining (the world), hast by thy 
strength slain Ahi with the resistless thunderbolt. 

124 

Rig-veda Translation 

[IV. 3.1 

w 6. Most powerful Indra, all these, thy exploits, are 
verily true: (through fear of thee), the showerer (of 
benefits), the cows shed (milk) from their udders: then, 
benevolent-minded (Indra), the rivers, fearing thee, flow 
with rapidity.
