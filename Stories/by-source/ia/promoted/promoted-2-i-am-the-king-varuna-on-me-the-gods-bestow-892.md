# 2 I Am The King Varuna On Me The Gods Bestow 892

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7229-7233

2. I am the king Varuna ; on me (the gods) bestow 
those principal energies (that are) destructive of the 
Asuras ; (they) associate me with the worship of Varuna : 
I rule over (the acts) of the proximate form of man.
