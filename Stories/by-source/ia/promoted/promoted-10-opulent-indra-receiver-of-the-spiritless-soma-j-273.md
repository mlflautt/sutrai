# 10 Opulent Indra Receiver Of The Spiritless Soma J 273

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2645-2649

10. Opulent Indra, receiver of the spiritless Soma juice, 
give to us riches in universally desired quantity ; grant 
us to live a hundred years ; bestow upon us, Indra with 
the handsome chin, numerous posterity.
