# 9 That Sage T Hat Is Vishwamitra Himself In 1718

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13234-13239

9. That Sage.—T hat is, Vishwamitra himself: in 
consequence of his discovery that Vaishwanara is Para-brahma 
the supreme spirit : as it would be scarcely decorous to say so 
much in his own commendation this stanza may be ascribed 
to Brahma*
