# 1 May That Indra Who Is The Donor Of Wealth Who 1263

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9844-9849

1. May that Indra who is the donor of wealth, who 
knows (how) to distribute riches, come (to our sacrifice) 
marching boldly like a warrior ; and may he, being thirsty, 
and desirous (of the draught), drink of the effused Soma 
juice.
