# 7 Affluent Ushas Bring Wealth And Abundant Food To 1622

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12388-12397

7. Affluent Ushas, bring wealth and abundant food to 
those who, liberal givers, bestow upon us riches with horses 
and cattle; bright-born goddess (who art) sincerely praised 
for (the gift of) horses. 

8. daughter of heaven, bring to us food and cattle, 
together with the pure rays of the sun, and the radiant 
flames (of the kindled fires): bright-born goddess, (who 
art) sincerely praised for (the gift of) horses.
