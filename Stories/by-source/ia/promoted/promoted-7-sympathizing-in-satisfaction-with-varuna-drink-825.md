# 7 Sympathizing In Satisfaction With Varuna Drink 825

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6780-6786

7. Sympathizing in satisfaction with Varuna, drink, 
Indra, the Soma juice ; drink it, thou who art entitled 
to praise, sympathizing with the Maruts: drink, sympa¬ 
thizing with the first drinkers, with the drinkers (at the 
sacrifices) of the Ritus; sympathizing with the protec¬ 
tresses of the wives (of the gods), the giver of wealth.
