# 3 Thou Art Born Unobstructed Of Two Mothers Pure 1097

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8682-8687

3. Thou art born unobstructed of two mothers: pure, 
-adorable, wise, thou hast sprung up from (the devotion of) 
the householder: they have augmented thee with butter : 
Agni, to whom burnt-offerings are made, the smoke is 
thy banner spread abroad in the sky.
