# 1 Auni Lights Up The Face Of The Dawns The Devout 1603

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12226-12230

1. Auni lights up the face of the dawns : the devout 
praisers of the pious have risen up : therefore, Ashwins, 
lords of the chariot, descending, come hither to-day to the 
splendid sacrifice, perfect (in all its parts).
