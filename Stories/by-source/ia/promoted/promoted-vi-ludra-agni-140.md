# Vi Ludra Agni 140

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11258-11273

VI.  ludra-Agni, 

GLORIFY  him  who  compasses  all  forests  with  his  glow- 
ing flame, 
And  leaves  them blackened  by  his  tongue. 

2  He  who  gains  Indra's  grace by  fire  enkindled, finds 

an  easy way 
Over  the  floods  to  splendid wealth. 

3  Give  us,  ye  twain,  swift steeds  to bring Indra and 

Agni,  and  bestow 
Abundant  food  with  wealth  on  us.
