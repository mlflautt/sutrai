# 1 In What Manner May Any One Extol The Mighty 736

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6019-6025

1. In what manner may (any one) extol the mighty 
Indra ? at the sacrifice of what worshipper may he, 
propitiated, be present, drinking the sacrificial Soma 
beverage, desirous of the (sacred) food, and pleased 
(by the oblation) ? the mighty Indra is borne (to the 
rite) for the purpose of bestowing brilliant wealth.
