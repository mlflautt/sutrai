# 1 Indra Wielder Of The Thunderbolt All The Protect 693

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5702-5706

1. Indra, wielder of the thunderbolt, all the protecting, 
deities who are reverently invoked, and both the heaven 
and the earth, glorify thee who art verily one alone, mighty* 
vast, and pleasing of aspect, for the destruction of Vritra..
