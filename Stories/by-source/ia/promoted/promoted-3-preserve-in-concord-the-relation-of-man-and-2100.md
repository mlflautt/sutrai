**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))

3. Preserve in Concord the Relation of Man and 
W ife. — Sam jaspatyam suyamam kurushva , make perfect the 
well-connected duty of wife and husband, an appropriate 
prayer for a female, Yajur- Veda , 33. 12.
