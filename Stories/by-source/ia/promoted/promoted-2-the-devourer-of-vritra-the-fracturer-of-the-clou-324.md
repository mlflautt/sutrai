# 2 The Devourer Of Vritra The Fracturer Of The Clou 324

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3077-3081

2. The devourer of Vritra, the fracturer of the cloud, 
the sender of the waters, the demolisher of cities, Indra, 
the destroyer of powerful enemies, has mounted his chariot 
to urge his horses to our presence.
