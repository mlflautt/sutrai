# 55 Also To Turn His Disturbance To Creatures 203

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5780-5795

55. Also, to turn his disturbance^ to creatures 
of even other kinds, there are demons and fiends 
of further descriptions (freh-altdn); and for the 
assistance of those combatants he established also 
those afflictions (ntvakin) of many, the witches of 
natures for gloomy places, whose vesture is the 
radiance of the lights ^ that fall, and rush, and turn 
below the luminaries which have to soar (v^^ijnt- 
kino) in stopping the way of any little conceal- 
ment of the spirits and worldly beings'^. 56. And 
they (the witches) overspread the light and glory 
of those luminaries, of whose bestowal of glory and 
their own diminution of it, moreover, for seizing 
the creatures, consist the pain, death, and original 
evil of the abode for the demon of demons *.
