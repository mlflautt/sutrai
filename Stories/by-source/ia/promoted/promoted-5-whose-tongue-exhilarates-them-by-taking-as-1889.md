# 5 Whose Tongue Exhilarates Them By Taking As 1889

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15193-15200

5. Whose Tongue Exhilarates them.— By taking as 
their mouth the oblation. 

The Humiliator of Demons.— Damunasam has various 
meanings : rakshasam damanakarana-manasopetam , being in¬ 
tent on destroying the Rakshasas, having a mind for taming, 
or a tamed or humble mind, a liberal or a domestic mind.
