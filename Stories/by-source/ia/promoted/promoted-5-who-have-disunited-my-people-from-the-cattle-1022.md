# 5 Who Have Disunited My People From The Cattle 1022

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8164-8170

5. Who have disunited my people from the cattle ? 
was there not for them an invincible protector ? may 
they who have seized upon that (people) perish, for he 
who knows (our wishes) approaches to (protect) the cattle. 

1.76 Rig-veda Translation [V. 1.2
