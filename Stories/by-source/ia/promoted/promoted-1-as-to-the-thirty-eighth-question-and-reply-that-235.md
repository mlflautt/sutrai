# 1 As To The Thirty Eighth Question And Reply That 235

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6918-6924

1. As to the thirty-eighth question and reply, that 
which you ask is thus : What are the reason and 
cause of tying on the sctcred tkread-g\.r(^lt (Icdstiko)^ 
which, when they shall tie it on is said to be so 
greatly valuable, and when they shall not tie it the 
sin is so grievous ?
