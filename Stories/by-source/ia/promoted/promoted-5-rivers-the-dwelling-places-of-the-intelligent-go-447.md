# 5 Rivers The Dwelling Places Of The Intelligent Go 447

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3904-3909

5. Rivers, the dwelling-places of the intelligent gods are 
thrice three: the measurer of the three (worlds) is the 
sovereign at sacrifices: three female (divinities) of the 
’waters charged with the rains descend from heaven at the 
thrice (repeated) solemnity.
