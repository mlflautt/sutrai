# Xv Indra 306

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16470-16490

XV.  Indra. 

WHO  knows  what  vital  power  he  wins,  drinking  be- 
side the  flowing  juice  ? 

This  is  the  fair-cheeked  God  who,  joying  in  the 
draught,  break?  down  the  castles  in  his  strength. 

2  As  a  wild  elephant  rushes  on,  this  way  and  that  way 

mad  with  heat, 

None  may  restrain  thee,  yet  come  hither  to  the 
draught !  Thou  movest  mighty  in  thy  power. 

3  When  he, the  terrible, ne'er o'erthrown,  stedfast, 

made  ready  for  the  fight- 
When  Indra  Maghavan lists  to  his praiser's  call, 
he  will  not  stand  aloof,  but  come.
