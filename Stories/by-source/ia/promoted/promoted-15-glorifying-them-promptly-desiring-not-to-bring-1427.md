# 15 Glorifying Them Promptly Desiring Not To Bring 1427

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10997-11001

15. Glorifying them promptly, desiring not to bring 
{other) deities to (his) presence, let (the worshipper) asso¬ 
ciate his gifts with those wise (divinities) renowned for 
their velocity, and distributors (of rewards).
