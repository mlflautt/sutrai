# 2 Gift Of Unlimited Sustenance Tasya Akujyarasya 2134

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17873-17880

2. Gift of Unlimited Sustenance.— Tasya akujyarasya 
davane , in the giving of that unlimited food, annasya : Yaska 
would fill up the ellipse with danasya, gift : he explains aku - 
para by durapara , or mahapara, having a distant or great 
opposite boundary, whence it is also an appellative of the 
sun and of the ocean : it also means a tortoise, one who does 
not go into a well, kupa : the verse occurs Sama-Veda , ii. 523.
