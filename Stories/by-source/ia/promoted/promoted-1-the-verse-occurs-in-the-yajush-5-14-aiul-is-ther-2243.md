# 1 The Verse Occurs In The Yajush 5 14 Aiul Is Ther 2243

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18983-18992

1. The verse occurs in the Yajush, 5. 14, aiul is there 
somewhat differently explained ; viprasya brihato vipashchitah, 
which Sayana regards as epithets of Savitri , are connected by 
Mahidhara , with vipra , and are rendered the priests of the 
eminent and wise (institutor of the rite): again, in the second 
hemistich it is not clear whom the commentator understands 
by eko vayunavid, the only one, knowing knowledge, although 
he possibly concurs with our Scholiast in identifying Savitri 
with Brahma.
