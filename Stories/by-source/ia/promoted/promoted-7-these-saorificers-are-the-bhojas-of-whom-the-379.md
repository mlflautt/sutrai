# 7 These Saorificers Are The Bhojas Of Whom The 379

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3464-3469

7. These (saorificers) are the Bhojas , of whom the 
diversified Angtrasas (are the priests): and the heroic 
sons of the expel)er (of the foes of the gods) from heaven, 
bestowing riches upon Vishwamitra at the sacrifice of a 
thousand (victims), prolong (his) life.
