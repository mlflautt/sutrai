# 10 Dadhikra Has Spread Abroad The Five Classes Of  864

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7049-7053

10. Dadhikra has spread abroad the five classes of beings 
by his strength, as the sun (diffuses) the waters by his 
radiance: may he, the giver of hundereds and thousands* 
associate these praises with agreeable (rewards).
