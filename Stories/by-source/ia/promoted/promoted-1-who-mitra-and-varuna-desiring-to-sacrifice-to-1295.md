# 1 Who Mitra And Varuna Desiring To Sacrifice To 1295

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10048-10053

1. Who, Mitra and Varuna, desiring to sacrifice to 
you, (is able to do so) '( do you, whether (abiding) in the 
region of heaven, or of the vast earth, or of the firmament, 
protect us, and give to the donor (of the oblation), and to 
the sacrifieer, cattle and food.
