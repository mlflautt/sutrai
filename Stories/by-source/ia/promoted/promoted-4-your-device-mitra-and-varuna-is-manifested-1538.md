# 4 Your Device Mitra And Varuna Is Manifested 1538

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11762-11767

4. Your device, Mitra and Varuna, is manifested 
in heaven, when the light (that is) the sun, your wonder¬ 
ful weapon, moves (in the firmament) : him you invest 
in the sky with the cloud (and) with rain ; and (thy) 
sweet drops, Parjanya, fall (at their desire).
