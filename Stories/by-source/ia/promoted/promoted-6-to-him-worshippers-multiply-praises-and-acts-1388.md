# 6 To Him Worshippers Multiply Praises And Acts 1388

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10707-10712

6. To him (worshippers) multiply praises and acts 
of adoration : for (him as for) a son, the (divine) mothers 
weave garments (of light) : rejoicing in the contact of 
their impregnation, the wives of the sun, (the solar rays), 
come to our presence by the path of the sky.
