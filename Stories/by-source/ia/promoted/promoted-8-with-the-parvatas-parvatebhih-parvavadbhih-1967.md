# 8 With The Parvatas Parvatebhih Parvavadbhih 1967

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15964-15972

8. With the Parvatas.— Parvatebhih , parvavadbhih, 
parvanyarchyamamir-devavisheshaih : a sort of deities to 
be worshipped at the Parvas, certain periods of the month,, 
as the new and full moon, etc.
