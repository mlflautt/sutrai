# 1 The Fourteenth Question Is That Which J Ou A Sk 96

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2921-2926

1. The fourteenth question is that which j-ou a.sk 
thus: Is the eradication of life the gnawing of dogs 
and birds upon the corpse ? And docs the sin of 
those who suppose f/'’a sin proceed from that origin, 
or not ?
