# 5 Thou Alone When Invoked By Many And The Slayer 175

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1960-1965

5. Thou alone, when invoked by many, and the slayer 
of Vritra by thy prowess, didst say firmly (to the gods), 
fear not! Indra, endowed with opulence, vast is thy 
grasp, wherewith thou hast seized these unbounded regions, 
heaven and earth.
