# V Natt This Is A Marine God Worshipped Generally 28

**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)
**Lines:** 2628-2636

V. Natt.—This is a marine god, worshipped generally 
along the coast between Kotonu and Bageida. At 
Whydah he is considered a subordinate of Wu. He 

TRIBAL DEITIES. 67 

is the store-keeper of Wu, and takes charge of the fish, 
hence he is especially worshipped by fishermen.
