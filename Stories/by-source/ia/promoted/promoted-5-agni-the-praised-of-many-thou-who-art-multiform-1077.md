# 5 Agni The Praised Of Many Thou Who Art Multiform 1077

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8559-8563

5. Agni, the praised of many, thou who art multiform, 
givest of old food to every mortal ; thou reignest with 
strength over the various (sacrificial) viands ; the splendour 
of thee when blazing brightly is not rivalled (by any).
