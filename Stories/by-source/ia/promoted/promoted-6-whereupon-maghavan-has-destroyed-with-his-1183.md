# 6 Whereupon Maghavan Has Destroyed With His 1183

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9312-9317

6. Whereupon Maghavan has destroyed with his 
thunderbolt at once hivS (Shambara’s) ninety and nine 
cities : the Maruts glorifying Indra in a common dwelling 
with the Trishtubh hymn, he destroyed the bright (city 
of the Asura).
