# 19 In Every Battle Maghavan May We Along With 657

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5454-5464

19. In every battle, Maghavan, may we, along with 
those men who trust in thee and offer rich gifts, like those 
who are resplendent with riches, triumphing over their 
foes, glorify thee many nights and years. 

Rig-veda Translation 

312 

[IV. 2. 7
