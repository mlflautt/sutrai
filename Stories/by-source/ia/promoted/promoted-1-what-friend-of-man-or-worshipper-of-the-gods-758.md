# 1 What Friend Of Man Or Worshipper Of The Gods 758

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6167-6172

1. What friend of man, or worshipper of the gods, 
deserving the friendship of Indra, has to-day enjoyed 
(it) ? or what offerer of the libation on the kindled fire 
praises him (sufficiently) for his great and unbounded 
protection ?
