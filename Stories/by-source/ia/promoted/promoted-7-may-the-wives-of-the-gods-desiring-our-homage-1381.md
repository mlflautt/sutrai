# 7 May The Wives Of The Gods Desiring Our Homage 1381

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10647-10659

7. May the wives of the gods, desiring (our homage), 
defend us : may they so protect us that (we may obtain) 
vigorous (offspring) and abundant food : whether terres¬ 
trial, or those in charge of the waters (in the firmament), 
do you, goddesses, earnestly invoked, bestow upon us 
felicity. 

V: 4.3]
