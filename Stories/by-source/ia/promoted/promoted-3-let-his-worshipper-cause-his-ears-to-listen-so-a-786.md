# 3 Let His Worshipper Cause His Ears To Listen So A 786

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6371-6382

3. Let (his worshipper) cause his ears to listen so as 
to invigorate him (by praise), and to give him pleasure in 
every acceptable place; and being well moistened with 
the Soma juice, may the vigorous Indra render the holy 
places (conducive) to our wealth, and free from danger. 

4. (That Indra), who repairs to the suppliant for (his) 
protection, to the sage in this manner invoking and praising 
him ; he who, armed with the thunderbolt, places, of 
his own accord, hundreds and thousands of swift-going, 
(horses) in the shafts (of their cars).
