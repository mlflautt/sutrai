# 12 The Unreviled Sages Abiding In The Dwellings Of 524

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4514-4518

12. The unreviled sages abiding in the dwellings of 
man have glorified the sage (Agni) ; therefore, lord of 
sacrifice, thou mayest- proceed with swift-moving feet to 
behold the admirable and marvellous deities.
