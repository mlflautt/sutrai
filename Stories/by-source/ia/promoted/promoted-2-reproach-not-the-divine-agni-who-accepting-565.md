# 2 Reproach Not The Divine Agni Who Accepting 565

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4785-4790

2. Reproach not the divine (Agni), who, accepting 
the oblation, has given this wealth to me, his mortal 
(worshipper) of mature (intellect) ; Agni, who is wise, 
immortal, discriminating, (who is) Vaishwanara, chief 
conductor (of rites), the mighty.
