# 4 Sharp Weaponed Agni Rise Up Spread Wide Thy 552

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4698-4702

4. Sharp-weaponed Agni, rise up; spread wide (thy 
flames) against (the Rakshasas) ; entirely consume the 
foes: blazing Agni, burn down him who acts as an enemy 
towards us like a piece of dry timber.
