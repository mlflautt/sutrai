# 1 Awakener Op Her Daughter Duhitur Bodhayanti 2168

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18212-18219

1. Awakener op Her Daughter.— Duhitur bodhayanti , 
blnumya bodham kurvana, making the awaking of the daughter, 
the comment says, of the earth : in a subsequent passage 
quoted by Sayana we have Ushas called divor duhita bhuva- 
<nasya patni, the daughter of heaven, the bride of earth : she 
is most usually designated the daughter of heaven : see Vol. 
ii, I. 18. 4. 3.
