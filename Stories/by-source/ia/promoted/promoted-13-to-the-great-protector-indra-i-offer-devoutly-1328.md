# 13 To The Great Protector Indra I Offer Devoutly 1328

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10260-10264

13. To the great protector, (Indra), I offer devoutly 
pious praise, new and originated (at this time): to him, 
the showerer, who, for his daughter (earth), giving form 
to the rivers, has provided this water for our (use).
