# 2 The Wise Savitri Comprehends All Forms In Himsel 1632

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12458-12462

2. The wise Savitri comprehends all forms (in himself): 
he has engendered what is good for biped and quadruped : 
the adorable Savitri has illumed the heaven, and shines 
in sequence to the passage of the Dawn.
