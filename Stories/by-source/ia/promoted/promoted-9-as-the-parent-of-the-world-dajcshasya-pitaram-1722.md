# 9 As The Parent Of The World Dajcshasya Pitaram 1722

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13273-13280

9. As the Parent of the World.— DaJcshasya pitaram 
tana is explained by Sayana as dakshasya prajajmter tanaya. 
the daughter of the Prajapati Daksha ; that is, the earth, 
here identified with the altar, vedirupa : she sustains, dhara- 
yati , understood, him. Agni, the protector or father of the 
world, mrvasya jagatah palakam dharayati : this and the two 
preceding stanzas occur in the Saman n. 827, 829.
