# 4 Soma Yields Its Pure J Uice The Text Has Only 2151

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18044-18056

4. Soma yields its Pure J uice.— The text has only 
shukram anshuh , which is thus explained, sa cha anshuh 

408 

Rig-veda Translation 

8hulcram nvrmalam rasam dugdhe , and that Soma has milked 
the pure juice ; or Anshu may be an ejnthet of Adhwaryu ,. 
the extensively present priest, anshur vyapto adhwaryuh.
