# V 2 10 1151

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9088-9112

V. 2. 10. 

The deity is Agni ; the Rishi* are termed Gaupayanas and Lau- 
payanas, and are four in number, named severally, Bandhu, Suban* 
dhu, Shrutabandhu, and Vjprabandhu, to each of whom a half 
stanza of tho two stanzas of which the •Sukta consists is attributed; 
the metre is Viraj. 

1, 2. Agni, who art to be adored, be ever nigh to us, 
our protector and benefactor: do thou, who art the 
giver of dwellings and dispenser of food, be present with 
us : bestow upon us most brilliant wealth. 

3, 4. Understand us, Agni ; hear our invocation ; 
defend us from all malevolent (people): most bright
