# 15 May We Seven Priests First In Order Engender Fr 527

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4532-4536

15. May we seven priests first in order engender from 
the maternal dawn the worshippers of the creator (Agni) ; 
may we Angirasas be the sons of heaven, and, radiant, 
divide the wealth-containing mountain.
