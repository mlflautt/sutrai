# Iv 5 13 This Sukta Which Is Probably Ancient Is A 2032

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16781-16787

IV. 5. 13.— This Sukta , which is probably ancient, is a 
.good specimen of Vaidik vagueness, and mystification, and 
of the straits to which commentators are put to extract an 
intelligible meaning from the text. 

Page 173.
