# 2 May These Sacrifices Be Acceptable To You In Hea 849

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6952-6956

2. May these sacrifices be (acceptable) to you in heart 
and mind: may to-day the sufficient (juices) mixed with 
butter flow to you: the full libations are prepared for you : 
m^y they, when drank, animate you for glorious deeds.
