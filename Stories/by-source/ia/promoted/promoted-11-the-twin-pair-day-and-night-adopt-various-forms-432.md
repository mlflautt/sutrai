# 11 The Twin Pair Day And Night Adopt Various Forms 432

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3800-3804

11. The twin pair (day and night) adopt various forms: 
one of them shines brightly, the other is black: twin 
sisters are they, one black and the other white: great 
and unequalled is the might of the gods.
