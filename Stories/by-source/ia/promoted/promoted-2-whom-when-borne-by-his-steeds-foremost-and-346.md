# 2 Whom When Borne By His Steeds Foremost And 346

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3229-3234

2. Whom, when borne by his steeds, foremost and 
eminent in battles, breaking in two the (hostile host), 
no one ever surpasses: supreme in sway, attended by his 
i&xthixxYMaruts, and hastening to combat, he has destroyed 
with foe-withering (energies) the existence of the Dasyu .
