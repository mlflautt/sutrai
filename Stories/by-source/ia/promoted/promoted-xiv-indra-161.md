# Xiv Indra 161

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12031-12045

XIV.  Indra. 

BOTH  boons, — may,  Indra,  hitherward turned, listen 

to  this  prayer  of  ours, 
And mightiest  Maghavan with thought  inclined to 

us  come  near  to  drink  to  Soma  juice  ! 
2 For him, strong, independent ruler, Heaven and 

E*rth  have  fashioned  forth  with  power  and might. 
Thou  seatest  thee  as  first  among  thy peers in  place, 

for  thy  soul  longs  for  Soma  juice.
