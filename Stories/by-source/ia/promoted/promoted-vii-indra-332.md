# Vii Indra 332

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17301-17337

VII.  Indra. 

THE  old  hath  waked  the  young  Moon  from  his  slumber 
who  runs  his  circling  course  with-many  round  him. 

Behold  the  God's  high  wisdom  in  its  greatness  :  he 
who  died  yesterday  to-day  is  living. 

Rigveda  IV.  10.  1—3.  The  Rishi  is  Vamadeva. The  metre  is  Pada- 
pankti  (5  x  5). Stanza  1  is  a  repetition  of  I.  v.  i.  9.  8. 
2  The  promoter :  literally,  the  charioteer. 

Rigveda  I.  44.  1,  2.  The  Rishi  is  Fraskanva. The'  metre  is  Brihatt 
in  stanza  1,  which  is  a  repetition  of  Li.  i.  4.  6,  and  Satobrihati  in  stanza  2*. 
2  Charioteer  :  promoter. 

Rigveda  X.  55.  5—7. The  Rishi  is Brihaduktha. The metre if* 
Trishtup. Stanza  1  is  a  repetition  of  I,  iv.  i.  4..  3.
