# 4 They All Ornamented Indra Standing In His Car 283

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2734-2745

4. They all ornamented (Indra) standing (in his car) ; 
and, clothed in beauty, he proceeds self-radiant: wonder¬ 
ful are the acts of that showerer (of benefits), the influ- 
encer (of consciences), who omniform, presides over the 
ambrosial (waters).
