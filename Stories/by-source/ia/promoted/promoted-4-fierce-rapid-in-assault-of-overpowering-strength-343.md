# 4 Fierce Rapid In Assault Of Overpowering Strength 343

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3203-3207

4. Fierce, rapid in assault, of overpowering strength,, 
he made his form obedient to his will: having overcome 
Twashtri by his innate (vigour), and carried off the 
Soma juice, he drank it (deposited) in the ladles.
