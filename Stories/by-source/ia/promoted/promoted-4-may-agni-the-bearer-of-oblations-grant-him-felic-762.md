# 4 May Agni The Bearer Of Oblations Grant Him Felic 762

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6191-6195

4. May Agni, the bearer of oblations, grant him felicity, 
/and long behold the rising sun (in the dwelling of him) 
who says, let us offer libations to Indra, leader (of rites), 
the friend of man, the chief leader amongst leaders.
