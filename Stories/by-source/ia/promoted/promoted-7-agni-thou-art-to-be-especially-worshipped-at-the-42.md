# 7 Agni Thou Art To Be Especially Worshipped At The 42

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1037-1041

7. Agni, thou art to be especially worshipped at the 
sacrifice : worship the gods (on behalf of) the devout 
(worshipper): thou shinest the invoker (of the gods) the 
exhilarator (of the pious) overcoming thy foes.
