# 1 As Trita Confutes The Words Of His 2260

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 19127-19133

1. As Trita Confutes the Words of His 
Opponents.— Vanir-iva Tritah : the comment explains this, 
Trita rishi prativadavakyani prabhinatti, as the Rishi Trita 
breaks or refutes controversial arguments : or Trita may 
mean Agui, who, in the three regions, disregards or 
dissipates reproaches.
