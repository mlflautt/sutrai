# 2 Ask Of The Lords Of Earth The Holy Teachers The 281

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2721-2726

2. Ask of the lords (of earth, the holy teachers), the 
birth of those deified sages, who with well-governed minds, 
and diligent in sacred rites, fabricated the heavens: and 
may these propitiatory (praises), augmenting (thy power) 
and rapid as the wind, reach thee at this solemn rite.
