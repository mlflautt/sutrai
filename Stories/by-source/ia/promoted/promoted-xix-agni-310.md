# Xix Agni 310

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16567-16582

XIX.  Agni. 

To  give  eternal  glow,  we  pray  Vaisvanara  the  holy  one, 
Lord  of  the  light  of  sacrifice. 

2  Who,  furthering  the  light  of  Law,  hath  spread  himself 

to  meet  this  work  : 
He  sends  the  seasons,  mighty  one. 

3  Love of  what  is  and  what  shall  be,  Agni,  in  his  be- 

loved forms, 

Shines  forth  alone  as  sovran  Lord.
