# 6 Eddu D Hddu The Monkey With Long Black 36

**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)
**Lines:** 3888-3903

6. Eddu-dé (Hddu, the monkey with long black 
hair), Monkey Clan. 

6 is sometimes used instead of dé. It equally 
means “ people, clan, or tribe.” 

The usual reverence is paid by the members of a 
clan to the animal or plant from which the clan takes 
itsname. It may not be used as food, or molested in 
any way; but must always be treated with veneration 
and respect. The general notion is that the members 
of the clan are directly descended from the animal, or 
plant, eponymous.
