# 7 On Their Approach The Earth Becomes Capable Of 1490

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11425-11430

7. On their approach the earth becomes capable of 
fertility, and they deposit in her water as their germ, 
as the husband generates the embryo of the child : they 
have harnessed their horses fleet as the wind : the sons- 
of Rudra have emitted their perspiration, (the rain).
