# Vi Aeni 56

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7850-7865

VI.  Aeni. 
AGNI  we  choose as  envoy, skilled  performer  of  this 

holy  rite, 
Hotar,  possessor  of  all  wealth. 

2  With  constant  calls  they  invocate  Agni,  Agni,  Lord 

of  the  house, 
Oblation-bearer,  much-beloved. 

3  Bring  the Gods hither, Agni,  born for  him who 

trims  the  sacred  grass  ; 
Thou  art  our  Hotar,  meet  for  praise  !
