# 10 Immortality The Immortality That Is Defined 2055

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17036-17042

10. Immortality. —The immortality that is defined 
as the unbroken succession of descendants, amritatwam 
santatyavichchhedalakshanam: another text to the same 
effect is cited, prajam anu prajayate tad u te martyamritam, 
when progeny is born after progeny, that verily is the immor¬ 
tality of thy mortality.
