# 4 They Favour The Zealous Worshipper Ritam 2220

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18729-18738

4. They Favour the Zealous Worshipper. —Ritam 
ritena sapanta ishiram dakskam ashate is explained udakena 
nimittena yajnam sprishantau eshanavantam pravriddham 
yajamanam hctvir va vyapnutah , they two, for the sake of water, 
touching or affecting the sacrifice, they pervade the inquiring, 
powerful institutor of the rite, they reward him : or it may 
be, they pervade or accept the efficacious, adequate oblation.
