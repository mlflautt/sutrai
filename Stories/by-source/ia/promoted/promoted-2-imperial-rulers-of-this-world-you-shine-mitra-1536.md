# 2 Imperial Rulers Of This World You Shine Mitra 1536

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11751-11755

2. Imperial rulers of this world, you shine, Mitra 
and Varuna, at this sacrifice, the beholders of heaven : 
we ask of you the wealth (that is) rain, and immortality, 
for your forms traverse earth and heaven.
