# 8 Far Seeing Padavih Kavitama Most Wise Or Far 1741

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13494-13510

8. Far-seeing. — Padavih , kavitama , most wise or far- 
seeing as to the past, from pada , an object, and vih, who 
knows. 

May He be Free from all Reproach --Munchannira- 
mdyat , always, nitaram , be free, munchatu —from such a 
fault as that of the nature of the death of Vritra : Vritra- 
mdharupad-doshat; but this is a Pauranik notion, Vritra, 
according to the Puranas, being a Brahman, and by killing 
him Indra was guilty of the heinous sin of Brahmahatya .
