# V 2 18 1164

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9201-9208

V. 2. 18. 

The deity as before, but in the sixth stanza Indra is associated 
with Aon i; the Rishis are three Raja* , Tryaruna, the son of Tri- 
vrishna, Trasadasyu, the son of Purukutsa, and Ashwamedha, 
the son of Bharata ; or Atri alone may be the Rishi : the metre 
of the three first stanzas is Trishtubh , of the last three Anushtubh .
