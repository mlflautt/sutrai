# 5 Warriors Well Mounted Ardent For Contest Invoke 895

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7244-7251

5. Warriors well mounted, ardent for contest, invoke 

tipi Rig-veda Translation [IV. 4.11 

me: selected (combatants invoke) me in battle: I, the 
affluent Indra, instigate the conflict, and, endowed with 
'victorious prowess, I raise up the dust (in the battle).
