# 3 As The People Of The Earth Etc Milhushmativa 2200

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18510-18517

3. As the People of the Earth, etc. — Milhushmativa 
prithivi parahata is explained prabalaswamika prithivi parair- 
anyair abhibhuta , the earth having a powerful master when 
oppressed by others : earth, says Say ana, is put for its 
inhabitants, praja , people or subjects, who, when oppressed, 
have recourse to their own ruler, sa yatha swaswaminam 
upadruta abhigachchhati.
