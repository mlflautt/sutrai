# 3 The Ribhus The Performers Of Good Works The 476

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4112-4118

3. The Ribhus, the performers of (good) works, the 
grandsons of a man, have attained the friendship of Indra : 
they have perpetuated (existence): the sons of Sudhan¬ 
wan have attained immortality: performers of pious acts 
influencing (the result), through their devotion (they have 
attained divinity) by their works.
