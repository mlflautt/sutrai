# V 1 6 1053

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8374-8385

V. 1. 6] 

Fifth Mandala 

181 

4. (Grass), soft as wool, be spread; the worshippers, 
praise thee : be to us radiant (grass the source of) liberality. 

Open divine doors, our passages to preservation; 
fill full the sacrifice (with its rewards).
