# 4 The Great Indra The Victorious In Battle The Def 267

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2606-2617

4. The great Indra, the victorious in battle, the defier 
of foes: his fierce strength and resolute vigour are exerted: 
verily the earth does not contain him, neither (does the 
heaven), when the Soma libations exhilarate the lord of 
the tawny steeds. 

44 

Rjg-veda Translation 

\m. 3.7
