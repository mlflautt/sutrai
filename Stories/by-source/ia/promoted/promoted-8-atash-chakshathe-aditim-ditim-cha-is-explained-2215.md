# 8 Atash Chakshathe Aditim Ditim Cha Is Explained 2215

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18672-18689

8. Atash-chakshathe aditim ditim cha, is explained,, 
aditim, akhandaniyam bhumimi, the indivisible earth, and 

422 

Rig-veda Translation 

ditim f khandaniyam prajadikam , the divisible people and the 
rest: the stanza occurs in the Yajush , 10. 15, with a various 
reading of the first half; but Mahidhara explains aditim by 
adinam, not base, that is, vihitanushthataram, one who follows 
what is prescribed, that is, performs his religious duties, and 
ditim by dinam, base, or nastikavrittam , an infidel; the phrase 
meaning that Mitra and Varum observe that this man is a 
sinner, that man righteous, ay am papi. ararn punyamn.
