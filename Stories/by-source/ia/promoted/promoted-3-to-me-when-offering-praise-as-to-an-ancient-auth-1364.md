# 3 To Me When Offering Praise As To An Ancient Auth 1364

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10540-10545

3. To me, when offering praise, as to an ancient author 
of sacred songs, the burthen of the cloud (descends); the 
cloud parts (with its burthen) ; the sky performs (its 
office): the assiduously worshipping Angirasas are 
exhausted by much (adoration).
