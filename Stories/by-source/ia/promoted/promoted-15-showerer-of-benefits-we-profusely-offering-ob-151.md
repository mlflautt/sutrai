# 15 Showerer Of Benefits We Profusely Offering Ob 151

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1782-1791

15. Showerer (of benefits) we profusely offering (ob¬ 
lations) kindle thee, mighty and resplendent Agni, who 
art the sender of showers. 

Ill. 2. 16. 

The deity and Rishi are as before ; the metre of the third stanza 
is Ushnih ; of the fourth Trishtubh; of the fifth Jagati ; of the rest 
Oayatri.
