# 4 They Who Encouraged Thee Mag Ha Van To Slay Ahi 337

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3164-3169

4. They who encouraged thee, Mag ha van, to slay Ahi,. 
who (aided thee) in the conflict with Shambara, and in 
the recovery of the cattle, and who, possessed of wisdom,, 
contribute verily to thy exhilaration, with them, the troop 
of the Maruts, do thou Indra, drink the Soma .
