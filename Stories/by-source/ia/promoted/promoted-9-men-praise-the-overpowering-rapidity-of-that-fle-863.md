# 9 Men Praise The Overpowering Rapidity Of That Fle 863

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7044-7048

9. Men praise the overpowering rapidity of that fleet 
(steed), who is the accomplisher (of the desires) of man¬ 
kind, and, following him to battle, they have said, Dadhikra 
with (his) thousands has gone forth against the foe.
