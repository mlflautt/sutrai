# 1 Observer Of Truth Anushatya From Anu And 1715

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13177-13183

1. Observer of Truth. Anushatya, from anu and 
satya : in this and similar compounds which are of frequent 
recurrence, the notion, at least according to the Scholiast, is 
not that of observing truth in the abstract, but of keeping 
faith with the sacrifices by giving him the desired recompense 
to winch he is justly entitled.
