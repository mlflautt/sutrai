# 2 With Thee Soma For His Ally Indra Has Quickly 780

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6326-6331

2. With thee, Soma, for his ally, Indra has quickly 
taken off by force the wheel of the chariot of the sun, 
abiding above with the vast and stationary (firmament): 
the everywhere-going wheel (of the car) of the great 
oppressor has been taken aw T ay.
