# Xi Agni 258

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15166-15186

XI.  Agni. 

O  AGNI  thou  who  art  the Lord  of  wealth  in  kine, 

thou  Son  of  Strength, 
Bestow  on  us,  O  Jatavedas,  high  renown! 

2  He,  Agni,  kindled,  good  and  wise,  must  be  entreated 

with  a  song  : 

Shine,  thou  of  many  forms,  shine  thou  with  wealth 
on  us  ! 

3  And,  Agni,  shining  of  thyself  by  night  and  when  the 

morning  breaks, 

Burn,  thou whose teeth are sharp, against the 
Rakshasas !
