# 13 Inasmuch As Our Devout Praise Has Appointed A 204

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2167-2177

13. Inasmuch as our devout praise has appointed a 
thriving ruler for the regulation of heaven and earth, to 
whom faultless and appropriate commendations (are due), 
therefore are all the energies ot Indra spontaneously 
exerted. 

14.1 wish, Indra, for thy friendship, and (the exercise 
of) thy powers: many horses come to the slayer of Vritra : 
we offer to thee, sage, great praise and oblations : consider 
thyself, Maghavan as our protector.
