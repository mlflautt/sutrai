# 17 Darbhya The Patronymic Is Also Read In Some 2209

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18608-18618

17. Darbhya.— The patronymic, is also read in some 
places Dalbhya. 

Oh Night.- -Upon concluding the praises of the Maruts. 
and thus having attained the rank of a Rishi, Shyavashwa. 
summons the night, here called Urmya , to convey the intelli¬ 
gence to Rathaviti, who gives him his daughter, with many 
valuable presents, but at the end of the ceremony the Rishi 
departed for the woods to resume his austerities : it is not 
said if he took his wife vith him. but it is to be so inferred.
