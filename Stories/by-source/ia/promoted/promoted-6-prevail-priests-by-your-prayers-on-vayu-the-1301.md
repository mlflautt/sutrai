# 6 Prevail Priests By Your Prayers On Vayu The 1301

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10085-10090

6. Prevail, (priests), by your prayers, on Vayu, the 
divine, the fulfiller of desires, the adorable, to harness, 
his chariot: may the light-moving accepters of sacrifice, 
the lovely and excellent wives (of the gods), come hither 
to our rite.
