# Xv Indra 338

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17508-17524

XV.  Indra. 

RICH  be  the  praiser  of  one  rich  and  liberal, Lord of 

bays  !  like  thee  : 
High  rank  be  his  who  lauds  the  juice  ! 

2  His  wealth  who  hath no  store of  kine hath ne'er 

found  out  recited  laud, 
Nor  song  of  praises  that  is  sung. 

3  Give  ue  not,  Indra,  as  a  prey  unto the  scornful or 

the  proud  : 
Help,  mighty  one,  with  power  and  might !
