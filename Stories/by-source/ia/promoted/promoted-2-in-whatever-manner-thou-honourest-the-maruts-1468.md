# 2 In Whatever Manner Thou Honourest The Maruts 1468

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11278-11289

2. In whatever manner thou honourest the (Maruts 
Agni), in thy heart, may they come to me as benefactors : 

Rig-vkda Translation 

250 

LV.4.12 

gratify, (by oblations), those fierce-looking Maruts, who 
most promptly come to thy invocations.
