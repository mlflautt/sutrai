# 5 May Ample Wealth Devolve Upon Those Who Have 1396

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10792-10797

5. May ample wealth devolve upon those who have 
presented victims to the Vasus, and upon those who 
have repeated praises to Mitra and Varuna : confer upon 
them, (gods), felicity, and may we rejoice in the protec¬ 
tion of heaven and earth.
