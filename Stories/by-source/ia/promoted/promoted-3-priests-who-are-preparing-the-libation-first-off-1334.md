# 3 Priests Who Are Preparing The Libation First Off 1334

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10305-10310

3. Priests, who are preparing (the libation), first offer 
to Vayu the delightful and brilliant (So?na) ; and do thou, 
divine Vayu, like the Hotri, first drink of this sweet juice, 

which) we offer for thine exhilaration.
