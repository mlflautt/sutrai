# 5 He Is Hansa The Sun Dwelling In Light Vasu 877

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7131-7140

5. He is Hansa, (the sun), dwelling in light; Vasu, 
((the wind), dwelling in the firmament; the invoker of the 
gods (Agni), dwelling on the altar; the guest (of the 
worshipper), dwelling in the house (as the culinary fire) ; 
the dweller amongst men, (as consciousness), the dweller 
in the most excellent (orb, the sun), the dweller in truth, 
the dweller in the sky (the air), born in the waters, in the 
rays of light, in the verity (of manifestation) in the (eastern) 
mountain, the truth (itself).
