# Xix Soma Pavamana 183

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12931-12943

XIX.  Soma  PavamAna, 

FLOW  onward,  Soma,  flow  for  mighty  strength, as  a 
strong  courser,  bathed,  to  win  the prize. 

2  The  pressers  clarify this  juice  of  thine, the  Soma 

for  delight  and  lofty  fame. 

3  They deck  the gold-hued  infant,  newly-born,  even 

Soma,  Indu,  in  the  sieve, for  Gods.
