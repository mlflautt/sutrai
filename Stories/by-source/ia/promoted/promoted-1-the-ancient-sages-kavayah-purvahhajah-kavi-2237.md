# 1 The Ancient Sages Kavayah Purvahhajah Kavi 2237

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18906-18912

1. The Ancient Sages. — Kavayah purvahhajah : kavi 
is said to mean one learned in the Vedas, on the authority of 
the Aitareya Brahmana, ye va anuchanas-te kavayah, those 
who are anuchanas are hams, and anuchana is explained in 
the commentary on the same work, shadangasahita vedadhya- 
yinali , students of the Vedas with the six supplements.
