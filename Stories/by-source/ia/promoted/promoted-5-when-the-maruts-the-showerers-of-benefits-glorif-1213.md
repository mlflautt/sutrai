# 5 When The Maruts The Showerers Of Benefits Glorif 1213

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9507-9512

5. When the Maruts , the showerers (of benefits), glorify 
thee, Indra, the showerer (of desires), with praises, and 
the exulting stones delight (to bruise the Soma), then, 
without horses, without chariots, they, the purifying 
(Maruts), dispatched by Indra, have overcome the Dasyus .
