# 6 The Seven Elements Saptadhatu The Scholiast 1867

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14992-15003

6. The Seven Elements.— Saptadhatu , the Scholiast 
says, means seven sorts of animals, agreeably to the text, 

22 

338 Rig-veda Translation 

Sapta gramyah pashavah saptaranyah , seven tame, seven wild 
animals. 

Page 97.
