# 2 Hundreds Op Suvarnas The Text Has Only 2098

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17514-17525

2. Hundreds op SuvARNAS.—The text has only 
shatani , the hundreds : the Scholiast adds suvarnanam, of 
suvarnas : it is not impossible, however, that pieces of money 
are intended ; for if w T e may trust Arrian, the Hindus had 
coined money before Alexander: the people of Sambas, 
Raja of Sindomana, present him with told money. 

Triple-mixed Soma. —Mixed with curds, milk, and 
parched grain.
