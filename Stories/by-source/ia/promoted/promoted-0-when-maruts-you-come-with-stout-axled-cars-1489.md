# 0 When Maruts You Come With Stout Axled Cars 1489

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11420-11424

0. When, Maruts, you come with stout-axled cars 
drawn by spotted steeds, then the waters descend, the 
forests are damaged, and the bright showerer (of the rain), 
influenced by the solar rays, may emit a downward sound.
