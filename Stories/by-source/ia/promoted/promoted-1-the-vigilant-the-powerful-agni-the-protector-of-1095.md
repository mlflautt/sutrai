# 1 The Vigilant The Powerful Agni The Protector Of 1095

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8664-8675

1. The vigilant, the powerful Agni, the protector of 
man, has been engendered for the present prosperity (of 
the world): fed with butter, (blazing) with intense" 
(radiance) reaching to the sky, the pure Agni shines- 
brilliantly for the Bharatas.
