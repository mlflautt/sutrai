# 2 The Priests Have First Kindled In Three Places A 1096

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8676-8681

2. The priests have first kindled, in three places, Aqni, 
the banner of sacrifice, the family priest, (riding) in the 
same car with In dr a and the gods : he, the performer of 
pious acts, the invoker (of the gods), has sat down on the 
sacred grass for the (celebration of the) rite.
