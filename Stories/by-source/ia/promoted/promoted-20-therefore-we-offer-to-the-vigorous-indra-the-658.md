# 20 Therefore We Offer To The Vigorous Indra The 658

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5465-5470

20. Therefore we offer to the vigorous Indra, the 
showerer (of benefits), holy adoration, that he may never 
withdraw his friendly (actions) from us, and that he may 
be our powerful protector, the defender of (our) persons, 
as the Bhrigus (fabricate) a car (for use).
