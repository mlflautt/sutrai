# 21 Indra The Slayer Of Vritra The Lord Of Herds Ha 211

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2218-2223

21. Indra, the slayer of Vritra, the lord of herds, has 
discovered the cattle, and by his radiant effulgence driven 
aw r ay the black (A.sums), and indicating with veracity 
(to the Angirasas) the honest (kine), he shut the gate upon 
all their own cattle.
