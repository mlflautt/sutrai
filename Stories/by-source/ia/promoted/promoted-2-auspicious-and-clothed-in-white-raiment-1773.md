# 2 Auspicious And Clothed In White Raiment 1773

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13823-13831

2. Auspicious and Clothed in White Raiment.— 
Bhadra vastrani arjuna vasana-vach: speech auspicious, 
wearing white garments, that is, tejansi , according to the 
Scholiast, splendours, energies. 

Our Ancient and Paternal Hymn. —Pitrya dhih, 
pitrikramagata stuti , praise come in the succession of proge¬ 
nitors.
