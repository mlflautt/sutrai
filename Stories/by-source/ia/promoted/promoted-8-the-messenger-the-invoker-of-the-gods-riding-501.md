# 8 The Messenger The Invoker Of The Gods Riding 501

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4349-4354

8. The messenger, the invoker (of the gods), riding 
in a golden chariot with a tongue of flame, he frequents 
all the chambers (of sacrifice) ; drawn by red horses, em¬ 
bodied, resplendent, always agreeable, as a dwelling well 
supplied with food.
