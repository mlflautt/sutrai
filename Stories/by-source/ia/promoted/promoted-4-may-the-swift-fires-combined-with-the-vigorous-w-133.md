# 4 May The Swift Fires Combined With The Vigorous W 133

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1672-1676

4. May the swift fires combined with the vigorous wfinds 
proceed to the pure (region of the water), uniting the drops 
{of rain): the Maruts irresistible, all-knowing, agitate 
the copious-shedding clouds.
