# 6 The Adorable Agni The Offerer Of The Oblation Ha 1010

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8090-8095

6. The adorable Agni, the offerer of the oblation, has 
sat down in a fragrant place on the top of his mother 
(earth): youthful, wise, many-stationed, the celebrator 
of sacrifice, the sustainer (of all), kindled (he abides) 
amongst men.
