# 20 The Spiritual Good Works Are Attached A A 161

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4494-4503

20. The spiritual good works are attached (a&a- 
yfikhtd) to the soul, and in the degree and pro- 
portion which are their strength, due to the 
advancement of good works by him who is right- 
eous, tJiey are suitable as enjoyment for him who is 
righteous. 21. He obtains durability thereby ^ and 
necessarily preparation, conjointly with constant 
pleasure and without a single day’s vexation (ay6ra- 
a^-b^shih^).
