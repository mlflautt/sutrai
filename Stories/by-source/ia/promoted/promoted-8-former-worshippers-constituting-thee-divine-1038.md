# 8 Former Worshippers Constituting Thee Divine 1038

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8258-8269

8. Former (worshippers) constituting thee, divine 
(Agni), the messenger (of the gods), have worshipped thee 
with oblations at the dawn of day ; when thou proceedest 
to the place (of the aggregation) of the wealth (of sacri¬ 
fice), bright-shining, kindled by the mortals who have 
given thee an abode. 

&. Convey him (to safety) in war, son of strength, who 
like a wise son offers to thee (reverence) as to a father: 
when, sagacious Agni, dost thou look upon us; when, 
director of sacrifice, dost thou direct us (to good ways).
