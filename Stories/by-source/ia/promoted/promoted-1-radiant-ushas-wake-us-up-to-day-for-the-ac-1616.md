# 1 Radiant Ushas Wake Us Up To Day For The Ac 1616

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12349-12354

1. Radiant Ushas, wake us up to-day for (the ac¬ 
quisition of) ample riches, in like manner as thou hast 
awakened us (of old): bright-born, and praised sincerely 
for (the gift of) horses, shew favour to Satyashravas, the 
son of Yayya.
