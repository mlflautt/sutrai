# V 2 13 Iryaruna Etc Of These Princes We Have 2097

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17495-17513

V. 2. 13. 'Iryaruna, ETC.- -Of these princes we have 
yet met only with the second, vol. I, I. 16. 7. 14 : in the 
Vishnu Parana a Trayyaruna occurs, (p. 371), but he is the 
son of Tridhanwan, and the seventh in descent from 
Trasadasyu , with whom, therefore, he could not be contem¬ 
porary ; so that either the Veda or the Parana is wrong : 
the latter enumerates a Trayyaruna among the Vyasas, 
p. 273 : no other authority gives Bharata a son named Ashvxi- 
medha. 

Atri alone may be the Kishi. —For in fact the Rajas 
are rather the Devatas , they do not commemorate their own 
donations : it is Atri. or some member of his family, who 
speaks. 

-396
