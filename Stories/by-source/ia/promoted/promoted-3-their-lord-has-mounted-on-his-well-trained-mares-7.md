# 3 Their Lord Has Mounted On His Well Trained Mares 7

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 828-832

3. Their lord has mounted on his well-trained mares ; 
the sagacious selector of treasures, the blackbacked, many- 
limbed (Agni) has granted them a place of rest for the 
cherishing of (their) rapid speed.
