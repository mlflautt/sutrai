# 15 The Kushikas The First Born Of Brahma Armed 169

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1913-1918

15. The Kushikas , the first-born of Brahma, armed 
against enemies like the energies of the Maruts, comprehend 
the universe : they have uttered the prayer accompanied 
by the oblation : they have one by one lighted Agni in 
this dwelling.
