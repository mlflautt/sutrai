# Chapter Iii 232

**Source:** The Text Is Fly (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8018-8025

Chapter III. 

I . And as to that which is asked (2) thus : ‘ Why 
does not the creator AHh.zrmasd keep Aharman back 
from evil doing and evil seeking, when he is the 
mighty maker? 3. As I assert that no mighty 
maker is afterwards imperfect nor yet unresisting.’
