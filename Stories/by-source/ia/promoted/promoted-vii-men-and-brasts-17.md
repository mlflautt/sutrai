# Vii Men And Brasts 17

**Source:** A Man Mead (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8576-8584

VII. MEN AND BRASTS. 
14,—xInp, BEABT AND FISH ROOT, OR “‘ ONE GOOD TURN DESERVES ANOTHER” ROOT. 
A man is asked to aid an animal to escape from confinement, to recover 

from sickness &c. 
He aids it with good humour. 
The man falls into trouble, 
The animal aids him in the nick of time.
