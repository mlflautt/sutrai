# 4 May The Divine Accepter Of Sacrifice Of Whom The 1298

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10064-10070

4. May the divine accepter of sacrifice, of whom the 
Kanwas are the priests, Trita, Vayu, and Agni, concur¬ 
ring in satisfaction with (the ruler of) heaven, or (Surya 
.and) Pushan, and Bhaga, and they who are the protectors 
-of the universe, (come quickly) to the sacrifice, as the 
fleetest coursers rush to battle.
