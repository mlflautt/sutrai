# 16 May We Who Are Wishing For Cattle For Horses 673

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5568-5573

16. May we (who are) wishing for cattle, for horses, 
for food, for wives, through his friendship induce Indra, 
the showerer (of benefits), the giver of wives, the un¬ 
wearied granter of protection, to come down, as a bucket 
(is lowered) into a well.
