# 7 Powerful Agni Bestow Upon Us The Institutors Of 1086

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8611-8620

7. Powerful Agni, bestow upon us the institutors (of 
pious rites), that wealth (which we desire): may he 
discomfit (our foes): may he cherish us: may he be 
ever ready to bestow upon us food: and do thou, Agni, 
be present in battles for our success. 

y. i. io. 

The deity, Rishi, and metre as before.
