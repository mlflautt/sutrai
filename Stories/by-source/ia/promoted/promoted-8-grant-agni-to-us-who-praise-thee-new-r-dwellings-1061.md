# 8 Grant Agni To Us Who Praise Thee New R Dwellings 1061

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8453-8457

8. Grant, Agni, to us who praise thee, new r dwellings, 
and (abundant) food: may we be those who worship thee, 
having thee for a messenger (to the gods) in every house: 
bring, Agni, food to thine adorers.
