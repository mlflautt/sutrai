# 15 Like Two Distinguishing Impressions They Day 436

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3821-3826

15. Like two (distinguishing) impressions, they (day 
and night) are placed visible in the midst (between heaven 
and earth), one hidden, one manifest: the path (of both)* 
is common, and that is universal (for good and evil): 
great and unequalled is the might of the gods.
