# 2 When The Ribhus By Honouring Their Parents With 809

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6674-6679

2. When the Ribhus, by honouring their parents with 
renovated (youth), and by other works, had achieved 
enough, they thereupon proceeded to the society of the* 
gods, and, considerate, they bring nourishment to the 
devout (worshipper).
