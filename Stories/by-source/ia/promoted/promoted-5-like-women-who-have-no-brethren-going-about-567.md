# 5 Like Women Who Have No Brethren Going About 567

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4803-4808

5. Like women who have no brethren, going (about 
from their own to their father’s house), women adverse 
to their lords going astray, so the wicked, false (in thought), 
false (in speech), they give birth to this deep abyss (of 
hell).
