# 11 Ascend To Day Radiant Agni Thy Resplendent 1014

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8118-8122

11. Ascend to-day, radiant Agni, thy resplendent, 
well-conducted chariot, together with the adorable (gods) : 
cognizant of the ways (of worship), bring hither, by the 
vast firmament, the gods to partake of the oblation.
