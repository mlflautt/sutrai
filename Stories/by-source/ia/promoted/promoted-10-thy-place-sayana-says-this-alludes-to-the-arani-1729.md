# 10 Thy Place Sayana Says This Alludes To The Arani 1729

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13346-13351

10. Thy Place.— Sayana says this alludes to the Arani , 
the stick of vetasa or ashwattha , or other trees : Mahidhara , 
Yajur-Veda , in. 14, explains it the Qarhapatya , or household 
fire : he renders the concluding phrase also differently, give 
us increase of riches.
