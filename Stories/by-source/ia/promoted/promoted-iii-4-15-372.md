# Iii 4 15 372

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3421-3427

III. 4. 15. 

Indra and Parvata are the deities, the Rishi is, as before, Vishwa- 
mitra : the metre of th8 tenth and sixteenth verses is Jagati ; of the- 
thirteenth Oayatri ; of the twelfth, twentieth, and twenty-second 
Anushtubh ; of the eighteenth Brihati ; of the twenty-third Trishtubh ..
