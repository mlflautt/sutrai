# 5 Rise Up Agni Chastise Those Who Overpower Us 553

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4703-4707

5. Rise up, Agni, chastise those who overpower us ; 
manifest thy divine energies ; slacken the strong (bow 
strings) of the malignant kings; destroy those (who are 
hostile), whether kindred or unallied.
