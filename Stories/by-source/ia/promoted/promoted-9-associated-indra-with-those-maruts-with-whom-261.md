# 9 Associated Indra With Those Maruts With Whom 261

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2561-2566

9. Associated, Indra, with those Maruts with whom 
thou hast shared in the libation, who encouraged thee 
(in battle), and are thv attendant troop, drink, desirous 
(of the beverage), the Soma juice, along with them, with 
the tongue of Agni.
