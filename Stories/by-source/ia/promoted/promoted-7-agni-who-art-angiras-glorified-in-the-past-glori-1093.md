# 7 Agni Who Art Angiras Glorified In The Past Glori 1093

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8653-8658

7. Agni, who art Angiras, glorified in the past, glori¬ 
fied (at present), invoker (of the gods), bring unto us 
riches (enabling us) to overcome the mighty: give to 
thy praisers (ability) to praise thee, and be (at hand) for 
our success in battles.
