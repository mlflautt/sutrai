# 5 Indra Entered The Confiding Hostile Hosts Like A 247

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2465-2474

5. Indra entered the confiding hostile (hosts) like a 
man distributing many (gifts) to (his) leaders: he has 
animated these dawns for the worshipper, and heightened 
their bright radiance (by his own). 

(>. They celebrate many great and glorious exploits 
•of this mighty Indra: he crushed the strong by his 
strength, and, of overpowering prowess, he overcame the 
Dasyus by delusions.
