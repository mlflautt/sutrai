# 11 Bhamam Te Vishwam Bhuvanant Adhisritam The 2031

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16763-16780

11. Bhamam te vishwam bhuvanant adhisritam : the 
first is explained by Sayana , tejah-sthane, in the place of bright¬ 
ness or heat ; Mahidhara , by vibhutyam , superhuman power ; 
the purport is the identification of all things with Agni, present 
in the ocean as submarine fire : in men, as vaishwanara , 
which is an etymological pun from vishwa , all, and nara a man ; 
in life, ayushi , as the vital principle, or ayus may mean food, 
anna, when the digestive fire is intended ; in the.assembled
