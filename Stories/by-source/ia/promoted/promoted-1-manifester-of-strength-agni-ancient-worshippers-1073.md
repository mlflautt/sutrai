# 1 Manifester Of Strength Agni Ancient Worshippers 1073

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8528-8532

1. Manifester of strength, Agni, ancient worshippers 
have kindled thee of old for their preservation: thee, the 
delighter of many, the adorable, the all-sustaining, the 
lowly-minded, the lord of the house, the excellent.
