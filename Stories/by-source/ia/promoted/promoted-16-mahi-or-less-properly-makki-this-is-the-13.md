# 16 Mahi Or Less Properly Makki This Is The 13

**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)
**Lines:** 446-456

16. Mahi, or, less properly, Makki. This is the 
most north-easterly territory occupied by the Ewe- 
speaking peoples, and the distance to which it extends 
inland is unknown. It les north of Dahomi, and is 
supposed to extend to the west of that kingdom in a 
broad belt to Krikor and Agotine. Little, however, is 
known about it, and it is probable that it is inhabited 
by semi-independent tribes, such as the Aja and the 
Attakpami, who live immediately to the north-west of 
Dahomi.
