# 5 Ancient Chroniclers Puravidah Says Sayana Tell 2239

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18928-18943

5. Ancient chroniclers, puravidah , says Sayana , tell 
this story: the sons of the brothers of Saptavadhri being 
determined (it is not stated why) to prevent his having inter¬ 
course with his wife, shut him up every night in a large basket, 
which they locked and sealed, letting him out in the morning : 
in this dilemma the Rishi prayed to the Ashwins, who came to 
his succour, and enabled him to get out of his cage during the
