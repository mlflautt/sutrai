# 1 Descend To Us Riding In Thy Car For Verily Thine 310

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2985-2989

1. Descend to us riding in thy car, for verily thine is 
this ancient libation: unharness thy beloved and friendly 
(horses) near to the sacred grass, for these offerers of the 
oblation present it to thee.
