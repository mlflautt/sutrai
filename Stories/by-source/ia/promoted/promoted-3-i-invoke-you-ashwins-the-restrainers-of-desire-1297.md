# 3 I Invoke You Ashwins The Restrainers Of Desire 1297

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10059-10063

3. I invoke you, Ashwins, the restrainers (of desire), 
for the acceleration of your chariot with the swiftness of 
the wind : (priests) offer praise and oblations to the celestial 
destroyer of life, to the accomplisher of the sacrifice.
