# 12 Where The Mother And The Daughter Two Productiv 433

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3805-3809

12. Where the mother and the daughter, two productive 
milch kine, unite, they nourish each other: I worship* 
them both in (the firmament), the dwelling of the waters : 
great and unequalled is the might of the gods.
