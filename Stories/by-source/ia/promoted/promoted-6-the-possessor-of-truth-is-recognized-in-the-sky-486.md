# 6 The Possessor Of Truth Is Recognized In The Sky  486

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4183-4194

6. The possessor of truth is recognized in the sky by 
her rays: the possessor of wealth has taken a marvellous 
station in earth and heaven: Agni, soliciting alms of the 
radiant advancing Us has, thou obtainest thy desired 
treasure (of oblations). 

7. 'Che showerer (of rain, the sun), urging on the lawn, 
at the root of the truthful (day) hast pervaded the vast 
heaven and earth: the mighty Ushas, the golden light, 
as it were, of Mitra aud Varuna, diffuses her lustre in 
different directions.
