# 8 Do You Who Are Wise Bestow Upon Us In Requital 845

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6925-6929

8. Do you who are wise, (bestow) upon us, in requital 
of our praises, all enjoyments that are good for man, and 
fabricate for us, Ribhus, riches and food, resplendent,, 
invigorating, overpowering (foes), and most excellent.
