# 5 May The Divine Bhaga Savitri The Lord Of Wealth 1320

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10212-10216

5. May the divine Bhaga, Savitri, the lord of wealth, 
Ansha, Indra (the slayer) of Vritra, (all) the conquerors 
of riches, Ribhukshin, Vaja, and also Purandhi, im¬ 
mortals, hastening (to our sacrifice) preserve us.
