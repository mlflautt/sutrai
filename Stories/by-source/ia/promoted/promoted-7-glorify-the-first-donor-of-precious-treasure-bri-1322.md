# 7 Glorify The First Donor Of Precious Treasure Bri 1322

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10222-10226

7. Glorify the first donor of precious treasure, Brihas- 
pati, the distributer of riches, the bestower of great happi¬ 
ness upon him who recites or chants (his praise), who comes 
laden with ample wealth to his invoker.
