# 9 May Our Repeated Adoration Of You Two Who Are 1592

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12149-12153

9. May our repeated adoration of you two, who are 
desirous of the libation, be productive of felicity : descend¬ 
ing to our presence, and exceeding in wisdom, travel with 
Tapid (steeds), swift as two falcons.
