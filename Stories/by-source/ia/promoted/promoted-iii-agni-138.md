# Iii Agni 138

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11173-11224

III.  Agni. 

AGNI Vaisvanara, born in course of  Order, the 
messenger  of  earth,  the  head  of  heaven, 

The  Sage, the  Sovran, guest  of  men,  our  vessel  n't 
for  their  mouth,  the  Gods  have  generated. 

2  To  thee,  immortal  !  when  to life thou  springest all 
the  Gods  sing  for  joy  as  to  their  infant. 

They  by  thy mental powers were made  immortal, 
Vaisvanara !  when  thou  shonest  from  thy  parents. 

b  Well  knowing :  that  ia,  the  waves  or  streams  which,  through  Hie 
power  of  Soma,  know  the  way  they  should  go.  'The  worshippers 
knowing  its  (virtues  are  rewarded)  with  happinesa.' — Wilaon. 

10  That  chariot-steed  of  thine  :  meaning  the  flowing  Soma itself. 

Kigveda  VI.  7.  1,  4,  2.  Ascribed  to  Bharadvaja.  The  metre  is 
Trishtup.  Stanza  1  is  a  repetition  of  I.  i.  ii.  2.  5. 

2  Were  made  immortal:  Agni  alone,  it  is  said,  was  originally  im- 
mortal, and  the  other  Gods  obtained  immortality  through  him 
Of.  Rigveda  V.  3.  4.

3  Him  have  they  praised,  mid-point  of  sacrifices,  great 

cistern  of  libations,  seat  of  riches. 
Vaisvanara,  canveyer  of  oblations,  ensign  of  worship, 
have  the  Gods  engendered. 

IV".  Mitra-Varuna. 

SING  forth  unto  your  Varuna  and  Mitra  with  a  song 

inspired  : 
They,  mighty  Lords,  are  lofty  law. 

2  Full  springs of fatness, sovran Kings, Mitra and 

Varuna,  the  twain, 
Gods  glorified  among  the  Gods. 

3  So  help  ye  us  to  riches,  great  celestial  and  terrestrial 

wealth ! 
Vast  is  your  sway  among  the  Gods.
