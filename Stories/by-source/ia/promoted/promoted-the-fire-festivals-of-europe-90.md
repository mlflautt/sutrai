In  China  also  the  faith  in  similar  transformations  is 
reflected  in  the  following  tale.  A  certain  man  in  Sung- 
yang  went  into  the  mountains  to  gather  fuel.  Night  fell  and 
he  was  pursued  by  two  tigers,  but  scrambled  up  a  tree  out 
of  their  reach.  Then  said  the  one  tiger  to  the  other  tiger, 
"If  we  can  find  Chu-Tu-shi,  we  are  sure  to  catch  this  man 
up  the  tree."  So  off  went  one  of  them  to  find  Chu-Tu-shi, 
while  the  other  kept  watch  at  the  foot  of  the  tree.  Soon 
after  that  another  tiger,  leaner  and  longer  than  the  other 
two,  appeared  on  the  scene  and  made  a  grab  at  the  man's 
coat.  But  fortunately  the  moon  was  shining,  the  man  saw 
the  paw,  and  with  a  stroke  of  his  axe  cut  off  one  of  its  claws. 
The  tigers  roared  and  fled,  one  after  the  other,  so  the  man
