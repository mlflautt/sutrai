**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))

5. I proclaim this great device of the renowned Varuna, 
the destroyer of the Asuras , who, abiding in the mid¬ 
heaven, has meted the firmament by the sun, as if by a 

measure.
