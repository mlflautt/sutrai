# 5 When Thou Hast Been Born Most Excellent And 1197

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9415-9419

5. When thou hast been born most excellent and 
supreme, bearing a name widely renowned, then the goda 
have been in dread of Indra, and he has subjugated 
all the waters, the brides of the slave (Vritra).
