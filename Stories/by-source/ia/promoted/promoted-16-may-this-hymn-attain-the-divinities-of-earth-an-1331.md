# 16 May This Hymn Attain The Divinities Of Earth An 1331

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10275-10281

16. May this hymn attain (the divinities) of earth and 
heaven, the trees, the herbs, for (the sake of) wealth: 
may each individual deity be successfully invoked by me : 
let not mother earth take us into unfavourable thought. 

226 Rig-veda Translation [V. 3.11
