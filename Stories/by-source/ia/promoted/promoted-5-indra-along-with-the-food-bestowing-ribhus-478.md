# 5 Indra Along With The Food Bestowing Ribhus 478

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4125-4133

5. Indra, along with the food-bestowing Ribhus, 
accept with both hands the (cup of the) effused Soma 
libation: excited Maghavan, by worship, rejoice with 

-80 Rig-veda Translation [IU.5.8 

the human sons of Sudhan*Wan in the dwelling of the 
donor (of the offering).
