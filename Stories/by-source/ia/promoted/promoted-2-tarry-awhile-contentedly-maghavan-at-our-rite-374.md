# 2 Tarry Awhile Contentedly Maghavan At Our Rite 374

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3434-3439

2. Tarry awhile contentedly, Maghavan, (at our rite): 
go not away ; for I offer to thee (the libation) of the copious¬ 
ly-effused Soma : powerful Indra, I lay hold of the skirts 
(of thy robe) with sweet-flavoured commendations, as a 
son (clings to the garment) of a father.
