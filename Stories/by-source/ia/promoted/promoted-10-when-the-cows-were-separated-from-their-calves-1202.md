# 10 When The Cows Were Separated From Their Calves 1202

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9441-9447

10. When the cows were separated from their calves, 
they wandered about hithc r and thither; but when the well- 
offered libations had exhilarated him, then Indra, with 
his vigorous (Maruts), reunited them (with their calves). 

206 Rig-veda Translation [V. 2. 17
