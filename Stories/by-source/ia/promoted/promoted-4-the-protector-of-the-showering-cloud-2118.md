# 4 The Protector Of The Showering Cloud 2118

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17713-17718

4. The Protector of the Showering Cloud.— 
Miho-napatam, meghasya rakshitaram : napat, here meaning 
patri , a protector, a preserver, as in the Brahnana cited by 
the Scholiast, pram vai tanunapat sa hi tanvah pati : vital air 
is called the body-preserver, for it preserves the limbs.
