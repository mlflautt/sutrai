# 33 Nf A La M Db Natsili A Ahazlago Dahitago Naaain 212

**Source:** Collected And Translated (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14571-14578

33. Nf/a'la/M'db natsili/ a^/ahazlago dahitago naAainiya'. 

Your wings on ends of rainbow high hanging high flying come to us. 

34. Kds^i/yi/, mltsabaka, a'dilyi/, niltsabaad bi/ benatsi</asydlgo na^amiya*. 

Cloud dark, rain male, mist dark, rain female with it made of near darkness come to us.
