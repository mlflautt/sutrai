# 6 Indra The Praised Of Many Associated With Ribhu 479

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4134-4139

6. Indra, the praised of many, associated with Ribhu, 
and with Vaja, exult with Shachi, at this our sacrifice: 
these self-revolving (days) are devoted to thee, as well as 
the ceremonies (addressed) to the gods, and the virtuous 
acts of man.
