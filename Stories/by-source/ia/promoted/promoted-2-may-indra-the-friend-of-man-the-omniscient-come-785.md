# 2 May Indra The Friend Of Man The Omniscient Come 785

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6365-6370

2. May Indra, the friend of man, the omniscient, come 
to the sacrifice when invoked by the offerers of libations; 
he who is possessed of good horses, who is fearless,, 
honoured by the effusers of libations, who rejoices with 
the heroes (the Maruts).
