# 2 Mitra And Varuna The Cows Are Full Of Milk Throu 1568

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11974-11978

2. Mitra and Varuna, the cows are full of milk through 
your (command), and the rivers yield through your (will) 
sweet water: through you the three radiant receptacles 
and showerers of rain stand severally in their three spheres.
