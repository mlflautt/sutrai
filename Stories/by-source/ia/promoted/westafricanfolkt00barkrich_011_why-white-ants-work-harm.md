them all except the father. 

White Ant, on reaching home again, in great

VIII. THE SQUIRREL AND THE 

SPIDER 

A HARD-WORKING squirrel had, after much 
labour, succeeded in cultivating a very fine 
» farm. Being a skilful climber of trees, he 
had not troubled to make a roadway into his farm. 
He used to reach it by the trees. 

One day, when his harvests were very nearly ripe, 
it happened that Spider went out hunting in that 
neighbourhood. During his travels, he arrived at 
Squirrel's farm. Greatly pleased at the appearance 
of the fields, he sought for the roadway to it. Finding 
none, he returned home and told his family all about 
the matter. The very next day they all set out for 
this fine place, and set to work immediately to make a 
road. When this was completed Spider — who was 
very cunning — threw pieces of earthenware pot along 
the pathway. This he did to make believe that his 
children had dropped them while working to prepare 
the farm.
