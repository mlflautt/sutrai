# The Whirlwind 51

**Source:** Translated From The Sanskrit Text (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4309-4345

THE WHIRLWIND 

by 
N. S. PHADKE 

WHIRLWIND is not only a story of men 
and women, of villages and cities, it is a story 
of a nation, a country. It is a story of an 
era that will go down as the most outstanding 
in the history of this country. It is a story 
of a heroic struggle that rocked the founda- 
tions of a mighty Empire. The beginning of 
the Twentieth Century ushered in an era of 
awakening, particularly in political sphere, 
which culminated in the great 1942 movement. 
The worn-out and age-old concepts of life 
crumbled down, a new consciousness took birth 
in the minds of people, man began to judge 
himself and the world around him from 
entirely new perspectives, and a strong feeling 
of freedom, the birth-right of. man, galvanized 
the whole nation into action. 

WHIRLWIND is a picture of this great pro- 
cess of change, a Story of men and women 
of this period who brought about a new phase 
of re-birth in our history. 

Prof. Phadke, a noted Marathi author, has 
many books of outstanding merit.to his credit. 
This is the first English translation of 
WHIRLWIND, which he originally wrote in 

Marathi.
