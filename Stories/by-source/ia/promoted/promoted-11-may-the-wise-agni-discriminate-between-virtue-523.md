# 11 May The Wise Agni Discriminate Between Virtue 523

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4507-4513

11. May the wise Agni discriminate between virtue 
and vice, between (virtuous and wicked) men, as a (groom 
distinguishes between) the strong and weak backs (of 
horses): enrich us with wealth accompanied by virtuous 
offspring: be bountiful to the liberal giver; shun him 
who gives not.
