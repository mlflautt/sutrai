# 4 Shakra Does Not Turn Away From Him Whose Father 1249

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9750-9754

4. Shakra does not turn away from him whose father, 
whose mother, whose brother he has slain, but is willing 
to accept his offerings : the regulator (of acts), the bestower 
of riches, does not turn away from iniquity.
