# Xvii Ludra 35

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6974-7011

XVII.  ludra. 

To  Indra,  to  the mighty let these golden-coloured 

juices  go, 
Drops  born  as  Law  prescribes,  that  find  the  light  of 

heaven ! 

2  This  juice  that  gathers  spoil  flows,  pressed,  for  Indra, 

for  his  maintenance. 
Soma  bethinks  him  of  the  conqueror,  as  he  knows. 

3  Yea,  Indra  in the  joys of  this obtains the grasp 

that  gathers  spoil, 
And,  winning  waters,  wields  the  mighty  thunderbolt. 

3 Vritra-slayer  :  or  '  slayer  of  the  foe.' 

Rigveda  IX.  108.  1,  2,  ascribed  to  Gauriviti.  The  metre  is  Kakup 
in  stanza  1,  and  Satobrihati  in  2.  Stanza  1  is  a  repetition  of  I.  vi.  4.  1.
