# 6 We Solicit The Might Of The Maruts The Irradiati 135

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1682-1694

6. We solicit the might of the Maruts , the irradiation 
♦of Agni with solemn prayers in every assembly and 

22 

Rig-veda Translation 

[III. 2. 15* 

company (of worshippers) ; those Maruts who have partly- 
coloured steeds, who are dispensers of unfailing wealth, 
firm (of purpose), and frequenters of sacrifices.
