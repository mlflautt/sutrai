# 2 In The Vienna And Its Sister Codex We Have At Th 14

**Source:** Facsimile Of An (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 596-601

2. In the Vienna and its sister-Codex we have, at the 
present day, two of the finest examples of Mexican pic- 
tography in existence, which conspicuously bear the same 
date (1 Acatl = 1519 Jul. Cal.) and are unquestionably the 
work of the same artist.
