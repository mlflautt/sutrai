# 22 Not Regardless Of Thy Worshippers He Napat 1959

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15896-15902

22. Not regardless of thy Worshippers. — He napat 
na patayitah, stotrin avinashayitah , kintu palayitar , ityarlhah; 
oh thou, not casting down, not injuring or destroying, those 
who praise, that is, cherishing them.
