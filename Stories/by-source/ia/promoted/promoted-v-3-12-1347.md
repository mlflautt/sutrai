# V 3 12 1347

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10402-10415

V. 3. 12. 

The deities are the same; the Rishi is Avatsara of the race of 
Kashyafa ; the metre of the fourteenth and fifteenth verses is Trish- 
tubh f of the rest Jagati. 

1- In like manner as the ancient (sages), those our 
predecessors, as all sages, and those of the present period, 
(have obtained their wishes by his praise), so do thou by 
praise extract (my desires) from him who is the oldest and 
best (of the gods), who is seated on the sacred grass, who 
is cognizant of heaven, coming to our presence, vigorous, 
swift, victorious, for by such praises thou exaltest him.
