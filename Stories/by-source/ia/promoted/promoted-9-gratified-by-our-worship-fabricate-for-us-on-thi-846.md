# 9 Gratified By Our Worship Fabricate For Us On Thi 846

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6930-6934

9. Gratified (by our worship), fabricate for us, on this 
occasion, progeny, and wealth, and reputation, with 
numerous adherents: grant to us, Ribhus, abundant 
sustenance wherewith we may greatly excel others.
