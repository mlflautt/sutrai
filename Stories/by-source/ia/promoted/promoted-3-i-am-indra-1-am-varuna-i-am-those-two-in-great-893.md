# 3 I Am Indra 1 Am Varuna I Am Those Two In Great 893

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7234-7238

3. I am Indra, 1 am Varuna, I am those two in great¬ 
ness: (I am) the vast, profound, beautiful, heaven and. 
earth: intelligent, I give like Twashtri animation to all 
beings: I uphold earth and heaven.
