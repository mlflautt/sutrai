# 3 Since Mighty Indra Those Who Differing From Us 1236

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9670-9675

3. Since, mighty Indra, those who, differing from us, 
and not united with thee through their lack of devotion, 
are not thine ; therefore, divine holder of the thunderbolt, 
who art possessed of excellent horses, ascend the car, whose 
reins thou guidest, (to come to our sacrifice).
