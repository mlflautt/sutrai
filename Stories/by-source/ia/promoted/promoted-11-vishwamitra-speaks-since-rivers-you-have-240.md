# 11 Vishwamitra Speaks Since Rivers You Have 240

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2417-2424

11. Vishwamitra speaks] Since, rivers, (you have 
allowed me to cross), so may the Bharatas pass over (your 
united stream): may the troop desiring to cross the water, 
permitted (by you), and impelled by Indra, pass; then 
let the course appointed for your going (be resumed): I 
have recourse to the favour of you who are worthy of 
adoration.
