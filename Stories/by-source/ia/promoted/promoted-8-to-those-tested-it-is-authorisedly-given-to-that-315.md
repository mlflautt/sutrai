# 8 To Those Tested It Is Authorisedly Given To That 315

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9518-9525

8. To those tested it is authorisedly given to that 
amount through which the experimenting that is 
mentioned has extended ; and to him who it is proved 
will become worse through the drinking of wine, that 
amount, through the drinking of which, when given ® 
in the experiment, it was seen that he became worse, 
is not authorisedly given.
