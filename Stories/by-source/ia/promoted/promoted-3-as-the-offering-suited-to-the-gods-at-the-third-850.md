# 3 As The Offering Suited To The Gods At The Third  850

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6957-6962

3. As the offering suited to the gods at the third (daily) 
sacrifice supports you, Vajas, Ribhukshans ; as the 
praise (then recited supports you): therefore, like Manu, 
I offer you the Soma juice, along with the very radiant 
(deities) among the people assembled at the solemnity.
