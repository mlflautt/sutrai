# 11 Thou Goest With Kutsa In The Same Chariot Deter 649

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5403-5409

11. Thou goest with Kutsa in the same chariot, deter¬ 
mined to defend him ; (thou who art) the tormentor 
(of foes), the lord of horses (of the speed) of the wind: 
on the same day wherein, yoking (to the car) the straight- 
going steeds, as if to receive food, the sage (Kutsa) has 
been enabled to cross over (the sea) of calamity.
