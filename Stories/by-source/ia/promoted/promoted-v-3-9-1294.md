# V 3 9 1294

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10041-10047

V. 3. 9. 

The deities are the Vishwadevas; the Rishi is Bhattma ; the 
metre is Trishtubh, except in tho sixteenth and seventeenth verses, in 
which it is Atijagati , and in the twentieth, in which it is Viraj, and of 
■one hemistich.
