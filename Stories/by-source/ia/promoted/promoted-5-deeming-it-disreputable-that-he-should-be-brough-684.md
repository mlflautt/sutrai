# 5 Deeming It Disreputable That He Should Be Brough 684

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5646-5651

5. Deeming it disreputable (that he should be brought 
forth) in secret, his mother endowed (Indra) with (extra¬ 
ordinary) vigour: therefore, as soon as born he sprung 
up of his own accord, invested with splendour, and filled 
both heaven and earth.
