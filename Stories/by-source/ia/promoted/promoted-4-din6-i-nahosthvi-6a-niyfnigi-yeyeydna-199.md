# 4 Din6 I Nahosthvi 6A Niyfnigi Yeyeydna 199

**Source:** Collected And Translated (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13804-13814

4. Din6 i nahosthVi. 6a' niyfnigi, yeyeydna. 

People are restored. For me he brings, (meaningless.) 

In line 1, stanza I., Naydnezgani is changed to Yeinaezgani, and in line 1, stanza 
IV., Binaye A/fcani is changed to Binaye Tj-agani. Nahostlf^i in the last line of 
each stanza is rendered here "restored," but the more exact meaning is, not that 
the original people are called back to life, but that others are given in place of 
them. This verb is used if a man steals a horse and gives another horse as 
restitution for the one he stole.
