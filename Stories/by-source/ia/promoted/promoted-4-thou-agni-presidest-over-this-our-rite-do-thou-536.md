# 4 Thou Agni Presidest Over This Our Rite Do Thou 536

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4594-4599

4. Thou, Agni, presidest over this our rite: do thou, 
who art cognizant of truth, and the author of good works, 
recognize this our adoration, whenever these exhilarating 
prayers (are addressed) to thee, whenever friendly re- 
Jations with thee are (established) in our dwelling.
