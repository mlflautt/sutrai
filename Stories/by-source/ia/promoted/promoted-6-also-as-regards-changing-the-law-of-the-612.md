# 6 Also As Regards Changing The Law Of The 612

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17162-17168

6. Also, as regards changing the law of the 
fifteen times washing^, just as it is for Irin in which 
purifiers are to be found, it is ordered for places to 
be found without purifiers ; and tt is in the countries 
of Iran that the order is given regarding purifiers 
not thus appointed for the work.
