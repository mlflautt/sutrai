# 27 Intsekawo Natsilt A Ahazlago Dahitago Na Ainiya 211

**Source:** Collected And Translated (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14547-14570

27. IntsekaWo natsilT/ a</ahazlago dahitago na/^ainiya'. 

Your head over rainbow high hanging high flying come to us. 

28. Ni/a'la/M'</o kds^i/yi/ beaUadasyelgo <s?ahi/ago na^amiya'. 

Your wings on ends of cloud dark made of far darkness high flying come to us. 

29. Ni/a'la/M'afo niltsabaka beatya^/asyelgo <^ahi/ago na//ainiya'. 

Your wings on ends of rain male made of far darkness high flying come to us. 

30. Ni/a'la^a'afo a'dilyil beatya^asyelgo dahitago na^ainiya'. 

Your wings on ends of mist dark made of far darkness high flying come to us. 

31. Ni/a'la/M'db niltsabaad beatra^/asyelgo dahitago na^ainiya'. 

Your wings on ends of rain female made of far darkness high flying come to us. 

32. Nlta'lat/ia'do atsinikli'ji hadahati']go dahitago naAafniya'. 

Your wings on ends of zigzag lightning high out flung high flying come to us.
