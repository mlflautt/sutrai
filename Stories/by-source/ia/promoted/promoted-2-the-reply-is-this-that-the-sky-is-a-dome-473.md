# 2 The Reply Is This That The Sky Is A Dome 473

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13067-13080

2. The reply is this, that the sky is a dome 
(gar^/hn), wide and lofty; its inside and whole 
width and boundaries (ikhyaklhcl), besides its 
material existence, are the stone of light, of all 
stones the hardest ^ and most beautiful ; and the 
grandeur of its spirit and even its internal bow ® are 
like those of mighty warriors arrayed. 3. And that 
material of the sky reached unto the place where 
promise-breaking words exist % and was without 
need of preparation ; <25 it is said of places such as 
those — where wisdom is a witness about them — that 
that which is not even itself a place, and its place does 
not yet exist, is without need of any preparing*.
