# 21 The Same As The Last Verse Of The Preceding Suk 1910

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15377-15391

21. The same as the last verse of the preceding Sukta- 

Sukta 8.—According to the legend recited by Sayana , 
the Rishi , Vamadeva , whilst yet in the womb, was reluctant 
to be born in the usual manner, and resolved to come into 
the world through his mother’s side : aware of his purpose, the 
mother prayed to Aditi, who thereupon came, with her son 
Indra , to expostulate with the Rishi : this is the subject of 
the Sukta : the interesting part of this absurd story is its 
accordance with the birth of Shakya, according to the 
Buddhists, who may possibly have borrowed the notion from 
the Veda.
