# 3 The Plants That Desire For The Showerer Indra 453

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3947-3952

3. The plants that desire for the showerer (Indra) 
the power (of sending rain) appreciate, when manifest, 
the embryo (blossom) deposited in him: the kine desirous 
of reward come to the presence of the calf, invested with 
many forms.
