# 4 A Woman Requiring A Husband Though A Com 341

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10019-10025

4. A woman requiring a husband — though a com- 
plete worshipper — or a foreigner, or an infidel, or 
07 ie having sins worthy of death, is unfit for adoption ; 
so also those who are demon-worshippers, she who 
is a concubine (shfisar n^Jman) or courtezan, and 
she who is menstruous are unfit.
