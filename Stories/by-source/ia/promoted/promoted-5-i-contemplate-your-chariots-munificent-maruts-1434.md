# 5 I Contemplate Your Chariots Munificent Maruts 1434

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11047-11055

5. I contemplate your chariots, munificent Maruts, 
with delight, like wandering lights in the rains. 

(>. Leaders (of the rain), munificent givers, they cause 
that which is the treasury (of water) to fall from the sky 
for (the benefit of) the donor (of the offering): they let 
loose the rain cloud, and the shedders of rain spread (every¬ 
where) with (abundant) water.
