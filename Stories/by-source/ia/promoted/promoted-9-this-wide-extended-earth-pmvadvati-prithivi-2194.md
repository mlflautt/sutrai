# 9 This Wide Extended Earth Pmvadvati Prithivi 2194

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18462-18474

9. This Wide-extended Earth.— Pmvadvati prithivi : 
a set of changes is here rung upon the double compound, 
pra-vat , having extent, extension, and vat , again, having or 
possessing, pravadvat , extensive, or it may imply, having 
pre-eminence, as indicated by pm, pree : another meaning is 
also given to it, tat-para, being subject to it, as the earth is 
subject to the Maruts , or they pervade the whole earth, 
kritsnam bhumim vyapnuvanti : so the heaven is pmvadvati , 
spacious, or subject to the pravats , pravadbhyah , the pre-emi¬ 
nent or spreading Maruts , and so on.
