# 9 Thou Hast Fixed Tranquil In Its Position The Vas 179

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1988-1992

9. Thou hast fixed tranquil in its position the vast 
unbounded and wandering earth: the showerer (of bene¬ 
fits) has upheld the heaven and the firmament: let the 
waters engendered bv thee here descend.
