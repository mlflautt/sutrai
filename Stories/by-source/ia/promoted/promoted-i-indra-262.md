# I Indra 262

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15283-15297

I.  Indra. 

MEN  with  their  lauds  are  urging  thee,  Indra,  to  drink 

the  Soma  first. 
The  Ribhus  in  accord  have  lifted  up  their  voice,  and 

Rudras  sung  thee  as  the  first. 
2  Indra,  at  sacrifice,  increased  his manly  strength,  in 

the  wild  rapture  of  this  juice  : 
And  living  men  to-day, even  as  of  old,  sing forth 

their  praises  to  his  majesty.
