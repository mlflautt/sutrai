# 2 The Destruction Op Decaying Trees Jared 2068

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17172-17179

2. The Destruction op Decaying Trees. — Jared - 
visham is, literally, that which is poison to the old : the 
commentator explains it jaratam vrikshanam vyapakam , the 
pervader or consumer of old trees ; or it may mean jirno- 
dakam , that by which water is dried up. 

Page 185.
