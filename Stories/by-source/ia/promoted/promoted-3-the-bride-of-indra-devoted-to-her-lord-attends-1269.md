# 3 The Bride Of Indra Devoted To Her Lord Attends 1269

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9899-9904

3. The bride (of Indra), devoted to her lord, attends 
him (to the rite), who thus brings (with him) his accompany¬ 
ing queen : may his car convey to us ample food; may 
it sound loudly ; may it scatter around many thousands 
(of riches).
