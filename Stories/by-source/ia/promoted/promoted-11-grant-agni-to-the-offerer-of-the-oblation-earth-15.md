# 11 Grant Agni To The Offerer Of The Oblation Earth 15

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 881-886

11. Grant, Agni, to the offerer of the oblation, earth 
the bestower of cattle,* the means of many (pious rites), 
such that it may be perpetual: may there be to us sons 
and grandsons, and may thy good-will ever be productive 
of benefits to us.
