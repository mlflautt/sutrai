# 9 Ribhus Who By Your Assistance Gratified The 827

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6792-6798

9. Ribhus, who by your assistance (gratified) the 
Asiiwins, who (renovated your) parents, who (restored) 
the cow, who fabricated the horses, who made armour 
(for the gods), who separated earth and heaven, and who, 
the all-pervading leaders (of rites), accomplished (acts 
productive of) good results.
