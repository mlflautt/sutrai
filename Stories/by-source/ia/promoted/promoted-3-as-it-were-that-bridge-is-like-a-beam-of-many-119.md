# 3 As It Were That Bridge Is Like A Beam Of Many 119

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3533-3544

3. As it were that bridge is like a beam of many 
sides, of whose edges (po,9td) there are some which 
are broad, and there arc some which are thin and 
sharp; its broad sides (shkthh) are so large that 
its width is twenty-seven reeds (ndi), and its sharp 
sides are so contracted (tang) that in thinness it is 
just like the edge of a razor. 4. And wlmn the 
.souls of the righteous and wicked an-ivti it turns to 
that side which is snitablc io their necessitic^s, through 
the great glory of the creator and the command of 
him who fakes the just account''.
