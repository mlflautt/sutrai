# I The Thirtieth Question Is That Which You Ask 153

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4209-4215

I. The thirtieth question is that which you ask 
thus ; When a soul of the righteous goes on to 
heaven, in what manner does it go; also, who 
receives it, who leads ® it, and who makes it a house- 
hold attendant ® of Ahharma^rdf ? Also, does any one 
of the righteous in heaven come out to meet it, and 
shall any thereof make enquiry of it, or how?
