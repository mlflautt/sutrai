# 11 Verily I Hear Of Thee As Chief Amongst Men The 1231

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9634-9639

11. Verily I hear of thee as chief amongst men, the 
protector of the good, friendly to the five classes of beings, 
the begotten, the renowned ; may my (progeny), represent¬ 
ing (their wishes), and uttering his praises night and day, 
propitiate the glorified Indra.
