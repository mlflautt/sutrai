# 8 Lords Of The Niyiu Steeds Overcomers Of Multitud 1447

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11148-11153

8. Lords of the NiyiU steeds, overcomers of multitudes, 
leaders (of rites), radiant as the Adilya *, are the dispensers 
of water : when sovereign lords, they fill the clouds, and, 
loud sounding, moisten the earth with sw'eet (watery) 
sustenance.
