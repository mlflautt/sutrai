# 6 Ushas The Daughter Of Heaven Tending To The West 1630

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12444-12448

6. Ushas, the daughter of heaven, tending to the west 
puts forth her beauty like a (well-dressed) woman, bestow¬ 
ing precious treasures upon the offerer of adoration : she, 
ever youthful, brings back the light as (she has done) of old.
