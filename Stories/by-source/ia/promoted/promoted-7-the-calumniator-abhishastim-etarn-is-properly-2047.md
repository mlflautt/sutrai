# 7 The Calumniator Abhishastim Etarn Is Properly 2047

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16969-16976

7. The Calumniator- Abhishastim etarn, is, properly 
this calumny, but as the antecedent of yah , he who, in the 
masculine, the relative requires the sense of abhishastri, 
accuser or calumniator. 

In these Two Ways.- -Offence, agas or aparadha , and 
sin or wretchedness, enas, or papa.
