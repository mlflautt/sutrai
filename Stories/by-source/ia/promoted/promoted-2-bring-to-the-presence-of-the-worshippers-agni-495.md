# 2 Bring To The Presence Of The Worshippers Agni 495

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4305-4310

2. Bring to the presence of the worshippers, Agni, 
thy brother Varuna, as a participator of the sacrifice, 
with a willing mind, the elder participator of the sacrifice ; 
the ruler of the water, the Aditya , the supporter of men, 
the sovereign venerated by mankind.
