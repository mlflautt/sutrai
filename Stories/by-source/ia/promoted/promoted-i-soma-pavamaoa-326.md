# I Soma Pavamaoa 326

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17160-17176

I.  Soma  Pavamaoa, 

FORWARD  have  flowed  the  streams  of  power, of  thia 

the  mighty  one  effused, 
Of  him  who  waits  upon  the  Gods. 

2  The  singers  praise  him  with  their  song, and  learned 

priests  adorn  the  steed 
Born  as  the  light  that  merits  laud. 

3  These  things  tbou  winnest  quickly,  while  men  cleanse 

thee,  Soma,  nobly  rich  ! 
O  meet  for  praise,  fill  full  the  sea  !
