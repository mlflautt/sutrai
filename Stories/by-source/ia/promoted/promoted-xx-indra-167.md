# Xx Indra 167

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12175-12215

XX.  Indra. 

RENDER  of  forts,  the young, the wise, of  strength 

unmeasured,  was he born, 
Sustainer  of  each  sacred  rite,  Indra,  the Thtinderer, 

much-extolled. 

2  Who  merits  praise  :  prasansyam  :  the Rigveda has  prachetasam  : 
1  foreseeing,'  '  wise.' From  the  olden  time:  dvitd:  or  in  twofold wise; 
in  he.tven  aud  on  earth. 

3  And of  thyself  preserve their seed:  or,  And  guard  our  offspring 
aud  ourselves, 

Rigveda VIII. 87. 4—6. The  Rishi  is  Nriaedha. The  metre ia 
Ushnih. Stanza  1  is  a  repetition  of  I.  v.  i.  1.  3. 

3 Who  upholdeth :  dhartd  :  the Rigrveda, has dartd  : 'the breaker' 
or  '  stormer'  of  all  strongholds  of  the  foe. 

Rigveda  I.  11.  4,  5,  8. The  Rishi  is  Jetar. The  metre  is  Auuihtup 
Stanza  1  is  a  repetition  of  I.  iv.  ii.  2.  8. 

224  THEHYUXSOF  [PART  77. 

2  Thou  wi elder  of. -the  stone,  didst burst the cave of 

Vala  rich  in  kine. 

The  Gods  came  pressing  to  thy  side,  and free from 
terror  aided  thee. 

3  They glorified with hymns of praise Indra  who 

reigneth by his might, 

Whose  bounteous  gifts  in  thousands  come,  yea,  even 
more  abundantly.
