# 4 The Maruts Radiant With Light Purifiers Of The R 1479

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11352-11356

4. The Maruts, radiant with light, purifiers of the rain, 
like twins of goodly aspect and graceful form, masters- 
of tawny and of ruddy steeds, devoid of guile, thinners. 
(of foes), and vast in magnitude as the sky.
