# 5 The Repository Of Sweetness Madhudha Rather 1837

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14592-14601

5. The Repository of Sweetness.— Madhudha rather 
puzzles the Scholiast: madhu may mean, he sa^s, sweet 
words of |)raise, madhurcini stuiilakshanani vakyani , or simply 
praise, stoma ; or, without referring to its etymology, it may 
be a name of Ushas: dha is that which has or holds. 

Lights the Regions. —As in S. 19, v. L of the first 

Ashtaka, vol. 1, p. 72.
