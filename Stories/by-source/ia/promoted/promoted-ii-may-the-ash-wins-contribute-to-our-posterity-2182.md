# Ii May The Ash Wins Contribute To Our Posterity 2182

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18352-18357

II. May the Ash wins Contribute to Our Posterity. 
—The term in this and the following similar passages is always 
swasti-swasti no mimitam ashwina, interpreted avinasham 
kshemam, imperishable prosperity, or, literally, well-being, 
welfare.
