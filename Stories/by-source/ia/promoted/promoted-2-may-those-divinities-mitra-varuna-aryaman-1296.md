# 2 May Those Divinities Mitra Varuna Aryaman 1296

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10054-10058

2. May those divinities, Mitra, Varuna, Aryaman. 
Ayu, Indra, Ribhukshin, the Maruts who accept pious 
praise, be pleased by our adorations, partaking of the 
gratification afforded to Rudra, the showerer (of benefits).
