# 2 We Invoke Thee For Our Own Protection And For Th 131

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1661-1666

2. We invoke thee for our own protection and for the 
devotions of mankind, the radiant Agni, Vaishwanara, 
the illuminator of the firmament, the adorable lord of 
sacred rites, the wise, the hearer (of supplications), the 
.guest (of man), the quick-moving.
