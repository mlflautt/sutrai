# 1 I Have Recourse In This Sacrifice To Agni The In 92

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1411-1416

1. I have recourse in this sacrifice to Agni, the invoker 
and praiser (of the gods), the intelligent, the all-knowing, 
the unbeguiled ; may he, the adorable, sacrifice for us to 
the gods, (and) accept the precious (offerings) we make 
for food and riches.
