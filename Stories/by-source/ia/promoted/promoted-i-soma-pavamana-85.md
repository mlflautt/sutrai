# I Soma Pavamana 85

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8816-8837

I.  Soma  'Pavamana. 

FLEET  as  swift  steeds  thy  cows  celestial  have  been 
poured,  O  Pavamana,  with  the  milk  into  the  vat. 

Sages  who  make  thee  bright,  O  friend  whom  Rishis 
love,  have  shed  continuous  streams  from  out  the 
realm  of  air. 

2  The  beams  of  Pavamana,  sent  from  earth  and  heaven, 

his  ensigns who  is  ever  stedfast,  travel  round. 
When  on  the  sieve  the  golden-hued  is  cleansed  he  rests 
within  the  jars  as  one  who  seats  him  in  his  place. 

3  O  thou  who  seest  all  things,  sovran as  thou art  and 

passing  strong, thy  rays  encompass  every  form. 
Pervading  with thy  natural  powers  thou flowest  on, 
and as  the  whole  world's  Lord,  O  Soma,  thou  art 
King.
