# 11 Invigorate Thy Body With The Soma That Has Been 365

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3363-3372

11. Invigorate thy body with the Soma that has been 
poured out as food for thee: may it exhilarate thee, 
•delighting in the Soma draught. 

]2. May this libation, Indra, penetrate to thy flanks; 
may it, (aided) by prayer, reach thy head ; may it spread, 
hero, through thy arms, (that they may distribute) wealth. 

62 Rig-veda Translation [III. 4. L4
