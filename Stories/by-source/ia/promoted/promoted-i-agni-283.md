# I Agni 283

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15761-15776

I.  Agni, 
WITH  all  thy  fires,  O  Agni,  find  pleasure  in  this  our 

sacrifice, 
And  this  our  speech,  O  son  of  Strength  ! 

2  Whate'er,  in  this  perpetual  course,  we  sacrifice  to  God 

and  God, 
That  gift  is  offered  but  in  thee. 

3  May  he  be  our  beloved  King  and  excellent  sweet-toned 

Ho  tar :  may 
We  with  bright  fires  be  dear  to  him  !
