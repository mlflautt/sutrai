# 8 Accept Agni Our Sacrifice Son Of Strength The 1049

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8339-8343

8. Accept, Agni, our sacrifice: son of strength, the 
abider in the three regions, (accept our) oblation: mav 
we be (regarded) amongst the gods as doers of good: 
cherish us with triply-protected felicity.
