# 11 The Gods Verily Have Given You The Beverage At 818

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6728-6739

11. The gods verily have given you the beverage at 
the (third sacrifice of the) day, and its exhilaration, not 
through regard, but (as the gift of one) wearied out (by 
penance): Ribhus, who are so (eminent), grant us, verily, 
wealth at this third (diurnal) sacrifice. 

142
