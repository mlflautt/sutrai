# 17 Pluck Up The Rakshasas Indra By The Root Cut 187

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2040-2044

17. Pluck up the Rakshasas , Indra, by the root; cut 
asunder the middle, blight the summit: to whatever 
remote (regions) thou hast driven the sinner, cast upon 
the enemy of the Veda thy consuming weapon.
