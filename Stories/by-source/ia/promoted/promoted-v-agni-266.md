# V Agni 266

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15382-15396

V.  Agni. 

To  him  who  dealeth  out  all  wealth,  the  sweet-toned 

Hotar-priest  of  men, 
To  him,  like  the  first  vessels  filled  with  savoury  juice, 

to  Agni  let  the  lauds  go  forth  ! 
2  Votaries, bounteous  givers, deck him with their 

songs,  even  as  the  steed  who  draws  the  car. 
To  both, strong Lord of  men!  to  kith and kin 

convey  the  bounties  of  our  wealthy  lords  !
