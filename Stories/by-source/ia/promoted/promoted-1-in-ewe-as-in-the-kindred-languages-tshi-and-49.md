# 1 In Ewe As In The Kindred Languages Tshi And 49

**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)
**Lines:** 8541-8553

1. In Ewe, as in the kindred languages Tshi and 
Ga, each simple root, formed of a consonant followed by 
a vowel, is a verb. These may be called simple-root 
verbs, and are undoubtedly the oldest in the language. 

Examples— 
du, to eat. mi, to swallow. so, to walk. 
Ja, to come. ku, to die. to, to own. 

yt, to go. nu, to drink. no, to sit. 

230 THE EWE-SPEAKING PEOPLES.
