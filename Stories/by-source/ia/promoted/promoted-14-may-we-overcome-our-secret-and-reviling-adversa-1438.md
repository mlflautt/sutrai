# 14 May We Overcome Our Secret And Reviling Adversa 1438

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11086-11090

14. May we overcome our secret and reviling adversaries, 
having departed from iniquity by good deeds : may we 
possess, Maruts, through the rain (sent by you), unmixed 
happiness, water, cattle, and herbs.
