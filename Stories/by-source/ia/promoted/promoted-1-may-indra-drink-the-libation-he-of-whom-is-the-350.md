# 1 May Indra Drink The Libation He Of Whom Is The 350

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3264-3270

1. May Indra drink the libation; he of whom is the 
Soma, having come to the sacrifice ; he who is the repeller 
(of adversaries), the showerer (of benefits), the lord of the 
Maruts ; may he, the wide-pervading, be pleased with 
these (sacrificial) viands: may the oblation be adequate 
to the wants of his body.
