# 2 And You Two Have Given The Swift Dadhikra The 856

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7002-7006

2. And you two have given the swift Dadhikra , the 
repeller of many (foes), the defender of all men, the straight¬ 
going, the graceful-moving, the resplendent, the rapid, 
the destroyer of enemies like a heroic prince.
