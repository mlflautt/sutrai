VII.  Indra. 

0  HERO,  with  what  aid dost  thou  delight  us,  with 
what  succour  bring 

Riches  to  those  who  worship  thee  ? 

Rigveda  VIII.  92.  6,  7.  The  Rishi  is  Sobhari.  The  metre  is  Brihatl 
in  stanza  1,  which  is  a  repetition  of  I.  i.  i.  4.  10,  and  Safobrihatl  in 
stanza  2. 

2  Of  our  wealthy  lords:  maghondm:  or,  of  the  mighty  Gods.  The 
line  is  obscure.  'Graceful  lord  of  men,  grant  wealth  to  us  rich  in 
children  and  grandchildren.' — Wilson. 

Rigveda  I.  25.  19. The  Rishi  is  Sunahaepa.  The  metre  is  Gayatri, 

Rigveda  VIII.  82.  19.  The  Rishi  is  Sukaksha.  The  metre  is 
Gayatri. 

1  Bring:  d  bkara,  the  imperative  form, is  explained  by  Sayana 
as  =  dbibarthi,  the  indicative. The  translation  might  b«:  with what 
succour? — Bring,  etc.
