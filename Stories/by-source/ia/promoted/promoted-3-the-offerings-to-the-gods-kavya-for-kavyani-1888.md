# 3 The Offerings To The Gods Kavya For Kavyani 1888

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15184-15192

3. The Offerings (to the Gods).— Kavya for kavyani 
is explained by Sayana, acts in connection with fire, such as 
bringing the deities, conveying oblations and the like, or it 
may mean the functions of the adhwaryu ; otherwise it might 
have been thought to refer to the kavya , or offerings to the 
Pitris or manes. 

Page 105.
