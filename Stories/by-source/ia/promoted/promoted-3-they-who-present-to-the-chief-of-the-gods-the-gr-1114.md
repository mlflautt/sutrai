# 3 They Who Present To The Chief Of The Gods The Gr 1114

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8828-8833

3. They who (present) to the chief (of the gods) the great 
food (of sacrifice), unattainable (by the Rakshasas), enjoy 
forms exempt from defect: may that new-born Agni 
scatter assembled (hosts): let them stand from around 
me, as (deer avoid) an angry lion.
