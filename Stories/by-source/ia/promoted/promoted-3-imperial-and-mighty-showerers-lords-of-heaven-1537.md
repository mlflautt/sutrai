# 3 Imperial And Mighty Showerers Lords Of Heaven 1537

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11756-11761

3. Imperial and mighty showerers, lords of heaven 
and earth, beholders of the universe, you approach, 
Mitra and Varuna, with variegated clouds to hear the 
sound (of your praises), and cause the sky to (send down) 
rain by the power of the emitter of showers.
