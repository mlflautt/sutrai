# 2 Desiring The Soma Thou Honourest The Dawn 319

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3044-3051

2. Desiring (the Soma), thou honourest the dawn; 
# desiring the Soma , thou hast lighted up the sun ; knowing 

•54 Rig-veda Translation [III. 4.7 

n'-d discriminating (all our wishes), lord of the tawny 
steeds, thou augmentest upon us all (sorts of) prosperity.
