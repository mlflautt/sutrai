# 3 The Most Prophetic Op The Prophets Kavita 2146

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18011-18019

3. The Most Prophetic op the Prophets. — Kavita• 
man kavinam : kavi, as before observed, is usually explained 
kranta-darshi, a seer or shewer of the past, whence may be 
.inferred the application of his knowledge to the future, 

“Till old experience doth attain. 

To something of prophetic strain.”
