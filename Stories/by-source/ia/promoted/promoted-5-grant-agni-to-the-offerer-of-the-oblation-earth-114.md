# 5 Grant Agni To The Offerer Of The Oblation Earth  114

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1546-1550

5. Grant, Agni, to the offerer of the oblation earth the 
giver of cattle, the means of many sacred rites, such that 
it may long endure : may there be to us sons and grandsons, 
and may thy favour, Agni, be productive of good unto us:
