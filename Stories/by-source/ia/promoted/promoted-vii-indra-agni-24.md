# Vii Indra Agni 24

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6730-6746

VII.  Indra- Agni. 

INDRA  and  Agni,  moved  by  songs,  come  to  the  juice, 

the  precious  dew  : 
Drink  ye  thereof,  impelled  by  prayer  ! 

2  Indra  and Agni, with the man who lauds  comes 

visible  sacrifice  : 
So  drink  ye  both  this  flowing  juice  ! 

3  With  force  of  sacrifice  I  seek  Indra,  Agni who love 

the  wise  : 
With  Soma  let  them  sate  them  here  !
