# 2 Vex Thoroughly Agni Our Assailing Enemies Dis 88

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1374-1379

2. Vex thoroughly, Agni, our assailing enemies, dis¬ 
appoint the purpose of the adversary who offers not 
worship: giver of dwellings, who art cognizant (of sacred 
rites), vex those who have no thought (of pious acts) so 
that thy undecaying, all-pervading (rays), may ever abide.
