# 6 Your Ancient Friendship Is Desirable And Auspici 462

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4015-4019

6. Your ancient friendship is desirable and auspicious ; 
your wealth, leaders (of rites), is in the family of Jahnu : 
renewing that auspicious friendship, may we, your associ¬ 
ates, delight you with the sweet (Soma juice).
