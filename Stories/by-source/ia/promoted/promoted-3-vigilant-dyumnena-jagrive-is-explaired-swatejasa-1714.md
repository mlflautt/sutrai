# 3 Vigilant Dyumnena Jagrive Is Explaired Swatejasa 1714

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13165-13171

3. Vigilant.— Dyumnena jagrive , is explaired swatejasa 
sarvada jagaranopeta , always endowed with his vigilance by 
own lustre or energy, loka-rakshartham , for the preservation 
of the world.
