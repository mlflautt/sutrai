# 5 Blazing With His Fiery Tongue In The Four Quarte 1392

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10752-10768

5. Blazing with his (fiery) tongue in the four quarters, 
(of the horizon) he proceeds (to the sacrifice), wearing 
beautiful (lustre), the disperser of darkness, extirpating 
foes : we know him not (as endowed) with manhood* 
whereby this adorable Savitri bestows desirable (wealth). 

Y. 4. 5. 

The Cities are tho same; the Rishi is Pratiprabha ; the metm 
is Triahtubh. 

L For you, (worshippers), I approach to-day the divine 
Savitri and Bhaga, the distributors of precious (wealth) 
amongst men : Ashwins, (leaders of rites), enjoyers of 
many (good things), desiring your friendship, I solicit 
your daily presence.
