# 6 The Glance Of This Auspicious Deity Directed Tow 499

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4338-4343

6. The glance of this auspicious deity, directed towards 
men, is most excellent, most wonderful, acceptable (to all), 
as the pure warm butter (from the milk) of the cow (is 
acceptable) to the deity ; as the gift of a milch-cow (is to a 
man).
