# 10 Or May The Bright And Active Steeds Bestowed Up 1243

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9714-9719

10. Or may the bright and active steeds bestowed upon 
me by Dhwanya, the son of Lakshmana (bear me): the 
riches, endowed with greatness, that have been presented, 
have passed (to the dwelling) of the Rishi Samvarana, 
like cows to their stalls.
