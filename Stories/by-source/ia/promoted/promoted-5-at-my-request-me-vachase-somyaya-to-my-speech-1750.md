# 5 At My Request Me Vachase Somyaya To My Speech 1750

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13601-13608

5. At my Request. — Me vachase somyaya , to my speech 
importing the Soma , i.e., according to the Scholiast, the object 
of my arhlfcss is, that having crossed over, I may go to gather 
the Soma plant: Yaska agrees with Sayana in the inter¬ 
pretation of this stanza, Nir,' n. 25. 

The River before Me.—T hat is, the Shutudri.
