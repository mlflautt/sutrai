# Vi Soma Pavarnaua 153

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11805-11821

VI.  Soma  Pavarnaua. 

FLOW onward, ludu, with  this food for him who 

in  thy  wild  delights 
Battered  the  nine-and-ninety  down, 

2  Smote swiftly forts, and  Sambara,  then  Yadu  and 

that  Turvasa, 
For  pious  Divodasa's  sake  1 

3  Finder of  horses, pour on us horses  and wealth 

in  kine  and  gold, 
And,  Indu,  food  in  boundless  store  !
