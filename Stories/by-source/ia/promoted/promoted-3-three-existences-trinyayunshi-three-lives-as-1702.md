# 3 Three Existences Trinyayunshi Three Lives As 1702

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13006-13019

3. Three Existences.— Trinyayunshi, three lives, as 
supported by butter, by fuel, and by the Soma plant. 

Parent Dawns. —Tiara ajanirushasah: Ajani may 
mean sisters, or mothers : the dawns personified as the parents 
or sisters of Agni, as prior or subsequent to the lighting of 
the sacrificial fire in early morning ; why “three” does not 
appear, unless the three diurnal fires are alluded to : as sisters, 
a text is quoted by Sayana assigning them separate offices ; 
Prajam eka rakshaty-urjam eka , rashtram eka rakshati, one 
preserves the people, one vigour, one the kingdom.
