# 1 As A Quick Horse T He Institutor Of The Rite Is 1767

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13759-13781

1. As a Quick Horse.-—T he institutor of the rite is 
supposed here to address the officiating priests, urging them 
to be diligent labourers, like the carpenter or the pack-horse. 

Acts Acceptable to Indra.. etc.— Abhipriyani marmri- 
shat parani kavin ichchhami sandrishe : the meaning is not 
very obvious, and the coherence still less so. although we have 
the additional aid of the explanation of the passage in the 
Aitareya Brahmana, vi. 20 : priyani is said by Sayana to be 
Indrasya priyatamani , and parani to imply uttamani , best 
and most dear to Indra, karmani. acts ; or parani may mean 
the acts to be done on subsequent days, uttareshwahassu 
kriyavmnani: kavi, which is usually explained kranta darshi 

310 

Rig-veda Translation 

a seer of the past, is said to mean here a holy person who, for 
Jiis assiduous devotion, has attained deification, yah purvam 
anushtiihayajnad devabhuyam agamat; so the Brahmana. ye 
vai tena Rishayah purve pretas-te vai kavayah.
