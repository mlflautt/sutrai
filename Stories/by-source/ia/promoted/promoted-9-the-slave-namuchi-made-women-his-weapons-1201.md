# 9 The Slave Namuchi Made Women His Weapons 1201

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9436-9440

9. The slave (Namuchi) made women his weapons : 
what will his female hosts do unto me ? the two his best 
beloved, (Indra) confined in the inner apartments, and. 
then went forth to combat against the Dasyu.
