# 7 May The Divine Savitri Approach Along With The 965

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7771-7776

7. May the divine Savitri approach along with the 
Ritus, prosper our dwelling, and bestow upon us good 
progeny and food: may he be favourable to us by night 
and by day: may he heap upon us wealth comprehending 
offspring.
