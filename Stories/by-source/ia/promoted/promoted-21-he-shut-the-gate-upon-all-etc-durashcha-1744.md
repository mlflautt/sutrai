# 21 He Shut The Gate Upon All Etc Durashcha 1744

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13535-13541

21. He Shut the Gate upon All, etc .—Durashcha 
vishwa avrinod apa swah ; that is, according to Sayana, hav¬ 
ing placed the cattle in the cow-pens, he covered or closed 
the doors ; vraje gah sthapayitwa tani dwaranyachchhaditavan .
