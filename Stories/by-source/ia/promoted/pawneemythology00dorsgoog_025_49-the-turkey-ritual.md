> Told by Thief, Kitkehahki. This tale is similar to many others in which the 
buffalo voluntarily offer themselves to people for food. Besides the interesting 
references in the tale which afford explanations of certain rites, the story is in- 
teresting because it teaches the young men that a prophet is without honor in his 
own country. In other words, to obtain the favor of the youns^women they must 
go off into the enemy's country and perform deeds of vafor. The paints referred 
to in the tale are supposed to he those found in the color of the turkey's coat.
