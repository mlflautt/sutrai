# 5 Radiant Agni Who Art Made Manifest By The Wind 1135

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8982-8986

5. Radiant (Agni), who art made manifest by the wind, 
and art sporting amidst the ashes (of the forest), be pre¬ 
sent with us : and may the fierce fiery flames, destructive 
of foes, be gentle to this thy worshipper.
