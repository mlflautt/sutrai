# 2 The Reply Is This That It The Shirt Is Needful 250

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7431-7437

2. The reply is this, that it (the shirt) ^ is needful 
to be perfectly pure white and single, which one fold 
is because Vohiman also is thus the one creature 
who was firsts and afterwards from him the gar- 
ment which is innermost and concealed is called in 
revelation®.
