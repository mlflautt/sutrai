# 4 The Four Chief Priests T He Text Has Only Chat 2170

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18238-18248

4. The Four Chief Priests.—T he text has only chat- 
varah, four : the comment supplies Ritvijah. 

His Three Elementary Rays.— Tridhatavo gavah, 
supposed to be the causes of cold, heat, and rain. 

5. (Behold).—-I t may be rendered, also, men, look at 
the form which is to be seen : the text has no verb.
