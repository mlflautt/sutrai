# 1 May We Repeatedly Recite The Praise Of Dadhikrav 873

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7102-7106

1. May we repeatedly recite (the praise) of Dadhikravan ; 
may all rising dawns excite me (to the adoration) of the 
waters, of Agni, of Us has, of Surya, of Brihaspati, and 
of Jisiinu, the son of Angiras.
