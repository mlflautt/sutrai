# 4 Irrepressible And Powerful Agni The Drops Of Mar 107

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1501-1505

4. Irrepressible and powerful Agni, the drops of marrow 
and of butter distil for thee ; therefore do thou, who art 
praised by sages, come with great splendour, and be pleased, 
ever intelligent Agni, with our oblations.
