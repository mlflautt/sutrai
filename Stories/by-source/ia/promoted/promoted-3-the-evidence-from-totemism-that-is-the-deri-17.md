# 3 The Evidence From Totemism That Is The Deri 17

**Source:** Custom and Myth (Comparative mythology)
**Lines:** 9529-9535

3.  The  evidence  from  totemism—  that  is,  the  deri- 
vation of  the  family  name  and  crest  or  badge,  from 
some  natural  object,  plant  or  animal1  Persons  bearing 
the  name  may  not  intermarry,  nor,  as  a rule,  may 
they  eat  the  object  from  which  they  derive  their  family 
name  and  from  which  they  claim  to  be  descended.
