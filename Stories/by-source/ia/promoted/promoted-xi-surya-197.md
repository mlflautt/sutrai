# Xi Surya 197

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13377-13395

XI.  Surya, 

THIS  spotted Bull  hath  coine and sat before the 

mother  in  the  east, 
Advancing  to  his  father  heaven. 

2  As  expiration  from  his  breath, his radiance pene- 

trates within  : 
The  Bull  shines  out  through  all  the  sky. 

3  Song  is  bestowed  upon  the  Bird  :  it  reigns supreme 

through  thirty  realms 
Throughout  the  days  at  break  of  morn. 

CHAPTER. II
