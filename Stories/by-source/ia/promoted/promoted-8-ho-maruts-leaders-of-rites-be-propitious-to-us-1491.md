# 8 Ho Maruts Leaders Of Rites Be Propitious To Us 1491

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11431-11435

8. Ho, Maruts, leaders (of rites), be propitious to us. 
you who are infinitely opulent, immortal, shedders of rain, 
renowned for truth, wise, young, greatly glorified, and 
worshipped with copious oblations.
