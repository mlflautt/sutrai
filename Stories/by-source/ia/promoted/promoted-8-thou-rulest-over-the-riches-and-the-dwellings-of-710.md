# 8 Thou Rulest Over The Riches And The Dwellings Of 710

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5823-5827

8. Thou rulest over the riches and the dwellings of 
men; thou art the rescuer of the herd of cattle; thou 
art the giver of instruction, the smiter in battles, and the 
distributor of great heaps of riches.
