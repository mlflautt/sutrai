# 16 Seven Times Seven A Ccording To Another Text 2188

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18416-18421

16. Seven Times Seven.—A ccording to another text, 
there are seven troops of the Maruts, each consisting of seven, 
making up the usual number of forty-nine, suggesting, most 
probably, rather than suggested by, the absurd legend given 
in the Puranas, Vishnu Pur. 151.
