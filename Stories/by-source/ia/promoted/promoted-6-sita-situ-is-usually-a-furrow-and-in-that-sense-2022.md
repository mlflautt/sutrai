# 6 Sita Situ Is Usually A Furrow And In That Sense  2022

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16582-16597

6. Sita. — Situ is usually a furrow, and in that sense it 
occurs as a personification of the deity, or object of four 
stanzas in the YajusK 12. 69—72, four furrows being drawn 
at the ceremony at which these stanzas are to be recited ; 
but in the next stanza Sayana explains it the wood supporting 
the furrow, sitadharakashtham, which Indra is to take hold 
of, Indra grihnatu ; unless kashtha in the fem. retain its usual 
acceptation of qirirter of the horizon, when Sita may mean 
the sky, as in the next line, sa, she, is interpreted by the 
Scholiast, dyau. the sky. 

Page 171. 

7 and 8.—The two last verses occur also in the Atharvan,. 
in. 17, 4, in. 17, 13.
