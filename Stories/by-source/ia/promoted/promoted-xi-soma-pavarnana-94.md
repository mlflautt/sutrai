# Xi Soma Pavarnana 94

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9152-9165

XI.  Soma  Pavarnana. 

O  SOMA,  Tndu,  every  day thy  friendship hath been 
my  delight. 

Many  fiends  follow  me  ;  help  me,  thou tawny-hued  : 
pass  on  beyond  these  barriers  ! 

2 Close  to  thy bosom am I,  Soma, day and  night 
draining  the  milk,  O  golden-hued. 

Stirya himself refulgent with his glow have we, 
as  birds,  o'ertaken  in  his  course.
