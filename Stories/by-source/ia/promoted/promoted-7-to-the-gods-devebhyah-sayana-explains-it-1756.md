# 7 To The Gods Devebhyah Sayana Explains It 1756

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13672-13680

7. To the Gods.— Devebhyah: Sayana explains it 
Motrebhyah , to the praisers or worshippers. 

In the Dwelling of the Worshipper.— Vivaswatah 
eadane: vivaswatah is here explained, of whom dwelling, 
vasatah , where various (w) religious rites are performed.
