# 10 Bring Hither Jatavedas Who Art Invoked By Us 1341

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10352-10357

10. Bring hither Jatavedas, who art invoked by us 
all, the Maruts, under their several names and forms : 
come, all ye Maruts, with all your protecting faculties, 
to the sacrifice, the praises, and the adoration of the 
worshipper.
