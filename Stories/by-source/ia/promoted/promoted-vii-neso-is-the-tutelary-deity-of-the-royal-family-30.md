# Vii Neso Is The Tutelary Deity Of The Royal Family 30

**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)
**Lines:** 2654-2675

VII. Neso is the tutelary deity of the royal family 
of Dahomi, and hence of the kingdom, throughout which 
his worship is general. His shrines, called Nesu-we— 
‘“‘ Houses of Nesu,” consisting of long sheds open at the 
sides, are to be seen adjoining the royal palaces and 
public buildings. According to tradition, his worship 
was established by Guadja 'T'rudo about 1720. 

Nesu has a number of “ wives,” who are distinguished 
by armlets and long necklaces of cowries, in which the 
shells are separated by red and black seeds. Water is 
largely used in the rites of Nesu, which are performed 
in secret, and one of the chief duties of the “‘ wives” is 
to fetch water for this purpose. While so employed 
they are guarded by Amazons. Before changing his 
residence from one palace to another the king always

god.
