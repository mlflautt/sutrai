# 1 Which Of You Vasus Is A Defender Which Is A 973

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7825-7829

1. Which of you, Vasus, is a defender ? which is a 
protector ? heaven and earth and Aditx preserve us; 
defend us, Mitra and Varuna, from the strong man: 
who is it, gods, that offers you wealth at the sacrifice ?
