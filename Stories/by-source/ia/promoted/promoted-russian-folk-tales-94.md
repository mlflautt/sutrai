# Russian Folk Tales 94

**Source:** Translated From The Russian (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6668-6691

RUSSIAN FOLK-TALES 

146 

“ you are here with the boyars to value these cases, 
what they are worth.” 

Then the boyars valued the silver case at a high price, 
and the golden one at a liigher price stiU, and did not 
deign to look at the wooden one. 

And Evstafi Tsarevich said : “ Now open the cases and 
see what is in them.” 

And they opened the golden case and there were 
snakes and frogs and all sorts of dirt in it ; and looked 
into the silver one, and they saw the same ; and looked 
into the wooden one, and there trees with leaves and 
fruit were growng, wliich emitted sweet odours, and in 
the middle there was a church and an orchard. 

And the Tsar was humbled ; and did not bid Evst.afi 
be punished.
