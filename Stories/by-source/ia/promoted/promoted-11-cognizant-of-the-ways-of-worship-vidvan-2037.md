# 11 Cognizant Of The Ways Of Worship Vidvan 2037

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16825-16832

11. Cognizant of the Ways of Worship. — Vidvan 
pathinam: the first is rendered yashtavyadevaparijnanavan, 
having knowledge of the gods who are to be worshipped ; 
the last, with regard to the ways of worshipping them, deva- 
yajanamargan prati. 

Page 175.
