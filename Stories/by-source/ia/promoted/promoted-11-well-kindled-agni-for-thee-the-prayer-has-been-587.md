# 11 Well Kindled Agni For Thee The Prayer Has Been 587

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4939-4950

11. Well-kindled Agni, for thee the prayer has been 
composed: may (the priest) propitiate (thee) by (his) 
praise: the (sacrificer) offers worship: bestow upon us 
manifold (wealth): desiring (riches), men sit down adoring 
Agni, the invoker of the gods, the glorifier of mankind. 

100 

Rig-veda Translation 

[IV. 1. 7
