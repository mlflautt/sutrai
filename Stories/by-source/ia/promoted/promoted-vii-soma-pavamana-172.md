# Vii Soma Pavamana 172

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12534-12617

VII.  Soma Pavamana, 

THIS  Soma,  strong,  effused  for  draught,  flows to  the 

purifying  sieve, 
Slaying  the  fiends,  loving  the  Gods. 

2  Far  sighted,  tawny-coloured,  he  flows to the sieve, 

intelligent, 
Bellowing,  to  his  place  of  rest. 

3  This  vigorous  Pavamana  runs  forth  to  the luminous 

realm  of  heaven, 
Fiend-slayer,  through  the  sheep's  long  wool. 

4  This  Pavamana, up above on Trita's ridge, hath 

made  the  Sun, 
Together  with  the  Sisters, shine. 

5  Effused,  this  Soma,  Steer, who slays Vritra, room- 

giver,  unbeguiled, 
Hath  gone  as 'twere  to  win  the  spoil. 

6  Urged  by  the  sage upon  his  way, this  God speeds 

forward to  the  vats, 
Indu  to  Indra,  giving  boons.
