# 1 Mitra And Varuna You Uphold The Three Realms 1567

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11969-11973

1. Mitra and Varuna, you uphold the three realms 
of light, the three heavens, the three regions (of the earth), 
augmenting the force of the vigorous (Indra), and pro¬ 
tecting the imperishable rite.
