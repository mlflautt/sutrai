# 1 The Praise That Is Prompted By The Heart And Ia 290

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2792-2797

1. The praise that is prompted by the heart, and ia 
uttered by the reciters of sacred hymns, proceeds to the 
presence of the lord, and is his awakener when repeated 
at the sacrifice: be cognizant Indra, of this praise, which 
is born for thee.
