# 4 Indra The Giver Of Swarga Generating The Days 246

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2460-2464

4. Indra, the giver of Swarga, generating the days, 
has subdued victorious with the eager ( Angirasas ) the 
hostile hosts, and illumining for man the banner of the 
days, he obtained light for the great conflict.
