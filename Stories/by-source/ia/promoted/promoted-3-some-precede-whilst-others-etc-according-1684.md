# 3 Some Precede Whilst Others Etc According 1684

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12815-12820

3. Some precede whilst others, etc— According 
to Sayana allusion is made to the sixteen priests, of whom 
the Adhwaryu and eleven others take an active part in the 
ceremonial, whilst the Udgatn and other three are fitting 
by, engaged in the recital of the prayers and hymns.
