# 10 And They Who Are Defiled By A Propensity To 397

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11280-11315

10. And they who are defiled by a propensity to 
stench are thereby welcoming the demons and fiends^, 
and are far from good thought^ through vexing it; 
and a distance from them is to he maintained of 
necessity in sitting and eating with them, except so 
far as it may be opportune for the giving of incite- 
ment by words for withdrawing (paa^ili^no) from 
their sinfulness, while converting them from that 
propensity, ii. Should one die, to order a ceremo- 
nial for him is indecorous, and to perform it would 
be unauthorised ; but if he were to do so penitently 
would then be authorised to perform his cere- 
monial after the three nights for it is the remedy 
for atonement of sin. 12. And so long as he is 
living he is in the contingencies (vakhtaginb) 
owing to the sickness through which he is in that 
way an infamous one (akhamt^afAr), and there are

for it; these are teachings also for the duty and 
good works of a ceremonial for the soul ^
