# 6 Purifier Agni Bestow On Me Not Neglecting Thy 568

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4809-4814

6. Purifier, Agni, bestow on me, not neglecting thy 
worship, this acceptable and vast (wealth), like a heavy 
load on a feeble (bearer), together with invigorating food ; 
(wealth), secure, abundant, tangible, and consisting of 
the seven elements.
