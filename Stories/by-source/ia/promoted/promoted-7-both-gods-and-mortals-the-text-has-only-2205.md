**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))

7. Both Gods and Mortals- The text has only 
ubhaye yatha viduh , as both know : Sayana explains it, men 
of course know by perception the. setting in of the rains ; the 
gods know it by the agrayana and other sacrifices which are 
oflered at that season.
