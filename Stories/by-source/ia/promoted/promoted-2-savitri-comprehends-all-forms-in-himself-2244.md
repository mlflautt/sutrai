# 2 Savitri Comprehends All Forms In Himself 2244

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18993-19004

2. Savitri Comprehends All Forms in Himself.-- 
Vishwa rupani pratimunchate, he liberates, severally,all forms : 

that is, according to Sayana , atmani badhnati or dharayati 
he binds or holds in himself : Mahidhara , Yajur-Veda, 13. 3, 
explains it, he makes all forms manifest in their own sub¬ 
stance, by removing darkness. 

Has Illumed the Heaven. — Nakam vyakhyat , swargam 
prakashayati, he makes manifest Swarga as the reward of the 
Yajamana.
