# 3 Those Steeds Brihaspati Which Had Come From 939

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7570-7575

3. Those (steeds), Brihaspati, which had come from 
that distant (region), the best (of all), have sat down in 
connection with the ceremony, and to thee the Soma juices 
expressed by the stones flow copiously, (accompanied) by 
the sounds of praise, like deep wells that supply water.
