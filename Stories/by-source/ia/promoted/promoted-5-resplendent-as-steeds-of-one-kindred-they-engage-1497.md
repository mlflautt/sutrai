# 5 Resplendent As Steeds Of One Kindred They Engage 1497

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11469-11473

5. Resplendent as steeds, of one kindred, they engage 
in combat like valiant heroes : like (prosperous) men, 
they, the leaders (of rites), have increased (in power), 
and cover the eye of the sun with showers.
