# Iv Soma Pavamam 71

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8345-8360

IV.  Soma  Pavamam. 
FOR  food,  flow  onward  with  thy  stream,  cleansed  and 

made  bright  by  sapient  men  : 
Indu,  with  sheen  approach  the  milk  ! 

2  While  thou  art cleansed,  song-lover, bring  comfort 

and  vigour  to  the  folk, 
Poured,  tawny  one  !  on  milk  and  curds  ! 

3  Purified  for  the feast of  Gods, go  thou to  Indra's 

resting,  place, 
Resplendent,  furthered  by  the  strong !
