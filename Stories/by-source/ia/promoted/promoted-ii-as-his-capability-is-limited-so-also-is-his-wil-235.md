# Ii As His Capability Is Limited So Also Is His Wil 235

**Source:** The Text Is Fly (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8047-8053

II. As his capability is limited, so also is his will, 
thereby. 12. For he is sagacious, (13) and the will 
of a sagacious beizig is all for that which is possible 
to be, (14) and his will does not pass on to that 
which is not possible^, (15) because he wills all that 
which is possible and fit to be.
