# 7 Verily Those Auspicious Dawns Have Been Of Old 953

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7664-7669

7. Verily those auspicious Dawns have been of old, 
rich with desired blessings, truthful (bestowers) of the 
results of sacrifice ; at which the sacrifices adoring with 
(silent) praise, glorifying (with hymns), has quickly ob¬ 
tained wealth.
