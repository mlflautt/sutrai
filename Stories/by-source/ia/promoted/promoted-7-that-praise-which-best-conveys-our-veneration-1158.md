# 7 That Praise Which Best Conveys Our Veneration 1158

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9144-9154

7. That (praise) which best conveys (our veneration, 
is due) to Agni : affluent in splendour, grant us, (Agni). 
great (wealth), for from thee vast riches and ample food 
proceed. 

y. 2.12] 

Fifth Mandala 

199
