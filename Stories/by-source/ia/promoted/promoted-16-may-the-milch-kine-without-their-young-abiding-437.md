# 16 May The Milch Kine Without Their Young Abiding 437

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3827-3838

16. May the milch kine without their young, abiding 
(in the heaven), and though unmilked, yet yielding milk., 
and ever fresh and youthful, be shaken (so as to perform 
their functions): great and unequalled is the might of the- 
gods. 

m; 5.2] 

Third Mandala
