# 11 Tanunapat H E Who Does Not Consume The Persons 1730

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13352-13361

11. Tanunapat.—H e who does not consume the persons 
of the worshippers yashtrinam tanuh , sharirani na patayati , 
wa dahati , or it may have the meaning previously given, the 
grandson of the waters. 

Matarishwan.—-W ho breathes, shwasiti , in the maternal 
atmosphere, matari.
