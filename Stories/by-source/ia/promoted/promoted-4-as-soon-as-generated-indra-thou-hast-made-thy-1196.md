# 4 As Soon As Generated Indra Thou Hast Made Thy 1196

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9409-9414

4. As soon as generated, Indra, thou hast made thy 
mind resolved: thou hast gone alone to contend against 
numerous (foes): thou hast rent asunder the rock by 
thy strength : thou hast rescued the herd of milk-yielding 
kine.
