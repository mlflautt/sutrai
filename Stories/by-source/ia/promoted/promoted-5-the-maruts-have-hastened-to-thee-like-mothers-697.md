# 5 The Maruts Have Hastened To Thee Like Mothers 697

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5726-5731

5. The Maruts have hastened to thee like mothers 
to their young: like chariots they have rushed in along 
(with thee) ; thou, Indra, hast satisfied the flowing 
streams ; thou hast shattered the clouds: thou hast set 
free the obstructed rivers.
