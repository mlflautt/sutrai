# 3 The Verse Is Unintelligible Without Such Filling 1784

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13940-13951

3. The verse is unintelligible without such filling up of 
the several ellipses as the Scholiast suggests : we may say, 
however, of it, as of many other passages, with Professor 
Benfey, doch bleibt die wendung dunkel: Sama-Veda, ii. 
1070 : the same has also the preceding verse, ii. 1069. 

314 

Rig-veda Translation 

Page 56.
