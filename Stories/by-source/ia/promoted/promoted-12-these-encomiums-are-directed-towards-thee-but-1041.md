# 12 These Encomiums Are Directed Towards Thee But 1041

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8281-8291

12. These (encomiums) are directed towards thee, but 
perhaps an offence has thereby been uttered to (thee), 
the giver of dwellings: may Agni, augmenting (by our 
praise), yield us not up to the maligner or the malevolent. 

V.1.4] 

Fifth Mandala 

179
