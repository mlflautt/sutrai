# 1 Indra The Possessor Of Opulence Directs Down 1209

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9477-9482

1. Indra, the possessor of opulence, directs down¬ 
wards the car over which, intended (to receive sacrificial) 
viands, he presides: he proceeds unimpeded, the first of 
the gods, driving (his enemies before him), as a herdsman 
drives the herds of cattle.
