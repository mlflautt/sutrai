# 3 Filling Both Dwibarlia Is Explained By The 1865

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14974-14984

3. Filling both.— Dwibarlia, is explained by the 
Scholiast, dwayor madhyamottamayoh sthanayoh parivridhah. 

Who Comprehends, etc. — S a mu - m,a h i-padam na goh 
apagulham vividwan agnir nuihyam predu vochan manisham : 
the detached position of seveial of these words makes the 
sense somewhat uncertain ; manisham , the Scholiast connects 
with Santa, and explains it jnatweyam, what is to be known : 
apagulham atyantarahasyam he would seem to attach to 
padam , but both renderings are perhaps questionable.
