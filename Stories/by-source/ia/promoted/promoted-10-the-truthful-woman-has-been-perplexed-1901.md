# 10 The Truthful Woman Has Been Perplexed 1901

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15296-15300

10. The 'Truthful Woman has been perplexed.— 
After the destruction of the enemies of Kutsa , Indra conveyed 
him to his palace, where Shachi , the wife of Indra , could not 
tell which was her husband as they were both exactly alike.
