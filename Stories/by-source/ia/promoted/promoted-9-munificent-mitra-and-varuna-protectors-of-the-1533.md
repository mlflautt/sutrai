# 9 Munificent Mitra And Varuna Protectors Of The 1533

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11728-11733

9. Munificent Mitra and Varuna, protectors of the 
universe, (it is yours to grant) exceeding and perfect 
felicity, such as it is impossible to disturb : bless us with 
that (felicity), and may we ever be (possessed) of the riches 
we desire, and be confident of victory (over our enemies).
