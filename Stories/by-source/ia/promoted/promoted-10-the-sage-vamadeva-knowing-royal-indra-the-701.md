# 10 The Sage Vamadeva Knowing Royal Indra The 701

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5762-5766

10. The sage, (Vamadeva), knowing, royal Indra, the 
ancient deeds of thee who art all-wise, has proclaimed 
the actions, such as thou hast performed them, generative 
of rain, self-evolved, and beneficial to man.
