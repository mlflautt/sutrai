# 1 Associator Of The Solar Ray Sangatim Goh Is 1995

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16295-16300

1. Associator of the Solar Ray.— Sangatim goh , is 
only explained , goh sangamayitamm, the hringer into anion, 
or associator of Go: vhat the latter is intended for is not 
explained, and the translation is purely conjectural, founded 
upon the connection of the Ashwins with light, or the sun.
