# 16 To Me Inquiring Of Their Kindred The Sage Marit 1428

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11002-11006

16. To me, inquiring of their kindred, the sage (Maritts) 
have uttered a reply : they have declared Prishni (to be 
their) mother ; the mighty ones have declared the food- 
bestowing Rut>ra (to be their) father.
