# 21 And This Epistle Is Written By Me In My Own 627

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17701-17709

21. And this epistle is written by me, in my own 
^<2:^-writing, for the sake of all members whatever 
of the good religion of the country of Irin becoming 
aware of the opinion, apprehension, a'nd apprecia- 
tion of the commands of religion entertained by me, 
MinfijrMar, son of Yfidin-Yim; and several copies 
are finished in the month of the triumphant Hor- 
vada(2? of the year 250 of Ya^dakard^.
