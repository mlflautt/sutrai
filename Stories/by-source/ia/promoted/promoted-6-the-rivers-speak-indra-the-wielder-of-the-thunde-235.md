# 6 The Rivers Speak Indra The Wielder Of The Thunde 235

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2381-2386

6. The rivers speak] Indra, the wielder of the thunder¬ 
bolt, dug our channels when he slew Ahi the blocker-up of 
rivers: the divine and well-handed Savitri has led us 
(on our path), and obedient to his commands we flow (as) 
ample (streams).
