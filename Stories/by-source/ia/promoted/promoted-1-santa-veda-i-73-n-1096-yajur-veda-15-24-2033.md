# 1 Santa Veda I 73 N 1096 Yajur Veda 15 24 2033

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16788-16795

1. Santa,-Veda, i. 73, n. 1096, Yajur-Veda , 15. 24: 
Mahidhara explains prati dhenum ayatim ushasam , as the cow 
is awakened in the morning by the calf, and men by day¬ 
break : he differs also in the explanation of the simile in the 
second hemistich, yahva iva pra vayarn ujjihanah, rendering it, 
as large (birds) springing from the branches of the trees, or 
as the largest birds from their nests.
