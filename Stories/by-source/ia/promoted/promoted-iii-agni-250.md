# Iii Agni 250

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14969-14998

III.  Agni. 

THY mighty flames, O Agni,  when thou  art  en- 
kindled, rise  on  high, 
Thy  bright  flames,  thou  refulgent  one ! 

2  Beloved  !  let  my  ladles  full  of  sacred  oil  come  nigh 

to  thee  : 
Agni,  accept  our  offerings  ! 

3  I  pray  to  Agni — may he  hear  ! — the  Hotar  with 

sweet  tones,  the  Priest 
Wondrously  splendid,  rich  in  light. 

Rigveda  III.  27.  13 — 15.  The  Rishi  is  Visvamitra.  The  metre  is 
Gayatri. 

2  The  strong :  vrishd  :  the  word  originally  meant  the  male,  whether 
applied  to  men  or  other  animals;  then,  specially,  a  bull  or  a  stallion 
&c.,  and  afterwards,  generally,  manly,  strong,  heroic.  Professors  Lud- 
wig  and  Grassmann  translate  the  word  in  all  three  stanzas  by  Stier, 
bull.  '  Agui,  the  bull,  is  kindled  well.  We  will  enkindle  thee,  the 
bull,  we  who  are  bulls  ourselves,  0  bull.' 

RigvedaVIII.  44.  4—6.  The  Rishi is  Virupa.  The  metre  is  Gayatri.
