# 3 He Auni Is Associated With The Perpetual Oblatio 1349

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10423-10429

3. He, (Auni), is associated with the perpetual oblation 
that is the true (source of good) ; the sustainer of all, he is 
of unrestricted movement, the offerer (of burnt-offerings), 
the cherisher of strength : gliding especially over the sacred 
grass, the showerer (of benefits); an infant, a youth, 
exempt from decay, whose place is among plants.
