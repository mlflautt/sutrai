# 22 Taste Agni The Oblations Make Manifest For Us 420

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3718-3723

22. Taste (Agni) the oblations ; make manifest (for us) 
abundant food: measure out the viands before us: thou 
overcomest all those (who are) our enemies in conflict: 
favourably inclined towards us lighten up all our (cere¬ 
monies) day by day.
