# Vii Indra 222

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14140-14154

VII.  Indra. 

PROTECT  us,  Indra, each  to-day, each morrow, and 

each  following  day  ! 
Through  all  the days  shalt  thou, Lord  of  the  brave, 

preserve  our  singers  both  by  day  and  night  ! 
2  A crushing warrior, passing rich, is Maghavan, 

endowed  with  all  heroic  strength. 
Thine  arms,  O  Satakratu,  are  exceeding  strong,  those 

arms  which  grasp  the  thunderbolt.
