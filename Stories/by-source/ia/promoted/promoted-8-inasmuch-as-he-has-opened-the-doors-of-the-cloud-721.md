# 8 Inasmuch As He Has Opened The Doors Of The Cloud 721

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5905-5910

8. Inasmuch as he has opened the doors of the cloud, 
and has supplied the rapid courses of the waters with 
(additional) torrents, so when the pious have recourse 
to Indra for food, he finds (it) in the haunt of the Gaura 
and Gavaya .
