# Xviii 248

**Source:** Ratan Tata Library (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16054-16086

XVIII 

We salute thee as Chief, (with 
the title) Kwa,*^ 

We salute thee as ’Birempon, 
(with the title) Kwa, 
Chief, Kwa, 

• The drums have seven damirifa, but the record ran out at the word 

Amponsa in the Ashanti translation. * Cylinder no. 5. 

• Osafo’s father was one Osai Fwidie. Osai is the title given to any one of 
the Bosomuru ntoro. The Ashanti translation in the record only contains 
one damirifa, the drums give eight, as above. 

• Asiase Pepra was the tenth ruler of the Beretuo. Odii awu means lit. 
* the murderer * but here implies he slew his enemies. The present Omanhene 
of Mampon is often greeted by this title: Odii awu machct lit. ‘ Slayer (of 
men), good morning 

• Every one of the name * Pepra * has the sobriquet * drummer ’ whether 
he be a drummer or not. 

• Pepra Firampon, omitted in error in both drum and Ashanti translation 
on the phonograph cylinder. 

’ Kwa, which is a title of honour and respect, is not to be confused with 
kwa, worthless.
