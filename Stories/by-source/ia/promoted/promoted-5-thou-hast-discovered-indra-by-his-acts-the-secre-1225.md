# 5 Thou Hast Discovered Indra By His Acts The Secre 1225

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9593-9597

5. Thou hast discovered, Indra, by his acts, the secret 
vital part of him who thought himself invulnerable, when, 
powerful Indra, in the exhilaration of the Soma , thou 
hast detected him preparing for combat in his dark abode.
