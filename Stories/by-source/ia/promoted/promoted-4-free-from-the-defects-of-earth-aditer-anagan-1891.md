# 4 Free From The Defects Of Earth Aditer Anagan 1891

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15210-15216

4. Free from the Defects of Earth.- Aditer anagan 
is rendered by Sayana , hhumer anagasah , paparahitan : in 
what sense the sins of earth, or against the earth, is to be 
understood, must be a matter of conjecture. 

Page 106.
