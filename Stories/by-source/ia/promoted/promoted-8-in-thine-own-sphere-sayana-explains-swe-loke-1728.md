# 8 In Thine Own Sphere Sayana Explains Swe Loke 1728

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13326-13342

8. In Thine Own Sphere. — Sayana explains swe loke 
by uttaravedyah nabhau on the centre of the north altar : 
Mahidhara , Yajur-Veda , XI. 35, says, krishnajwe on the hide 
of the black antelope. 

300 

Rig-veda Translation 

In the Chiep Place op the Sacred Rite.— Sadaya 
yajnam sukritasya yortau; yajna , according to Sayana , is 
put for the yajamana, yajna sya kartaram , who is to he stationed 
uttameloke , in the best place, or that which enjoys the fruit 
of the holy rite, sukritajanyopabhogasya* sthane : Mahidhara 
says, sukrita yonih means the krishnajina , the black antelope- 
hide.
