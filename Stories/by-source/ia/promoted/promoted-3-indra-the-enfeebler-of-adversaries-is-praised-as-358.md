# 3 Indra The Enfeebler Of Adversaries Is Praised As 358

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3316-3321

3. Indra, the enfeebler (of adversaries), is praised as a 
mine of wealth ; he is propitiated by righteous commenda¬ 
tions ; he is gratified in the dwelling of the offerer of 
oblations: glorify Indra, the conqueror, the destroyer* 
of his enemies.
