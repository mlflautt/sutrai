# 3 Traverses The Darkness Artham Hyasya Tarani 1688

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12869-12873

3. Traverses (the Darkness). —Artham hyasya tarani , 
his meaning or object is crossing, or that which crosses ; his 
light or radiance is the end or object of Agni , which passes 
over or through, darkness understood.
