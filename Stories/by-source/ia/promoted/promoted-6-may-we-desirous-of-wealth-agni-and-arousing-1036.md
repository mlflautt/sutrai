# 6 May We Desirous Of Wealth Agni And Arousing 1036

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8246-8253

6. May we, desirous of wealth, Agni, and arousing 
thee by oblations, secure in thy protection, acquire (riches): 
may we (be victorious) in battle, (successful) in sacrifices 
every day; and may we, son of strength, obtain with 
riches male (descendants). 

178 . Rig-veda Translation [V. 1.3
