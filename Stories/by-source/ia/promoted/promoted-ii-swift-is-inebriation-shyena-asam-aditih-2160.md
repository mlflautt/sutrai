# Ii Swift Is Inebriation Shyena Asam Aditih 2160

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18138-18147

II. Swift is.Inebriation. — Shyena asam aditih 

kaksliyo madah: shyena is interpreted shighra, quick, and 
aditi i atisamriddhah: asam, of these, implies the Soma juices ; 
trnda , intoxication, is the devata of the verse. 

410 

Rig-veda Translation
