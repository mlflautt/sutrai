# 9 Vishwamitra Speaks Listen Sister Streams Kind 238

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2405-2410

9. Vishwamitra speaks] Listen, sister (streams), kind¬ 
ly to him who praises you ; who has come from afar with 
a waggon and chariot: bow down lowlily; become easily 
fordable; remain, rivers, lower than the axle (of the 
wheel) with your currents.
