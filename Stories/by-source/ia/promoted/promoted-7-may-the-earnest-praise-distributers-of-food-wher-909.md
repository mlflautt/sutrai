# 7 May The Earnest Praise Distributers Of Food Wher 909

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7325-7335

7. May the earnest praise, distributers of food, where¬ 
with I associate you both like-minded at this sacrifice, 
be (beneficial) to us: do you protect your worshipper: 
my desire, Nasatyas, directed towards you is gratified.
