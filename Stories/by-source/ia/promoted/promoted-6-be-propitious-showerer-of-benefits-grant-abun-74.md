# 6 Be Propitious Showerer Of Benefits Grant Abun 74

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1272-1277

6. Be propitious, showerer (of benefits) ; grant (abun¬ 
dant) food, Agni ; (make) heaven and earth yield us 
milk: divine (Agni), associated with the gods, shining 
with bright radiance, let not the ill-will of any mortal 
prevail against us.
