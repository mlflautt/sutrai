# 8 The Rivers Speak Praiser Of Indra Forget Not Thi 237

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2393-2404

8. The rivers speak] Praiser of Indra, forget not this 
speech, nor the words that (thou hast addressed to us) for 
future ages (to reverence): celebrator (of holy rites), be 
favourable to us in solemn prayers; treat us not after 
the (arrogant fashion) of men: salutation be to thee! 

Third Mandala 

3i* 

III.3.5]
