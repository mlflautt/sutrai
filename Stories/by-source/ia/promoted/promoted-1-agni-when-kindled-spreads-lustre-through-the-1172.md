# 1 Agni When Kindled Spreads Lustre Through The 1172

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9252-9257

1. Agni, when kindled, spreads lustre through the 
firmament, and shines widely in the presence of the dawn : 
Vjshwavara, facing the east, glorifying the gods with 
praises, and bearing the ladle with the oblation, proceeds 
(to the sacred fire).
