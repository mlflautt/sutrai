# 4 Heaven And Earth Endowed With Truth They Who 401

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3600-3605

4. Heaven and earth, endowed with truth, they who 
are the ancient declarers of the truth, have obtained from 
you (what they desired) ; and so, earth, men cognizant 
(of the greatness of you both) have worshipped you for 
the sake of triumphing over (hostile) heroes in battle.
