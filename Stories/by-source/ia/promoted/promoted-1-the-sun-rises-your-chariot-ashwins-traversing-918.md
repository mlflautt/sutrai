# 1 The Sun Rises Your Chariot Ashwins Traversing 918

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7388-7393

1. The sun rises: your chariot, (Ashwins), traversing 
(the regions), is associated with the divine (orb) on the 
summit (of the eastern mountain): in it are the three 
analogous kinds of food, and the leather vessel of the sweet 
Soma juice appears as the fourth.
