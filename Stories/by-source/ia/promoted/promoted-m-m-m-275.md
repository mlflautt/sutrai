318 ASHANTI GOLDSMITHS AND GOLD WEIGHTS 

Remarks,

tankyinanka wo tumi twa eda 
wo so ; se wo ntumi ntwa a 
eda wo so (' The straggling 
creepers if you do not cut 
them they entwine you; if 
you cut them, they entangle 
you ’). 

Made of strips of hide bound 
together. 

Lent by Capt. O. F. Ross. 
Not actually in use. 

There is a saying. Wo aso 
sansa *to (‘You have held a 
hawk's bottom *), meaning 
you have got into serious 
trouble.
