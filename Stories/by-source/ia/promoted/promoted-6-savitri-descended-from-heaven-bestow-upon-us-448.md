# 6 Savitri Descended From Heaven Bestow Upon Us 448

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3910-3918

6. Savitri, descended from heaven, bestow upon us 
blessings thrice every day: Bhaga, saviour, grant us 
thrice a day riches of three elements: Dhishana enable 
.us to acquire (wealth). 

Third Mandala 

75
