# 1 Adverbs Of Place 77

**Source:** The Ewe-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa (African)
**Lines:** 9397-9412

1. Adverbs of place. 
Out, by go, place, edge. 
Up, by ta, head, summit. 

LANGUAGE. 249 

Far, by diduve, distance. 

Above, by dsz-wo : dsi, upper part, and wo, contraction 
of weo, plural of we, place. 

Here, by afi, place. 

There, by afi-ma; afi, place, and me, interior ; or by 
the verb de, to be in a place.
