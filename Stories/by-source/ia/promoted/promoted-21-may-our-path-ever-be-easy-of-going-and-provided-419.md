# 21 May Our Path Ever Be Easy Of Going And Provided 419

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3712-3717

21. May our path ever be easy of going, and provided 
with food: sprinkle, gods, the plants with sweet water: 
{safe) in thy friendship, Agni, may my fortune never be 
impaired, but may I occupy a dwelling (abounding) with 
riches and ample food.
