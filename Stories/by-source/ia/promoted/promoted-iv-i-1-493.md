# Iv I 1 493

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4290-4296

IV. I. 1. 

The deity is Agni, or, it may be, Varuna in the second, third, 
and fourth stanzas: the Rishi is Vamadeva : the metre of the first 
verse is Ashti ; of the second, Atijagati ; of the third, Dhriti ; and 
Trishtubh of the rest.
