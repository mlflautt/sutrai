# 17 The Horses Gavau Gachchhata Iti Gavau Ashwau 1804

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14195-14201

17. The Horses.— Gavau-gachchhata, iti , gavau ashwau: 
(java implies, those who go, or, in this place, horses. 

Gar be Ready for Us.— Vishwamitra, says the com¬ 
mentator, being about to depart from the sacrifice of Sudas . 
invokes good fortune for his conveyance.
