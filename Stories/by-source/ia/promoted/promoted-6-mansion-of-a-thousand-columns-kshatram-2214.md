# 6 Mansion Of A Thousand Columns Kshatram 2214

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18663-18671

6. Mansion of a Thousand Columns .—Kshatram 
sahasrasthunam , anekavaslitambhakastambhopetam saudhadiru - 
pam griham, a house, such as a palace and the like, possessing 
many supporting pillars : the expression is noticeable as 
indicating the existence of stately edifices : Sayana purposes 
also to render kshatram by dhanam, wealth, or balam, strength,, 
applying the last to the ratha , the strong chariot of the deities 
supported by innumerable columns.
