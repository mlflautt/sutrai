**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3536-3545

19. Fix firmly the substance of the Ichayar (axle), give 
.solidity to the shishu (floor) of the car: strong axle, 

5 

66 Big-veda Translation [III. 6. 1 

strongly fixed by us, be strong; cast us not from out of
