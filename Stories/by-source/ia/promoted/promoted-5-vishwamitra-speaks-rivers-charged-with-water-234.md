# 5 Vishwamitra Speaks Rivers Charged With Water 234

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2375-2380

5. Vishwamitra speaks] Rivers, charged with water, 
rest a moment from your course at my request, who go to 
gather the Soma (plant): I, the son of Kuskika , desirous 
of protection, address with earnest prayer especially the 
river before me.
