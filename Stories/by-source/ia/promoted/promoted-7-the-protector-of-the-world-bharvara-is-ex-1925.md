# 7 The Protector Of The World Bharvara Is Ex 1925

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15533-15545

7. The Protector of the World.— Bharvara is ex¬ 
plained as the patronymic of Bharvara , which means jagad- 
bharta , the protector of the world, or Prajapati . 

360 

Rig-veda Translation 

In the Secret Thoughts. —The text has only guka 
pra, which Sayana expands into guharupa-hridaye prabhavati: 
it, that is, the strength, balam, of Indra , prevails or presides 
•over the heart, in the nature of secrecy or mystery.
