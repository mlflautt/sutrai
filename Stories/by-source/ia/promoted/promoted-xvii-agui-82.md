# Xvii Agui 82

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8727-8738

XVII.  Agui. 

SING  forth  to  him,  the  Holy,  most  munificent,  sublime 

with  his  refulgent  glow, 
To  Agni,  ye  UpastutasJ 
2  Worshipped  with  gifts, enkindled,  splendid,  Magha- 

van  shall  win  himself  heroic  fame  : 
And  will not  his  more  plentiful  benevolence  come  to 
us  with  abundant  strength  ?
