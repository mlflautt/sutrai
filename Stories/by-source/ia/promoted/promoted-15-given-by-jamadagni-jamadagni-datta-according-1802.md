# 15 Given By Jamadagni Jamadagni Datta According 1802

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14165-14182

15. Given by Jamadagni. — Jamadagni-datta, according 
to Sayana , may mean, given by the Rishis , those w T ho 
maintain a blazing jamat-jwalat, fire, Agni; a sense 
• confirmed by the use of the plural in the next verse : the 

daughter of Surya , so given, is said to be speech, or its 
personification, wk-devata. 

Notes on Volume III 

319 

Mighty Sound.—T he .sound of thunder or the like in 
the sky. 

Food among the Gods. —As the prayer or exclamation 
which accompanies the burnt offering.
