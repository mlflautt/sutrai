‘ Ta Kese Birimpon (the god), your grandchild is Kwesi Ntwi; 
he says that to-day is Muruwukuo and that this is an offering 
of wine from his hands; he begs you for life, &c., &c. ; when 
he seeks for money let him get some; life to the people of his 
village ; do not let him get into trouble with the white man ; 
do not permit him to offend (his chief) Yao Kramo; when this 
white man goes to the forest, permit that he kill an elephant 
that we may have something to eat.’ 

The wine was poured out at the foot of the altar, as before. 
What was left was handed round and all sipped a little. We

general, but conducted in subdued voices. The death of the
