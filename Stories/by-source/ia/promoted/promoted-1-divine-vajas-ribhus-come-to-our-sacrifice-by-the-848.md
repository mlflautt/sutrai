# 1 Divine Vajas Ribhus Come To Our Sacrifice By The 848

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6940-6945

1. Divine Vajas, Ribhus, come to our sacrifice by the 
path travelled by the gods, inasmuch as you, gracious 
(Ribhus), have maintained sacrifice amongst the people,, 
•{the progeny)]of Manu, for (the sake of) securing the 
prosperous course of days.
