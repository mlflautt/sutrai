# 8 Maruts Devoid Of Enmity Come To Our Proffered 1676

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12742-12747

8. Maruts, devoid of enmity, come to our proffered 
praise, hear the invocation of your adorer, Evayamarut : 
do vou, who are associated in the worship of Vishnu, 
drive away, as warriors (scatter their enemies), our secret 
foes.
