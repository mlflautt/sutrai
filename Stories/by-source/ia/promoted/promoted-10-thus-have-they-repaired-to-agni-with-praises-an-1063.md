# 10 Thus Have They Repaired To Agni With Praises An 1063

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8465-8470

10. Thus have they repaired to Agni with praises and 
with sacrifices successively, and have established (the 
manner of his worship) ; and he has given us male descen¬ 
dants, and wealth of fleet horses: bring. Agni, food to 
thine adorers.
