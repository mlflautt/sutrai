# 3 You Bear For Your Decoration An Excellent Diadem 1495

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11456-11462

3. You bear, for your decoration, an excellent (diadem) 
Jike the horn of cattle : as the sun, the eye (of day, dis¬ 
penses light), so (are you diligent) in the distribution of 
the rains : graceful are you, and rapid as horses, and, 
like (pious) mortals, you, leaders (of rites), consider (holy 
ceremonies) for their glory.
