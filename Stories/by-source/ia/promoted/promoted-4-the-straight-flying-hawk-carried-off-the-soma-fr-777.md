# 4 The Straight Flying Hawk Carried Off The Soma Fr 777

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6298-6309

4. The straight-flying hawk carried off the Soma from 
above the vast heaven, as (the Ashwins carried off) Bhujyu 
from the region of Indra, and a falling feather from the 
middle of the bird dropped from him wounded in the 
conflict. 

132 

Kig-veda Translation 

[IV. 3.7
