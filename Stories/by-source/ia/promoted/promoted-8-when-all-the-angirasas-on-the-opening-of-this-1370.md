# 8 When All The Angirasas On The Opening Of This 1370

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10576-10581

8. When all the Angirasas, on the opening of this 
adorable dawn, came in contact with the (discovered) 
cattle, then milk and the rest were offered in the august 
assembly, for Sarama had found cows by the path of 
truth.
