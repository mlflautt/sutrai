# 6 The Near Advancing Rays Dispersing The Darkness 923

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7418-7423

6. The near-advancing (rays), dispersing (the darkness) 
by the (light of day), are overspreading the firmament 
with lustre like the sun: the sun, harnessing his horses, 
(proceeds on his way): do you make known all his paths 
by (following) after (him) with sacrificial food.
