# 1 He Who Knows How To Honour You Two Amongst 1550

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11839-11843

1. He who knows (how to honour you two), amongst 
the gods, is the performer of good works : let him commu¬ 
nicate (that knowledge) to us, he of whom the graceful 
Varuna or Mitra accepts the laudation.
