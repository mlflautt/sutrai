In  the  Bocage  of  Normandy  the  peasants  used  to  repair, 
often  from  a  distance  of  miles,  to  the  churches  to  hear  the 
midnight  mass  on  Christmas  Eve.  They  marched  in  pro- 
cession by  torchlight,  chanting  Christmas  carols,  and  the 
fitful  illumination  of  the  woods,  the  hedges,  and  the  fields  as 
they  moved  through  the  darkness,  presented  a  succession  of 
picturesque  scenes.  Mention  is  also  made  of  bonfires  kindled 
on  the  heights  ;  the  custom  is  said  to  have  been  observed  at
