# 2 First Thou Engenderest For The Adorable Gods The 968

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7789-7793

2. First thou engenderest for the adorable gods the 
best portion, immortality; then, Savitri, thou settest 
open (the day) to the donor (of the oblation), and (grantest) 
successive existences to men.
