# 3 He Indra Has Asserted That It Will Cause The 682

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5635-5640

3. He, (Indra), has asserted (that it will) cause the 
death of my mother: let me not proceed by the usual 
way, but proceed quickly, according (to my will): in the 
dwelling of Twashtri Indra drank the costly Soma from 
the vessels of the offerers.
