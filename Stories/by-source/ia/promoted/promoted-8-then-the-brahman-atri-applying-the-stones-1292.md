# 8 Then The Brahman Atri Applying The Stones 1292

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10026-10036

8. Then the Brahman, (Atri), applying the stones 
together, propitiating the gods with praise, and adoring 
them with reverence, placed the eye of Surya in the sky 
he dispersed the delusions of Swarbhanu.
