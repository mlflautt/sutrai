# 20 May The Divine Show R Erers Of Benefits The Dei 418

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3704-3711

20. May the (divine) show r erers (of benefits), the deities 
of the mountains, and those abiding in fixed habitations, 
propitiated by the sacrificial food, hear us: may Aditi, 
with the Adityas, hear us: may the Maruts grant us 
auspicious felicity. 

70 Rig-veda Translation fill. 5.2
