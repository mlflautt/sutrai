# 1 Shitiprishthasya Dhaseh Agni Is Understood So Is 1678

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12762-12769

1. Shitiprishthasya dhaseh; Agni is understood, so is 
sashmayahy rays, according to Sayana : he explains dkasi by 
sarvasya dharayitri. 

Sapta vanih the commentator explains by sarpana- 
stoabhava nadih; otherwise sapta might be thought to mean 
seven, its more usual acceptation.
