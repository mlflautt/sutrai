# 3 For Thy Glory The Maruts Sweep The Firmament 1033

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8229-8233

3. For thy glory the Maruts sweep (the firmament), 
when thy birth, Rudra, is beautiful and wonderful: 
the middle step of Vishnu has been placed, so thou 
cherishest the mysterious name of the waters.
