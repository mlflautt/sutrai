# 2 The Sky Traversing Steeds Of The Showerer Of Ben 6

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 822-827

2. The sky-traversing steeds of the showerer (of benefits) 
are the milch kine (of Agni) ; as he attains the divine 
(rivers), bearers of sweet (water). One sacred sound 
glorifies thee (Agni), who art desirous of repose, pacifying 
(thy flames) in the abode of the water (the firmament).
