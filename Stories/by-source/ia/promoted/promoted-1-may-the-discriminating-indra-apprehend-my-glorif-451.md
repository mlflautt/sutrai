# 1 May The Discriminating Indra Apprehend My Glorif 451

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3935-3940

1. May the discriminating Indra apprehend my glorifi¬ 
cation (of the gods), which is (free) as a milch cow, grazing 
alone, without a cowherd, one who is readily milked, 
yielding abundant nourishment, and of whom Agni and 
Indra (and we), are the commenders.
