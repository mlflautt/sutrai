# Xviii Agni 309

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16539-16566

XVIII.  Agni. 

O  CHILD of  Strength,  to  thee  whose  look  is  lovely, 

with  oblations  we, 
O  Agni,  have  poured  forth  our  songs. 

2  To thee for shelter  are we  come,  as  to  the  shade 

from  fervent  heat, 
Agni,  who  glitterest  like  gold  ! 

3  Mighty  as one who  slays  with  shafts,  or  like  a  bull 

with  sharpened  horn, 
Agni,  thou  brakest  down  the  forts. 

Rigveda  III.  12.4—6.  The Rishi is Visvamitra. The  metre  is 
Gayatri. Stanzas  2  and  3  are  repetitions  of II. vii. iii.  2. land 2 

Rigveda  VI.  16. 37—39. The  Rishi  is  Vitahavya  or  Bharadvaja. 
The  metre  is  Gayatri. 

1  Child  of  Strength  :  svhaskrita  : literally, made  or produced  by 
strength,  that  is,  by  violent  agitation  of  the  fire-drill.
