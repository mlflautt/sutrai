# 1 We Solicit Of The Divine Powerful And Intelligen 959

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7731-7736

1. We solicit of the divine, powerful, and intelligent 
Savitri that desirable and ample (wealth), along with 
which he grants a dwelling to the offerer of the oblation 
of his own accord: may the great deity grant us such 
every day.
