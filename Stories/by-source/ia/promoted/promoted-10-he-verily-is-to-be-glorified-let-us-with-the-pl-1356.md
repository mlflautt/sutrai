# 10 He Verily Is To Be Glorified Let Us With The Pl 1356

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10476-10480

10. He verily (is to be glorified) : let us, with the plea¬ 
sant thoughts of Ksiitra, Manasa, Avada. Yajata, 
Sadhri, and Avatsara, fill up the invigorating food, 
(the portion) to be shared by the wise.
