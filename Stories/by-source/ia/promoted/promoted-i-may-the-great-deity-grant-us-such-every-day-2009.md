# I May The Great Deity Grant Us Such Every Day 2009

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16434-16438

I. May the Great Deity grant us such Every Day.— 
Tachchhardir no rmhan udayan devo aktubhih ; chJtardih is 
explained by griha, a house, or it may mean light: aktubhih 
is lit. by nights, by metonymy for days.
