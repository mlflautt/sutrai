# 12 The Observers Of The Nine Month S Celebration 1188

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9353-9357

12. The observers of the nine month’s celebration, 
those of the ten months, pouring out libations, worship 
Indra with hymns : the leaders (of rites), glorifying him r 
have set open the cave (concealing the cattle).
