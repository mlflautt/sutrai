# Xiii Indra 147

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11438-11454

XIII.  Indra, 

O  INDRA,  bring  great  strength to us, bring valour, 

Satakratu,  thou  most  active,  bring 
A  hero  conquering  in  war ! 

2  For,  gracious  Satakratu,  thou  hast  ever  been  a  mother 

and  a  sire  to  us, 
So  now  for  bliss  we  pray  to  thee. 

3  To  thee,  strong ! much-invoked  !  who showest forth 

thy  strength,  made  very  mighty  !  do  1 speak  : 
So  grant  thou  us  heroic  power  !
