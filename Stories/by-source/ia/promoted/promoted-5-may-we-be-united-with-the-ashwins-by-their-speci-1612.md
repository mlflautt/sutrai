# 5 May We Be United With The Ashwins By Their Speci 1612

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12282-12300

5. May we be united with the Ashwins, by their special 
protection, which is the source of happiness, the guide to 
good : bestow upon us, immortals, wealth and all good 
things.
