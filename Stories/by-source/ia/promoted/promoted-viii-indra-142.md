# Viii Indra 142

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11317-11331

VIII.  Indra, 

No one by deed attains to him who works  and 

strengthens  evermore  : 
No,  not  by  sacrifice,  to  Indra  praised  of  all,  resistless, 

daring,  bold  in  might ; 
2  The  powerful  conqueror,  invincible in war, him at 

whose  birth  the  mighty  ones, 

The  kine  who  spread  afar,  sent  their  loud  voices out, 
heavens,  earths  sent  their  loud  voices  out.
