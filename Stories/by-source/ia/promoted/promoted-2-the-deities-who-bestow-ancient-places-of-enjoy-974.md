# 2 The Deities Who Bestow Ancient Places Of Enjoy 974

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7830-7836

2. The (deities) who bestow ancient places (of enjoy¬ 
ment on their worshippers), and (with minds) unperplexed, 
are the separators of light (from darkness) ; they, the 
eternal distributors (of rewards), grant (what is desired), 
and shine of pleasing aspect, the true (recompensers) 
of pious acts.
