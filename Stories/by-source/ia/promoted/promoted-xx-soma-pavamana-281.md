# Xx Soma Pavamana 281

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15696-15712

XX.  Soma  Pavamana. 

FLOW  to  us,  Indu,  very  strong,  effused,  with  wealth 

of  kine  and  steeds, 
And  do  thou  lay  above  the  milk  thy  radiant  hue  ! 

2  Lord  of  the  tawny,  Indu,  thou  who  art  the  Gods' 

most  special  food, 
As  friend  tofriend,  for  splendour  be  thou  good  to  men! 

3  Drive  utterly,  far  away  from  us  each  godless,  each 

voracious  foe ; 
O  Indu,  overcome  and  drive  the  false  afar  !
