# 1 Thou Indra Hast Rent The Cloud Asunder Thou 1221

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9566-9570

1. Thou, Indra, hast rent the cloud asunder, thou 
hast set open the flood-gates, thou hast liberated the 
obstructed streams, thou hast opened the vast cloud, and 
hast given vent to the showers, having slain the Danam.
