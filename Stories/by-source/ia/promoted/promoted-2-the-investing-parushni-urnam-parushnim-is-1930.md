# 2 The Investing Parushni Urnam Parushnim Is 1930

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15583-15595

2. The Investing Parushni. — Urnam parushnim is 
explained achkadilcam parvavatim nadim , the river having 
joints or bends covering—the comment does not say what. 

Whose Bordering Districts he has frequented.— 
The phraseology here is somewhat obscure, and the Scholiast 
does not materially enlighten us : the text is, yasyah parvani 
sakhyaya vivye ; lit., whose joints through friendship he has 
approached: Sayana explains it, yasyah nadyah bhinnan 
deshan sakhikarmane samvriiavan , the separate districts of 
which river lie has, for the sake of friendly acts, covered 
or concealed.
