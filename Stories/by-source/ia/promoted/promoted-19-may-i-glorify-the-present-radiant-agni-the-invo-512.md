# 19 May I Glorify The Present Radiant Agni The Invo 512

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4424-4428

19. May I glorify the present radiant Agni, the invoker 
dof the gods), the supporter of the universe, most deserving 
•of adoration, without milking the pure udder (of the cow), 
without the purified food of the Soma offered in libation.
