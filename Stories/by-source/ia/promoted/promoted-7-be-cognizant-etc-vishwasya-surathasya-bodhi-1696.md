# 7 Be Cognizant Etc Vishwasya Surathasya Bodhi 1696

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12954-12959

7. Be Cognizant, etc. — Vishwasya surathasya bodhi : 
the scholiast explains the verb, know that I am his protector, 
gopayita bhavami-iti budhyaswa : Suratha is literally one who 
has a'good chariot, and is so rendered in the commentary 
shobha naya nopetasya .
