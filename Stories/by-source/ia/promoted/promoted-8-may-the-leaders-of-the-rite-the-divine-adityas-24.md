# 8 May The Leaders Of The Rite The Divine Adityas 24

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 936-940

8. May the leaders of the rite, the divine Adityas ,, 
Rudras , Vasus , Heaven and Earth, the Earth, the firma¬ 
ment, well pleased, protect our sacrifice : let them raise 
aloft the standard of the ceremony.
