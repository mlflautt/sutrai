# 8 Having Slain Vritra He Has Liberated Many Mornin 699

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5743-5748

8. Having slain Vritra, he has liberated many mornings 
and years (that had been) swallowed up by darkness, and 
has set the rivers free: Indra has released the imprisoned 
rivers, encompassed (by the cloud), to flow upon the 
earth.
