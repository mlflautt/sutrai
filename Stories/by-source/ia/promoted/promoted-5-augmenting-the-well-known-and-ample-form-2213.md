**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18652-18662

5. Augmenting the Well-Known and Ample Form.— 
Shrutam amatim anuvardhad-urmm , which the Scholiast inter¬ 
prets, vishrutam sharimdiptim anuvarddhayardau , augmenting 
the renowned bodily lustre, and separates nrvim , tad vad 
urvim palayantau, but gives no equivalent lor the word, which 
usually means great: the passage is obscure. 

Ascend Your Car, etc. —Garte mitra asathe: garta, it 
is said here means ratha , a car : Nirvkta , 3. 5, and as it recurs 
in verse 8, arohatho Vanina, Mitra, gartam.
