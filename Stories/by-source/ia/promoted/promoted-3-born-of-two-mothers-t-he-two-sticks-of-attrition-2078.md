# 3 Born Of Two Mothers T He Two Sticks Of Attrition 2078

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17261-17269

3. Born of Two Mothers.—-T he two sticks of attrition. 

Thou hast sprung up from the Devotion of the 

Householder. — Udatishtho vivasvatah : the latter is said to 
designate the Yajamana , from his especially, vi, abiding, 
vasata, in the chamber where the agnihotra , and other rites 
with fire, are celebrated.
