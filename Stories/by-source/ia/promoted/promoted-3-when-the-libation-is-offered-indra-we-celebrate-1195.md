# 3 When The Libation Is Offered Indra We Celebrate 1195

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9396-9402

3. When the libation is offered, Indra, we celebrate 
thy exploits ; those (exploits) which thou hast been 
pleased (to achieve) for us : Jet him who is ignorant 
acquire knowledge (of them) ; let him who is acquainted 
(with them) make them known : this Maghavan (is) 
the lord of hosts.
