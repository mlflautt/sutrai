# 64 Of This Too That This Otie Is No Creator Of The 306

**Source:** The Text Is Fly (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9472-9481

64. Of this, too, that this otie is no creator of the 
pain and death which are dissipaters of the body, 
who is good-willed and a maintainer and cherisher of 
the body, (65) the evidence is even from this, when 
the sagacious creator is not a sufferer from sorrow 
(apakhshaddr) and performing penitence, (66) and 
is no dissipater and disabler of his own achieve- 
ments \ (67) because he is sagacious and all- 
knowing-.
