# 4 The Ministering Priests Have Concentered In Thee 95

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1429-1433

4. The ministering priests have concentered in thee, 
Agni, who art divine, many hosts (of flames); do thou 
bring hither the gods, youngest (of the deities), that thou 
mayest worship to-day the divine effulgence.
