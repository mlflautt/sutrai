# 23 Kaninakem Vidradhe Nave Drupade Arbhake Nave 1960

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15903-15912

23. Kaninakem vidradhe nave drupade arbhake: nave 
and arbhake have their usual meanings, new, small : k an make 
is explained by shalabhanjike, dolls or puppets ; vidradhe Jby 
vyudhe , arrayed, or arranged : and drupade by drumakhya - 
sthane sthite , standing on a place fixed, termed either a tree, 
or from a tree, a plank or platform, probably, answering to 
a stage on which puppets were exhibited. 

Sukta—the Ribhus. —See vol. 1.5. 3.
