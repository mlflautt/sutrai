# 1 The Priest Glorifies You Maruts For The Good Of 1493

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11441-11449

1. The priest glorifies you, Maruts, for the good of 
the donor of the oblation : offer worship to the shining 
(heaven) : I bring offerings to the earth: they, the 
Maruts, scatter the rapid (rain) ; they traverse the 
firmament ; they combine their own radiance with (that 
of) the clouds. 

254 Rig-veda Translation [V. 5.3
