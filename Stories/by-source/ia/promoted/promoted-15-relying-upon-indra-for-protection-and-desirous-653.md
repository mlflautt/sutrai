# 15 Relying Upon Indra For Protection And Desirous 653

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5432-5438

15. Relying (upon Indra) for protection, and desirous 
of riches, (pious men) repair to him soliciting his presence 
in the sacrifice, as if in the battle ; asking for food, cele¬ 
brating his praise with hymns, for he is the refuge (of his 
worshippers), and resembles the grateful and lovely 
(goddess) of nutrition.
