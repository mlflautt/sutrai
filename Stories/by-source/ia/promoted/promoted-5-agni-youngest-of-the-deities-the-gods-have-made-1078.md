# 5 Agni Youngest Of The Deities The Gods Have Made 1078

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8564-8570

5. Agni, youngest (of the deities), the gods have made 
thee, when kindled, their messenger, the bearer of ob¬ 
lations ; thee, who art rapid in movement, and of whom 
butter is the source, they have made, when invoked, the 
brilliant eye (of the universe), instigated by the under¬ 
standing.
