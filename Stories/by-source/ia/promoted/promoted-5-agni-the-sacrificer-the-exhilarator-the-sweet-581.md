# 5 Agni The Sacrificer The Exhilarator The Sweet 581

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4909-4914

5. Agni, the sacrificer, the exhilarator, the sweet- 
spoken, the object of sacrifice, moving measuredly,. 
circumambulates (the victim) of his own (accord) ; the 
bright (rays) of him (fed) with (sacrificial) food, spread 
around: all the regions are alarmed when he blazes.
