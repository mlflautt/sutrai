# 4 Like Wealthy Bridegrooms Who Have Decorated 1505

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11519-11524

4. Like wealthy bridegrooms who have decorated 
their persons with golden (ornaments) and purifying 
waters, so the noble and powerful Maruts, associated 
together in their chariots, have made great (preparation) 
in their several persons for their embellishment.
