# 3 The Maruts Appear Radiant With Lightning Leaders 1444

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11120-11126

3. The Maruts (appear) radiant with lightning, leaders 
(of the rain), armed with weapons of adamant, blazing 
with the wind, the precipitators of mountains, the repeated 
distributors of water, wielders of the thunderbolt, roaring 
in concert, combining (to send rain), and of exceeding 
strength.
