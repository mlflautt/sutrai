# 4 Distinguished Devoted Embodied Adorer 2232

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18857-18861

4. Distinguished, Devoted, Embodied (Adorer).— 
The epithets are unusual, kakuha , mriga, vapusha, explained 
severally by the commentator, mahan , great, mrigayita, 
searcher, vapushman, having body, yajamana is understood.
