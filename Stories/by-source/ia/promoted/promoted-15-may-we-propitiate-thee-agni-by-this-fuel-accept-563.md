# 15 May We Propitiate Thee Agni By This Fuel Accept 563

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4765-4770

15. May we propitiate thee, Agni, by this fuel: accept 
the praise tha is recited by us: consume the unadoring 
Rakshasas; thou who art to be honoured by (thy) friends, 
preserve us from the reproach of the oppressor and the 
reviler.
