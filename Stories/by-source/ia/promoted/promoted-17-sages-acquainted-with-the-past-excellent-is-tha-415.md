# 17 Sages Acquainted With The Past Excellent Is Tha 415

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3688-3693

17. Sages acquainted with the past, excellent is that 
beautiful appellation under which you have both become 
gods in (the sphere of) Indra: do thou, Indra, the in¬ 
voked of many, a friend, (associated) with the beloved 
Ribhus, shape this prayer for our benefit.
