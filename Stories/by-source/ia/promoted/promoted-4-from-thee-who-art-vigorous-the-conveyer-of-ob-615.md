# 4 From Thee Who Art Vigorous The Conveyer Of Ob 615

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5153-5158

4. From thee, who art vigorous, the conveyer of ob¬ 
lations, the vast, the granter of what is desired, is born 
(a son) of real strength ; from thee comes wealth approved, 
of by the gods, the source of happiness ; from thee, Agni,. 
(is obtained) a swift unarrested horse.
