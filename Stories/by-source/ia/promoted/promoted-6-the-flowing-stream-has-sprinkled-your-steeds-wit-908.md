# 6 The Flowing Stream Has Sprinkled Your Steeds Wit 908

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7320-7324

6. The flowing (stream) has sprinkled your steeds with 
moisture: the radiant horses (like) birds (in swiftness) 
pass on, bright with lustre: well known is that quick- 
moving charriot, whereby you became the lords of Surya.
