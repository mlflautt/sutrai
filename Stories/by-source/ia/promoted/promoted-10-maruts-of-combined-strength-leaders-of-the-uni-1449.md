# 10 Maruts Of Combined Strength Leaders Of The Uni 1449

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11166-11171

10. Maruts of combined strength, leaders of the uni¬ 
verse, guides of heaven, when the sun has risen you rejoice 
(in the Soma beverage), then your rapid steeds know no 
relaxation, but quickly you reach the limits of this road 
(to the sacrifice).
