# 24 Intsekavo A I Yi Beat A Asyelgo Dahtiago Na Ami 210

**Source:** Collected And Translated (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14535-14542

24. IntsekaVo a'^i/yi/ beat?a</asyelgo dahtiago na^amiya'. 

Your head over mist dark made of far darkness high flying come to us. 

25. Intseka*/o niltsabaad beatea^asyelgo da.hila.go nahainiya'. 

Your head over rain female made of far darkness high flying come to us.
