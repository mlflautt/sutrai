# 3 Variously Do My Manifold Desires Alight Present 424

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3741-3745

3. Variously do my manifold desires alight: present 
at the solemnity, I recite ancient (hymns): when the fire 
is kindled we speak indeed the truth, for great and un¬ 
equalled is the might of the gods.
