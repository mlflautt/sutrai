# 6 Divine Agni Giver Of Dwellings Knower Of All Tha 456

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3970-3974

6. Divine Agni, giver of dwellings, knower of all that 
exists, extend to us that benevolence which, unshared by 
others, cherishes us like the showers of the rain-cloud; 
that kindness which is beneficent for all mankind.
