# 2 May The Munificence Of The Ribhus Come To Me On 830

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6822-6826

2. May the munificence of the Ribhus come to me on 
this occasion, (since) there has been the drinking of the 
effused Soma , in consequence of one ladle having been 
made fourfold by their dexterous and excellent work.
