# 9 With A Waggon And Chariot Anasa Rathena 1752

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13618-13623

9. With a Waggon and Chariot.— Anasa rathena : 
the commentator, and Yaska also, separate these words, 
shakatena ratliena cka, by a cart and a carriage : the anas , a 
•cart or waggon. or truck, would be. for the conveyance of the 
Soma plant.
