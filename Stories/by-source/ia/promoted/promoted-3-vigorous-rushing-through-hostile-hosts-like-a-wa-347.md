# 3 Vigorous Rushing Through Hostile Hosts Like A Wa 347

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3235-3241

3. Vigorous, rushing through (hostile hosts), like a war- 
horse, he has pervaded heaven and earth, sending down 
rain: he is to be worshipped with oblations at the solemn 
{rite) like Bhaga : he is as the father of those who adore 
{him): he is beautiful, worthy of invocation, the bestower 
of food.
