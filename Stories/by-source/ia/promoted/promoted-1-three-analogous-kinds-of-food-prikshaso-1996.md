# 1 Three Analogous Kinds Of Food Prikshaso 1996

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16314-16323

1. Three Analogous Kinds of Food .—Prikshaso 
mithum trayah: mithuna is properly twins, or a pair, but 
it may be used, according to Sayana , for a greater number of 
analogous or connected objects, as mata pita putrastadeva 
mithunam , mother, father, son, constitute a twin or pair: 
the three sorts of food are said to be ashanam , panam , 
khadah : in what the last differs from the first is not specified.
