# 12 The Reciters Of Sacred Metres Desirous Of Water 1424

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10976-10981

12. The reciters of sacred metres, desirous of water, and 
celebrating (the Maruts), have drawn them to (provide) 
a well (for Gotama) : some of them, (invisible) as thieves, 
have been my defenders : some have been (obvious) to 
view through the light (of life).
