# 15 And These Three Benefits Which Have Been 60

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2269-2275

15. And these three benefits^ which have been 
recited are sent down (fardstako) in two ways 
that the ancients have mentioned, which are that 
deliberately taken and that they should deliber- 
ately leave®, whose means are wisdom and proper 
exertion.
