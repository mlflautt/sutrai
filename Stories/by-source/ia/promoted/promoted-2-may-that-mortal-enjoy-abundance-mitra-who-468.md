# 2 May That Mortal Enjoy Abundance Mitra Who 468

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4052-4056

2. May that mortal enjoy abundance, Mitra, who 
presents thee, Aditya, (with offerings) at the sacred rite 
protected by thee he is not harmed; he is not overcome 
by any one ; sin reaches him not, either from afar or nigh~
