# 7 We Have Prepared The Parched Grain And Curds For 370

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3403-3408

7. We have prepared the parched grain and curds for 
thee, associated with Pdshan ; the fried barley for thee, 
lord of the tawny horses, associated with thy steeds: 
attended by the troop of Maruts , eat the cakes: hero, 
who art wise, and the slayer of Vritra, drink the libation.
