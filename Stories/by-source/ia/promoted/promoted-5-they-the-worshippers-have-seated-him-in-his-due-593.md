# 5 They The Worshippers Have Seated Him In His Due 593

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4975-4979

5. They (the worshippers) have seated him in his due 
order, the invoker (of the gods), the intelligent, the agree¬ 
able, the pur if yingly-radiant, the performer of frequent 
sacrifice, (brilliant) with seven flames.
