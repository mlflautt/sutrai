# 17 Day And Night T He Text Has Ubhe Krishne Both 1743

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13527-13534

17. Day and Night.—T he text has Ubhe krishne , both 
the blacks, meaning, the Scholiast affirms, ahoratre, day and 
night. 

By the Might of the Sun.— Suryasya mahana ; the 
commentator identifies Surya with Indra, the impeller, 
preraka , of the world.
