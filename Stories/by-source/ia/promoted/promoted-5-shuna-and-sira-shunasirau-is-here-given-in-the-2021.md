# 5 Shuna And Sira Shunasirau Is Here Given In The 2021

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16570-16581

5. Shuna and Sira. — Shunasirau is here given in the 
dual as the name of two divinities, of whom Shuna , according 
to Shaunaha, is the dyudevah, the deity of the sky, that is, 
Indra , when Sim will be Vayu , according to Sayana : Yaska 

374 

Rig-veda Translation 

NiruJcta , 9, 40, makes Shuna , Vayu, and Sira, Aditya : in 
ordinary acceptation, Shimasira is a name of Indra.
