# 1 Rushing From The Flanks Of The Mountains Eager 230

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2347-2352

1. Rushing from the flanks of the mountains, eager 
(to reach the sea) like two marcs with loosened reins con¬ 
tending (with each other in speed), like two fair mother 
cows (hastening) to caress (their calves), the Vipash and 
Shutudri , flow rapidly with (united) waters.
