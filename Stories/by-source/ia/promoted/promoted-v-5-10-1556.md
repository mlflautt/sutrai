# V 5 10 1556

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11872-11880

V. 5. 10. 

The deities and Rishi as before ; the metre is Anuaktubh . 

J. Man, endowed with intelligence, (adore) the two 
deities, the performers of good deeds, the destroyers of 
foes; offer (oblations) to the adorable accepter of 
{sacrificial) food, to Varuna, whose form is water.
