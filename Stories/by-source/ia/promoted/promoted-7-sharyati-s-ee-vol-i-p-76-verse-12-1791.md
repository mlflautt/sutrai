# 7 Sharyati S Ee Vol I P 76 Verse 12 1791

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14014-14022

7. Sharyati.—S ee vol. I, p. 76, verse 12. 

Being in Security.—T aw sharman a vivasanti, they 
worship thee in the security, or unassailable place, dependent 
on, or protected by thee, tavasambandhini sharmani nirbadha- 
sthane sthitah : Mahidhara , Yajur- Veda , vii. 35, explains 
sharman either by sukha-nimitte for the sake of happiness, or 
yajnagriha , the chamber of sacrifice.
