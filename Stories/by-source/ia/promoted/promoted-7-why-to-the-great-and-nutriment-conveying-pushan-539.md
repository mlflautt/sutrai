# 7 Why To The Great And Nutriment Conveying Pushan 539

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4612-4616

7. Why to the great and nutriment-conveying Pushan ?' 
why to Rudra, the object of worship, the giver of the 
oblation (to the gods) ? why to the many-hymned Vishnu ?' 
why tell our sin to the extensive year ?
