# 4 The Soma Juices Daily Poured Out Enter Into The  332

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3126-3137

4. The Soma juices daily poured out enter into the vast 
and profound Indra, fierce from his birth, all-pervading. 

Rig-veda Translation 

56 

[ 111 . 4.9* 

the protector of the pious (worshippers), as rivers flow 
into the sea.
