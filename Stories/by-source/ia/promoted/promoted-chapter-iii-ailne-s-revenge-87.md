# Chapter Iii Ailne S Revenge 87

**Source:** Gods and Fighting Men (Celtic)
**Lines:** 19944-20255

CHAPTER  III. AILNE'S  REVENGE 

One  day  Finn  and  his  people  were  hunting  on  Slieve 
Fuad,  and  a  stag  stood  against  them  for  a  while  and 
fought  with  his  great  rough  horns,  and  then  he  turned 
and  ran,  and  the  Fianna  followed  after  him  till  they  came 
to  the  green  hill  of  Liadhas,  and  from  that  to  rocky 
Cairgin.  And  there  they  lost  him  again  for  a  while,  till 
Sceolan  started  him  again,  and  he  went  back  towards 
Slieve  Fuad,  and  the  Fianna  after  him. 

But  Finn  and  Daire  of  the  Songs,  that  were  together, 
went  astray  and  lost  the  rest  of  their  people,  and  they 
did  not  know  was  it  east  or  west  they  were  going.

And  as  to  Finn  and  Daire,  a  Druid  mist  came  about
