# 1 The Bearers Of The Libations Desire Thee Thy Fri 171

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1932-1940

1. The bearers of the libations desire thee ; thy friends- 
pour forth the Soma juice ; they offer copious oblations: 
(supported by thee) they endure patiently the calumny* 
of men, for who is so renowned as thou art.
