# 1 The Bearer Of The Oblation Invokes With Praises 98

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1454-1458

1. The bearer (of the oblation) invokes with praises 
at the break of day, Agni, the dawn, the Ashwins, and 
Dadhikra: may the resplendent divinities, desirous of our 
sacrifice, hear in unison (the invocation).
