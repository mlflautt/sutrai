# 6 Leaders Of The Rains The Mighty Maruts Shine 1419

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10940-10945

6. Leaders (of the rains), the mighty Maruts shine 
with brilliant ornaments and weapons, and hurl javeline 
(at their foes, the clouds): the lightnings, like roaring 
(torrents), daily follow the Maruts : the radiance of the 
resplendent (cohort) spontaneously breaks forth.
