# 5 Him Neither Many Nor Few Can Molest May Aditi 763

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6196-6201

5. Him neither many nor few can molest: may Aditi 
grant him infinite happiness: the performer of pious acts 
is dear (to Indra) : dear to Indra is he whose mind is 
intent upon him ; dear is he who approaches him with 
homage ; dear to him is the offerer of the libation.
