# 5 Each Of The Three Lolcas Lias Three Divisions Th 1824

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14456-14465

5. Each of the three Lolcas lias three divisions : this 
is a piece of information addressed to the rivers, Sindhavah 
'being in the vocative case. 

The Measurer of the Three Worlds.—T he sun or 
the year. 

Three Female Divinities.— The Scholiast says they are 
Ila, Saraswati, and Bharati.
