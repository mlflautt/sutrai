# 5 Augmenting The Well Known And Ample Form Of Man 1530

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11707-11717

5. Augmenting the well-known and ample form (of man), 
in like manner as the sacred grass is preserved by prayer, 
do you, Mitra and Varuna, who are invigorated by 
(sacrificial) viands, and abound in food, ascend your car 
in the midst of the place of sacrifice. 

(). Be open-handed and benignant to the performer of 
pious acts, whom you protect in the midst of the place of 
sacrifice ; for you two, who are sovereigns, and free from 
wrath, uphold together a mansion of a thousand columns.
