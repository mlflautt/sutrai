# 15 My Praise Has Been Continually Proffered As A P 1310

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10147-10152

15. My praise has been continually proffered as a pro¬ 
tectress, powerful with (the means of) preservation: may 
the maternal and venerable earth accept our (praises), 
and, (pleased) with her pious (worshippers) be (to us) 
straight-handed, and the giver of good.
