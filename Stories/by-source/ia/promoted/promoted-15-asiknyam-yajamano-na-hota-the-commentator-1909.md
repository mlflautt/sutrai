# 15 Asiknyam Yajamano Na Hota The Commentator 1909

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15368-15376

15. Asiknyam yajamano na hota : the commentator 
considers hota as put for hotaram, the nom. for the accus.,. 
and explains it the invoker, Agni y ahvataram agnim: he 
supplies also the copulative, sinchati somam , he sprinkles 
the Soma ; the stanza consists but of one pada, and is 
considered to refer to the preceding one.
