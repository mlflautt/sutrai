# 6 This Hero Indra The Prompt Discomfiter Of Foes 764

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6202-6214

6. This hero, Indra, the prompt discomfiter (of foes), 
who is to be approached with homage, grants special 
maturity to the presenter of the libation: he is not the 
kinsman, nor friend, nor relative, of him who offers no 
oblation (to him): he is difficult of access? and the punisher 
of him who repeats not (his) praise. 

7. (Indra), the drinker of the effused Soma , contracts 
no friendship with the wealthy trader who offers not any 
libation; he takes away his wealth; destroys him when 
destitute ; but he is a special (friend) to him who presents 
the libation and oblation.
