# 9 Dexterous Handed Since You Have Instituted Throu 836

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6865-6869

9. Dexterous-handed, since you have instituted, through 
desire of good works, .the third sacrifice, which is the 
bestower of wealth, therefore, Ribhus, drink this effused 
Soma with exhilarated senses.
