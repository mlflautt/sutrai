# 3 Ushas Who Spreadest Over All The Regions Thou 483

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4164-4171

3. Ushas, who spreadest over all the regions, thou 
abidest on high, the ensign of the immortal (sun), purposing 

HI. 5.9] Third Mandala 81 

to travel the same road, repeatedly turn back ever new, 
.(revolving) like a wheel
