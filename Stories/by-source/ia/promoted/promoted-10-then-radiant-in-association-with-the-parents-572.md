# 10 Then Radiant In Association With The Parents 572

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4839-4845

10. Then, radiant in association with the parents, 
^(heaven and earth), he is awakened (to drink) the agreeable 
secretion of the cow, and the tongue of the assiduous 
(performer of holy rites), the resplendent showerer (of 
benefits), approaching the excellent station of the maternal 
(cow), seeks to drink the milk.
