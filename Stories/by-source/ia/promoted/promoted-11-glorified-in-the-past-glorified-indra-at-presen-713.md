# 11 Glorified In The Past Glorified Indra At Presen 713

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5846-5851

11. Glorified (in the past), glorified, Indra, at present r 
satisfy thy worshipper with food, as rivers (are filled with, 
water): lord of horses, a new hymn has been made for 
thee: may we, possessed of chariots, be ever delighted in. 
(thy) praise.
