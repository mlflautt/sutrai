# 6 Royal Indra And Varuna Embellish The Three 285

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2752-2757

6. Royal Indra and Varuna, embellish the three 
universal sacrifices (and make them) full (of all requisites) 
for this celebration: thou hast gone to the rite, for I have 
beheld in my mind, at this solemnity, the Gandharvas 
with hair (waving) in the wind.
