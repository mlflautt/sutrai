# 2 I Utter To Thee The Words Of Adoration Be Please 62

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1194-1200

2. I utter to thee the words of adoration, be pleased by 
them, observer of truth: endowed with strength, (they 
are addressed) to thee who art the expounder (of sacred 
rites): wise (as thou art) bring (hither) the wise: object 
of sacrifice, sit down in the midst upon the sacred grass 
for our protection.
