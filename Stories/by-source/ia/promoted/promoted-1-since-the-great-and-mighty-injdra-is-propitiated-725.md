# 1 Since The Great And Mighty Injdra Is Propitiated 725

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5942-5947

1. Since the great and mighty Injdra is propitiated by 
our (oblations), since he desires (them) from us, may he, 
the possessor of opulence, who comes wielding the thunder¬ 
bolt by his strength, accept the (sacrificial) food, the hymn, 
the Soma libation, and the prayers.
