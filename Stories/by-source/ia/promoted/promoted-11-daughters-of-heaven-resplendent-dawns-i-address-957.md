# 11 Daughters Of Heaven Resplendent Dawns I Address 957

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7686-7694

11. Daughters of heaven, resplendent Dawns, I address 
you (as) the announcer of the sacrifice : may we be (the 
possessors) of celebrity amongst men, and may heaven 
and the divine earth prepetuate (it). 

1Y. 5. 7. 

The deity and Bishi as before; the metre is Qayatri.
