# 5 All Powerful Indra Ahishushma A Rather Un 2122

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17748-17759

5. All-powerful Indra. — Ahishushma , a rather un¬ 
usual epithet of Indra : the Scholiast explains it, sarvata 
vyaptabala, of all-pervading strength. 

Like Bhaga in Battles. —Satwa Bhago na havyah 
frabhritheshu charuh may be explained differently, as fra- 
bhritha signifies war or sacrifice : may a faithful follower or 
ally, satwa-bhrityadih , come, like Bhaga , as an associate, 
charu sanganta, to be commended, havya, in sacrifices, or 
defied, 'pratibhatairahvatavya, in battles : as the divine Bhaga 
comes as our ally, so may followers and others come.
