# 4 Lucidly White Is She Occupying The Two Regions 1628

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12433-12438

4. Lucidly white is she, occupying the two (regions, 
the upper and middle firmament), and manifesting her 
person from the east: she traverses the path of the sun as 
if knowing (his course), and harms not the quarters of the 
horizon.
