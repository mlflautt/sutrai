# Vi Mitra Tar Ima 73

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8404-8421

VI.  Mitra- Tar  ima. 

MITRA  of  holy strength I call, and  foe-destroying 
Varuna, 

Who  perfect  prayer  with  offered  oil. 

2  By  Law,  O  Mitra, Varuna,  Law-strengtheners who 

cleave  to  Law, 
Have  ye  obtained  your  lofty  power. 

3  The  Sages,  Mitra,  Varuna,  of  wide  dominion,  mighty 

ones, 

Bestow  on  us  effectual  strength.
