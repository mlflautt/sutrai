# 20 May Agni Be The Aditi M Ay He Be The Cherisher 1848

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14738-14752

20. May Agni be the Aditi.—M ay he be the cherisher 
of the gods as if he were Aditi their mother ; or Aditi may 
mean the earth, that is, their stay or support: Mahidkara, 
on this verse, Yajur-Veda, xxxm. 16. explains it 

332 Hig-veda Translation 

etymologically ; without a defect, yasya khandanam nusti, 
adina , not mean or base. 

Receiving the Sacrificial Food. —Devanam am 
avrinanah, according to Sayana , is participating in the sacri¬ 
ficial food of the worshippers : Mahidhara explains it, deliver¬ 
ing the oblations offered to the gods, i.e through fire.
