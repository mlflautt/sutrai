# 7 Maghavan Who Art Glorified By Us Assailing With 1199

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9425-9430

7. Maghavan, who art glorified by us, assailing with 
the thunderbolt the antagonist (of the gods), thou hast 
slain those who were ever hostile (to thee) from thy birth : 
desiring to do good to Manu, thou hast bruised the head 
of the slave Namuchi.
