# Iii 2 10 Panchachitirupa Agnayo Devata Each Verse 1708

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13068-13078

III. 2. 10.— Panchachitirupa agnayo devata , each verse 
it is said being separately recited as the Adhwaryu constructs 

Rig-veda Translation 

294 

a pit or an altar, ishtaka , for collecting ckayana or chili, a 
sacrificial fire : the hymn occurs in the same order in the 
Yajush, xii. 47, 51.
