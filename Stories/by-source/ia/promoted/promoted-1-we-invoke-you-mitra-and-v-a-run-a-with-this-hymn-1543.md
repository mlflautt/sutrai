# 1 We Invoke You Mitra And V A Run A With This Hymn 1543

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11798-11802

1. We invoke you, Mitra and V a run a, with this hymn ; 
each the discomfiter of foes, the conductor to heaven, 
like (two herdsmen) driving, by (the strength of their) 
arms, the herds of cattle before them.
