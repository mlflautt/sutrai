# 1 As To Iht Thirty Ninth Question And Reply That 249

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7424-7430

1. As to iht thirty-ninth question and reply, that 
which you ask is thus ; What kind of goodness and 
want of goodness can there be in the sacred tkread- 
girdle and shirt; and what are the sin of running 
about uncovered, of prayer offered and prayer not 
offered, and the purpose of cleansing (mirn) ?
