# 1 Twofold Is My Empire Etc M Ama Dwita Rashtram 1989

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16231-16246

1. Twofold is My Empire, etc.—M ama dwita rashtram 
kshatriyasya vishwayoh : here, therefore, we have a positive 
indication of the military and regal order. 

All the Immortals are Ours. — Vishwe amrita yatha 
nah : therefore he is king also over Swarga. 

The Gods associate me with the Acts of Varuna.— 
Kratutn sachante Varunasya devah , rajami krishter-upamasya 
vaoreh : except the last word, which the Scholiast renders 
rupa , form, there is nothing unusual in the terms ; but even 
with the explanations attempted by Sayana , the purport of 
the sentence is quite unintelligibile, beyond its being probably 
the identity of Varuna and Trcisadasyu, as asserted in the next:, 
verse.
