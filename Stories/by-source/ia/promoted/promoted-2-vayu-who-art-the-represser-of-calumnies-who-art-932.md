# 2 Vayu Who Art The Represser Of Calumnies Who Art 932

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7501-7510

2. Vayu, who art the represser of calumnies, who art 
drawn by the Niyuts , and hast Indra for thy charioteer, 
come with thy brilliant car to drink the Soma juice. 

L60

[IV, 5. 4
