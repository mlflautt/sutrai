# Vii Gods 349

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17761-17776

VII.  Gods. 
PRAISE  to  the  friends  who  sit  in  front ! to  those  seated 

together,  praise  : 
I  use  the  hundred-footed  speech. 

2  I  use  the  hundred-footed  speech,  I  sing  what  hath  a 

thousand  paths, 
Gayatra,  Trishtup,  Jagat  hymn. 

3  Gayatra,  Trishtup,  Jagathymn,  the  forms  united  and 

complete, 
Have  the  Gods  made  familiar  friends.
