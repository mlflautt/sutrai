# 3 Moving You Proceed Rapidly By Day As Indra At 905

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7303-7307

3. Moving, you proceed rapidly by day, as Indra, at 
the end of the night, (manifests his) power: descended 
from heaven, divine, of graceful motion, (Ashwins), by 
which of (your) acts are you most distinguished ?
