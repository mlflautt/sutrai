# 18 May Aryaman Aditi The Adorable Gods And 416

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3694-3698

18. May Aryaman, Aditi, the adorable (gods), and 
the unimpeded functions of Varuna (protect) us: keep 
us from (following) the course unpropitious to offspring, 
and may our dwelling be abundant in progeny and cattle.
