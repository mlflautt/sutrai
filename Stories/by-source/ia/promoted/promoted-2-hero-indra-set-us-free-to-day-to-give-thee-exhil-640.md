# 2 Hero Indra Set Us Free To Day To Give Thee Exhil 640

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5342-5347

2. Hero, Indra, set us free to-day to give thee exhila¬ 
ration at this sacrifice, as (they let loose a horse) at the 
end of the road: may the worshipper, like Ushanas, 
repeat an acceptable prayer to thee, the knower (of all 
things), the destroyer of the Asuras.
