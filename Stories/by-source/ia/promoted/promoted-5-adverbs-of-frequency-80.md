Yes is expressed by e. It is used differently to the

CONJUNCTIONS. 
There are no conjunctions, properly speaking, their 
place being supplied by verbs, either alone or joined to 
nouns or pronouns. 

Examples— 

And, and with, by the verb gbiz, to take, receive. 

Then, by eyia; from y, to go, and a, the definite 
article ; literally, “ the going”. 

Therefore, by eyia-ta ; eyra, and ta, head. Literally, 
“the head of the going”. This is frequently con- 
tracted to eata. Or by eyra-nuti; nuti, thing. 
“The thing of the going”, 7. e. “ the cause of the 
going” 

But, and also, by na, to give. 

Yet, and nevertheless, by gake ; ga, to repeat and ke, 
rel. prn. 

On, by maha; ma, to divide, share, and ha, part. 
This is often contracted to ha, and @. 

Because, and that, by be, to say, or be-na, to say, to 
give. 

PREPOSITIONS. 
There are also no prepositions, properly speaking, 
their place being supplied by verbs or nouns, or by the 
two combined, as—
