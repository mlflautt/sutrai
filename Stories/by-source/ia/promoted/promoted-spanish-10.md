# Spanish 10

**Source:** Facsimile Of An (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 423-441

SPANISH 
Page 83. Afio de... numeral partly effaced; next 

to year-sign 12 Tochth. 

Page 82. Afio de 39, next to year-sign ro Calli. 

Page 80. Afio de... (remainder indecipherable) 
next to day-sign 8 tochtli, which thus appears to have 
been mistaken for a year-sign. 

Page 77. Afio de 52 de la ultima edad; next to day- 
sign 11 tochtli, which again appears to have been mistaken 
for a year-sign. 

Page 53. The word afio and the numerals 33 and 42, 
wrongly accompanying a day-sign. Then the detached 
numerals 42 and 46 equally written next to day-signs.
