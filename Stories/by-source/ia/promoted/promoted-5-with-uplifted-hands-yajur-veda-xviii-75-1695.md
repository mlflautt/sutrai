# 5 With Uplifted Hands Yajur Veda Xviii 75 1695

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12944-12953

5. With Uplifted Hands. — Yajur-Veda , xviii. 75: 
Mahidhara explains Uttanahastah, with open hands, not 
niggardly. 

Oblation.— Katmrn : both commentators consider this 
.synonymous here with oblation, purodashadihavis, as that 
which is desirable to the gods, kamaniyam.
