# Xi Agni 303

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16373-16413

XI.  Agni. 

SING  praise to  him,  the  Lord of  light. The  Gods 
have  made  the  God  to  be  their  messenger, 

To  bear  oblation  to  the  Gods. 

2  A^ni,  the  bounteous  giver,  bright  with  varied  flames, 
laud  thou,  O  singer  Sobhari, 

Hi  in  who  controls  this  sacred  food  with  Soma  blent, 
who  hath  first  claim  to  sacrifice  ! 

Rigveda  VII.  32.  14,  15.  The  Rishi  is  Vaswhtha.  The  metre  is 
Satobrihati  iu  stanza  1,  which  is  a  repetition  of  1.  iii.  ii.  4.  8,  and 
Brihati  in  stanza  2. 

Rigveda  VIII.  24.  16—18. The  Rishi  is  Visyamanas. The  metre  is 
Ushnih. Stanza  1  its  a  repetition  of  I.  iv.  ii.  5.  5. 

Rigveda  VIII.  19.  1,  2.  The  Rishi  is  Sobhari.  The  metre  is  Kakup 
in  stanza  1,  which  is  a  repetition  of  1.  ii.  i.  2.  3,  and  Satobrihati  in 
ttanza  2.

XII*  Soma  Pavamaua. 

EXPRESSED by  stones,  O  Soma, and  urged  through 

the  long  wool  of  the  sheep, 
Thou,  entering  the  press-board?,  even  as  men  a  fort, 

gold-hued,  hast  settled  in  the  vats. 
2  He  beautifies  himself  through the  sheep's  long  fine 

wool,  the  bounteous,  like  the  racing  steed, 
Even  Soma  Pavamana  who  shall  be  the  joy  of  sages 

and  of  holy  bards.
