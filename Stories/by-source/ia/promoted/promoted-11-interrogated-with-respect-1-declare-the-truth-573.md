# 11 Interrogated With Respect 1 Declare The Truth 573

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4846-4850

11. Interrogated with respect, 1 declare the truth, 
that this (wealth is acquired), Jatavedas, by the praise 
of thee: thou rulest over it, (over) all that (it may be), 
the wealth that is in heaven or on earth.
