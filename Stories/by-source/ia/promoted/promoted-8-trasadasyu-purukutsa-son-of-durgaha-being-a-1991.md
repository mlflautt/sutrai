# 8 Trasadasyu Purukutsa Son Of Durgaha Being A 1991

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16259-16269

8. Trasadasyu .—Purukutsa , son of Durgaha, being a 
prisoner, it is not said on what occasion, his queen propitiated 

Notes on Volume III 

367 

the seven Rishis to obtain a son who might take his father’s 
place : they advised her to worship Indra and Varwia , in 
consequence of which Trasadasyu was born.
