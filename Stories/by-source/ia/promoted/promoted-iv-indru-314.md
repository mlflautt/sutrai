# Iv Indru 314

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16683-16696

IV.  Indru. 

EVEN  as  the  wild-bull,  when  he  thirsts,  goes  to  the 

desert's  watery  pool, 
Come  hither  puickly  both  at  morning  and  at  eve,  and 

with  the  Kanvas  drink  thy  fill  ! 
2  May  the  drops  gladden  thee,  Lord  Indra,  aud  obtain 

bounty  for  him  who  pours  the  juice  ! 
Soma,  shed  in  the  press,  thou  stolest  and  didst  drink, 
and  hence  hast  won  surpassing  might.
