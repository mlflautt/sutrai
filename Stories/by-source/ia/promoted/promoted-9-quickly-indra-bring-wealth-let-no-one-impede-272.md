# 9 Quickly Indra Bring Wealth Let No One Impede 272

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2640-2644

9. Quickly, Indra, bring (wealth) ; let no one impede 
thee, for we know thee to be the lord of wealth, of all 
treasures: and since, Indra, thy greatness is munificence, 
therefore, lord of the tawny steeds, grant us (riches).
