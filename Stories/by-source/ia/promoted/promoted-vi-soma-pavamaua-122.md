# Vi Soma Pavamaua 122

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10474-10490

VI.  Soma  Pavamaua. 

FORTH  with  his  stream  who  gladdens  best  these  Soma 

juices  have  been  poured, 
Lauded  with  songs  for  mighty  strength. 

2  Thou  flowest  to  enjoy  the  milk,  and  bringest  valour, 

being  cleansed  : 
Winning  the  spoil  flow  hitherward  ! 

3  And, hymned by Jamadagni, let  all  nourishment 

that  kine  supply, 
And  general  praises,  flow  to  us  !
