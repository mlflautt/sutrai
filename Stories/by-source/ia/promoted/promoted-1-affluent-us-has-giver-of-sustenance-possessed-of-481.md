# 1 Affluent Us Has Giver Of Sustenance Possessed Of 481

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4152-4158

1. Affluent Us has, giver of sustenance, possessed of 
intelligence, be propitiated by the praise of him who lauds 
thee, (and worships) wdth (sacrificial) food: divine Ushas, 
adored by all, who (though) ancient art (still) young, the 
object of manifold worship, thou art present at the re¬ 
curring (morning) rite.
