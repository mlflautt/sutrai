# X India 157

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11921-11937

X.  India. 

WE  make  this Indra very strong to strike the 

mighty  Vritra  dead  : 
A  vigorous  hero  shall  he  be. 

2  This Indra, made for giving gifts, is stablished, 

mightiest,  in  strength, 
Bright,  meet  for  Sorna,  famed  in  song. 

3  By  song,  as  'twere, the powerful bolt which none 

may  parry,  was  prepared  : 
Strong  and  invincible  he  grew.
