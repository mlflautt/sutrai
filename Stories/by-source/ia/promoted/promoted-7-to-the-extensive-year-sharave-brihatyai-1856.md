# 7 To The Extensive Year Sharave Brihatyai 1856

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14862-14882

7. To the Extensive Year. — Sharave brihatyai: 
sharuh , sharat , samvatsarah, or sham , may mean nirriti, the 
female personification of evil, who, by some unaccountable 
inadvertence, I have turned, in a former passage* into a male

passage of the first Ashtaka adverted to, the epithets are 
feminine, and the change of sex is unwarranted.
