# 3 You Have Arrested One Luminous Wheel Etc 2224

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18759-18779

3. You have Arrested One Luminous Wheel, etc. — 
Irma any ad vapushe vapnsh-chakram rathasya yemathuh 

424 

Rig-veda Translation 

the passage is obscure, even with the aid of the Scholiast: 
irma he renders, rupam, form, vapushe, adityasya shobhayai , 
for the beauty or lustre’ of the sun ; vapus , he considers 
equivalent to vapushmat , having light or lustre, luminous, as 
an epithet of chakram : the fixing of one wheel of the car of 
the Ashwins has been alluded to before : see Vol. i, page 40, 
verse 19. 

The Ages of Mankind. — Nahusha yuga is the derivative 
•of nahusha , manushya , man ; yuga for yugani , is explained 
as used by metonymy for the divisions of time in general, as 
morning, noon, night, or the sacrifices offered at stated 
periods.
