# 1 The Righteous Agni When First Kindled On The 82

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1327-1332

1. The righteous (Agni) when first kindled on the 
several (altars) the object of adoration by all, whose hair 
is flame, and who is cleansed with butter, the purifier, the 
worthily-worshipped, is sprinkled with oblations for the 
worship of the gods.
