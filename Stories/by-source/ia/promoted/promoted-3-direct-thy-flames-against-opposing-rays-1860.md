# 3 Direct Thy Flames Against Opposing Rays 1860

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14923-14931

3. Direct thy Flames against Opposing Rays.— 
Prati spasho visrija : spashah is explained by Sayana, 
parabadhakan rashmir, or lie says it may mean charart , 
spies, sent to determine between true and false, satyan- 
ritavivekartham ; so Mahidhara interprets it, pranidhin, but 
he understands by it binders, imprisoTiers, bandhanakritah. 

Page 94.
