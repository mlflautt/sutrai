# 2 The Two Sons Of Bharata Devashravas And De 117

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 1565-1569

2. The two sons of Bharata, Devashravas and De- 
wavata, have churned the very powerful and wealth- 
bestowing Agni: look upon us, Agni, with vast riches, 
and be the bringer of food (to us) every day.
