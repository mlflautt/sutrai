# 1 This Invoker Of The Gods And Minister Of Frequen 589

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4956-4962

1. This invoker of the gods and minister of frequent 
worship, who is to be glorified at sacrifices, has been 
placed first (of the gods) by the performers of the rite: 
the Agni whom Apnavana and other Bhrigus lighted in 
the woods for the sake of all men, marvellous (in his acts), 
and sovereign (over all).
