# 1 Indra And Varuna Varuna And Indra What 879

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7146-7152

1. Indra (and Varuna), Varuna (and Indra), what 
praise of you accompanied by oblations may obtain for 
us felicity, (such as) the immortal invoker of the gods, 
.(Agni, may bestow): may (the praise) which is addressed 
by us to you both, Indra and Varuna, sanctified by acts 
and prompted by veneration, touch your hearts.
