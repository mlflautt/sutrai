# 4 The Minds Of The Devout Turn To Agni As The Eyes 1009

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8077-8089

4. The minds of the devout turn to Agni, as the eyes 
(of men) look towards the sun: when the multiform 
‘(heaven and earth) bring him forth along with the dawn, 
he is born as a white courser in the beginning of the days. 

5. (Agni), capable of birth, is born in the beginning 
of the days; radiant, he is deposited in the friendly 
woods, and then the adorable Agni, the offerer of the 
oblation, displaying seven precious (rays), is seated in 
•every house. 

174 Rig-veda Translation fV. 1.1
