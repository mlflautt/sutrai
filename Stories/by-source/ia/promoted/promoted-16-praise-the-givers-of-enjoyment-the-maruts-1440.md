# 16 Praise The Givers Of Enjoyment The Maruts 1440

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11095-11100

16. Praise the givers of enjoyment, (the Maruts), 
at the sacrifice of this worshipper, (for they) delight in 
(pious praise), like cattle in fodder : wherefore call upon 
them as if upon old friends ; praise them, desirous of 
praise, with a sacred hymn.
