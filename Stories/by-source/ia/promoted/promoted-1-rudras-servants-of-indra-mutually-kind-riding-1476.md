# 1 Rudras Servants Of Indra Mutually Kind Riding 1476

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11335-11340

1. Rudras, servants of Indra, mutually kind, riding 
in golden cars, come to the accessible (sacrifice): this our 
praise is addressed to you : (come to us as you came) from 
heaven, (bringing) oozing water to the thirsty (Gotama). 
longing for moisture.
