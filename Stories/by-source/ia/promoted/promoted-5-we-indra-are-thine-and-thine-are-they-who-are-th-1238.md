# 5 We Indra Are Thine And Thine Are They Who Are Th 1238

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9683-9688

5. We, Indra, are thine, and thine are they who are the 
leaders (of rites), promoters of (thy) strength, and willing 
applicants (to thee): all-powerful Indra, may adherents 
worthy of commendation, and faithful, come to us, like 
Bhaga, in battles.
