# 6 Your Wealth Is In The Family Of Jahnu 1828

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14503-14510

6. Your Wealth is in the Family of Jahnu.— 
Yuvor dravinam Jahnavyam : the latter is derived from 
Jahnu . and is explained merely by Jahnukulajayam , in her 
born in the race of Jahnu : it might imply the Ganges. 
Jahnavi, if wo had reason to suppose the legend of her 
origin from Jahnu was known to the Vedas: of course it 
was familiar to Sayana.
