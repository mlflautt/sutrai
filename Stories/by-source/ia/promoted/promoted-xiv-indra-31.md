# Xiv Indra 31

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6900-6913

XIV.  Indra. 

LOUD-SINGING  at  the  sacred  rite where Soma flows, 
we  priests  invoke 

With haste, that he may  help,  as  the  bard's  cheri- 
sher,  Indra  who  findeth  wealth  for you. 

2  Whom, fair of  cheek, in rapture  of  the  juice,  the 
firm  resistless  slayers  hinder  not : 

Giver of  glorious wealth to him who sings his 
praise,  honouring  him  who  toils  and  pours.
