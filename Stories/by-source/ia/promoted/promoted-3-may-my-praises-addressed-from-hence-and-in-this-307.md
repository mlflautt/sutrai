# 3 May My Praises Addressed From Hence And In This 307

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2949-2955

3. May my praises, addressed from hence and in this 
manner, attain Indra, to bring him hither to drink the 
Soma juice. 

4*. We invoke Indra to this rite with prayers to drink 
the Soma juice: may he, repeatedly invoked, come hither.
