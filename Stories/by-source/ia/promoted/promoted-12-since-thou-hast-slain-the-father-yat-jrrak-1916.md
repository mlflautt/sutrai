# 12 Since Thou Hast Slain The Father Yat Jrrak 1916

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15433-15438

12. Since thou hast Slain the Father.— Yat jrrak- 
tihinah pitaram padagrihya : the particulars of this incident 
are not related by Sayana, who contents himself with saying 
the allusions are variously explained by Taittiriyakas — 
TaiUiriya Sanhita, vi. i. hi. 6.
