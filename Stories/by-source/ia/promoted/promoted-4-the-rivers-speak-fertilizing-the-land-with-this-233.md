# 4 The Rivers Speak Fertilizing The Land With This 233

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2369-2374

4. The rivers speak] Fertilizing (the land) with this 
(our) current, we are flowing to the receptacle w T hich has 
been appointed by the deity (Jndra) : the course appointed 
for our going (permits) not of delay: of w-hat is the sage 
desirous, that he addresses the rivers ?
