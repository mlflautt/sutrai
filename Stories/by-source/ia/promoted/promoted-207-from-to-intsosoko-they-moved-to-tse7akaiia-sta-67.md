# 207 From To Intsosoko They Moved To Tse7Akaiia Sta 67

**Source:** Collected And Translated (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3755-3760

207. From To'intsosoko they moved to Tse7akaiia (Standing 
White Rock), and here they sojourned again for thirteen years. 
From the latter place they moved to Tse'pa^alkai (White on Face of 
Cliff), and here, once more, they remained for a period of thirteen 
years. During this time the monsters began to devour the people.
