# 14 Na Tapanti Rjharmani Yaska Explains The Last 1801

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14150-14164

14. Na tapanti rjharmani: Yaska explains the last 
Jiarmyam , a house ; but Sayana calls it a vessel termed 
Mahavira , used at tin* rite called Pragrihya , pragrihyakhya- 
karmopayuktam mahavirapatram, which the cattle do not 
warm by yielding their milk to it. 

Usurer.— Abhara pramagandasya vedas: maganda is 
explained by both scholiasts, kusidin, or usurer, one who says 
to himself, the money that goes from me will^eome back 
doubled, end pra, prefixed, is equivalent to a patronymic. 

Low Branches of the Community. — Naichashakham, 
that which belongs to a low (nicha) branch, or class ( shakha ); 
the posterity born of Shvdras? nd the like.
