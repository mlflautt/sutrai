# 13 Resplendent Agni Youngest Of The Gods The Satis 525

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4519-4524

13. Resplendent Agni, youngest of the gods, the satisfier 
of (the desires of) men, who art easily to be conducted 
(to the altar), bestow joy-yielding and abundant wealth 
for his preservation upon the worshipper who praises and 
worships thee and offers thee libations.
