# 8 Proceedest To The Wealth Op Sacrifice 2048

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16977-16991

8. Proceedest to the Wealth op Sacrifice.— 
Sansthe gad Agni iyase rayinam : lit. to the heap of riches,, 
but the riches, according to the Scholiast, are here those of 
the oblation, havirlakshananam. 

Kindled by Mortals, etc.— Martair vasubhir idhya- 
manah : the second, according to Sayana , is an epithet of the 

964 

Rig-veda Translation 

first, meaning, vasakaih , placing or fixing in an abode or place 
of dwelling, i.e., either the altar, or the chamber of sacrifice.
