# 14 Rending The Rocks They Worshipped Agni And 507

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4391-4396

14. Rending the rocks, they worshipped (Agni), and 
other (sages) taught everywhere their^(acts): unprovided 
with the means of (extricating) the cattle, they glorified 
the author of success, whence they found the light, and 
were thus enabled (to worship him) with holy ceremonies.
