# 6 None Of Them Are Older None Younger Than The 1498

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11474-11479

6. None of them are older, none younger (than the 
others): the destroyers of (foes), none hold a middle 
(rank), but all excel in glory : honourable by birth, 
having Prishni for your mother, do you, Maruts, favour¬ 
able to man, come from heaven to our presence.
