# 11 How Can We Offer Fit Praise To The Mighty Poste 1306

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10124-10129

11. How can we offer (fit praise) to the mighty posterity 
of Rudra, or to the all-knowing Bhaga, for (the sake of 
obtaining) riches : may the waters, may the plants, protect 
us, and the heaven, the woods, and the mountains, whose 
tresses are trees.
