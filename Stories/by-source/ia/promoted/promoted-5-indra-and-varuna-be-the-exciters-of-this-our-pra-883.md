# 5 Indra And Varuna Be The Exciters Of This Our Pra 883

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7176-7180

5. Indra and Varuna, be the exciters of this our praise v 
as the bull is of the milch cows : may that cow (of praise) 
yield us (reward), like a large cow that has gone forth to 
pasture, whose thousand channels (are filled) with milk.
