# 3 Adftwaryu Let Us Two Offer Praise Do Thou Concur 375

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3440-3445

3. Adftwaryu, let us two offer praise: do thou concur 
with me: let us address pleasing praise to Indra: sit 
down, Indra, on the sacred grass (prepared by) the insti- 
tutor of the rite ; and may our commendations be most 
acceptable to Indra.
