# 6 But These Wonderful Acts According To Sayana Are 2258

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 19106-19115

6. But these wonderful acts, according to Sayana , are 
not the acts of Varuna, but of Parameshwara, the Supreme 

432 

Rig-veda Translation 

Being, from his existing in the forms of Varuna and others, 
karma Parameshwarasyaiva uchitam na Varunasya , tasya
