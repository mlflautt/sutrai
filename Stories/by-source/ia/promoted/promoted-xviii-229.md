# Xviii 229

**Source:** Ratan Tata Library (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14944-14984

XVIII 

Ye nto wo 'Hene Kwa, 

Ye nto wo 'Birempon Kwa, 
Ohene Kwa, 

'Birempon Kwa, 

Ohene ne htian ? 

Ohene fata 'wurade, 

'Birempon fata 'wurade, 

Yerefie ne Ampasakyi Aniam- 
pam, 

Atakora bedi-ako e, 

Atakora kron kron, 

Atakora Kwaku Firampon, 
Damirifa / 

XIX 

Yerefi Ankamafo Akyaw Wusu, 
Akyaw 'Birempon, 

Owusu oka akyiri, 

Osai Tutu 'ba, 

Owusu Akwasi e, 

Owusu Sekyire, 

Onoborobo Osai Kojo 'Birempon, 
Osai Tutu 'ba.
