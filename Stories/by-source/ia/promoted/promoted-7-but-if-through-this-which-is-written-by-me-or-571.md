# 7 But If Through This Which Is Written By Me Or 571

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15857-15863

7. But if through this which is written by me, or 
through myself ^ he should come immediately (di- 
^andyi>f) unto P4rs, I shall then be seeking an 
opportunity even for the retirement of him himself ; 
I do not abominate it (madam 1 ^ m'an.f6m) when 
it is necessary for them and private, as is better.
