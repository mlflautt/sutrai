# 4 With A Willing Mind Indra Thou Associatest Us 1319

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10207-10211

4. With a (willing) mind, Indra, thou associatest us 
with cattle, with pious (priests) and, lord of steeds, with 
prosperity, with (sacrificial) food that is agreeable to the 
gods, and with the favour of the adorable deities.
