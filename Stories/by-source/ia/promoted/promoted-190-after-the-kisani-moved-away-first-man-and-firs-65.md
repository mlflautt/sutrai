**Source:** Collected And Translated (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3594-3722

190. After the Kisani moved away, First Man and First Woman, 
Black Body and Blue Body, set out to build the seven sacred moun- 
tains of the present Navaho land. They made them all of earth 
which they had brought from similar mountains in the fourth world. 
The mountains they made were Tslsnadsi'ni in the east, Tsotsi/ 
(Taylor, San Mateo) in the south, ZtokoslW (San Francisco) in the 
west, 2?epe'ntsa (San Juan) in the north, with DsT/nao^i/, Trolfhi, 
and Aku/anas^ani (Hosta Butte) in the middle of the land. 51
