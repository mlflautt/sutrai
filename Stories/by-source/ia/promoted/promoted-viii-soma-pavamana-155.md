# Viii Soma Pavamana 155

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11863-11879

VIII.  Soma  Pavamana 

FLOW onward with that stream wherewith thou 

gavest  splendour  to  the  sun, 
Speeding  the  waters  kind  to  man  ! 

2  He, Pavamana,  high o'er man, yoked the Sun's 

courser  Etasa 
To  travel  through  the  realm  of  air. 

3  Yea, those bay  steeds  he harnessed to  the  chariot 

that  the  Sun  might  come  : 
Indu,  he  said,  is  Indra's  self.
