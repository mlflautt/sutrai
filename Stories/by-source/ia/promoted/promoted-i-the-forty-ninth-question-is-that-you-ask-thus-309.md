# I The Forty Ninth Question Is That You Ask Thus 309

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9396-9413

I. The forty-ninth question is that you ask thus : 
If they should sell wine unto foreigners and infidels 
what is then the decision about it } 

* And, therefore, likely to be correct, as it is given in opposition 
to his own interest. 

* Mr4 has ‘begged at a price,’ by inserting a stroke. 

* Without holding it back for an exorbitant rise in prices. 

* That is, there is no harm in speculating upon prices, except 
in the case of necessaries of life. 

CHAPTER XLIX, Q-L, 4. 

177
