# 5 Phased With The Sacrifice As If It Was That Of M 217

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2260-2266

5. Phased with the sacrifice, as if it was that of Manu, 
drink, Indra, the Soma juice, for the sake of perpetual 
vigour: come,.lord of the tawny steeds, with the adorable 
Maruts , and with the traversers (of the air): send down 
the waters (of the firmament) to (blend with) the waters 
of the earth.
