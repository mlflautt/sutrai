# 10 May We Glorifying You Both Be Delighted By Rich 900

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7273-7277

10. May we, glorifying you both, be delighted by riches ; 
may the gods be pleased by oblations, the cows by pasture: 
and do you, Indra and Varuna, daily grant us that same 
milch cow, (riches), free from any imperfection.
