# 2 A Mysterious Name That Of Vaishwanara Ac 2045

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16953-16959

2. A Mysterious Name.— That of Vaishwanara , ac¬ 
cording to the' Scholiast, the friend of all, vishwa , men, nara. 

With Milk and Butter.— Anjanti mitrarn sudhitam na 
gobhih : the last, lit. with cows, is put by metonymy for the 
products of the cow.
