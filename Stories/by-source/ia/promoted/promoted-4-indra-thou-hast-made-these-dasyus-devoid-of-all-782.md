# 4 Indra Thou Hast Made These Dasyus Devoid Of All 782

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6338-6342

4. Indra, thou hast made these Dasyus devoid of all 
(good qualities) ; thou hast made the servile races abject: 
may you, (Soma and Indra), repel (and) destroy (your) 
enemies: accept (our) homage for their destruction.
