# 2 As Elders Send Forth Their Young So The Gods Hav 694

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5707-5715

2. As elders (send forth their young), so the gods have 
sent thee (against Vritra) : thence thou becamest, Indra. 
who art the abode of truth, the sovereign of the world; 
thou hast slain the slumbering Ahi for (the release of) the 
water, and hast marked out (the channels of) the all, de¬ 
lighting rivers. 

118 Rig-veda Translation [IV. 2.9
