# 4 These Well Combined Rays Of The Sun Are Descendi 1350

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10430-10435

4. These well-combined rays (of the sun) are descending 
with intent to proceed on the path (of the sacrifice), ampli¬ 
fiers of the ceremony for this (its institutor): operating 
with these swift-moving, all-regulating rays, he, (Ajditya), 
steals the waters (that flow) in low places.
