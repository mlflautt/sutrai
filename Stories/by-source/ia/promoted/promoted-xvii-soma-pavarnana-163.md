# Xvii Soma Pavarnana 163

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12103-12115

XVII.  Soma  Pavarnana. 

FLOW onward, Soma,  as a  mighty  sea,  as  Father  of 
the  Gods,  to  every  form  ! 

2  Flow  on,  O  Soma, radiant for  the Gods,  blissful  to 

heaven  and  earth  and  living  things  ! 

3  Thou  art,  bright  juice,  sustainer  of  the  sky  :  flow, 

mighty,  in  accordance  with  true  law  !
