# 4 Well Clad And Hung With Wreaths Comes The Youthf 20

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 908-912

4. Well clad and hung with wreaths comes the youthful 
(pillar): most excellent it is as soon as generated : steadfast 
and wise venerators of the gods, meditating piously in 
their minds, raise it up.
