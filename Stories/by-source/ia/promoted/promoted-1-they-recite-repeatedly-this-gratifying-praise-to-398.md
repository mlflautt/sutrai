# 1 They Recite Repeatedly This Gratifying Praise To 398

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3577-3587

1. They recite repeatedly this gratifying praise to the 
great product of sacrifice (Agni) : may he who (is endowed) 
with domestic radiance hear us: may the imperishable 
Agni, (endowed) with divine radiance, hear us. 

hi. 5.1] 

Third Mandala 

67
