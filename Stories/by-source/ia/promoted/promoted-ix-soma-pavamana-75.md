# Ix Soma Pavamana 75

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8497-8515

IX.  Soma  Pavamana. 

THE  living  drops  of  Soma  juice  pour,  as  they  flow 
the  gladdening  drink, 

Intelligent  drops  above  the  station  of  the  sea,  ex- 
hilarating, dropping  meath. 

2  May  Pavamana,  King  and  God,  speed  with  his  wave 

over  the  sea  the  lofty  rite ! 

Do  thou  by  Mitra's  and  by  Varuna's  decree  flow 
furthering  the  lofty  rite  : 

3  Far-seeing, lovely, guided by the men, the God 

whose  habitation  is  the  sea  !
