# 13 Preserved The Blind Son Etc A Llusion Is Made 1864

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 14961-14973

13. Preserved the Blind Son, etc.--A llusion is made, 
according to the commentator, to the well-known filthy legend 
of the birth of Dirghatamas, who, it is here said by Sayana , 
recovered his sight by worshipping Agni. 

Cherished those Benevolent Rays. —Raraksha tan 
sukrito vishwavedas : it is not very clear whether vishwavedas 
applies to Agni or to Dirghatamas : Sayana , by inserting 
hhavan , your honour, adopts the former, but it seems 
questionable. 

Page 96.
