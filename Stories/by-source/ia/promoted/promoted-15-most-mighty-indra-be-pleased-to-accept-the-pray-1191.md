# 15 Most Mighty Indra Be Pleased To Accept The Pray 1191

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9372-9377

15. Most mighty Indra, be pleased to accept the pray¬ 
ers which we are about to offer, and the present praises 
which we repeat: firm, doing pious acts, and desirous 
of wealth, I have fabricated acceptable and pious works 
like (rich) garments, and like a chariot.
