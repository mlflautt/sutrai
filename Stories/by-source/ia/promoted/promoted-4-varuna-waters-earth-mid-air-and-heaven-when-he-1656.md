# 4 Varuna Waters Earth Mid Air And Heaven When He 1656

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12626-12631

4. Varuna waters earth, mid-air, and heaven, when he 
pleases (to send forth) the milk (of the cloud) : thereupon 
the mountains clothe (their summits) with the rain-cloud, 
and the hero, (Maruts), exulting in their strength, compel 
(the clouds) to relax.
