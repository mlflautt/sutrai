# 13 Go Not Ever Agni To The Sacrifice Of Any One Wh 545

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4643-4648

13. Go not ever, Agni, to the sacrifice of any one who 
injures us; nor to that of a malevolent neighbour; nor 
to that of an (unnatural) relation: accept not the due 
(oblation) from an insincere brother: let us not derive 
enjoyment from the enemy of a friend.
