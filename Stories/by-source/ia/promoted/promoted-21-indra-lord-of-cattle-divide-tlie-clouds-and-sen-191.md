# 21 Indra Lord Of Cattle Divide Tlie Clouds And Sen 191

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2067-2072

21. Indra, lord of cattle, (divide tlie clouds and send us) 
rain, whence nutritious food may come to us: showerer 
{of benefits), thou art spread through the sky, and powerful 
through truth: Maghavan, consider thyself as our 
protector.
