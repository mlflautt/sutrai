# V 3 8 1284

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9977-9983

V. 3. 8. 

Indra is tho divinity of the first four verses, Surya of the fifth, 
Atri of the last four, but he is also the Rishi of the whole ; the metre 
of the throe first stanzas in Ushnih, of the fifth and ninth Anushtubh, 
of the rest Trishtubh.
