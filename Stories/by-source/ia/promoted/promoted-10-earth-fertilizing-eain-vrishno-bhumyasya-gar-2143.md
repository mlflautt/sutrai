# 10 Earth Fertilizing Eain Vrishno Bhumyasya Gar 2143

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17952-17970

10. Earth-fertilizing Eain. — Vrishno bhumyasya gar- 
bham : bhumya 3 Sayana explains by, either suited to the 

406 

Rig-veda Translation 

earth or the firmament, bhumir-antarikskam : in the latter 
case the embryo of the firmamental rain will mean Agni as 
the lightning, according to Sayana. 

Page 222. 

With his Withering Rays. — Orinite Agni etari na 
shushaih ; mayi gantari na krudhyati sukhakarai rashmibhih, 
who is not angry upon me when going with his agreeable 
rays ; but this seems inconsistent with the sense given to 
the verb : shusha, drying up, would be more congruous.
