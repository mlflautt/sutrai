# 5 And The Third Night On The Fresh Arrival Of A 138

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3899-3911

5. And the third night, on the fresh arrival of a 
dawn, its sin, in the frightful, polluted shape of a 
maiden (/^aritik) who is an injurer, comes to meet 
it with the store of its sin; and a stinking northerly' 
wind comes out to meet it, and it comes on shud- 
deringly, quiveringly and unwillingly running to 
the account. 6. And through being deceived and 
deceiving, heresy (a&4rfin-dhr61h), unrelenting and 
false® accusation of constant companions, and the 
v{ldiQ.-spread sinfulness of a fiend-like existence 
(drii_^-stihih) it is ruined, falls from the bridge, 
and is precipitated to hell.
