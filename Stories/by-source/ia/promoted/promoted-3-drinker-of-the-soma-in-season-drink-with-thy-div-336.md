# 3 Drinker Of The Soma In Season Drink With Thy Div 336

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3158-3163

3. Drinker of the Soma in season, drink with thy divine 
friend the Maruts the Soma that is presented by us : 
those Maruts whose aid you have enjoyed (in battle) r 
and who, following thee, have given thee the strength 
whereby thou hast slain Vritra.
