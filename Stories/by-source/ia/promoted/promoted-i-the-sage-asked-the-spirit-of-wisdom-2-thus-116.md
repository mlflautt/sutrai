# I The Sage Asked The Spirit Of Wisdom 2 Thus 116

**Source:** The Text Is Fly (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4849-4853

I. The sage asked the spirit of wisdom (2) thus: 
‘ Wherefore have the people who were from Gayd- 
mar^!^^, and those, too, who were lords and monarchs, 
from Hoshing^, the Pd^d^fS?, even unto Vutisp the 
king of kings, been such doers of their own wills ?
