# 153 The Custom Of Easter Smacks 181

**Source:** The Golden Bough (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 16172-16377

153.  The  custom  of  "Easter  Smacks" 
can  be  traced  back  to  the  twelfth 
century,  when  the  practice  was  for 
women  to  beat  their  husbands  on 
Easter  Monday  and  for  husbands  to 
retaliate  on  their  wives  on  Easter 
Tuesday.  See  J.  Belethus,  Rationale 
Divinorum  Officiorum,  cap.  1 20,  ap- 
pended to  G.  Durandus's  Rationale 
Divinorum  Officiorum  (Lyons,  1584), 
p.  546  recto:  "  Notandum  quoque  est 
in  plerisqtie  regionibus  secundo  die  post 
Pascha  mulieres  viaritos  suos  verberare, 
ac  vicissim  viros  eas  tcrtio  die  quemad- 
modum  licebat  servis  in  Decembri 
dominos  suos  impime  accusare. "
