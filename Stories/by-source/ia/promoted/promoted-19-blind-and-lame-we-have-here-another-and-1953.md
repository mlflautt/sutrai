# 19 Blind And Lame We Have Here Another And 1953

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15840-15850

19. Blind and Lame. —We have here another, and 
somewhat contradictory statement from that of a former 
passage, when the blindness and lameness were attributed 
to one person, Paravrij , Vol. n. 142, verse 12, and note: 
here we have not only the dual, jahita for jahitau , but also 
dwa for dwau , the two, which agrees better with the still 
earlier allusion to the same defects in two different individuals ; 
Vol. i. 170, verse 8, and note. 

Page 136.
