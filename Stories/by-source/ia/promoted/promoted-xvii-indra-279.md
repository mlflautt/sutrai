# Xvii Indra 279

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15639-15652

XVII.  Indra. 

LET  us  not  tire  or  be  afraid  with  thee,  the  mighty, 
for  our  friend ! 

May  we  see Turvasa  and  Yadu !  thy  great  deed, 
O  hero,  must  be  glorified. 

2  On  his  left  hip  the  hero  hath reclined  himself ;  the 
proffered  feast  offends  him  not. 

The  milk  is  blended  with  the  honey of  the  bee : 
quickly  come  hither,  haste,  and  drink  !
