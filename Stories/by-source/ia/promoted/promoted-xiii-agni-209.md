# Xiii Agni 209

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13714-13726

XIII.  Agni. 

THEE  have  we  chosen,  skilfullest  in  sacrifice,  immortal, 

Priest,  among  the  Gods, 
Best  finisher  of  this  holy  rite  : 
2  The  Waters'  Child,  the  blessed,  brightly-shining  one, 

Agni  whose  light  is  excellent. 
May  he  by  sacrifice  win  us  in heaven the grace of 

Mitra,  Varuna,  and  the  Floods  !
