# 17 May The Horses Be Steady The Axle Be Strong The 389

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3527-3531

17. May the horses be steady, the axle be strong, the 
pole be not defective, the yoke not be rotten ; may Indra 
preserve the two yoke-pins from decay: car with un¬ 
injured felloes, be ready for us.
