# Iii Indra 328

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 17210-17228

III.  Indra. 

EVEN  as  a  car  to  give  us  aid,  we  draw  thee  nigh to 

favour  us, 
Strong  in  thy  deeds,  quelling  attack, Indra, Lord, 

mightiest !  of  the  brave. 

2  Great in thy power and wisdom, strong, with 

thought  that  comprehendeth  all  ! 
Wide  hast  thou  spread  in  majesty. 

3  Thou  very  mighty  one,  whose hands by virtue  of 

thy  greatness  wield 
The  golden  bolt  that  breaks  its  way  !
