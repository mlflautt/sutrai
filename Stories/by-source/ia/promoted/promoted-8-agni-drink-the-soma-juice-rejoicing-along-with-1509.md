# 8 Agni Drink The Soma Juice Rejoicing Along With 1509

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11547-11552

8. Agni, drink the /Soma-juice, rejoicing, along with 
the Maruts, resplendent, adorable, associated in troops, 
purifying all, animating and long-lived: drink, 
Vaishwanara, who art identified with the ancient emblem 
(of flame).
