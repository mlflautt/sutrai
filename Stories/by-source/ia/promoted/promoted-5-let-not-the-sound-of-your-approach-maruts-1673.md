# 5 Let Not The Sound Of Your Approach Maruts 1673

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 12722-12729

5. Let not the sound (of your approach, Maruts), 
which is mighty, the announcer of rain, the shedder of 
light, diffusive, loud, alarm Evayamarut: that sound 
wherewith, overcoming (your foes), you who are self- 
irradiating, lasting-rayed, ornamented with golden orna¬ 
ments, self-weaponed, bestowing food, accomplish your 
functions.
