# Russian Folk Tales 53

**Source:** Translated From The Russian (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 3683-3690

RUSSIAN FOLK-TALES 

So the boy got a fine bride, married her, and drove to 
see his parents. They had long given Mm up for dead, 
and it was no wonder ; for he had been away for three 
years, although it had seemed to Mm only twenty-four 
hours that he had stayed with the DeUl.
