# I Soma Pavamana 18

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 6574-6590

I.  Soma  Pavamana. 

SING  forth to Ijidu, O  ye  men, to  him who  now 

is  purified, 
Fain  to  pay  worship  to  the  Gods  ! 

2  Together with thy pleasant  juice the Atharvans 

have  commingled  milk, 
Divine,  God-loving,  for  the  God. 

3  Bring  health  to  cattle  with  thy  flow, health to  the 

people,  health  to  steeds, 
Health,  O  thou  King,  to  growing  plants  !
