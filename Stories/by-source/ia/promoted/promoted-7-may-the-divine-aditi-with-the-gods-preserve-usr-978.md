# 7 May The Divine Aditi With The Gods Preserve Usr 978

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7860-7864

7. May the divine Aditi, with the gods, preserve usr 
may the ever-attentive protecting (deity, Indra,) protect 
us : we are not able to withhold the elevated (sacrificial) 
food of Mitra, of Varuna, of Agni.
