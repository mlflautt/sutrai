# 2 We Seek To Unite With Our Desires Te Raya Y 2178

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18314-18323

2. We Seek to Unite with Our Desires.— Te raya y 
te hi a priche sachemahi sachathyai, is not very perspicuous, 
and the explanation of Sayana, followed in the translation, 
does not materially improve it, te ubhaye dhanena sachemahi , 
te hi te aparchaniyah sarvaih kamaih sangachchhemahi. 

414
