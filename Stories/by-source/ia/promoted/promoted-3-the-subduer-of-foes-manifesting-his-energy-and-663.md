# 3 The Subduer Of Foes Manifesting His Energy And 663

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5493-5498

3. The subduer of foes, manifesting his energy and 
hurling his thunderbolt, shattered the mountain by his 
strength: he slew Vritra with the thunderbolt, exulting, 
and the waters whose obstructor was destroyed rushed 
forth with rapidity.
