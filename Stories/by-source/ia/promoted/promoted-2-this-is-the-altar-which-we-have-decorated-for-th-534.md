# 2 This Is The Altar Which We Have Decorated For Th 534

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4582-4587

2. This is the altar which we have decorated for thee, 
as a wife attached to her husband puts on elegant garments 
(to gratify him): maturer of good works, sit down in our 
presence invested (with radiance), while thy flames incline 
towards thee.
