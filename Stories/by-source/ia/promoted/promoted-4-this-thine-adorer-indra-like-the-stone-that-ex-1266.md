# 4 This Thine Adorer Indra Like The Stone That Ex 1266

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9861-9883

4. This thine adorer, Indra, like the stone (that ex- 
presses the Soma juice), urges praise to thee, participating 
in the great (reward) : thou bestowest, Maghavan, riches 
with thy left hand, thou bestowest them, lord of horses, 
with the right: be not reluctant.
