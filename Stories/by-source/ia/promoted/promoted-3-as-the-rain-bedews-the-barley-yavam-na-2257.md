# 3 As The Rain Bedews The Barley Yavam Na 2257

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 19098-19105

3. As the Rain Bedews the Barley. — Yavam na 
vrishtir, vyunatti bhurm : Sayana is disposed to render vrishti 
by sekta, the sprinkler, the man, puman , who waters the soil, 
but this does not seem necessary, and it is not so explained 
in the Nirukta, 10. 4. 

Page 282.
