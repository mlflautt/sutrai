# 15 Wide Fields Vast Treasure Spacious Pastures 205

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2178-2182

15. Wide fields, vast treasure, (spacious) pastures, 
has the much-knowing (Indra) bestowed upon his friends : 
the radiant Indra with the leaders (of rites, the Maruts) 
generated the sun, the dawn, the earth, and fire.
