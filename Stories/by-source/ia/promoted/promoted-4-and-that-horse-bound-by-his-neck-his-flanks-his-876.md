# 4 And That Horse Bound By His Neck His Flanks His 876

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7126-7130

4. And that horse bound by his neck, his flanks, his 
mouth, accelerates his. paces: Dadhikra increasing in 
vigour after the (sacred rite), following the windings of 
the roads, goes still more rapidly.
