# V 3 9 1300

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 10078-10084

V. 3. 9] 

may the ministrant priest, (Atri), of the son of Ushij 
(Kakshivat), be made happy by those swift-going (horses) 
which are the swift-going (horses), Maruts, of you who 
are rapid in motion.
