# 4 Thou Art The Showerer Of Benefits Thou Art Born 1259

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 9811-9815

4. Thou art the showerer (of benefits); thou art born 
to (bestow) riches ; thy strength rains (blessings); thy 
self-invigorated mind is the restrainer (of adversaries) ; 
thy manhood, Indra, is the destroyer of multitudes.
