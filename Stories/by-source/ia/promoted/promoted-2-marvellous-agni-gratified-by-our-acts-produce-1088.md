# 2 Marvellous Agni Gratified By Our Acts Produce 1088

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8631-8635

2. Marvellous Agni, (gratified) by our acts, (produce)* 
in us greatness of vigour: in thee abides the strength 
destroying evil spirits: thou who art to be worshipped,, 
like Mitra, art the doer (of great deeds).
