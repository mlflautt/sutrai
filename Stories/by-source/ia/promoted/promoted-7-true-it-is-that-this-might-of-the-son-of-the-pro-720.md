# 7 True It Is That This Might Of The Son Of The Pro 720

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5898-5904

7. True it is that this might of the son of the protector 
of the world, the showerer (of benefits), affects for his 
advantage the offerer of praise: it (prevails) in the secret 
(thoughts) of the worshipper, and in his dwelling, for 
(the accomplishment of his) pious acts, (the attainment 
of his) desires, and his delight.
