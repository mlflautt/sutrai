# Ii Adityas 189

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 13113-13129

II.  Adityas. 

So  when  the  Sun  hath  risen  to-day  may  sinless  Mitra, 

Aryaman, 
Bhaga,  and  Savitar  send  us  forth  ! 

2  May this our home be guarded well  : forward, 

ye  bounteous,  on  the  way, 
Who  bear  us  safely  o'er  distress  ! 

3  Yea, Aditi,  and  those  great  Kings  whose  statute  is 

inviolate, 
Are  sovrans  of  a  vast  domain.
