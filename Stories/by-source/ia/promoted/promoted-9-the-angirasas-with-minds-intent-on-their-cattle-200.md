# 9 The Angirasas With Minds Intent On Their Cattle  200

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2136-2140

9. The Angirasas , with minds intent on their cattle, (sat 
down to worship Indra) with hymns, following the road 
to immortality: great was this their perseverance, by 
which they sought for months to accomplish (their ends)..
