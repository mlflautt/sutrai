# 7 The Streams Of Ghi Fall Copious Swift As The Win 1001

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8018-8022

7. The streams of Ghi fall copious, swift as the wind, 
and rapid as the waters of a river down a declivity, break¬ 
ing through the confining banks, and hurrying on with 
their waves, like a high-spirited steed.
