# V Soma Pavamana 55

**Source:** Hymns Of The Samaveda (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7833-7849

V.  Soma  Pavamana. 

WE seek  to win  thy  friendly  love,  even  PavainAna'a 

flowing  o'er 
The  limit  of  the  cleansing  sieve. 

2  With  those  same  waves which  in  their  stream  o*er- 

flow  the  purifiying  sieve, 
Soma,  be  gracious  unto  us! 

3  O  Soma,  being  purified,  bring  us  from  all  sides — for 

thou  .canst — 
Riches  and  food  with  hero  sons  !
