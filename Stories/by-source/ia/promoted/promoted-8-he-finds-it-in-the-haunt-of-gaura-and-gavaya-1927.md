# 8 He Finds It In The Haunt Of Gaura And Gavaya 1927

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 15551-15564

8. He finds it in the Haunt of Gaura and Gavaya 
— Vidat gaurasya gavayasya yoke : vidat here has no govern¬ 
ment, and goha for griha, a dwelling, is a strange term as 
applicable to the Gaura and Gavaya which Sayana says, 
are two species of mriga , a deer, or any wild animal ; but 
they are in fact two kinds of wild cattle, Bos-gavceus, or 
Gavazus-frontalis , and Bibos gaurus , or B. cavifrons , con¬ 
founding the latter also with the Bibos asil of Silhet : the pur¬ 
port of the expression, according to the Scholiast, is, that 
Indra obtains those two animals tau dwau pashu labhate , 
either for himself as sacrificial flesh, or for his worshippers, 
some of whom, at least, even now. would not object to eat 
the flesh of the wild oxen.
