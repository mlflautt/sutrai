# 9 No Vulture May Be Molested 30

**Source:** Bought With The Income (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 11025-11035

9.  No  vulture  may  be  molested. 

10.  No  horse-hide  sandals  may  be  worn  in  the  palace. 

11.  No  load  packed  in  palm  branches  may  be  carried  into  Coomassie. 

12.  No  one  may  look  at  the  king's  wives,  and  every  one  is  to 
hide  when  the  king's  eunuchs  call  to  announce  their  approach. 

13.  No  one  may  use  the  king's  oath  without  due  cause.
