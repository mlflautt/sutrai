# 2 Ushas Who Art Divine And Immortal Mounted In A 482

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4159-4163

2. Ushas, who art divine and immortal, mounted in a 
golden chariot, do thou shine radiant, causing to be heard 
the sounds of truth: may thy vigorous and well-trained 
horses bring thee, who art golden-haired, (hither).
