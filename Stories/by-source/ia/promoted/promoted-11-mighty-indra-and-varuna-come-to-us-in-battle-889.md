# 11 Mighty Indra And Varuna Come To Us In Battle 889

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7212-7216

11. Mighty Indra and Varuna, come to us in battle 
with (your) powerful protections, and where the bright* 
(weapons) play amidst the (hostile) hosts, may we be 
triumphant in that conflict (through) your (favour).
