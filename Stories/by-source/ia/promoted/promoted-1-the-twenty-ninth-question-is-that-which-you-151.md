# 1 The Twenty Ninth Question Is That Which You 151

**Source:** Sacred Books Of The East (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4121-4126

1. The twenty-ninth question is that which you 
ask thus : The third night, in the light of dawn, 
what is the reason for consecrating separately the 
three sacred cakes* with three dedications (shnh- 
man)?
