# New York 3

**Source:** Gods and Fighting Men (Celtic)
**Lines:** 115-147

NEW  YORK 

My  Friends,  those  I  know  and  those  I  do  not  know, 
I  am  glad  in  the  year  of  the  birth  of  your  Society 
to  have  this  book  to  offer  you. 

It  has  given  great  courage  to  many  workers  here 
— working  to  build  up  broken  walls — to  know  you 
have  such  friendly  thoughts  of  them  in  your  minds. 
A  few  of  you  have  already  come  to  see  us,  and  we 
begin  to  hope  that  one  day  the  steamers  across  the 
Atlantic  will  not  go  out  full,  but  come  back  full, 
until  some  of  you  find  your  real  home  is  here,  and 
say  as  some  of  us  say,  like  Finn  to  the  woman  of 
enchantments —
