# 2 Decorating Thee I Address Sincere Prayers To The 244

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 2449-2453

2. Decorating thee, I address sincere prayers to thee who 
art adorable and mighty, for the sake of (obtaining) food : 
thou, Indra, art the preceder of men the descendants of 
Manit, and of the descendants of the gods.
