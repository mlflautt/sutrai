# 7 Ten Months Worship Or When The Priests Of 2164

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 18172-18181

7. Ten-Months Worship.— -Or, when the priests of 
both the nine and ten months rites offered worship : see VoL 
I, p. 276, note on I. 11. 5. 4. 

Page 233. 

Sarama. — Sarama , according to Sayana , may here signify 
either flowing, eulogistic, or sacred speech, sharanashila 
stutirupa wk, or, as usual, the bitch of Indra.
