# 9 Associated With Sacrifice And Knowing Those Men 502

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 4355-4360

9. Associated with sacrifice, and knowing those men 
(who are engaged in good works), they lead him with the 
strong cord (of praise): he, the divine Agni, fulfilling 
(all desires), abides in the dwelling of this mortal, and 
obtains fellowship in his wealth.
