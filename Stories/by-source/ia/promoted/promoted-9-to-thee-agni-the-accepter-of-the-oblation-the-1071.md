# 9 To Thee Agni The Accepter Of The Oblation The 1071

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 8514-8518

9. To thee, Agni, the accepter of the oblation, the 
upholder (of all), there is pleasure (from our praise): do 
thou bestow upon these thy worshippers wealth, and food, 
and a heart (grateful for thy favour).
