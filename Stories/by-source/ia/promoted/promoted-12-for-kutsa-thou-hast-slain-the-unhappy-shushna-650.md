# 12 For Kutsa Thou Hast Slain The Unhappy Shushna 650

**Source:** Htst Airp Swirtc (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 5410-5416

12. For Kutsa, thou hast slain the unhappy Shushna, 
and, in the forepart of the day, attended by thousands, 
(thou hast slain) Kuyava with the thunderbolt: thou 
hast swiftly destroyed the Dasyus , and thou hast cut them 
to pieces in the battle, with the wheel (of the chariot of) 
the sun.
