# 30 The Kar Fish Too Ever Circles Around It And 206

**Source:** The Text Is Fly (Internet Archive (unmapped))
**Lines:** 7497-7506

30. The Kar fish too, ever circles around it, and 
always keeps the frog and other noxious creatures 
away from it. 

31. ‘ G6pait6shah® is in Airdn-v6^6, within the 
region of Khvaniras®. 32. From foot to mid-body 
he is an ox, and from mid-body to the top he is a 
man. 33. And at all times he sits on the sea-shore, 
(34) and always performs the ceremonial of the 
sacred beings, and pours holy-water into the sea.
